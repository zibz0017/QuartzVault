# Basic Background
1. There is rule that EIN איסור CHAL AL איסור.
2. However, if an איסור is considered a "davar mosif", then we do say that both issurim do apply.
3. The 'גמ discusses what happens when you cook/eat/get hannah from niveilah or cheilev (foods that carry their own איסור) with milk.
# HALACHA
## Eating
1. There is no איסור of בב״ח. This is considered Ein איסור Chal Al איסור and so their is no issue. (Obviously, you still can't eat it because there is still an איסור of Niveilah/cheilev).
## Cooking
1. There is an איסור of בב״ח. There is no איסור of cooking niveilah/cheilev, so Ein איסור Chal Al איסור does not apply.
## HANNAH
1. Hannah is tricky because eating is a form of hannah, and there is an איסור of eating niveilah/cheilev.
   At the same time, hannah includes more than just eating, and there is no איסור on getting hannah from niveilah/cheilev (aside from eating it).
2. This leads to a machlokes about this case.
3. As such, we פסקען that במקום הפסד, one can be מקל, but otherwise, it is אסור.
# Potential Applications
1. Sometimes pet food can come with niveilah and milk. May one buy this pet food to feed their animal?
2. Can one work in a non-kosher restaurant if they will have to cook cheeseburgers or something like that?
3. Can one do Uber eats if they will have to deliver cheeseburgers or foods like that? Is that considered getting hannah from these foods?
