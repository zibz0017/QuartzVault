# סתירה in the רמ"א's שיטה
1. In the ת"ח, the רמ"א, says that one may put foods that are not normally washed onto a plate of a גוי.
2. However, over here (as well as elsewhere in the ת"ח), the רמ"א, based off the טור, says that one may not put food on a plate that has absorbed taste through heat if it is not a food that is normally washed.
## Answers
### First Answer
1. The ת"ח was talking about a case where the plate did not absorb taste through heat. The רמ"א here was talking about such a case.
### Second Answer
1. The ת"ח was talking about a case where the plate was clean. The רמ"א here was talking about a case where the plate was dirty.
# Opinion of the ש"ך
1. The ש"ך asks numerous questions on the רמ"א's answers. Instead, he gives his own understanding of the טור.
# Opinion of the ט"ז
1. The ט"ז asks a question on the second answer.
2. Why would the טור be talking about a case where there are ממשות of איסור on the plate? If there were ממשות on the plate then one may not put anything there at all.
3. I don't really get this question. Why can't the טור be telling us a חידוש that one may put a food that will normally be washed onto a plate that has ממשות of איסור, and one may not put a food that will not normally be washed, because we don't assume that the person will remember. The ט"ז himself [[Not Allowing Something לכתחילה because a Person may Forget|discusses why we don't rely on the person to remember to wash the food before he eats it]].
