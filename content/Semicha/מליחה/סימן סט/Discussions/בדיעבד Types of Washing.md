# Washing the Meat for הדחה ראשונה with only a Little Water
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Whether washing the meat a little makes it מותר depends on the reason that one washes the meat.
   According to the שיטה that holds that the reason for הדחה ראשונה is because we need to soften the meat, doing a small הדחה ראשונה will not help. According to this שיטה, the meat would be אסור.
   However, according to those who say that the reason for doing הדחה ראשונה is to get rid of the blood on the surface, this would be effective.
   As such, we are willing to rely on the שיטה that says that the reason for הדחה ראשונה is to get rid of blood on the surface.
   (This discussion also applies to the התר for meat that is 60x the surface blood which is salted without any הדחה ראשונה at all.)[^1]</span>
# Washing the Meat for הדחה ראשונה with a Lot of Water without Soaking
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">From the previous discussion, it seems that we are even more מקל by a case where the meat is scrubbed because this would certainly be effective according to those who say the reason for הדחה ראשונה is to get rid of the blood on the surface and perhaps would even be effective according to those who say that the reason for soaking the meat is to soften it for the salting process.
   Perhaps this last point isn't so clear and so this is only מותר once one has already done it.[^2]</span>
