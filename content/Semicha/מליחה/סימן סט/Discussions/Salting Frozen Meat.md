# Salting Frozen Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the meat is frozen, one needs to wait for the meat to thaw before salting it.</span>
# Meat Froze after it Has Been Salted
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the meat was salted and then it was frozen before the שיעור מליחה passed, one should resalt it when it thaws and wait the שיעור מליחה.
   If one did not resalt it after it thawed but waited שיעור מליחה before cooking it, it is מותר בדיעבד.&lrm;[^16]</span>
