# Reasoning Behind מחלוקת whether Meat Becomes אסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Whether the meat becomes אסור or not is dependent on why we do הדחה ראשונה.
   According to the שיטה that the reason for הדחה ראשונה is to wash off the blood on the surface, the meat becomes אסור if it is salted. According to the שיטה that the reason is to soften the meat, the meat remains מותר.</span>
## Meat Becomes אסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are multiple reasons why the שיטה that holds that the reason for הדחה ראשונה is to wash off the surface blood holds that salting without doing הדחה ראשונה makes the meat אסור:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The surface blood is בולע into the meat. When it comes to דם בעין don't say any of the usual התירים that would cause this not to be בולע or to come out later (כבולעו כך פולטו,&lrm; איידי דטריד למיפלט לא בלע, &lrm;דם מישרק שריק)[^1]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The blood is absorbed into the meat. If the meat still had some of its own blood in there, this surface blood would come out with the blood that is still in there. However, the blood comes out when you salt it so there is none of the blood left in there. As such, the surface blood can't be pulled out.[^2]</span>
## Meat Stays מותר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are multiple reasons why the שיטה that holds that the reason for הדחה ראשונה is to soften the meat holds that salting without doing הדחה ראשונה does not make the meat אסור:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is no blood on the surface.[^1]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat needs to be softened so the blood inside can come out. Because it has not been softened, the blood remains inside. As such, even though there is surface blood and it gets absorbed into the meat, it comes out with the blood inside when it gets salted later.[^2]</span>
