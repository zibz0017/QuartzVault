# Basic שיטות
## מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One needs to do נפיצה or שטיפה and then wash it twice.[^1]</span>
## רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One must wash it three times. However, doing נפיצה or שטיפה count as one of the washings.[^2]</span>
# Question on the רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The לשון of the רמ"א sounds like he is coming to add something to the דין of the מחבר. However, what he actually says comes out to be the same as the מחבר.&lrm;[^3]</span>
# Answers
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ש"ך and ט"ז come up with alternative ways to read the מחבר and רמ"א so that it comes out that they are arguing, For more details, see [[סימן סט הלכות#Getting the Salt off]].</span>
