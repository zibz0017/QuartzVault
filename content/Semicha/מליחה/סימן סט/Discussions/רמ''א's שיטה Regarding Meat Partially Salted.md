# Has not Been Cooked yet
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a potential concern that if you salt the second side, the blood being expunged will infect the first side again and won't be able to be pulled out. As long as the first side is being פולט ציור it can't get infected. How long it is being פולט ציור is subject to a מחלוקת.&lrm;[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat may be roasted in any case that the meat has not been cooked yet.[^4]</span> ^3be561
## Meat Has not Been Washed after Being Salted
### Within 12 Hours of Being Salted
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may salt the other side like usual and then cook the meat.[^4]
   The meat expunges ציור for 12 hours.[^5]</span>
### After 12 Hours but within 24 Hours
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א according to the ט"ז: You can no longer salt the meat to be able to cook it. (You must roast it.)[^6]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א according to the ש"ך: In a case of הפסד מרובה, one may resalt the meat and then cook it.[^7]</span>

<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For the reasoning behind the מחלוקת, see [[Meat Partially Salted, Resalting within 24 Hours]].</span>
### After 24 Hours
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You can no longer salt the meat to be able to cook it. (You must roast it.)</span>
## Meat Has Been Washed
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Washing causes the meat to stop being פולט ציור. As such, one is not able to resalt the meat as this would infect the first side.[^8]</span>
