# שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א's שיטה about meat that has been salted on one side, or on both sides but some spots were missed, that has been left for more than 12 hours but less than 24, is subject to a מחלוקת:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: In a case of הפסד מרובה, one may resalt the meat and then cook it.[^1]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: One may not resalt the meat.[^2]</span>
# Reasoning
## Backgrounod
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א in סימן ע סעיף ו <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to add link</span> says (in a different, but related case) that in a case of הפסד מרובה, one can be מקל say that the meat is being פולט ציור for 24 hours (and can't absorb blood).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, in our case, if it is a case of הפסד מרובה, we should say that meat is פולט ציור for 24 hours and so you should be able to salt it.</span>
## Explanation of the ט"ז
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Our case can't ever be a case of הפסד מרובה because one always has the option to roast the meat, as [[סימן סט הלכות#^3be561|discussed]]. The meat will never be lost.[^2]
   (For this reason, if one has already cooked the meat, if it is a case of הפסד מרובה, the meat is מותר, because in this case, the meat will be lost because roasting wouldn't help.)</span>
## Explanation of the ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even though the person has the option to roast the meat, since the person wants to cook the meat, regarding that option there is nothing one can do. As such, this is viewed a הפסד מרובה and so one has until 24 hours.[^1]</span>
