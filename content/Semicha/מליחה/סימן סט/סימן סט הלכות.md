# &lrm;הדחה ראשונה
## Reasoning
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Meat must be washed before it is salted. There is a מחלוקת about what the reason for this is:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">הגהות מיימוני, סמ"ק: There is blood on the surface (דם בעין). One needs to wash this off or the salt can potentially cause the meat to absorb the blood.[^1]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ר"ן,&lrm; יש אומרים in the מרדכי: The surface of the meat is hard. It needs to be soaked to soften it so the blood can leave.[^1]</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מרדכי: There can be dirt on the surface that will prevent the salt from pulling out the blood. This dirt must be cleaned off.[^2]</span>
## The Washing Process
### לכתחילה
#### Background
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the שיטה that the we require הדחה ראשונה because there is דם בעין on the surface, one needs to wash the meat to make sure that there is no more blood on the surface.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the שיטה that the reason for הדחה ראשונה is to soften the meat, one needs to soak the meat for 30 minutes.[^4]</span>
#### הלכה למעשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לכתחילה, we are מחמיר for both שיטות. As such, one needs to soak the meat for 30 minutes and scrub it.[^5]</span>
### בדיעבד
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[#Salting Meat without Doing a Full הדחה ראשונה|below]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Butchers used to wash the meat in the slaughterhouse. For a discussion of whether that can work as a הדחה ראשונה, see [[Butchers Washing the Meat Regarding הדחה ראשונה]].</span>
## Maximum Amount of time to Soak
### Background
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the meat soaks in water for 24 hours, there is a potential problem that the bllod that is still in the meat can undergo כבוש into the meat and make the meat אסור.&lrm;[^6]</span>
### Water is not 60x the Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one soaked the meat for 24 hours, the meat and the כלי become אסור. There is a מחלוקת about whether one can roast the meat:[^7]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: One may roast the meat.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">הג"ה: There is nothing one can do. The meat is אסור.</span>
### Water is 60x the Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the water is 60x the size of the meat, both the meat and the water remain מותר. (This is different than what we say about cooking meat that has not been salted yet. Regarding cooking, the רמ"א says the meat is אסור, even if there is 60x. Even though we say כבוש כמבושל, we don't think that they are literally the same. We are slightly more מקל on כבישׁה).[^8]</span>
## The כלי for הדחה ראשונה
### Using the כלי for other Things
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should not use a כלי that one has soaked the meat in for other things.[^9]
   Technically, there is really nothing wrong with using the כלי as it only was used for cold, non-salty food for less than 24 hours. The reason we are מחמיר is because we are concerned that there is some blood in the כלי that will now stick to the food.[^10]</span>
### Using an אסור כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may use an אסור כלי to soak the meat since it is not being soaked for 24 hours.&lrm;[^11]
   (If a piece of meat that has been properly washed, soaked, and salted, is left in liquid for 24 hours in an אסור כלי, it is still fine בדיעבד.&lrm;[^12])</span>

## Liquids that May Be Used הדחה ראשונה
### לכתחילה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: One should only use water. (However, in one place the רמ"א seems to say that fruit juice is effective.)[^13]</span>

### בדיעבד
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one used fruit juice (or, presumably, any liquid) it would be effective as this would clean the דם בעין.&lrm;[^14]</span>
# Salting
## Wait for Liquid to Dry
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should wait for the meat to dr</span>y somewhat but not too much. If the meat is too wet, the liquid will ruin the salt. At the same time, the salt needs to be a little wet to be able to pull blood out.[^15]</span>
## Frozen Meat
1. See [[Salting Frozen Meat]].
## Size of the Salt
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The salt grains should not be too small (the size of flour) or too big (bigger than sea salt) because it will be absorbed or not stick and fall off meat, respectively, and won't get the blood out.[^16]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one only has salt that is too large or small, it can be used. If one is using salt that is too large, they just have to stick the salt well.[^17]</span>
## How much Salt
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One must salt the meat to the point that it can't be eaten because of the saltiness.[^18]</span>
### Covering the Meat with Salt
#### לכתחילה
##### Regular Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should cover the whole piece of meat with salt on both sides. One does not have to pile the salt on the meat. It just needs to be spread over the whole piece.[^19]</span>
##### Hollow Pieces
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the piece is hollow (like birds or lungs), one needs to salt the inside and outside. (One would need to open up the piece to get the salt inside.)[^20]</span>
#### בדיעבד
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, (ש"ך, ט"ז): The meat is מותר even if only one side was salted or if the salting left spots and then the meat was cooked, even in cases where it is not a הפסד מרובה.&lrm;[^18]
   The ש"ך and ט"ז agree to the מחבר when one salts both sides but missed some spots and then cooks it.[^21]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: See [[רמ''א's שיטה Regarding Meat Partially Salted]].</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: Even if the meat has been washed or it has been left for a long time (more than 12 hours), one can resalt and cook it.[^22]
   </span>
## Length of Time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">How long the salt must stay on the meat is subject to a מחלוקת:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר:&lrm; 18 minutes.[^23]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: &lrm;לכתחילה,&lrm; 1 hour. בדיעבד, or in a case of need (like ערב שבת), 18 minutes.[^24]</span>

### כלי שאינו מנוקב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Meat salted in a כלי שאינו מנוקב becomes אסור. It can't even be roasted. How fast and how much of the meat becomes אסור is subject to a מחלוקת. #### How much of the Meat Becomes אסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: All of the meat under the liquid as well as a קליפה above the liquid.[^25]</span>

2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: The meat becomes אסור when the ציור starts pooling.[^26]</span>
# Salting Meat without Doing a Full הדחה ראשונה
## Meat is Scrubbed but not Soaked
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one scrubs the meat well but doesn't soak it for 30 minutes, the meat is fine. We don't even require the person to soak it at this point.[^27]
   For why this is, see [[בדיעבד Types of Washing#Washing the Meat for הדחה ראשונה with a Lot of Water without Soaking]].</span>
## Meat is Washed a Little Bit or is 60x the Surface Blood
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the meat is not washed at all before being salted but is 60x the blood on the surface or it was washed a little bit, the meat is מותר. If the person already salted it, they don't need to go back and wash it. However, if the person didn't salt it yet, they would have to wash it.
   For why this is, see [[בדיעבד Types of Washing#Washing the Meat for הדחה ראשונה with only a Little Water]].[^28]</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(In most cases the meat is not 60x the surface blood. One would need to know that this particular piece was 60x the blood to rely on this התר.&lrm;[^29])</span>

# הדחה אחרונה
## Getting the Salt off
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Getting the salt off requires shaking (נפיצה) and/or dunking the meat in water (שטיפה), followed by washing it well in water either two or three times (replacing the water and washing the כלי out between each washing). What you must do is subject to a מחלוקת:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the ש"ך's first answer (his other answer is his main answer):[^38]</span>
		1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: The meat needs נפיצה or שטיפה and then washed twice. However, if you wash it well while holding it up, that counts as both the נפיצה and the first washing.</span>
		2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat needs נפיצה or שטיפה. &lrm;מעיקר הדין, the meat needs three washings. However, the נפיצה and שטיפה count as the first washing.</span>
		   
		   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In other words, the מחבר and רמ"א argue about when one holds it up and washes it, how many washings do you need after that. The מחבר says you need one and the רמ"א says you need two.</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the ש"ך's main answer:</span>
		1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר and רמ"א agree that one needs to do נפיצה or שטיפה followed by washing well in water twice but it is preferable for one to wash it well in water three times.[^38]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the ט"ז:&lrm;[^39]</span>
		1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: One must do נפיצה or שטיפה and then wash it well in water twice.</span>
		2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: One must do נפיצה or שטיפה and then wash it three times. However, if one does נפיצה and שטיפה, one only needs to wash it twice (you really need three but doing both שטיפה and נפיצה counts as one of the washings).</span>

	<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For  further discussion see [[מחלוקת מחבר and רמ''א about הדחה אחרונה]].</span>
## Liquids that May Be Used
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may use fruit juice for הדחה אחרונה.&lrm;[^40]</span>
## Didn't Do נפיצה or שטיפה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As long as the meat is washed with a little bit of water, the salt and ציור will not cause the blood it has absorbed to go back into the meat.[^41]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one has puts the meat into the כלי to do הדחה אחרונה but the כלי doesn't have water in it, is similar to a case of salting meat in a כלי שאינו מנוקב. Practically speaking, the meat will not be in the כלי long enough to become אסור in the amount of time it takes to prepare the כלי and put water in it. (See [[#How long One Has before Meat Becomes אסור in a כלי שאינו מנוקב]].)[^42]</span>

# Cutting Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one cuts the meat after הדחה ראשונה and before the מליחה process finishes (meaning, before it was salted, or after it is salted but during שיעור מליחה) one needs to rescrub it.
