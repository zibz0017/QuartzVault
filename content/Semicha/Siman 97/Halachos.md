# Unorganized Notes
- One may not make or eat dairy or meat bread unless:
	- It is only one serving, or[^1]
	- The person made it in a unique shape.[^1]
- If you bake food in the same oven as something meaty/dairy, there is a concern that some meat will flow into the other dish.[^1]
- For this reason, לכתחילה, one may not bake bread with meaty dishes.[^2]
- בדיעבד, this is okay. However, if the oven is slanted (and the bread is lower down and the meat is higher up), or you see a flow from the meat dish, even if you don't see it reach the bread, the bread is אסור, even בדיעבד.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3]
- The reason for these הלכות is as follows: There is a sfek sfeika: perhaps nothing from the meaty dish spread to the bread, and even if it did, that doesn't necessarily make the bread meaty. There is a discussion if the fat will permeate the dish. (See סימן צב). As such, if the oven is slanted, or you do see some fat spreading, you can assume it reached the bread and now you are only down to one ספק. That is why when you have a sfek sfeika, we allow this (בדיעבד), but when you see it spreading, or the oven is slanted, it is אסור, even בדיעבד.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3]
- According to the ב"ח, though, you need to see it actually reach the bread. If you don't see it reach the bread, the bread is מותר, בדיעבד.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3]
- We don't say that there is another ספק that maybe you won't eat it with dairy, because it is normal to eat bread with anything, and so it is likely that you will eat it with dairy.[^2`]
- You can't have a fleishig pestle and mortar unless there is a sign on it. This is even בדיעבד.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^4]
- The ט"ז agrees with the ש"ך that if there was a flow, the bread is אסור, even if it didn't reach the bread. This is because the בליעות may travel into the oven and from there, go into the bread.[^5]
- Questions:
	- Do the ט"ז and ש"ך agree?
- If there is ממשות in the oven, you have to burn it out from the inside. Cleaning it doesn't work. This is even true for ovens that have a designated space for the fire, and above it, a different space for the food.[^6]
- Some foods, however, don't leave ממשות. For these foods, one doesn't even need to clean the oven out if one just puts food in כלים there.[^7]
- If you mix a bread that has been kneaded with dairy/meat with other breads, all of the breads must be thrown out, even if this was done by accident.[^8]
- Changing the appearance of the bread works for home usage, but not for selling, because other people won't understand the fact that it is different.[^9]
- When making a שינוי in the bread this must be done before the bread is baked. Making a שינוי in the bread after it has been baked does not help.[^9]
- If the bread is something that will not be mixed with meat/dairy (like a pastry), then this bread is מותר, even if there is no שינוי.[^9]
- One may bake bread without a שינוי if it just has dairy/meat בליעות. This is because the only concern is that there is בליעות in the bread and that is נ"ט בר נ"ט.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> בדיעבד, נ"ט בר נ"ט is מותר. As such, we aren't גוזר in this case.[^10]
- If the בליעות from the meat just went into the flor of the oven and from there? It should just be נ"ט בר נ"ט, and as we said before, we are not going to אסור the bread if it is just נ"ט בר נ"ט? (כריתי ופלתי)[^11]
- The answer is that when both נ"טs are happening at the same time while the food is cooking (like in this case, the בליעות are transferring to the oven floor and from there into the bread while it is cooking, all in the same cooking process) this is not considered to be נ"ט בר נ"ט. (חוות דעת)[^11]
- The בית אפרים brings down a story where there were breads baking in an oven and someone put a dairy food in there which leaked some dairy בליעות and the הלכה was that the breads were מותר because the leaked stuff did not go under the breads themselves, and because there was not enough ממשות to infect the bread.[^11]
- The first reason argues on the חוות דעת.[^11] 
- If the bread is 60x the amount of the meat/dairy there is machlokes. 
	- The Maharit and חוות דעת say that if the meat/dairy is mixed into the dough, then it could be מבטל, but if it the ממשות was stuck on the oven, or some ממשות fell onto the bread, the הלכה is that it is not מבטל.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^12]
	- The בית אפרים says that in all cases, the meat/dairy can be מבוטל. The only difference is that if the ממשות fell onto the bread, a נטילה of the bread needs to be removed.[^12]
- Parve food that is cooked in a small oven with meat/dairy, can't be eaten with the opposite. Bietzem, this is מותר to eat even with the same type בדיעבד, but since you always have the option of eating it with the other type, it is not considered to be בדיעבד.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^13]
- What a "small oven" means is subject to מחלוקת. This is either 12 esronim in the whole volume of the oven, or the bottom of the oven needs to be 12 esronim (which is measured 1 tefach thick).[^14]
- An esron is 43.2 ביצים.[^15]
- If the meat/dairy is covered, whether it is covered by a cover, or if it covered by the food itself (like a cheese Danish that the dough totally covers the cheese) the parve food remains parve, even if the oven is small.[^16]
