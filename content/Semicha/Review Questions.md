# Question 4 (Soaking Dairy/Meat Utensils together)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Can one soak dairy and meat utensils in the same container (for 24 hours)?</span>
## Answer
It depends on the case:
### Both כלים Have ממשות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The water becomes נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא and then that makes both כלים, אסור.</span>
### Both כלים Are בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is subject to a מחלוקת between the מחבר and the רמ"א. The רמ"א holds that the בליעות from both of these dishes mix immediately and so this is considered נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא. (סימן צה סעיף ג)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר holds that the בליעות mix in the water first. As such, the בליעות are נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא and so the spoons are fine. (סימן צה סעיף ג)</span>
### One כלי Has ממשות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The clean dish becomes אסור. The dirty dish remains מותר.</span>
### One כלי is a בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The water becomes a נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא, as such both of the כלים are מותר.</span>
### Summary
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If both dishes were בן יומו, or there were ממשות from both dishes, everything is אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one dish If not, everything is מותר.</span>
