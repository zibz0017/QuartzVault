# Cutting a דבר חריף
## The Knife
1. Some say that a knife can אסור כדי קליפה only if it wasn't particularly cleaned or if it is a בן יומו.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^1]
2. Others say that a knife does this even if it is cleaned and not a בן יומו.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^1]
3. We פסקען like the second שיטה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^2]
4. For the reasons behind these שיטות, see [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור|here]].
## הלכה about the Food
1. If the knife is a problem (according to each שיטה, as discussed [[#The Knife|above]]) and you cut a דבר חריף:
	1. מחבר: You need to remove a כדי נטילה of the food before cooking it with its opposite.[^1]
	2. רמ"א: <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>לכתחילה, you may not use the food with the opposite of the knife (if you cut a דבר חריף with dairy knife, you can't use the food with meat, and vice-versa).[^3]
	   However, if one is in a place that the only דבר חריף that one can get has been cut with a treif (or meat/dairy) knife, one can cut off a נטילה and use the food. (The רמ"א regards this as a בדיעבד situation.)[^4]
2. For details about whether one can have someone taste the food and rely on that, see [[Relying on Taste When Cutting a דבר חריף|here]].
## If One Cooked with the דבר חריף
### Cut with Dairy/Meat Knife and Cooked with Opposite Food
1. If one cooked the דבר חריף into food that is the opposite of the knife (a דבר חריף cut with a dairy knife into meat food or vice-versa), the food must be 60x the נטילה of the food around the cut (or 60x the whole food if it was diced), or 60x the amount of the blade that touched the food, whichever is less.[^5]
   For further discussion about how much of the blade counts, see [[בליעות Extracted from Knife to Food|here]].
2. Where the בליעות in the דבר חריף were מבוטל, the דבר חריף does *not* need to be removed.[^6]
   For example, if someone cuts an onion with a dairy knife and puts it into a meat soup and the soup is more than 60x the בליעות in the knife, one may eat the soup with the onions. The onions would not have to be removed.
### Cut with אסור Knife and Cooked with מותר Food
1. If one cut the דבר חריף with an אסור knife and cooked it in מותר food:
	1. מחבר: In terms of how much food is needed to be מבטל the איסור, there is no difference between this case and the case with the dairy/meat knife.
	2. רמ"א: The food must be 60x the נטילה of דבר חריף (or 60x the whole food if it was diced). *60x the blade doesn't help*.[^5]
2. According to all opinions, even if the בליעות in the דבר חריף were מבוטל, the דבר חריף itself *must* be removed.[^6]

# Alternative Parts of דבר חריף
1. Other parts of a דבר חריף are not considered to a be a דבר חריף (like the leaves, or stems, or things like that).[^3]
# דבר חריף Cut by a גוי
## Cut with an Unknown Utensil
1. For most דברים חריפים, if they were cut with an unknown utensil, and it is reasonable that they were cut with something other than a knife (like with a hoe or spade), one can eat the דבר חריף. For why this is, for more details, and exceptions, see [[Why One Can Eat a דבר חריף Cut with an Unknown Utensil|here]].
## Cut with an אסור בן יומו Utensil
1. In some cases, if many דברים חריפים were cut in a row with the same אסור knife, even if the knife is a בן יומו, some are מקל and eat these foods.[^8] For why this is and more details, see [[Many דברים חריפים Cut in a Row|here]].
# Spice Crusher/Grinder
1. Someone crushes spices in a בן יומו/non-בן יומו spice grinder:
## בן יומו
1. According to all opinions, the spice gets the same dairy/meat (or אסור) status as the spice crusher. It is not a נ"ט בר נ"ט, it is viewed as a נ"ט (like a דבר חריף cut with a knife).[^9]
## Non-בן יומו
1. מחבר (here): There is no problem with using the spices for food of the opposite type as the crusher/grinder. (The בית יוסף holds like the שיטה over [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור#First שיטה|here]] that non-בן יומו doesn't doesn't make things אסור.)[^9]
2. רמ"א, ש"ך, ט"ז, בית יוסף (elsewhere): There is no difference between non-בן יומו and בן יומו. (These שיטות hold like the שיטה over [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור#Second שיטה|here]] that non-בן יומו is a problem by דבר חריף.)[^10]
