# Unorganized Notes
- Knives that haven't been cleaned מסתמא have shamnunis.[^1]
- As such, if you cut a דבר חריף even with a clean, non-בן יומו knife, the you need to remove a כדי נטילה of the food before cooking it with its opposite.[^2]
- Relying on taste:[^3]
	- רא"ש, מחבר, טור, רשב"א, Ashiri: We can rely on taste, even לכתחילה
	- רמב"ם, סמ"ג, סמ"ק, מרדכי, מהרש"ל, ש"ך: We can only rely on taste בדיעבד.
	- רמ"א: We can rely on taste of a Jew, בדיעבד. We can't ever rely on a non-Jew.
	- Ateres Zahav: We can't every rely on taste.
- If the knife is clean and not a בן יומו and the food is not a דבר חריף, there is not transfer of בליעות.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^4]
- Rules when cutting a דבר חריף:
	- מחבר: The בליעות can travel as far as כדי נטילה. As such, if someone cuts a דבר חריף with an an אסור knife, or with a dairy/meat knife and want to use the food with its opposite, the person must remove כדי נטילה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^2]
	- How much בליעות actually goes into the food is the lesser of the following:[^5]
		- כדי נטילה of the cut.
		- The thickness of the knife that touched the food.
	- The reason is because there are two "bottlenecks". The knife can't contain more בליעות than how big it is, and the food can't receive more בליעות than how big it is.[^5]
	- Practically speaking, most cases the size of the knife will be smaller and so we will go with that. If the food is very small and the knife is very big, and the person slid a lot of the knife through the food, then in that case, כדי נטילה of the food may be smaller and we will go with that.[^5]
	- If the דבר חריף is diced, then we would need 60x the amount of the whole food or the מקום hasakin, whatever is smaller. The reason we would potentially need 60x the whole food is because since it was diced, the whole thing is כדי נטילה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^6]
	- As said before, practically, the מקום hasakin will usually be smaller.
	- The previous הלכות are when the knife is meat/dairy and the דבר חריף is going to be used wit its opposite.
	- If the knife has איסור, then the כדי נטילה becomes חנ"ן and so you would need 60x כדי נטילה, even if the מקום hasakin is smaller.[^7]
	- For an alternate שיטה and the ש"ך's disagreement, see [[שיטה of the Ateres Zahav on איסור Spreading in a דבר חריף|here]].
- רמ"א: <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>לכתחילה, one should not use a דבר חריף that has been cut with an אסור knife for anything, and one that has been cut with a dairy/meat knife for its opposite.[^8]
- בדיעבד, the הלכות are as discussed before.[^8]
- Other parts of a דבר חריף (like the leaves of the plant) are not considered דבר חריף.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^9] However, it seems that scallions are considered to be a דבר חריף.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^10]
- If a גוי cut a דבר חריף and you don't know the status of his knife, you can assume that he used a clean, non-בן יומו knife unless it was a radish.[^11] This is because some say that the only דבר חריף is a radish. An additional התר is that stam knives of גויים are not בן יומו, and some say that even by דבר חריף, בליעות do not transfer when the knife is not a בן יומו.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^12] (I think he is saying that you are allowed to use the whole דבר חריף, but I am not sure.) An additional התר (for anything other than chaltis) is that according to some, the only דבר חריף is a chaltis.[^13]
- Some שיטות hold that chaltis is the only food that is a דבר חריף.[^14]
- This seems to be in סתירה to סימן צד סעיף ז. For more details, see [[סתירה between סעיף א and סימן עד סעיף ז|here]].
- How much is considered מקום hasakin?
	- According to the ש"ך, if the person knows how much of the knife touched the food, only that part is considered מקום hasakin.[^15]
	- According to the ט"ז, we don't trust the person, since the amount of knife was not relevant at the time he was cutting. As such, one would require the amount of the whole blade.[^16]
- If the דבר חריף was cut with a meat/dairy knife and then put into the opposite food, if the food is 60x the כדי נטילה of the onion or the מקום hasakin (see above for the exact details) one would not even have to remove the onion. This is because when the onion was cut, it didn't absorb איסור. It only absorbed meat/dairy. As such, it can have those בליעות removed.[^17]
- If the knife had absorbed איסור, however, then you would have to pull the onions that you found out.[^17]
- According to the מחבר, we don't say חנ"ן by all איסורים except for בב״ח. As such, there is not different about whether I cut the דבר חריף with a dairy/meat knife and use it for its opposite, or I cut it with a knife of איסור and use the SCH in a מותר food. The הלכה is going to be that at most, I need 60x the כדי נטילה or the מקום hasakin, whatever is less. This is the same whether I dice it or just cut it once.[^18]
- According to the רמ"א that we do say חנ"ן, then there is one difference. By a knife of איסור, if you dice the דבר חריף, then you need 60x the amount of the whole דבר חריף. If you did the dairy/meat then you would only need מקום hasakin (if that was less).[^18]
- The ט"ז asks on the רמ"א why he doesn't say this in the name of the יש אומרים (meaning, his own opinion that he argues on the מחבר about if we say חנ"ן by other איסורים).[^18]
- The ט"ז also asks that according to the רמ"א, if you just cut it with an אסור knife, then we should always require 60x כדי נטילה. But the רמ"א doesn't say that.[^18]
- When the only radishes you can buy are ones that have been cut by a dairy/meat knife or an אסור, you can treat this case like בדיעבד and just remove the נטילה.[^2]
- If you have a cut דבר חריף and you don't know how it was cut, you can assume that it was cut with something מותר and eat it without removing anything.[^2]
- The ש"ך disagrees in one case. He says that if the דבר חריף was a radish and  you know that it was cut with a knife (as opposed to perhaps having been cut with a gardening tool), then you have to be מחמיר. (Seemingly, לכתחילה the whole thing is אסור and בדיעבד, only כדי נטילה is.)[^19]
- Extremely salty fish is a דבר חריף. If it is only somewhat salty, it is not.[^20]
- According to the חב"ד, fatty meat cut by a dairy knife needs to be scrubbed.[^21]
