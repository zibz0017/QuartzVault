# הלכה
1. There is מחלוקת about whether a knife cutting a דבר חריף can אסור only if it is dirty or is a בן יומו, or if it can even אסור when it isn't either of these.
# Reasoning
## First שיטה
1. According to the first שיטה (the knife can only אסור if it is a בן יומו or it is dirty) the knife can אסור:
	1. If it is a בן יומו the בליעות come out of the knife. Because this is a דבר חריף, the we do not treat this food as a נ"ט בר נ"ט (original dairy/meat to knife and knife to this food). We treat it as having received taste directly from the original dairy/meat.
	2. If it is dirty a סתם knife has שמנונית. The שמנונית can go straight into the food.
## Second שיטה
1. According to the second שיטה (the knife can אסור even if it is not a בן יומו and is clean) the knife can אסור because the דבר חריף can "reawaken" the non-בן יומו בליעות in the knife and they get extracted into the food. As said before, we do not treat this food as a נ"ט בר נ"ט (original dairy/meat to knife and knife to this food). We treat it as having received taste directly from the original dairy/meat.
# הלכה למעשה
1. We פסקען like the second שיטה.
