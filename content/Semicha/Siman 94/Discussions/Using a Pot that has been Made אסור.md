# Holding foods
## Used to Hold Dry Foods
1. One may use the pot to hold dry foods because one is not getting hanna from the אסור בליעות in the pot when it is used for this.
## Used to Hold Wet Foods
1. באר הגולה, B"Y (according to the באר הגולה): One *may not* use the pot to hold wet foods.
2. ש"ך, B"Y (according to the ש"ך):  One *may* use the pot to hold wet foods.
# Cooking with the pot
## לכתחילה
1. According to all opinions, one may not cook with the pot, לכתחילה, in any case.
## בדיעבד
### בן יומו
1. According to all opinions, if the pot is a בן יומו, it makes the food cooked in it אסור (unless the food inside is more than 60x the amount of the pot).

### Not a בן יומו
#### Status of the food
1. According to all opinions, the food that was cooked in the pot is מותר.
#### What one should do if they did this
1. מרדכי, מהרש"ל, אור זרוע: One should take the value of the pot and throw it out.
2. רמ"א, או"ה, ש"ד: There is no need to do this.
