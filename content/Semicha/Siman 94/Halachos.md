# Meat/Dairy Utensil in a Pot of Food of Its opposite
1. A meat/dairy utensil was dipped into a pot of its opposite type of food. The utensil was a בן יומו and the pot is a כלי ראשון. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to figure out if these הלכות are for all כלי ראשון or just when it is on a fire.</span> 
2. If the pot is not a כלי ראשון, everything is fine.
## Status of the Pot
1. The food needs to be 60x the size of the amount of the utensil that was stuck into the food,[^1][^2] no matter the material of the utensil.[^3] For further discussion of this topic, see [[Material of Utensil|here]]. If the food is not 60x the food and pot become אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^2] For discussion about using the אסור pot, see [[Using a Pot that has been Made אסור|here]].
2. If one doesn't know how much of the utensil was put into the pot, he should assume a "normal amount" was put in (like where he holds it from and probably a little more than that was not put into the food, but after that, the person should probably assume that that part was put in the food).[^4]
3. If one does think that they know how much of the spoon was stuck into the food, there is a מחלוקת if we can rely on on the person's assessment:
	1. נודה ביהודה: We can rely on the persons assessment.[^5]
	2. פתחי תשובה: We cannot rely on the person's assessment, unless there is some sign on the utensil.[^5]
## Status of the Utensil
### All Cases
1. If the utensil becomes אסור, it does not אסור any food that it is used with if it was not used for 24 hours after being made אסור. In other words, a non-בן יומו כלי can't make anything אסור. (It still may be אסור to do anyway, as will be discussed.)
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Perhaps this next part should be moved to [[#When בן יומו Begins]]?</span>
   The "בן יומו timer" begins from when the utensil was made אסור. Even if the the utensil had not been used for the thing that it is usually used for in over 24 hours, that doesn't matter.
   For example, if a dairy spoon was used for milk at 6:00 PM, and it is then used for meat at 6:00 AM, the בן יומו timer begins at 6:00 AM, even though it will be over 24 hours from when it was used for milk.[^6]
### Case 1
1. A בן יומו utensil is used for it's opposite.
#### הלכה
1. It may not be used with anything.[^7] If it was used it for another food, the next food becomes אסור, as well (unless it is 60x the בליעות in the utensil).[^2]
### Case 2
1. A בן יומו pot was used for parve. A בן יומו utensil was then stuck into the pot.
#### הלכה
1. רמ"א:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> לכתחילה, the utensil may not be used with another food. However, if it was used it for another food, the next food is still מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^8]
2. מחבר: The utensil is perfectly מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^8]
### Case 3
1. The utensil is not a בן יומו and then it is used for the opposite.
#### הלכה
1. The utensil may not be used with milk or meat but may be used with parve. According to the רמ"א, even though it is מותר to use with parve, one still shouldn't.[^9]
2. If you do use it with any food, the next food is fine.[^9]
## The Utensil Was Dipped Twice (or more)
### You Didn't Realize between the Dippings
1. מחבר: You need 120x times the amount of בליעות from the utensil.[^10]
2. רמ"א: You still only need 60x the amount of the בליעות. The second dipping doesn't add anything.[^11]
For a discussion of the שיטות and why dipping it more than twice doesn't make a difference, see [[Dipping the Utensil Twice (or More)|here]].
### You Did Realize between the Dippings
1. According to all שיטות, in this case you only need 60x. For why this is different than when you don't realize, see [[Dipping the Utensil Twice (or More)|here]].
# Parve Food Cooked with Dairy/Meat and then in Opposite Pot
## Case
1. One cooks parve food with meat/dairy. Then, they cook this food in a בן יומו pot of its opposite.
## הלכה
1. The food and pot are אסור unless the pot is 60x the amount of בליעות in the food or the food is 60x the amount of בליעות in the pot.[^12]
# When בן יומו Begins
## Removing a Pot from a Fire
1. If a pot with food is removed from the fire, בן יומו begins from when the food cools to lukewarm. (It seems that this is around 98.6$\degree$F.)[^13]
## Cooking Something in a Pot
*ש"ך ס"ק כב*
### Basic Rules
1. Every time there is a transfer of taste, the "בן יומו timer" begins for that taste.
### Applications
1. In all of these cases, we have to evaluate the original taste, and the new taste that occurs when we cook the parve thing in the pot.
2. If it has been over 24 hours since the original taste, the taste is now not a בן יומו.
3. When we cook the thing in the pot, the taste from the pot may enter the food and then transfer back into the pot. There is a new "בן יומו timer" for this, which has to be evaluated.
#### Case 1
1. Someone cooked meat/dairy in a pot. While it was a בן יומו, they cooked something parve.
2. While it was a בן יומו of the parve thing, but *not* a בן יומו from the original meat/dairy, they cook the opposite of they cooked before.
For example, someone cooks meat at 7:00 AM. They cook water at 7:00 PM. They cook milk at 8:00 AM.
##### הלכה
1. The food is fine. This is because:
	1. The original taste is not בן יומו, so it can't affect the food.
	2. The taste from the pot entered the parve thing and then went back into the pot. Even though that taste *is* a בן יומו, it is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא, so it can't אסור the food.
#### Case 2
1. Someone cooked pig in a pot. While it was a בן יומו, they cooked something parve.
2. While it was a בן יומו of the parve thing, but *not* a בן יומו from the pig, they cook some other (מותר) food in the pot.
##### הלכה
1. The food is אסור. This is because:
	1. The original taste is not בן יומו, so it can't affect the food. But…
	2. According to רמ"א, the איסור is transferred to the parve thing and becomes חנ"ן, because according to the רמ"א, there is חנ"ן created by all איסורים.
	3. According to the מחבר, there is no חנ"ן created in this case because there is no חנ"ן by שאר איסורים.
	   However, the taste being transferred from the pot to the parve food and from the parve food back to the pot is אסור taste. This is נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא. As such, since the "בן יומו timer" for this taste starts when the taste transfers, when you cook the the מותר food, it receives נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא, and this makes it אסור.
	4. In summary, according to everyone, the food is אסור. The only question is why.
#### Case 3
1. Someone cooked meat and dairy in a pot at the same time, or they cook one followed by the other while the pot is a בן יומו.
2. They then cook something parve in the pot while it is a בן יומו.
3. While the pot is a בן יומו from the parve thing, but *not* from the meat and dairy, a person cooks something (מותר) in the pot.
##### הלכה
1. The food is אסור. This is because:
	1. The original taste is not בן יומו, so it can't affect the food. But…
	2. According to the בית יוסף, when the parve food is cooked in the pot and חנ"ן taste is transferred to it, that is creates a new חנ"ן taste. In other words, according to the בית יוסף, we treat חנ"ן like בב״ח, not like שאר איסורים.
	   As such, there is now a new בן יומו timer from this brand new איסור. This makes the next food אסור quite simply because it is cooked in a בן יומו pot that was used for איסור.
	3. According to the ש"ך in the מחבר's opinion that by שאר איסורים we don't say חנ"ן, cooking the parve food in the pot does not create a *new* חנ"ן. It simply transfers the חנ"ן taste from the pot to the water and then back again.
	   However, as said before, this is considered נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא. As such, since the בן יומו timer for this נ"ט בר נ"ט taste starts when the taste is transferred, the מותר food is being cooked in a בן יומו pot that has נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא taste. As such, the food is אסור.
	4. According to the רמ"א, as said before, there is חנ"ן even by שאר איסורים. That would certainly apply to this case. As such, the מותר food is being cooked in a בן יומו pot that has been used for איסור. As such, the food is אסור.
	5. In summary, according to everyone, the food is אסור. The only question is why.
# Can חנ"ן Create חנ"ן
## Case
1. Someone puts dairy/meat into a food of its opposite type while it is in a כלי ראשון, and it is not מבוטל. The pot of food becomes אסור. It is חנ"ן.
2. Some of that food falls into another food that is כלי ראשון and it is not מבוטל. That food also becomes אסור. However, does that food become חנ"ן?
3. This is relevant if food from that last pot falls into another pot. If we say that all of the food in that pot became חנ"ן, then the whole drop that fell into the next pot is איסור, and to make that next pot מותר, it would have to be מבטל the whole drop.
   If it is not חנ"ן, then only part of the drop is איסור, and so you would only need enough to be מבטל that איסור.
## הלכה
1. ש"ך:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> חנ"ן can create more חנ"ן.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^14]
2. בית יוסף:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> חנ"ן can't create more חנ"ן.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^14]
# Using אסור כלים
## בן יומו
1. It is אסור to use בן יומו כלים.
## Non-בן יומו
1. One should not use a אסור dish, even if it is no longer a בן יומו.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^15]
2. If one does use such a dish:
	1. ש"ך: One should throw out money equal to the value of the food.[^16]
	2. ט"ז: There is no reason to do this. One doesn't need to do anything at all.[^17]
3. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This הלכה does not belong here</span> מעיקר הדין, if one dips a non-בן יומו meat/dairy כלי into a hot food of its opposite, the כלי may be used for hot parve food. This is because the כלי doesn't have חנ"ן בליעות because non-בן יומו בליעות can't become חנ"ן. As such, when you stick the כלי into the parve food, the parve food doesn't get any חנ"ן בליעות from the כלי, because it doesn't have any.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^15] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not sure if this source is correct. # דבר גוש in a כלי שיני
## Cases
1. Someone uses a בן יומו כלי with a דבר גוש that is in a כלי שיני.
2. Someone cuts a דבר גוש with a בן יומו knife that is in a כלי שיני.
## All Opinions
1. If the דבר גוש is in liquid in a כלי שיני, everyone agrees that this is considered to be a כלי שיני.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^18]
## שיטות
1. מחבר, רמ"א: This is totally considered to be a כלי שיני. As such, the הלכה in the first case is that it is totally מותר, and the הלכה in the second case is the same as what usually happens when [[#Cutting Cold Food with opposite Knife|one cuts cold food]].[^19]  (Regarding these הלכות, there is no difference between a כלי שיני and something cold.)
2. מהרש"ל, או"ה, ש"ך:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> דבר גוש is considered to be a כלי ראשון. The הלכה in both cases is that the food (if it is not 60x the בליעות) and the utensil are אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^20] For more details, see [[Semicha/Siman 93/Halachos#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]] and [[Semicha/Siman 93/Halachos#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a Non-בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]].
3. ט"ז: We take a middle ground. If there is no דוחקא דסכינא, we treat it as a כלי שיני. If there is דוחקא דסכינא, we treat it like a כלי ראשון.
   As such, in the first case, everything is מותר. In the second case, the food (if it is not 60x the בליעות) and the utensil are אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^21] For more details, see [[Semicha/Siman 93/Halachos#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]] and [[Semicha/Siman 93/Halachos#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a Non-בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]].
# כלי Shlishi
1. There is a מחלוקת about how we treat food in a כלי shlishi:
	1. Chasam Sofer (and others): We are מקפיד on a כלי shlishi unless it is a case of הפסד מרובה and there is another factor to be מקל.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^18]
	2. פתחי תשובה: We are מקל on a כלי shlishi.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^18]
2. It seems that according to most opinions, even those who say we are מחמיר on a כלי shlishi, we do not say that there is בשול. As such, even according to those who are מחמיר, we are only dealing with an איסור דרבנן.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^18]
# Hot כלים with Food
## Basic case
1. A hot כלי has אסור food on it, or the plate is dairy/meat and there is food of the opposite type put on it.
## הלכה
1. If the food is totally dry, one needs to remove כדי קליפה of the food.[^22]
2. If the food has a drop of moisture, one needs to remove כדי נטילה of the food.[^22]
3. If the food is totally wet, the whole food is אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^22]
# Knives
## Cutting Hot Food with opposite Knife
### Status of the Food
#### All Cases
1. At the very least, the קליפה must be removed.[^23] (Potentially, the entire food may be אסור.)
#### בן יומו Knife
1. If someone cuts hot food *in a כלי ראשון* with a בן יומו knife of the opposite type, the whole food becomes אסור unless it is 60x the בליעות in the knife.[^24] (The case discussed is a dairy knife cutting meat, but this would presumably apply even for a meat knife cutting dairy food.)
   For why this is, see [[Why Cutting Hot Food with בן יומו opposite Knife Becomes Totally אסור|here]].
   For more details about what 60x the בליעות in the knife means, see [[בליעות Extracted from Knife to Food|here]].
#### Non-בן יומו Knife
1. If it is not בן יומו (or if you unsure if it is a בן יומו), you only need to remove the קליפה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^25] For why the meat needs to have a קליפה removed even by a non-בן יומו knife, see [[Why a Non-בן יומו Knife can אסור כדי קליפה|here]].
### Status of the Knife
1. This is the same as any other כלי used for its opposite type of food. This depends on if it is בן יומו or not, as discussed [[Semicha/Siman 93/Halachos#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]] and [[Semicha/Siman 93/Halachos#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a Non-בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]].
