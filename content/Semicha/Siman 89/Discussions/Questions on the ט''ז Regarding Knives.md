# Basic ט"ז
1. The ט"ז explains the רמ"א to be saying that one may cut dairy with their meat knife if they do נעיצה first. Additionally, one just has to clean the knife before using the knife for bread that will be eaten with dairy.
2. The reason for these הלכות is as follows:
	1. The knife may not be used with meat (or with dairy if it is a meat knife) because we say that there is נ"ט בר נ"ט לאיסורה.
	2. The knife may be used for the bread because there is נ"ט בר נ"ט להתירה.
	3. The ט"ז then adds that even without this point of נ"ט בר נ"ט להתירה, we still don't need to worry because the taste won't transfer from the knife because we only say that taste can transfer from the knife (דוחקא דסכינא) if it is a דבר חריף.
