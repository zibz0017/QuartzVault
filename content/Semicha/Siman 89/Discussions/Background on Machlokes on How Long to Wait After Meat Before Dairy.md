1. There is a מחלוקת about how long one needs to wait after eating meat before eating dairy. The 'גמ says that one needs to wait "from one meal to the next". There are two interpretations of this:
	1. This means that one needs to end their meal and that is it. Once one ends their meat meal, they can begin a new meal of dairy. (They just may have to do kinuach and hadacha.)
	2. The average amount of time between two meals. This is generally around six hours.
