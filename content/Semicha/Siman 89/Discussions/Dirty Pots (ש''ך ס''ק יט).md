*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק יט, רע"א ד"ה דהוי נ"ט בר נ"ט, בית מאיר ד"ה עיין בה"ט, פתחי תשובה ס"ק ז*
# The ש"ך's question
1. The רמ"א says that one cannot eat dairy with something that has been cooked in a meat pot, even though there was no meat that was cooking in it.
2. This seems to go against our understanding of נ"ט בר נ"ט. We would say that this is fine.
# ש"ך's Answer
1. Really you are allowed to eat the dairy with the item cooked in the meat pot. When the רמ"א said that you can't eat the dairy with the food cooked in the meat pot, he was referring to a case where the pot wasn't cleaned well and there was some meat residue in the pot.
# Question on the ש"ך
1. It is difficult to understand why this is different than any other case of eating something that has been cooked in a pot with meat.
# Answers
## בית מאיר and רע"א
1. He gives his own answer to the ש"ך's question. He says that the רמ"א was only saying that to include all cases, even cases of a דבר חריף for which we say that one can't cook dairy with something that has been cooked in a meat pot.
## פתחי תשובה
1. He agrees to the ש"ך and says that if there is a small amount of meat in a dish, even if it is more than 1/60, one doesn't have to wait before eating dairy.
2. However, this is only if there is no taste. If you can taste the ממשות then one can't be מקל.
3. Relatedly, one will taste fat if it is more than 1/60, so if there is fat, one can't be מקל.
