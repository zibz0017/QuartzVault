# Reasons
1. The meat gets stuck in one's teeth. After 6 hours, the meat is considered to no longer be meat.
2. The taste of meat sticks around in one's mouth.
# Halacha
*Presented by ט"ז ס"ק א, ש"ך ס"ק ב, פתחי תשובה ס"ק א*
1. We are makpid on חומרות of both reasons. As such, if someone chews the food but doesn't swallow, one must wait since the meat gets stuck in the teeth (like the first reason).
   At the same time, if one find food in their teeth even after 6 hours, they have to remove it (and perhaps do kinuach and hadacha) as according to the second שיטה, we do not consider the meat do be considered to have been digested.
   Similarly, if one's teeth are clean after eating, they still must wait because of the second reason.
2. In fact, we are even makpid in cases where both reasons don't apply. For example, if someone chews up a fleishig dish but doesn't actually eat it, and he doesn't chew any meat, according to both reasons, the person doesn't have to wait. However, because of לא פלוג, the person should wait.
# שיטה of the Ateres Zahav
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ב*
1. The Ateres Zahav says that if one chews food and doesn't swallow, one must wait because of the meat taste.
2. This, seemingly, is against the simple understanding of the שיטות, as when a person chews but doesn't swallow, there is seemingly no taste in the person's mouth and the reason to wait is because it is stuck in the person's teeth.
# לא PLUG
*Presented by פתחי תשובה ס"ק א*
1. Even if one chewed a תבשיל of meat and spat it out, they need to wait. This is even though none of the reasons apply. (The person didn't swallow the food so there is no taste the lingers and the person there wasn't actually any meat here, as it was just a תבשיל of meat).
