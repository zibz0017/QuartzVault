## One Later Finds Meat in One's Mouth
### All Opinions
1. One must remove the meat and do kinuach and hadacha.[^5]
2. One does not need to wait anew.[^6]
# Waiting After Eating Dairy before Eating Meat
## All Opinions
1. In most cases, there is no length of time one needs to wait after eating dairy before eating meat.[^7]
## One's hands
### When One Needs to Wash
1. מחבר:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^8] If one has good lighting, they need to just check their hands to make sure that there is no dairy on them. If there isn't good lighting, one needs to wash their hands.
2. טור, רי"ף:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^9] One always needs to wash their hands.
3. פתחי תשובה: One should wash their hands if it is not difficult.[^10]
### Requirements when Washing
#### All Opinions
1. A person can't have הסח הדעת.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^11]
2. A person must dry their hands.[^11]
#### Usable Water
1. מחבר, ש"ך:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^11] One must use water.
2. מהרש"ל:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^11] One can use any liquid.

# תבשיל
## What is a תבשיל
1. There is a מחלוקת if liquid in meat/dairy is considered to be a תבשיל or the actual thing itself.[^16]
2. However, according to all opinions, a thick stew with mushed meat/dairy mixed in is considered to be the actual thing itself.[^16]
3. As such, מעיקר הדין, in such cases one needs to wait, according to all שיטות.
## Eating Dairy After Eating a תבשיל of Meat
1. If one wants to eat actual meat/dairy after eating a תבשיל of its opposite, then:
	1. מחבר: One can eat dairy right after eating a תבשיל of meat.[^17] (The בית יוסף in או"ח says that one should wait, so הלכה lemysa, ספרדים do wait.)
	2. רמ"א: One should wait the usual time that one waits after eating meat itself.[^18]
## Washing Hands Between the Two Foods
1. If one ate a food cooked with dairy without eating the dairy and then wants to eat meat (or the other way around) they should wash their hands between the two.[^17]
2. If, however, they are just eating a תבשיל of both of them:
	1. מחבר: They don't need to wash their hands.[^17]
	2. רמ"א: They do need to wash their hands.[^18]
## נ"ט בר נ"ט
1. If one wants to eat a food that was cooked in a meat/dairy בן יומו כלי and then eat a food of its opposite, they do not need to wait or wash their hands. For more details, see here. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">add link to נ"ט בר נ"ט</span>

# Unorganized Notes
## סעיף 3
1. Can't eat תבשיל of meat and milk together.
2. ספרדים: Can eat milk after תבשיל of meat (and vice versa).
3. אשכנזים: Can not.
4. Can eat milk with parve food cooked in fleishig pot. (ש"ך 19)
5. ש"ך: Can eat milk after parve food cooked in meat pot even though meat pot has meat residue. (ש"ך 19)
   בית מאיר attacks this mima nafashach: if it is batul בששים, then you should be allowed to eat it with milk. And if it is not, then you should have to wait. בית מאיר says that חידוש of רמ"א is that one may eat milk with parve food cooked in meat pot, even if meat is דבר חריף.
6. אשכנזים: treat תבשיל of meat and milk like actual meat and milk (meaning, need to wait after תבשיל of meat and need kinuach/hadacha after תבשיל of milk).
7. בית יוסף and Rema: A server doesn't need to wash hands between serving milk and meat.
   ש"ך: Server does need to. (ש"ך 21)
8. There is a machlokes about the status of meat juice. According to some, it is the same as meat. According to others, if it is thick it is like meat, and if it is thin, it is only like a תבשיל of meat. (Practically, there doesn't seem to be a difference according to אשכנזים.) (ט"ז 5)
## סעיף 4
1. After eating meat/milk, you must clean the table from crumbs/take off the tablecloth before switching to the other one.
2. You can't use a knife used for meat (milk) for food that you will eat with milk (meat).
3. Rema and ש"ך: Midina, if you do נעיצה, you can use the knife for the other one, לכתחילה (a meat knife to be used for dairy and vice versa). However, כלל ישראל have separate meat and milk knives and this is proper. If someone is in a situation that they only have one knife, they can use נעיצה and use the knife.
   מהרש"ל: You can only do נעיצה on a knife if it has been made אסור.
   In short, it seems that one should have separate meat and milk knives. If someone is in a situation that they only have one knife then they may do נעיצה to use the knife for the other one. (סעיף 4 and ש"ך 22)
4. One may use a meat (dairy) knife to cut bread that they will eat with dairy (meat). They just have to make sure the knife is clean (meaning, even without נעיצה). As worst this is only nat bar nat (בליעות from the food into the knife and from the knife into the bread). And it isn't even that because בליעות only transfer with knives when there is a דבר חריף. (ט"ז 6)
