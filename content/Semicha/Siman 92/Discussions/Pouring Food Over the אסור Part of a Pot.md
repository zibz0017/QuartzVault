# Case
1. A drop of meat/dairy fell on a pot above the food.
# Status of the Pot
1. We are treat the pot as if the בליעות have spread as far as they can go without being מבוטל.
## Pot is בן יומו
1. If the pot is a בן יומו, part of the pot just under 60x the size of the drop becomes חנ"ן.
## Pot is not בן יומו
1. If the pot is not a בן יומו, part of the pot just under 60x the size of the drop gets the בליעות of the drop, but it does not become חנ"ן.
# How One Should Pour the Food out of the Pot
1. One should wait for the pot to cool down and then pour the food out. This is even if the food is enough to be מבטל the איסור in the pot because we are not מבטל איסור, לכתחילה.
# If One Poured over the Spot
## Pot is בן יומו
1. If you pour over the spot while the pot is hot, you are pouring over חנ"ן. As such, the food needs to be 60x the amount of the spot. The spot is just under 60x the amount of the drop, so you need just under 60 x 60 (just under 3600x) the amount of the drop.
2. In addition, you need 60x the amount of the drop itself (not just the spot on the pot). As such, you end up needing just under 61 x 60 (just under 3660x) the amount of the drop.
## Pot is not בן יומו (or Brand New)
1. Since there are no other (non-פגום) בליעות at that spot, the בליעות from the drop don't turn the whole area into חנ"ן.
2. As such, you only need 60x the drop.  <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Why isn't it just under 120x? Just under 60x for the בליעות in the pot, and 60x for the drop.</span>
