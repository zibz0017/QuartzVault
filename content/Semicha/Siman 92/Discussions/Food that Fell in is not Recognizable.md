# Case 1
1. The food that fell in is not recognizable because there are similar foods in the pot. For example, a non-kosher piece of meat falls into a soup, and there are other pieces of meat that look the same.
## הלכה on the Pieces
1. The pieces of meat follow the standard DIN of ROV.
### Chaticha Hariuya Lehischabeid
#### בב״ח and non-Kosher
1. If the piece is אסור because of בב״ח, or because it is from a non-kosher animal, then if the piece is a chaticha hariuya lehischabeid, it can't be מבוטל. As such, all of the pieces of meat become אסור.
2. If it is not a chaticha hariuya lehischabeid, it is מבוטל if there are two other pieces of meat in the pot.
#### איסור balua
1. If it is only an איסור balua (the piece of meat has "swallowed" non-kosher), this is subject to whether we say חנ"ן or not (which is a מחלוקת between the מחבר and רמ"א) regarding if we say that this can't be מבוטל if this is a chaticha hariuya lehischabeid.
2. If we don't say חנ"ן, the piece itself is bietzem fine, and so we won't say that the piece can't be מבוטל because the piece is chaticha hariuya lehischabeid. The only possible issue is that there are בליעות in the food. בליעות are not riuya lehischabeid, and so that will be able to be מבוטל, as well.
3. If we do say חנ"ן, then the piece itself becomes אסור. As such, if the piece is a chaticha hariuya lehischabeid, the piece will not be מבוטל.
# Case 2
1. The food that fell in totally dissolved in the food.
## הלכה
1. If the rest of the food is 60x the איסור, everything is מותר.
