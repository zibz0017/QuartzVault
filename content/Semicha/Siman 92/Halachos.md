# Meat/Milk That Falls into a Hot Pot of Its Opposite
## Asking a גוי
1. מחבר: If the food that fell in is removed quickly and a גוי tastes the food and doesn't taste meat, it is fine.[^1] (The גוי must be asked משיח לפי תומו.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^2])
   If the meat stays there for the amount of time that it takes to boil, we can't rely on the גוי's taste. For the reason, see [[Relying on a גוי for Food that Stays in the Pot Longer than it Takes to Cool Off|here]].
2. רמ"א: We can't ever rely on a גוי.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3]
## Status of the Food that Fell in
1. The food that fell in is always אסור if it is recognizable.[^1] (Practically, this is more relevant when the food that fell in is solid \[like a piece of meat or cheese\], as opposed to liquid \[like milk\] which mixes in.)
2. If it is not recognizable, see [[Food that Fell in is not Recognizable|here]].
## Status of the Food that Was in the Pot
### Case 1
1. The food that fell in is *mixed in to the pot before it dissolves*. For a discussion about what is considered mixed, see [[What is Considered Mixed|here]].
#### Examples
1. Meat/milk fell straight into liquid in a pot of its opposite.[^4]
2. Liquid milk/meat fell onto a solid food in a pot of its opposite and the pot was mixed immediately after the food fell in, or the pot was covered.[^5]
#### הלכה
1. If the food in the pot is מבטל the food that fell in (either the food that is in the pot is 60x the amount of the drop, or, according to the מחבר, if a גוי doesn't taste the drop, as discussed [[#Status of the Food that Fell in|above]]), the food in the pot is fine.
2. If it is not, then all of the food is אסור.
### Case 2
1. The food that fell in landed on a piece of solid food and *was absorbed into that piece*.
#### Example
1. Milk falls directly onto a piece of meat in a pot and the pot is not mixed or covered.[^5]
#### הלכה
##### Status of the Piece
1. מחבר: If the piece is 60x the amount of the drop or a גוי doesn't taste drop in the food it fell on, everything is fine.[^5]
   Otherwise, the entire piece is אסור. For a discussion of why this is, see [[Why Entire Piece is אסור|here]].
2. רמ"א: If the piece is 60x the amount of the drop everything is fine. Otherwise, the entire piece is אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^6]
##### Status of the Rest of the Food
###### רמ"א
1. If the rest of the food in the pot is not 60x the amount of the *drop* the *rest of the food* is אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^6]
###### מחבר
1. ש"ך: *If the piece is mixed in*, you need 60x the amount *of the piece* in the rest of the pot. If there is not, the rest of the food is אסור.
	   If the piece is not mixed in, the rest of the food is fine.[^7]
2. ט"ז: *Regardless of whether the piece is mixed in* you need 60x the amount *of the piece* in the rest of the pot. If there is not, the rest of the food is אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^8]
For a discussion of the underlying reason for these שיטות, see [[Food that is Partially Submerged in a Pot (Machlokes רש''י, ר''י)|here]].
### Case 3
1. A drop falls into a pot of food but we don't know which piece of food in the pot it fell onto.
#### הלכה
1. מחבר: One mixes the pot. If the מותר food is 60x the amount of the אסור food, or if a גוי tastes that food and doesn't taste the איסור, everything is fine. This is even if someone mixes the pot after some time.[^9]
2. רמ"א: One *immediately* mixes the pot. If the מותר food is 60x the amount of the אסור food, everything is fine.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^10]

For further discussion of the מחלוקת between the מחבר and רמ"א, see [[מחלוקת מחבר רמ''א about Being מבטל a ספק|here]].

# חנ"ן
## All Opinions
1. We do not say חנ"ן by two dry foods. For example, if a בב״ח cheerio gets mixed up with another cheerio and then both cheerios fall into something else, we only need to be מבטל that one אסור cheerio, not both of them.[^11] ^e58d34
## מחבר
1. חנ"ן only applies to בב״ח.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^12]
## רמ"א
1. חנ"ן applies to all איסורים. However, there are a few exceptions by שאר איסורים and one exception by בב״ח.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^13]
### Exceptions by שאר איסורים
#### Not Stuck together and Submerged in Sauce
1. If the איסור food is not stuck to the מותר food *and* the מותר food is submerged in מותר sauce, we don't say חנ"ן. In other words, if the איסור food is stuck to the מותר food *or* it is *not* submerged in מותר sauce, we will say חנ"ן.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^13]
2. This only applies to שאר איסורים.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^13]
#### Two Liquids
1. If אסור liquid and מותר liquid mix, we will not say חנ"ן *in a case of הפסד מרובה*. As such, if this mixture falls into a מותר food, the מותר food only needs to be 60x the אסור food *in a case of הפסד מרובה*.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^13]
2. This only applies to שאר איסורים.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^13]
#### Two Dry Foods
1. See [[#^e58d34|above]]. As noted above, this even applies to בב״ח, and presumably, the מחבר agrees to this exception as well.
#### Reason for These Exceptions
1. See [[Reason for רמ''א's Exceptions to חנ''ן by שאר איסורים|here]].
### Case 1
1. The איסור food is not stuck to the מותר food and the מותר food is not submerged in מותר sauce.
#### הלכה
1. If the איסור was בב״ח (meat and dairy are stuck together, or dairy/meat is in sauce and its opposite falls onto it), we say חנ"ן.
2. If the איסור was some other איסור (מותר food is stuck to cheilev, or מותר food is sitting in מותר sauce and some cheilev falls onto it), we don't say חנ"ן.

# בב״ח Into a Parve Food in a כלי ראשון Removed from the Fire
1. If a food has איסור בלוע and it falls into a food that was removed from the fire, it does not emit the איסור into the food and so the rest of the food remains kosher.[^14]
2. This even applies to a piece of dairy/meat that is אסור because of בב״ח and it falls into a food. This is considered to be an איסור בלוע and so the rest of the food is fine.[^14] (In other words, this is a slight exception to חנ"ן. If actual אסור food would fall into the pot in this situation, the food would release בליעות and potentially make the rest of the food אסור. In this situation, it is not able to.)
# Drop Falls onto the outside of a Pot
## The Pot is Hot
### Background
1. For the basic background, see [[Background for the הלכות for When a Drop Falls onto the outside of a Pot|here]].
### Drop Falls above the Food
#### The Pot
1. The drop spreads throughout the upper part of the pot, up until 60x the size of the drop.[^15]
2. As such, one can't pour the food while it is hot out of the pot. One would have to wait until the food cools down to pour it out.[^15] (For details about whether the food is אסור if one does pour it out while it is hot, see [[Pouring Food Over the אסור Part of a Pot|here]].)
#### The Food
##### מן הדין
1. The food is fine if it is more than 60x the drop (which it almost certainly is).
##### מנהג
1. מחבר, או"ה: We treat the food as אסור. For why this is and for some exceptions, see [[The Food in the Pot When a Drop Lands on the Pot above the Food Line|here]].
2. מהרא"י (ש"ד), מהרש"ל, ש"ך, ט"ז: We treat the food as מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^16]
### Drop Falls opposite the Food
#### The Food
1. If the food in the pot is 60x the amount of the drop, the food is מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^15]
#### The Pot
1. ש"ך: The pot becomes אסור, and so one should immediately pour the food out.[^17]
2. ט"ז:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> We are מספק about what happens to the בליעות from the drop:
	1. It doesn't go into the pot.
	2. It spreads directly through the pot and into the food.
	3. As such, the pot is מותר, but one should not use the pot because it looks like it is אסור.
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not sure what exactly the source is. Rabbi Hodkin said to focus on ס"ק יט, and ignore ס"ק יז</span>
3. חב"ד: We see from here the concept of that during בשול, the בליעות go straight from one thing to the next, skipping נ"ט בר נ"ט. We view the drop as going straight into the food. We don't view it as the בליעות going from the drop into the pot, and from the pot into the food. We view it as going straight into the food.[^18]
   An application of this is if we have milk in a כלי, and that כלי is put into a hot בן יומו meat pot, the הלכה will be that we view the milk as going straight through the כלי, straight through the water, and straight into the meat pot.[^18]
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't think the חב"ד belongs here</span>
### Exception
1. If the drop falls on the pot near the fire so that it is nearly instantly dried up, everything is מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^19]
## The Spill is Hot and the Pot is not
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">See סעיף ז</span>
1. If food in a pot on the fire was poured onto a cold כלי, then:
	1. If there is a stream connecting them, this is considered ערוי כלי ראשון and so the pot would be אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^20] For the status of the food, see [[Reason why Food is מותר even though קליפה is more than 1 in 60#Liquid|here]]. For a discussion about why this is considered תתא גבר, see [[Why a Stream Falling on the Side of a Pot is Considered תתא גבר|here]].
	2. If the stream is broken this is considered to be a כלי שיני and so everything is fine.[^20] If it stuck there (like if butter fell onto a meat pot), then it needs to be scraped off.[^21]
# Putting a Pot Down on a Spill
## Case 1
1. A hot pot, but removed from the fire, with food is put on a spill of the opposite type. It does not make a difference if the spill is hot. ^a65054
### הלכה
1. The pot is אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^22]
2. The food inside is מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^22]
For the reason, see [[Reason Behind the הלכות When a Pot is Put on a Spill#Case 1|here]]
## Case 2
1. A cold pot with food is put on a spill of the opposite type that is near the fire (and so the spill is being heated up by the fire). ^6fc0b0
### הלכה
1. The pot and food is אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^22]
For the reason, see [[Reason Behind the הלכות When a Pot is Put on a Spill#Case 2|here]]
## Case 3
1. A hot pot that is still on the fire with food is put on a spill of the opposite type. It does not make a difference if the spill is hot. ^e1e5af
### הלכה
1. The pot and food is אסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^23]
For the reason, see [[Reason Behind the הלכות When a Pot is Put on a Spill#Case 3|here]]
# Shiur Hamiduma
1. If there is a כלי is submerged in a mixture of meat/dairy and other things, it only becomes partially meat/dairy, in the same ratio as the mixture it fell into.[^24]
2. For example, if a parve spoon fell into a chulent that is 5% meat, the spoon only becomes 5% meat. As such, if the spoon then falls into hot milk, you only need 60x 5% of the spoon. If the spoon is 5 oz, you need 5% of 60x5 oz, which end up being just 15 oz.
# הגלה on כלים
1. Filling up a כלי with water and heating it up does not work for הגלה. One has to dip the כלי into boiling water.[^25]
# Steam
1. Steam from a dairy/meat dish that touches dishes or food of the opposite type can אסור them through and through.[^26]
## Requirements
1. The food getting hit with steam is not 60x the amount *of the food emitting the steam* (note, that it is 60x the size *of the food emitting steam*, not 60x the size *of the steam*).[^27]
2. The steam needs to be יד סולדת בו.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^27] However, if the steam is under יד סולדת בו and the top item is hot, it will become אסור כדי קליפה (like other cases of תתא גבר).[^31]
3. A covered pot will not emit steam.[^27] However, it seems that לכתחילה, one should not put two foods in a situation that if they were to be uncovered, there would be a problem.
4. According to some, the steam needs to be coming from a liquid. If it came from a solid (the example given is cheilev that somewhat melts), everything is fine.[^28]
## Status of the Bottom Food
1. דגול מרבבה: בליעות travel from the food that is hit by the steam back to the food that is emitting the steam and can אסור it.[^29]
2. None of the other מפרשים on the page discuss the status of the bottom food, so it would seem that according to everyone else, the bottom food is fine.
# Candles
1. If איסור from a candle (like if the candle is made of cheilev) falls onto a כלי, if the liquid fell from right by the flame, the כלי requires הגלה.
2. If it fell from a different part of the candle, then the [[#The Spill is Hot and the Pot is not|standard dinim of a hot liquid that falls onto a כלי apply]] (usually there won't be a continuous stream so the pot is fine and it just needs to have whatever came from the candle scraped off).
# Ladles
1. The מהרי"ל says that if you dip a ladle (or any כלי) into a boiling כלי that is on the fire and leave it there for some time, the liquid in the ladle when you pull it out counts as a כלי ראשון (regarding kashering for פסח).[^30]
2. For questions on this, see [[Ladles|here]].
# Unorganized Notes
## סעיף 2
### Milk that Falls into Boiling Meat in a Pot of Food
#### Pot is not Mixed or Covered
##### Status of the Meat
###### Shulchan ערוך
Have a גוי taste it. (Since the milk is liquid, the meat doesn't go into the milk and coming back out again, so there is no issue even if the milk stays there).
###### רמ"א
In all cases you need the meat to be 60x the amount of the milk. We don't rely on a גוי.
##### Status of the Rest of the Food
###### Shulchan ערוך
There is a 2 step process. The milk first attempts to "infect" the meat. If it successfully "infects" the meat, then the milk *and the meat* attempt to "infect" the rest of the pot. The rest of the food in the pot needs to be 60x the amount of the milk *and meat*.
###### רמ"א
The milk doesn't "infect" the meat first. You need the rest of the food to be 60x the amount of milk only.
#### Pot is Mixed or Covered
If the pot is mixed or covered quickly after the milk falls into the pot, the Shulchan ערוך agrees to the רמ"א that the milk doesn't "infect" the meat first. You need the rest of the food to be 60x the amount of milk only. If there is 60x, nothing is אסור. If there is not, then everything is אסור.
### סתירה in בית יוסף

#### Question
The B"Y seems to say that the milk can אסור the whole meat. But why doesn't it only אסור כדי klipah, like the Shulchan ערוך says in סימן 105 סעיף 4, where he says that when there is no rotev (and potentially, even if it is partially submerged in rotev), it only assurs כדי קליפה?
(Even though the milk is a liquid, the ש"ך is clearly not viewing it like rotev. It appears rotev needs to be a significant amount of liquid. A few drops of milk does not count. (Own thoughts))
#### Answer 1
Milk is fatty. Fatty things spread throughout.
##### Question on Answer
The Rashba is משמע that milk is not fatty and the Shulchan ערוך seems to agree with that.
#### Answer 2
The case that the Shulchan ערוך happens to be talking about is a case where the meat is fatty.
### Half in Half out of Rotev
*ש"ך 4*
#### ר"י
We view the piece of meat as being part of the rest of the dish. As such, if milk falls on it, the whole dish comes together to "fight" against the milk. The milk does not "infect" the individual piece of meat first.
#### רש"י
We view the piece of meat as on its own. The milk infects the piece of meat but not the rest of the pot.
#### Corollaries
##### From the ר"י
In a sense, the ר"י is more מקל. You are able to use more food to fight against the milk drop. You can use the whole pot.
However, in a sense he is more מחמיר, because the whole dish is at risk of becoming אסור (if there isn't 60x the amount of the milk in the rest of the dish).
##### From רש"י
In a sense, רש"י is more מקל because the rest of the dish is never at risk. Even if the rest of the dish is <60x the amount of milk, it can't become אסור because the meat is not viewed as part of the rest of the dish.
However, in a sense he is more מחמיר because there is less fighting against the milk drop. Only the individual piece of meat is fighting against the milk drop, not the rest of the dish.
#### How We פסקען
##### ש"ך
The ש"ך paskens like the ר"י. (ש"ך 4)
#### How to Understand רש"י
*ט"ז 2*
There is a machlokes between the S"A and the רמ"א.
##### Shulchan ערוך
The milk attempts to infiltrate the piece of meat. If the piece is 60x the size of the milk, there is no problem If it is not, the piece is אסור. But then, the milk *and the meat* infiltrate the rest of the pot unless the rest of the dish is 60x the amount of the milk *and the meat*. This is pshat in the Shulchan ערוך (who says exactly this, namely, that the milk can "double jump", first to the meat, and then to the rest of the dish.)
##### רמ"א
The milk infiltrates the meat and makes it אסור. However, it doesn't go any farther than that. (The ש"ך also says this.)
### How long Does it Have to Be Mixed?
*ש"ך 5*
Most פוסקים: It only needs to be mixed/covered shortly after falling in. It does not need to be continually mixed.
Potentialy the מחבר (according to some): It needs to be mixed/covered shortly after falling in and continually afterwards. (I think. He says "mitichilah vilibasof". Not sure what "libasof" means.)
### סתירה in the רמ"א
*ש"ך 6*
#### Question
The רמ"א says that if a drop of milk falls onto a piece of meat in a dish, the piece is אסור, and you need the rest of the dish to have 60x the amount of the drop.
How does this make sense? If the piece of meat is אסור, how does it make sense that the rest of the dish needs to be 60x the milk drop? If the meat is אסור, then the רמ"א is showing that he is viewing the meat by itself. As such, how could the rest of the dish be in danger of becoming אסור?
#### Answer
The רמ"א is מספק if we פסקען like רש"י or like the ר"י. As such, the piece of meat is אסור misafeik, as according to רש"י, the piece of meat is on its own (and the case is clearly where the piece of meat on its own is not 60x the size of the milk drops). However, we still need 60x the amount of the milk drop for the rest of the pot misafeik, as according to the ר"י, the piece of meat is viewed as part of the rest of the dish.
### Other Pieces of Meat Touching This Piece of Meat (According to the רמ"א)
*ש"ך 7*
You need to take off כדי נטילה from the other pieces of meat that are touching this אסור one.
