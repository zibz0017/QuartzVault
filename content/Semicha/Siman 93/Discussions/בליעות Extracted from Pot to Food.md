# Up to the 60x the Amount of the Previously Cooked Food or the Pot
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק א*
## הלכה
1. The food that is now in the pot needs to be 60x the amount of the previously cooked food or the thickness of the walls of the pot, whichever is less.
   For example, if someone cooked a meat soup in the pot in the previous 24 hours and then they cooked milk, the milk needs to be 60x the amount of meat soup that was in the pot, or the thickness of the walls of the pot, whichever is less.
## Reason
1. The reason is because בליעות from the previous cooked food spreads through the walls of the כלי and then comes out in the next food. As such, the current food needs to be 60x the amount of the previous food.
2. However, the pot can only hold the amount of בליעות equal to its size. It can't hold more בליעות than that. As such, if the food in the pot is 60x the size of walls in the pot, that would also be fine, regardless of how much food was previously cooked in the pot. A way to think about this is that the walls of the pot are a bottleneck on the amount of בליעות that can be absorbed.
## Person Doesn't Know
1. If the person doesn't know how much food was cooked in the previous 24 hours, he has to assume that the whole pot is full of בליעות.
# Up to the Height of the Currently Cooking Food
*Base on סימן צג ש"ך ס"ק א, סימן צד ט"ז ס"ק יב*
1. Additionally, the food only needs to be 60x the thickness of the walls of the כלי up to its own height.
   For example, if someone cooked meatballs in the pot in the previous 24 hours and it filled up the whole pot and then they cooked milk that *fills up half of the pot*, the milk only needs to be 60x the thickness of the walls of the bottom half of the pot.
2. This is because the בליעות only travel from the pot from where it is touching from the food. It does not travel from the rest of the pot to the food.
3. Usually, the food in the pot will not be 60x the walls of the pot (unless the pot is unusually large and the walls are unusually thin).
# Shiur Hamiduma
1. The walls of the pot only absorb בליעות as a percentage of how much dairy/meat was is in the food that was previously cooked there. For more details, see [[Semicha/Siman 92/Halachos#Shiur Hamiduma|here]].
