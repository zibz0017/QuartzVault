# Cooking Meat/Dairy in a Pot of Its opposite
## Status of the Food
1. For the food to be מותר, it needs to be 60x the size of the pot up to the height of food that was cooked in the pot in the past 24 hours.[^1]
   For example, if someone cooked meatballs in a pot and the height of the meatballs was halfway up the pot, and then the person cooked milk in the pot, the milk needs to be 60x the size of the bottom half of the pot for it to be מותר.
2. If the person doesn't know how much was cooked in the pot in the previous 24 hours, then we treat the whole pot as having been cooked in. As such, the dairy/meat in the pot now needs to be 60x the size of the pot.[^2]
3. Usually, the food in the pot will not be 60x the size of the pot.[^3]
4. For ספרדים, they may rely on a גוי tasting the food (as discussed previously) <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Add link</span>
## Status of the Pot
1. The pot becomes אסור.[^2] (This is even if the pot is 60x the size of the food. For a discussion of why, see [[Why the Pot is אסור even if it is 60x the Size of the Food|here]].)
2. The pot may not be used for milk or meat.[^2]
3. מעיקר הדין, the pot may be used for parve. However, the רמ"א says that one should not.[^4]
# If One Cooked in the Now-אסור Pot
## Basic Rule
1. If the pot is not a בן יומו, the food that is cooked in the pot afterwards is fine.[^5]
## Applications
### Case 1
1. Meat/dairy is cooked in a pot of the opposite and the pot *has not* been used in the previous 24 hours, the food is kosher. You then cook the same thing again with 24 hours (for example, you cook dairy in a meat pot that hasn't been used 24 hours, and then within 24 hours of cooking the dairy, you cook more dairy)
#### הלכה
1. The food is fine.[^5]
   This is because there are only dairy בליעות in the pot. Obviously, they are not going to make the dairy food אסור.
### Case 2
1. Similar to the previous case but you cook the opposite of the food you just cooked (for example, you cook dairy in a meat pot that hasn't been used for 24 hours, and then within 24 hours of cooking the dairy, you cook meat)
#### הלכה
1. The food is אסור (assuming the meat isn't 60x the size of the pot and a גוי tastes the meat/dairy in the food according to the ספרדים, as discussed <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">add link</span>).[^5]
   This is because there are dairy בליעות in the pot and they get into the meat.
### Case 3
1. Meat/dairy was cooked in the pot of its opposite and the pot *has* been used in the previous 24 hours. You then cook anything in the pot (meat, dairy, or parve) within 24 hours of the meat/dairy that you cooked in the pot. (For example, you cook dairy in a meat pot within 24 hours of having cooked meat in it. You then cook more food in it (whether meat, dairy, or parve) within 24 hours of cooking the dairy, regardless of whether the meat has been cooked within 24 hours before.)
#### הלכה
1. The food is אסור.
   This is because the first food you cooked becomes בב״ח and you are cooking that in the pot. As such, the "בן יומו timer" begins from then. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">need source</span>
