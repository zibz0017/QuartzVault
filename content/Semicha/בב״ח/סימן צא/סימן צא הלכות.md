# &lrm;מותר and אסור Food or Meat and Dairy Food that Touch
## Basic Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cold food that is מותר touches food that is אסור or meat and dairy food touch each other.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the foods are moist, they need to be washed off (and potentially scrubbed, see [[#Scrubbing Food|here]]). If they are not moist, you don't need to do anything at all.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בדיעבד, if you salt or cook/roast a food without washing it, it is fine, even without removing the קליפה.&lrm;[^2]</span>
## &lrm;דבר חריף
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one of the foods were a דבר חריף they would always need to be washed, even if it is dry.[^2]</span>
# Using an אסור Plate with מותר Food; Meat or Milk Plate with Its Opposite
## Basic Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person wants to put food onto a plate that has absorbed something which is אסור for this food. This could just be general איסור (like the plate has absorbed non-kosher food) or it could be specific to this food (the plate has absorbed meat/dairy and the person wants to put the opposite type of food onto the plate).</span>
## &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: One may not put a food onto a plate in a case that it needs to be washed off if it is not a type of food that people generally wash, as we are concerned that the person may forget to do that.[^3] (For a discussion about why we are concerned that the person will forget see [[Not Allowing Something לכתחילה because a Person may Forget|here]].)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (simple reading): If the food is moist or the plate has absorbed taste through heat, a food that is אסור with what was absorbed into the plate (meaning, a מותר food on an אסור plate, or meat/dairy food on a plate of its opposite) that is placed onto the plate needs to be washed off.&lrm;[^4]
   For further discussion of this שיטה, see [[Food on אסור Plates According to the רמ''א|here]].</span>
3.  <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז, רמ"א (according to the ט"ז):&lrm;[^5] One may even put dry hot food onto a plate that has absorbed איסור through something cold. One may certainly put cold wet food onto such a plate. One may put cold, dry food onto a plate that has absorbed taste through heat.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך:&lrm;[^5] If the plate is dirty, a food that is אסור with what is on the plate (meaning, a מותר food on an אסור plate, or meat/dairy food on a plate of its opposite) that is placed on the plate needs to be washed off.</span>
   If the plate is clean, then there is no issue, at all.
   If the plate is earthenware, however, then a moist food would need to be washed off. As such, one may not put a moist food on such a plate, לכתחילה.
# Scrubbing Food
*Presented by סימן צא פתחי תשובה ס"ק א, ג, סימן צו ש"ך ס"ק כא, ט"ז ס"ק יד*
## Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One puts מותר food onto actual אסור food (or dairy/meat onto its opposite).</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פמ"ג: One always needs to scrub the מותר food.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך, בה"ט, ב"ח: If the אסור food is fatty, the מותר food needs to be scrubbed. Otherwise, it just needs to be washed.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חב"ד, בית אפרים: If the אסור food is fatty and there is דוחקא דסכינא, the מותר food needs to be scrubbed. Otherwise, it just needs to be washed.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">All opinions: Soft foods need to be scratched because washing them will just drive the ממשות in more.
   Additionally, if the food is put on an אסור plate, it just needs to be washed, at worst.</span>
# Bread that Touches Meat/Dairy
*Presented by פתחי תשובה ס"ק ד*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פרי מגדים: The bread can't be eaten with the opposite type of food. There is nothing that can be done about this.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פתחי תשובה: If the outside of the bread was touched, you can rinse it or scratch it. If it was the inside, it must be scratched.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: One may wash the bread. He doesn't say if he is just referring to the outside or even the inside.</span>
# Dairy Falls into Meat and Vice-versa
## Liquid into Liquid
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Everything is אסור (unless one is 60x the other or a גוי tastes it and doesn't taste the meat/dairy, as discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#Asking a גוי|here]] and [[סימן צב הלכות#Status of the Piece|here]]). This is true even if both foods were cold.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is simply because you have actual meat and actual dairy together and you can't remove either one.</span>
## Bottom Food is Hot (or both Foods Are Hot)
### Basic Rule
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We say תתא גבר and so the heat travels from the bottom food to the top food. This can cause a serious transfer of בליעות.&lrm;[^6]</span>
### One is Liquid
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both foods are completely אסור.&lrm;[^7] (Unless it is 60x or a גוי tastes it and doesn't taste the meat/dairy in the opposite food, as discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#Asking a גוי|here]] and [[סימן צב הלכות#Status of the Piece|here]].)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Since we are dealing with liquid, the בליעות are able to travel completely through both foods.</span>
### Both Are Solid
#### At Least One is Fatty
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both foods are completely אסור.&lrm;[^8] (Unless it is 60x or a גוי tastes it and doesn't taste the meat/dairy in the opposite food, as discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#Asking a גוי|here]] and [[סימן צב הלכות#Status of the Piece|here]].)</span>
#### Both Are Lean
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: They are אסור כדי נטילה. Since we are dealing with solids, the בליעות go through the נטילה of both foods.[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: We are מחמיר and treat all meat as fatty. As such, both foods are completely אסור, as discussed.[^9]</span>
## The Top Food is Hot
### Both Are Solid
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both foods are אסור כדי קליפה. This is because we say תתא גבר and so the heat doesn't really go down and so it can't really אסור. (For what happens if you forgot to remove the קליפה, and you cooked the food into something else, see [[If One Forgot to Remove the קליפה|here]].)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the foods are fatty or liquid, the בליעות do not travel more than the קליפה.&lrm;[^10] (<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This הלכה is mentioned near the beginning of this ט"ז, but I don't know if this is really the main source for this.</span></span>
### One is Liquid
#### &lrm;לא נפסק הקילוך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the קליפה in the liquid around the solid is less than 1/60 of the rest of the liquid, the liquid is מותר. This is because the קליפה of the liquid instantly mixes in to the rest liquid and is מבוטל (since the rest of the liquid is 60x the amount). If it is not, see [[Reason why Food is מותר even though קליפה is more than 1 in 60#Liquid|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As for the solid, the קליפה needs to be removed, like always.[^7] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">However, the הלכה is different if there are cracks in the food. I need to add a link there when I finish writing about cracks</span></span>
#### נפסק הקילוך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: This is considered to be a כלי שיני and so there is no problem.[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: We are מחמיר even by נפסק הקילוך and treat it the same as לא נפסק הקילוך.&lrm;[^12]</span>

# Salted Foods
## One Food is Salty
### Dry Foods (that Are Moist)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Salt goes one way, meaning, בליעות from the salty go into the unsalted food, but not the other way. As such, the קליפה of the unsalted food would need to be removed, but the salty food just needs to be washed.[^14]</span>
### Liquid
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one of the items was a liquid, then if the solid food was salty, the salt travels both ways. In addition, because there is a liquid, the salt brings the בליעות completely through both foods. As such, both foods may becomes completely אסור.&lrm;[^15]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the liquid was salty, then the רמ"א holds that in a case of הפסד, the liquid remains מותר. However, there are many who argue and say that everything is אסור.&lrm;[^15]</span>
## Raw Foods

### Wet Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is wet because of the salt (the salt drew out liquid).[^16]</span>
#### Salted to Remove Blood
##### &lrm;מעיקר הדין
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Until the amount of time it takes for the salt remove the blood, the meat is considered to be "cooking". If the meat touches something at that point, it transfers בליעות of the blood to the other food, and can make it אסור. After that point, it no longer is potent.[^16]</span>
##### How We Are נוהג
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">After the amount of time it takes for the salt to remove the blood, one can be מקל in a case of הפסד מרובה. Otherwise, one cannot be.[^16]</span>
#### Salted to Preserve
##### &lrm;מעיקר הדין
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מחבר: Until the time it takes for salt to remove blood, the salt is *not* potent (even though we are not talking about a case of removing blood). Only after that mount of time, does the salt become potent and the food will be considered to be "cooking". As such, if the meat touches dairy at that point, they can become אסור.&lrm;[^16]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;רמ"א: Holds the exact opposite of the מחבר. Until the time it takes for salt to remove blood, the salt *is* potent and the food will be considered to be "cooking". After that amount of time, the salt is no longer potent.</span>
   According to the רמ"א, there is no difference in הלכה between the level of salt for removing the blood and for preserving.[^16]
##### How We Are נוהג
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In all cases (meaning, whether within the time it takes for the salt to draw out the blood or after the time it takes for the salt to draw out the blood) the food becomes אסור.&lrm;[^16]</span>
#### Salted to the Level of Cooking or Roasting
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מעיקר הדין, something needs to be salted to the level of something that is going to be cooked (as opposed to the level of something that is going to be roasted) and kept that way for the amount of time that it takes to heat something up is considered to be "cooking". If something touches it at that point, it is liked they are cooked together.[^16]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א says that in a case of הפסד מרובה, one can be מקל if the salt was already sitting on the food, for the amount of time it takes to heat it up.</span>
   In other words, if the amount of time is 18 minutes, then once the salt has sat on the food for 18 minutes, one can be מקל if the food comes into contact with other food that it is אסור with.
   If it touches food before the 18 minutes, though, then there may be a problem.[^16]
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;אשכנזים are מקפיד even if the level of salt is only the level of salt that it takes for roasting.[^16]</span>
#### Salted to the Level of Pickling
##### All Opinions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the salty food was allowed to sit in water, it loses its potency and it no longer "infects" the other food.[^16]</span>
##### &lrm;מחבר
###### Lean Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the salted food touches a different food that it is אסור with, one must remove a קליפה of the other food where they touched.[^16]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If both foods are salty, then one must remove the קליפה of both foods where they touched.[^16]</span>
###### Fatty Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one of the foods are fatty, both foods become אסור through and through, not just to the level of the קליפה.&lrm;[^17]</span>
##### &lrm;רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The salted food "infects" the other food through and through, not just to the level of the קליפה.&lrm;[^16]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, this is only if the salty food is on bottom.[^18] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Not sure about this. Need to double check this. Also see סימן 105 סעיף 11</span></span>
### Dry Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is actually dry or just wet from something other than the salt. </span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The foods just need to be washed off.</span>
### One Food is Dry

## Cooked Foods
*Note: Whenever we say "cooked" in this section, the same is true whether this was cooked or roasted*
### Basic Background
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cooked foods are softer than raw foods and so less salt can perhaps be enough to transfer בליעות.</span>
### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מחבר: Cooked food that is only salted to the level that the food is still edible is the same הלכה as raw food that is salted to the level that it is inedible.[^20] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I think the מחבר agrees to this next שיטה. Perhaps combine them.</span></span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;ספר התרומה, רא"ש, טור, מהרי"ל: Cooked meat that falls is only somewhat salty that falls into milk needs to have the קליפה removed.</span>
   If the meat has crevices or it was dipped in spices, the whole meat becomes אסור, even if the meat was raw.[^21]
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;ראב"ד, רשב"א, ר"ן: Cold meat, whether cooked or not, no matter has salty, and even if there are crevices in the meat, and there are spices, that falls into milk, will only need to be washed.</span>
   If it's hot, it needs to have the קליפה removed (as discussed [[#Dairy Falls into Meat and Vice-versa|earlier]]).
   If the meat is hot and it has crevices or it was dipped in spices, the whole meat becomes אסור.&lrm;[^21]
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;אור זרוע, הגהת מרדכי, סמ"ק, סמ"ג: Cold cooked meat that falls into milk needs to have the קליפה removed, even if it was not salty.</span>
   If cooked meat has crevices or was dipped in spices, the whole meat becomes אסור.
   If the meat was raw and this happened, then it does not.[^21] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I assume that it needs קליפה</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;רמ"א: In a case of הפסד מרובה, one can be מקל in the case of the meat with the crevices or the spices.[^21]</span>
   Even according to the רמ"א, if hot meat falls into cold milk (assuming it doesn't have crevices and wasn't spiced), only the קליפה needs to be removed. The רמ"א's usual rule that we treat all meat like it is fatty only applies when the bottom food is hot.[^22]
## Brine from Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the meat has been salted to the level of being roasted, the brine is considered "salty" and it can אסור other food, or even כלים (like if the brine falls onto a dairy כלי, or if brine from אסור meat falls onto any כלי).&lrm;[^14]</span>
# Dairy/Meat That Has Been Salted/Pickled together

2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, one is allowed to get הנאה.&lrm;[^23]</span>
