# The קליפה is not Recognizable
## Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is a liquid or the meat has fallen apart.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מחבר, רמב"ם, רא"ש, ר"י, רמ"א (according to the ש"ך):&lrm; בדיעבד, the food is fine, even if it is more than 1/60. For the reason why we allow this even though it is more than 1/60, see [[Reason why Food is מותר even though קליפה is more than 1 in 60|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;טור, ט"ז, מהרש"ל, מהרא"י (ש"ד), ריב"א: If the התר is not 60x the amount of the איסור (meaning, the קליפה), the food is אסור, even בדיעבד.</span>
## Milk Was Poured on the Meat and then it Was Dropped in the Pot
If the meat/dairy were poured onto its opposite, or איסור was poured onto התר, the הלכה is different. See [[Reason why Food is מותר even though קליפה is more than 1 in 60#Pouring איסור onto התר or Meat/Dairy onto it Opposite|here]].
# The קליפה is Recognizable
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, the food is אסור if the התר isn't 60x the amount of איסור. In addition, the קליפה of the אסור food must be removed even if there is over 60x.</span>
