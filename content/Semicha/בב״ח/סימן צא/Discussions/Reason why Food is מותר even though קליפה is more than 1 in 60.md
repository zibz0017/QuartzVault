# Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hot food is put onto cold food.</span>
# &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מעיקר הדין, the foods are all מותר because we say תתא גבר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, הלכה למעשה, we אסור a קליפה of both foods.</span>
# Applications because This is a חומרה
## Liquid
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one of the foods was a liquid, if it is not 60x its קליפה, if we were מקפיד that the קליפה needs to be removed, the whole liquid would become אסור, as the קליפה mixes in and there is no way to save the food (you can't remove the קליפה of a liquid).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, since this is just a חומרה, some are מקל in this situation. הלכה למעשה, this is a מחלוקת:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז, מהרא"י, מהרש"ל:&lrm; אסור.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ת"ח: Only מותר in a case of הפסד מרובה.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך, רמ"א: &lrm;מותר. (It would seem that the רמ"א here isn't דוקא, based off what he says in the ת"ח.)</span>
## Food Falls into Another Food
### &lrm;קליפה is not Recognizable
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Same as by [[#Liquid|liquid]].</span>
### &lrm;קליפה is Recognizable
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the קליפה is recognizable, the קליפה must be removed and you need 60x.</span>
## Pouring איסור onto התר or Meat/Dairy onto it Opposite
### Stream is not Broken
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If hot איסור is poured onto cold התר, or hot meat/dairy is poured onto its opposite and the stream isn't broken, the קליפה of the food is actually אסור, מעיקר הדין. As such, in such a case, if this food would fall into different food, we would אסור it if the קליפה is more than 1/60.</span>
### Stream is Broken
#### &lrm;בב״ח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the stream is broken, then it depends: if we are talking about meat/dairy onto its opposite, then we have two points to be מקל:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat/dairy needs to absorb into the food.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat/dairy needs to cook the food (at least, to make it אסור דאורייתא, as meat and dairy needs to be cooked together to be אסור דאורייתא).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, in a case of הפסד מרובה, one can be מקל.</span>
#### &lrm;איסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is איסור onto התר, the only discussion is if the איסור absorbed into the התר. It doesn't matter if anything is cooked.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, in such a case, the food will always be אסור.</span>
