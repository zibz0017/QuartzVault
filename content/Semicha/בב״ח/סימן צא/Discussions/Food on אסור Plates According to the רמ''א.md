# Background
## Earlier שיטות
### אורחות חיים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may put spices and other דברים חריפים on כלים of a גוי that have been washed well. This is because בליעות do not transfer from something hard (a כלי), one dry thing to another.</span>
### &lrm;רשב"א (1st הלכה)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may use a כלי that has been used for חמץ, for מצה. (According to the ט"ז, this case is even when מצה is hot, as will be discussed [[#^f30d90|below]].)</span>
### &lrm;רשב"א (2nd הלכה)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may salt meat in a כלי that has been used for יין נסך.</span>
### &lrm;בעל העיטור and טור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not put food onto an אסור plate as one would need to wash it off and we are concerned that the person may not do so. The only exception are foods that one generally washes off anyway.</span>
## Question
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the בעל העיטור, it would seem that לכתחילה, one may not put spices onto the plate of a גוי,&lrm; מצה onto a חמץ plate, or put (let alone, salt) meat in a כלי used for יין נסך.</span>
# רמ"א
## Answers
### 1st Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The cases of the אורחות חיים and רשב"א were where the כלי had not absorbed בליעות through heat. It had only absorbed בליעות in another way (מליחה or כבישׁה). In such a case, one may use the כלי for cold food.
   The case of the בעל העיטור was where the כלי had absorbed בליעות through heat. In such a case, there is concern about the transfer of בליעות.
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Rabbi Hodkin thought that the cases of the אורחות חיים and רשב"א were where the כלי absorbed בליעות just by being used for איסור, but not through כבוש or מליחה. I though that the cases were when they were used for איסור with כבוש or מליחה as I thought that was משמע from the לשון of the ש"ך on the first wide line on page נא. Rabbi Hodkin thought that the ש"ך was explaining this specific case (the case of the [[#אורחות חיים]]) but not that every case necessarily was where the כלי absorbed בליעות in this way. That just happened to be what happened in this case. Rabbi Hodkin pointed out that the ט"ז says that one may use the כלי which absorbed איסור without heat for hot food. If the כלי had actually absorbed אסור בליעות through כבוש or מליחה, it would not be allowed to be used for hot food.</span></span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This חילוק is supported by the טור in סימן קכא.</span>
### 2nd Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The case of the אורחות חיים was where the כלי wasn't cleaned well. The other cases are where they were.</span>
### 3rd Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The case of the אורחות חיים is where the מותר food was wet (the meat was moist). The other cases are where the foods are completely dry.</span>
## &lrm;פסק (Simple Reading)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may put מותר food on a plate that has absorbed אסור בליעות if:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The plate had not absorbed בליעות through heat (it is fine if it had absorbed בליעות through מליחה or כבישׁה), and</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מותר food is dry.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(It seems that the רמ"א is being מחמיר like all of answers.)</span>
## Questions on רמ"א
### From the ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א brings a proof from the טור. However, the טור seems to imply against the פסק of the רמ"א. The טור brings down the opinion of the ר"י who says that if a כלי was used for hot אסור food, it may be used for cold מותר food.
   This seems to contradict what the רמ"א said that if something was used for hot איסור it can't be used for התר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א implies that the רשב"א's second הלכה (that one may salt meat on a כלי that has been used for איסור) has relevance to this discussion.
   However, that seems incorrect as meat (which, in this case, is certainly raw) is something that is generally washed and so, as noted in the בעל העיטור, there is no issue of putting this on an אסור plate.
   (The fact that this case is a case of salting as opposed to simply placing it on the כלי is of no consequence.)</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכה about using a חמץ plate for מצה is a מחלוקת. Neither of the שיטות appear to fit with the שיטה of the רמ"א. The שיטות are:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רי"ף, רמב"ם, רמב"ן, רשב"א, (and others): The plate may not be used for מצה under any circumstances.
	   (This does not fit with the שיטה of the רמ"א who said that if the plate was not used for hot חמץ, it may be used for מצה since מצה is dry.)</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רא"ש, טור, ר"ן: The plate may be used for מצה, even if the plate has been used for hot חמץ.
	   (This does not fit with the שיטה of the רמ"א who said that if the כלי was used for hot חמץ, it may not be used for מצה.)</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the טור and others, a כלי that has been used for hot meat and dairy can be used for cold מותר food. (Because of the סתם לשון, this would seem to be true even for מותר liquids. Additionally, the case discussed is about putting fruit on the כלי which were not ordinarily washed)</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רשב"א has various פסקים that do not fit with the רמ"א's answers. The רשב"א holds the following:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a כלי has been used for hot אסור food, it may be used for מותר food.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Like the בעל העיטור (discussed [[#בעל העיטור|above]]).</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מותר liquid may be used in an אסור כלי.</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a proof to the opinion of the רמ"א from the גמ' (עבודה זרה לד.) which says that whether a כלי absorbed איסור through heat or cold makes a difference for what you are allowed to use the כלי for.
   However, this is only regarding a case where a person uses the כלי for hot התר. If someone wants to the כלי for something cold, there is no difference how the כלי acquired its אסור בליעות.</span>
### From the ט"ז
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Question on second answer) Presumably, the טור (and בעל העיטור) were dealing with a case where the כלי was clean as there is no חידוש in saying that one may not put מותר food onto actual ממשות of איסור.</span> ^2cb501
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Question on לשון) The רמ"א says "If the מותר food is completely dry, if the כלי has only absorbed בליעות from cold food, *one may put on it dry מותר food*" (emphasis added).
   Why does the רמ"א repeat that the מותר food is dry?</span> ^7f3877
# ש"ך
## Answer
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">To me, this answer seems to be largely the same as the רמ"א's second answer, but the ש"ך seems to present it as his own answer. I am not sure if I am reading too much into it, or if he is really adding things that I am not understanding.</span>
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The case of the אורחות חיים (that one may use a כלי from a non-Jew for spices) and the case of the טור (that one may use a כלי, potentially even for liquids, that has absorbed hot dairy and meat for מותר food) are where the כלי was clean (the אורחות חיים didn't specifically mean that the כלי was washed. Presumably, the cases that the רשב"א was referring to were also where the כלים were cleaned).
   The case of the בעל העיטור was where the כלי was not properly cleaned.</span>
## פסק
### Temporary Use
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the כלי is fully cleaned there is no problem putting מותר food on it. If the כלי was not cleaned, there is a problem, regardless of the how the כלי absorbed בליעות or whether the food is wet.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can't use a כלי חרס for anything (see [[#^8ebee6|below]] for why this is).</span>
### Regular Use
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not use any כלי that has absorbed איסור for regular use as we are concerned that the person may use it for hot food.</span>
## Questions on the ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מרדכי and תוס'&rlm both say that one can't use an אסור כלי for liquid.
   However, perhaps this was where:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The כלי wasn't properly cleaned, or</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Perhaps this is a כלי חרס, for which we have precedent from the רשב"א to be more מחמיר.
	   (The ש"ך prefers this second answer.)</span> ^8ebee6
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ט"ז pointed out that it seems illogical to assume that the טור and בעל העיטור were discussing a case where the כלי was dirty as it is obvious that one may not put מותר food there (see [[#^2cb501|here]]. Presumably, even though the ט"ז does not ask this on the ש"ך, the same question would apply).
   It would seem that the ש"ך clearly disagrees and feels that there is a חידוש here.</span>
# ט"ז
## Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ט"ז goes  with the [[#1st Answer|1st answer]] of the רמ"א. (The רמ"א himself in both the תורת חטאת and the דרכי משה says that this is the עיקר answer.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, this does not fit with the פסק of the רמ"א. The רמ"א says that even if the כלי only absorbed בליעות without heat, one can only use the כלי for מותר foods that are both dry *and* cold. However, according to the רמ"א's first answer, it should be fine to use for foods that are either dry *or* cold. It shouldn't have to be both.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ט"ז says that there is actually a טעות ספר (which would also answer the ט"ז's [[#^7f3877|other question]], as well). The correct לשון in the רמ"א is: "one may put totally dry מותר food onto an אסור כלי (that has absorbed איסור through heat), or, if the כלי has has not absorbed בליעות through heat, one may put on it wet, מותר food".
   This answers the question just previously mentioned because we are now saying that we only need the food to be dry or cold but it doesn't need to be both.
   This also answers the [[#^7f3877|לשון question from earlier]] because the רמ"א is now no longer repeating that the case is where the food is dry. The רמ"א is talking about a second case where the כלי only absorbed בליעות without heat and the רמ"א is permitting even putting wet food.
   </span>
## פסק
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may use a כלי that has only absorbed איסור without heat for dry, hot, מותר food (the ט"ז learns that the [[#&lrm;רשב"א (1st הלכה)|1st case of the רשב"א]] was where the מצה was hot and, even so, it is allowed to be put on the כלי. This is because the כלי did not absorb בליעות through heat).
   As such, one is certainly allowed to use the כלי for cold, wet, מותר food. This is because heat has more potency to transfer בליעות than simply being wet, so if we are not concerned for a transfer by hot food, we are certainly not concerned for a transfer for cold, wet food.
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am confused by the לשון of the רמ"א according to the ט"ז. The ט"ז learns that the רמ"א says that by כלים not used for hot אסור food, one may use them for cold, wet food. However, the ט"ז holds that according to the רמ"א, one may even use the כלים for hot, dry food, and the ט"ז pointed out that this is a bigger חידוש. Why wouldn't the רמ"א say that one may use the כלים for hot, dry food if that is a bigger חידוש and he holds of it, anyway?
   Perhaps it is to spell out the contrast more, that by a כלי that has absorbed בליעות through heat, one may not use it, even for cold, wet foods (and certainly not for hot foods, whether dry or wet).</span></span> ^f30d90
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For a כלי that has absorbed איסור through heat, one may use it for cold, dry food.</span>
## Questions on the ט"ז
1. <span style="display: block; text-align:justify;">According to the ט"ז, in some cases, there is a transfer of בליעות from a כלי that has absorbed בליעות into a cold food.
   This is very difficult to comprehend because as a far as we are aware, בליעות will only be transferred through heat, כבישה, or מליחה. If one has cold food that isn't there for 24 hours (assuming that it is not salty), there shouldn't be transfer of בליעות.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ט"ז says that as if a כלי has absorbed בליעות through heat, one may not use the כלי. However, the רמ"א himself in סימן קכא סעיף ה says that a כלי that has been used for hot איסור can be used for מותר food (for temporary use). In fact, he even says that it can be used for wet foods.</span>
