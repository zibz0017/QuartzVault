*Based on סימן צב ש"ך ס"ק כב, סימן צג מחבר סעיף א*
# &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A non-בן יומו pot that is cooked with its opposite may be used for parve but not for dairy/meat.</span>
# Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Non-בן יומו בליעות can't become חנ"ן.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, there are no חנ"ן בליעות in this pot.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You can't cook meat or dairy in the pot because there are both dairy and meat בליעות in the pot, and we are מקפיד not to cook food with a כלי with its opposite type of בליעות, even if they are not בן יומו.</span>
   In other words, you can't cook dairy because there are meat בליעות in the pot and you can't cook meat in the pot because there are dairy בליעות in the part. Whether any of these בליעות are a בן יומו are irrelevant (at least, regarding the הלכה, לכתחילה).
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, there is no problem with cooking parve in the pot. There is no problem with the fact that the parve food is getting נ"ט בר נ"ט בליעות from dairy and meat.</span>
