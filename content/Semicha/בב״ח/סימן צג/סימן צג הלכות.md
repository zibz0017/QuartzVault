# Cooking Meat/Dairy in a בן יומו Pot of Its opposite
*Note: There הלכות are the same for all בן יומו כלים used with their opposite type of food*
## Status of the Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For the food to be מותר, it needs to be 60x the בליעות in the pot.[^1] For further discussion about how much בליעות are in the pot and how much come out, see [[בליעות Extracted from Pot to Food|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the מחבר, someone may rely on a גוי tasting the food (as discussed previously). <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Add link</span></span>
## Status of the Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות will spread through the whole pot and make the בליעות in the wall חנ"ן, as can be derived from [[סימן צב הלכות#Drop Falls above the Food|here]]. (This is even if the pot is 60x the size of the food, as can be derived from the linked article.)</span>
# Cooking Meat/Dairy in a Non-בן יומו Pot of Its opposite
*Note: There הלכות are the same for all non-בן יומו כלים used with their opposite type of food*
## Status of the Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is fine because the pot is not a בן יומו and so the בליעות are נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^1]</span>
## Status of the Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pot may not be used for milk or meat.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מעיקר הדין, the pot may be used for parve. However, the רמ"א says that one should not.[^2] For why the pot can't be used with meat or dairy but can be used with parve, see [[Status of Non-בן יומו Pot Cooked with its opposite|here]].</span>
# If One Cooked in the Now-אסור Pot
## Basic Rule
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the pot is not a בן יומו, the food that is cooked in the pot afterwards is fine.[^3]</span>
## Applications
### Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Meat/dairy is cooked in a pot of the opposite and the pot *has not* been used in the previous 24 hours, the food is kosher. You then cook the same thing again with 24 hours (for example, you cook dairy in a meat pot that hasn't been used 24 hours, and then within 24 hours of cooking the dairy, you cook more dairy)</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is fine.[^3]</span>
   This is because there are only dairy בליעות in the pot. Obviously, they are not going to make the dairy food אסור.
### Case 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Similar to the previous case but you cook the opposite of the food you just cooked (for example, you cook dairy in a meat pot that hasn't been used for 24 hours, and then within 24 hours of cooking the dairy, you cook meat)</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is אסור (assuming the meat isn't 60x the size of the pot and a גוי tastes the meat/dairy in the food according to the ספרדים, as discussed <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">add link</span>).[^3]</span>
   This is because there are dairy בליעות in the pot and they get into the meat.
### Case 3
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Meat/dairy was cooked in the pot of its opposite and the pot *has* been used in the previous 24 hours. You then cook anything in the pot (meat, dairy, or parve) within 24 hours of the meat/dairy that you cooked in the pot. (For example, you cook dairy in a meat pot within 24 hours of having cooked meat in it. You then cook more food in it (whether meat, dairy, or parve) within 24 hours of cooking the dairy, regardless of whether the meat has been cooked within 24 hours before.)</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is אסור.</span>
   This is because the first food you cooked becomes בב״ח and you are cooking that in the pot. As such, the "בן יומו timer" begins from then. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">need source</span>
