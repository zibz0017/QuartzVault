# &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is מחלוקת about whether a knife cutting a דבר חריף can אסור only if it is dirty or is a בן יומו, or if it can even אסור when it isn't either of these.</span>
# Reasoning
## First שיטה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the first שיטה (the knife can only אסור if it is a בן יומו or it is dirty) the knife can אסור:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is a בן יומו the בליעות come out of the knife. Because this is a דבר חריף, we do not treat this food as a נ"ט בר נ"ט (original dairy/meat to knife and knife to this food). We treat it as having received taste directly from the original dairy/meat.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is dirty a סתם knife has שמנונית. The שמנונית can go straight into the food.</span>
## Second שיטה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the second שיטה (the knife can אסור even if it is not a בן יומו and is clean) the knife can אסור because the דבר חריף can "reawaken" the non-בן יומו בליעות in the knife and they get extracted into the food. As said before, we do not treat this food as a נ"ט בר נ"ט (original dairy/meat to knife and knife to this food). We treat it as having received taste directly from the original dairy/meat.</span>
# &lrm;הלכה למעשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We פסקען like the second שיטה.</span>
