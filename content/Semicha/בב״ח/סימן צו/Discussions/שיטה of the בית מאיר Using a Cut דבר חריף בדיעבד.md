# &lrm;שיטה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בית מאיר holds that if one cut a דבר חריף (except for radishes) with a non-בן יומו knife and then one uses the food, the הלכה is that the food is fine, even if the rest of the food is not more than 60x times the נטילה of the food or what the knife touched.</span>
# Reason
## &lrm;בליעות in the Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The only way the food can become אסור is only using two חומרות:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">דברים חריפים are treated as if they received the בליעות directly from the original food that the knife cut. We don't view it as a נ"ט בר נ"ט.</span>
	   (Regarding radishes, which are certainly a דבר חריף, this is true מעיקר הדין. This is not considered to be just a חומרה.)
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">דברים חריפים can reawaken non-בן יומו בליעות.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, it seems like a major חומרה to hold so strongly of both of these חומרות that we would אסור the food, even בדיעבד.</span>
## &lrm;שמנונית on the Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In nearly all cases, the rest of the food will be more than 60x the שמנונית on the knife.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, בדיעבד the שמנונית won't אסור the food.</span>
