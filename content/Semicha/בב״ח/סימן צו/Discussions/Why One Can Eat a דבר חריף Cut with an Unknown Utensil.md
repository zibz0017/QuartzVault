# צדדים to Be מקל
1. One can be מקל and eat the food in such a case because there are a variety of ספקות that one can rely upon in this case:
	1. The food may not have been cut with a knife at all (it may have been cut with a hoe or spade).
	2. The food may not be considered a דבר חריף. According to some, only radishes are considered to be a דבר חריף.
	3. סתם knifes are not בן יומו, and according to some, a non-בן יומו knife can't transfer בליעות, even with a דבר חריף.
2. (This is similar to what we say [[Many Foods Cut in a Row#הלכה למעשה|here]].)
# Potential Exception
## Radishes
### ש"ך
1. If we are dealing with a radish (which is certainly a דבר חריף), and we know that the גוי has a בן יומו knife, and the whole שאלה is if this food was cut with that בן יומו knife or something else, the הלכה is that one must be מחמיר.
#### Question on ש"ך
1. Why don't we say that the radish has a חזקה of being מותר, and so even in such a situation, we should permit the radish?
#### Answer
1. We only apply the concept of a חזקה when there was a ספק in the item itself. When there is a ספק that comes in from the outside (like over here, we are מספק about whether בליעות came in from the outside), we say חזקה.
### פתחי תשובה (פשוט פשט in רמ"א)
1. Even by a radish one can be מקל.
## Own Thoughts
1. The ש"ך seems to be mentioning quite a lot of factors for which if we have all of them one would have to be מחמיר. It sounds like if some of these factors were missing, one may potentially be מקל.
2. For example, if one finds cut up onions (as opposed to radishes) in the market and one knows they were cut with a knife, but one doesn't know if the person who cut them up had a בן יומו knife (putting aside the fact that these are probably cut in factories that have machines that only cut onions) it would be מותר to eat.
3. In fact, even if there was a בן יומו knife but we don't know if the person used the בן יומו knife, perhaps we can be מדייק from the ש"ך that one can be מקל if we are talking about onions as opposed to radishes.
4. That seems like a big חידוש.
