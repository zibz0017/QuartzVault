# Cutting with Dairy/Meat Knife
1. When you cut with a dairy/meat knife, בליעות from go from the knife and into the food.
2. The maximum amount of בליעות that can go from the knife and into the food is the amount of the knife that touched the food (as discussed [[בליעות Extracted from Knife to Food#How much בליעות Are Extracted|here]]).
3. At the same time, the maximum בליעות only travel to כדי נטילה within the food (at least בדיעבד). As such, the amount of בליעות that can get into the food is limited by the both of these factors:
	1. The maximum amount of בליעות that can come out of the knife.
	2. The maximum amount of בליעות that can be held by the food.
4. Practically, the knife will almost always be smaller than the נטילה of the food, unless someone uses a lot of the knife and is cutting a small דבר חריף, like scallions.
5. (For a discussion about whether we can rely on someone if they claim to know how much of the knife touched the food, see [[בליעות Extracted from Knife to Food#Person Claims to Know how much Blade Was Used|here]].)
# Cutting with an אסור Knife
1. If someone cuts with an אסור knife, then even though the בליעות that get into the food are limited, as [[#Cutting with Dairy/Meat knife|discussed]], those limited בליעות will make the whole נטילה into חנ"ן. As such, even if the knife is smaller than the נטילה, the whole נטילה will become אסור and one will need to be מבטל the whole נטילה.
# Differences
## The נטילה
1. When it comes to using the dairy/meat knife, there is no איסור here. As such, the נטילה can't become חנ"ן. There can only be חנ"ן if there is איסור.
2. When using the אסור knife, אסור בליעות go into the דבר חריף. As such:
	1. According to the מחבר (that only בב״ח can create חנ"ן), there is no חנ"ן here, so this is the same as by a dairy/meat knife.
	2. According to the רמ"א (that any איסור can make things into חנ"ן) this makes the whole נטילה into חנ"ן.
## Removing the Food
1. In terms of removing the food, בליעות can be removed from food itself if there are מותר בליעות in the food, but not if there are אסור בליעות.
2. As such, if the דבר חריף is cut with a dairy/meat knife, there are only מותר בליעות here and so if the בליעות are מבוטל (the rest of the food in the pot is 60x whatever it has to be, depending on the case), the food may be eaten, as the בליעות are also removed from the food itself.
3. However, if the food was cut with an אסור, knife, the בליעות in the food itself can't be removed, and so the אסור food may not be eaten, even if there is enough food in the pot to be מבטל the בליעות that get into the pot. As such, the rest of the food in the pot may be eaten (as the אסור בליעות that get into the rest of the food are מבוטל), but the actual דבר חריף may not be eaten as the בליעות in the דבר חריף will not be מבוטל.
