*Presented by סימן צו ש"ך ס"ק ה, סימן צח בדי השלחן ס"ק יד*
1. There is a מחלוקת about whether one can rely on someone tasting the food if someone cuts a דבר חריף with a knife:
	1. רא"ש, מחבר, טור, רשב"א, אשיר"י: We can rely on taste, even לכתחילה.
	2. רמב"ם, סמ"ג, סמ"ק, מרדכי, מהרש"ל, ש"ך: We can only rely on taste בדיעבד.
	3. רמ"א: We can rely on taste of a Jew, בדיעבד. We can't ever rely on a non-Jew. (This is different than other cases where we have a שאלה about whether the food is kosher or not, in which case a Jew can't test it. In this case, the שאלה is whether the food is dairy/meat or not, but it is definitely kosher, and so a Jew can taste test it.)
	   (This seems to contradict what we say [[סימן צח הלכות#Relying on a Jew|here]]. According to the בדי השלחן, the הלכה is like what we say over here, namely, that we only rely on the taste of Jew, בדיעבד.) ^ccc03d
	4. עטרת זהב: We can't every rely on taste.
