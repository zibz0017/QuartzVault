1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether one can rely on someone tasting the food if someone cuts a דבר חריף with a knife:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רא"ש, מחבר, טור, רשב"א, אשיר"י: We can rely on taste, even לכתחילה.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמב"ם, סמ"ג, סמ"ק, מרדכי, מהרש"ל, ש"ך: We can only rely on taste בדיעבד.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: We can rely on taste of a Jew, בדיעבד. We can't ever rely on a non-Jew. (This is different than other cases where we have a שאלה about whether the food is kosher or not, in which case a Jew can't test it. In this case, the שאלה is whether the food is dairy/meat or not, but it is definitely kosher, and so a Jew can taste test it.)</span>
	   (This seems to contradict what we say [[סימן צח הלכות#Relying on a Jew|here]]. According to the בדי השלחן, the הלכה is like what we say over here, namely, that we only rely on the taste of Jew, בדיעבד.) ^ccc03d
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">עטרת זהב: We can't every rely on taste.</span>
