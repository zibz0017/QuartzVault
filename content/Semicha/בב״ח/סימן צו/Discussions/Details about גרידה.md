# גרידה Vs Removing a קליפה
1. גרידה is less than removing the קליפה.
2. The קליפה is thick enough that one can remove the whole thing in one shot. If the layer that one is taking is thin enough that it won't stay together, this is less than the קליפה.
3. גרידה just literally means scratching.
# Why גרידה is Required

1. Because these foods are soft, they require גרידה.
2. This is different than most moist foods that just require הדחה or even nothing at all, as discussed [[סימן צא הלכות#מותר and אסור Food or Meat and Dairy Food that Touch|here]].
# If גרידה is not Practical
1. If גרידה is not practical, one may scrub the food. You need to scrub the food with the same amount of pressure as the knife pressed on the food when you cut it.
