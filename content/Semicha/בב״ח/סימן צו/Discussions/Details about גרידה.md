# &lrm;גרידה Vs Removing a קליפה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">גרידה is less than removing the קליפה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The קליפה is thick enough that one can remove the whole thing in one shot. If the layer that one is taking is thin enough that it won't stay together, this is less than the קליפה.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">גרידה just literally means scratching.</span>
