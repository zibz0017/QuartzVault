# שיטה of the אבן העוזר
## Basic הלכות
### נ"ט בר נ"ט
1. The reason for נ"ט בר נ"ט is that the taste get weaker by every transfer. ט"ז נ"ט בר נ"ט
### דבר חריף
1. A דבר חריף pulls all of the בליעות out of כלי that it is with.
2. However, a דבר חריף expunges its flavor into the next thing the same as anything else. It does not expunge all of its flavor (unless it is going into another דבר חריף).
## Applications
1. When someone cuts meat/dairy with a knife, there is a "step down". בליעות from the food go into the knife, but the knife doesn't pull all of the בליעות out of the food. The knife has the status of a נ"ט.
2. If someone then cuts a דבר חריף with that knife, the knife absorbs all of the flavor from the knife. It gets the same status as the knife, and so it also has the status of a נ"ט.
3. When you cut the דבר חריף with another knife (or crush it in a grinder), the next knife does not get all of the flavor from the דבר חריף. In terms of expunging flavor, a דבר חריף does not expunge any more than a non-דבר חריף.
4. As such, the second knife is only getting נ"ט בר נ"ט בליעות from the דבר חריף, so if the second knife was parve, it remains parve.
# Opposition
## Magen Avraham
1. The Magen Avraham says if the second knife was parve, it would get the status of the original food.
2. (The reason for the Magen Avraham is not presented here. He may hold that:
	1. A דבר חריף gets the status of the original food, or at least doesn't become less than a נ"ט,
	2. A דבר חריף expunges all of its בליעות.)
## ט"ז
1. The ט"ז holds that the reason for נ"ט בר נ"ט is not because the flavor becomes weaker.
2. He proves this from the fact that we say that נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא is אסור. If the reason for נ"ט בר נ"ט is that the taste becomes weaker, it shouldn't make a difference if this is in a case of נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא or נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא.
