# דבר חריף
## Background
### שבולי לקט
1. Some are מקל and eat דברים חריפים if there were many cut in a row with the same knife, even if the knife is has אסור בליעות.
2. This is because after cutting through a few דברים חריפים, the knife gets "kashered" by the food it is cutting through. As such, most of the דברים חריפים that were cut with the knife were actually cut when the knife was kosher.
3. If one doesn't know which were the ones that were cut earlier and which were the ones that were cut later, one may rely on רוב and eat any of them.
### טור
1. Olives which are cut with a חמץ knife, whether they are בן יומו or not, the olives may not be eaten on פסח (according to the שיטה that says that a דבר חריף can reawaken non-בן יומו בליעות, as discussed [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור#Second שיטה|here]]).
### מחלוקת how to Understand these הלכות
#### ב"ח
1. The הלכה of the שבולי לקט is only true because the knife is presumably not a בן יומו (most knives of non-Jews are not בן יומו), we are the שיטה that a דבר חריף can't reawaken non-בן יומו בליעות (as discussed [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור#First שיטה|here]]).
2. The only concern here is that there are ממשות on the knife. These ממשות gets cleaned off after the first cut or two.
#### ש"ך
1. The case of the שבולי לקט is specifically with דברים חריפים like lemons. These food are a middle level of חריף and so after a few cuts, they are מבטל the בליעות in the surface of the knife. At the same time, they are not so חריף that they can draw בליעות out from the middle of the knife.
2. Other kinds of דברים חריפים (like radishes) which are very spicy can draw בליעות out from the middle of the knife, but they can't be "kasher" than part of the knife.
3. The טור views olives are either a lower level of spice than the lemons and so they can draw out בליעות from the surface but can't "kasher" it, or as very spicy, like the radishes.
## הלכה למעשה
### ב"ח
1. The הלכה of the שבולי לקט is only according to the שיטה that a דבר חריף can't reawaken non-בן יומו בליעות. Since we don't פסקען like that שיטה (we hold that דבר חריף can reawaken non-בן יומו בליעות), this הלכה is irrelevant.
### ש"ך (בית יוסף, רמ"א)
1. We can use this התר in conjunction with other התירים.
2. For example, if the גוי may have used a new knife, or he may have a knife specifically for cutting vegetables, then we can say that we have the following ספקות at play:
	1. The גוי may have a knife that doesn't have אסור בליעות and he may have used it.
	2. The food may not be considered a דבר חריף. According to some, only radishes are considered to be a דבר חריף.
	3. סתם knifes are not בן יומו, and according to some, a non-בן יומו knife can't transfer בליעות, even with a דבר חריף.
	4. The knife may have been "kashered" by previous cuts it has made.
3. (This is similar to what we say [[Why One Can Eat a דבר חריף Cut with an Unknown Utensil#צדדים to Be מקל|here]].)
#### מטה יהונתן
##### Question
1. Why don't we say that that the as the knife cuts through the דבר חריף, it: 
	1. releases אסור בליעות into the food,
	2. the בליעות make the food into חנ"ן,
	3. the knife is now cutting through an אסור food, so
	4. the knife absorbs new אסור בליעות.
2. As such, there should be no התר of cutting through many דברים חריפים.
##### Answer
1. A דבר חריף can't pull out בליעות and put in בליעות at the same time. It can only do one or the other.
# Non-דבר חריף
1. By non-דבר חריף, the only concern is the ממשות on the knife. The בליעות can't come out because the food is not a דבר חריף.
2. As discussed, all opinions agree that the knife gets cleaned off by the first few cuts.
3. As such, one may eat a non-דבר חריף that has even been cut with a non-kosher knife. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Presumably even בן יומו. Need to confirm</span>
