# Cutting a דבר חריף Multiple Times After Cutting a Turnip
1. We said that if one cuts a turnip and then cuts a דבר חריף, they only need to wash the דבר חריף (or גרידה, presumably).
2. This is only for the first cut, meaning, if someone makes multiple cuts in the דבר חריף, the הלכה reverts back to the standard הלכות about cutting a דבר חריף.
3. Technically, if someone would keep going back and forth (they would cut the turnip, then cut the דבר חריף, then cut the turnip, then cut the דבר חריף, etc.), the דבר חריף would just require washing. The turnip would keep "getting in the way" of letting the בליעות get into the דבר חריף (or גרידה, presumably).
# לכתחילה Cutting a Turnip
1. מהרש"ל: One may purposely cut a turnip to "kasher" one's knife and then use it to cut a דבר חריף that they want to eat with the opposite type of food.
2. ש"ך, או"ה: One may not purposely do this.
