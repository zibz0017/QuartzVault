# Cutting a דבר חריף
## &lrm;לכתחילה
### The Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some say that a knife can אסור כדי נטילה only if it wasn't particularly cleaned or if it is a בן יומו.&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Others say that a knife does this even if it is cleaned and not a בן יומו.&lrm;[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We פסקען like the second שיטה.&lrm;[^2]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For the reasons behind these שיטות, see [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור|here]].</span>
### The Food
#### Dry Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is totally dry:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">או"ה: The food just needs to be washed.[^3]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (ת"ח): There is no difference between this and moist food.[^4]</span>
#### Moist Food
*I would assume that most sharp foods, like onions, are moist.*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the knife is a problem (according to each שיטה, as discussed [[#The Knife|above]]) and you cut a דבר חריף:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: You need to remove a כדי נטילה of the food before cooking it with its opposite.[^1]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: &lrm;לכתחילה, you may not use any of the food with the opposite of the knife (if you cut a דבר חריף with dairy knife, you can't use the food with meat, and vice-versa).[^5]</span>
	   However, if one is in a place that the only דבר חריף that one can get has been cut with a treif (or meat/dairy) knife, one can cut off a נטילה and use the food. (The רמ"א regards this as a בדיעבד situation.)[^6]
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For details about whether one can have someone taste the food and rely on that, see [[Relying on Taste When Cutting a דבר חריף|here]].</span>
## &lrm;בדיעבד (One Cooked with the דבר חריף)
### Cut with Dairy/Meat Knife and Cooked with Opposite Food
#### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר (according to the ש"ך, a perhaps the ט"ז), רמ"א: if one cooked the דבר חריף into food that is the opposite of the knife (a דבר חריף cut with a dairy knife into meat food or vice-versa), the food must be 60x the נטילה of the food around the cut (or 60x the whole food if it was diced), or 60x the amount of the blade that touched the food, whichever is less.[^7]</span>
   For further discussion about how much of the blade counts, see [[בליעות Extracted from Knife to Food|here]].
   Where the בליעות in the דבר חריף were מבוטל, the דבר חריף does *not* need to be removed.[^8]
   For example, if someone cuts an onion with a dairy knife and puts it into a meat soup and the soup is more than 60x the בליעות in the knife, one may eat the soup with the onions. The onions would not have to be removed.
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בית מאיר, מחבר (according to the בית מאיר): If the knife is not a בן יומו, the דבר חריף can't אסור the food, even if it the rest of the food is less than 60x the נטילה or what the knife touched.[^9] For why this is, see [[שיטה of the בית מאיר Using a Cut דבר חריף בדיעבד|here]].</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה למעשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can use the שיטה of the בית מאיר as a tziruf with other potential התירים.</span>
### Cut with אסור Knife and Cooked with מותר Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one cut the דבר חריף with an אסור knife and cooked it in מותר food:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: In terms of how much food is needed to be מבטל the איסור, there is no difference between this case and the case with the dairy/meat knife.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: The food must be 60x the נטילה of דבר חריף (or 60x the whole food if it was diced). *60x the blade doesn't help*.[^7]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, even if the בליעות in the דבר חריף were מבוטל, the דבר חריף itself *must* be removed.[^8]</span>
### Reasoning
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[בליעות Extracted from Knife to דבר חריף|here]].</span>
### Basic Summary of what Cutting דבר חריף Does
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As discussed [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור|here]], cutting a דבר חריף can:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">"Reawaken" non-בן יומו בליעות (meaning, even if the knife is non-בן יומו, the בליעות are treated like they are בן יומו).</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The דבר חריף is treated like it got בליעות directly from the original food that the knife was used to cut (for example, if it is a meat knife, we treat the דבר חריף like it got בליעות directly from meat). We do not treat it like a נ"ט בר נ"ט (original food to knife and knife to דבר חריף)</span>
## Cutting a Turnip
### Basic Idea
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Turnips have a very strong and unique flavor.[^10]</span>
### Applications
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone cuts a turnip and then cuts anything else, even a דבר חריף that they plan on eating with the opposite type of food of the knife, they just need to wash that thing[^11] (or do גרידה, presumably, if it is soft). For more details, see [[Cutting a Turnip and then Cutting a דבר חריף|here]].</span>
## Alternative Parts of דבר חריף
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Other parts of a דבר חריף are not considered to a be a דבר חריף (like the leaves, or stems, or things like that).[^5]</span>
## &lrm;דבר חריף Cut by a גוי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For most דברים חריפים, if they were cut with an unknown utensil, and it is reasonable that they were cut with something other than a knife (like with a hoe or spade), one can eat the דבר חריף. For why this is, for more details, and exceptions, see [[Why One Can Eat a דבר חריף Cut with an Unknown Utensil|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If many דברים חריפים were cut in a row with the same knife, even though you know that it was a knife (as opposed to some other utensil), many are מקל and allow one to eat these foods. For why this is and more details, see [[Many Foods Cut in a Row#&lrm;דבר חריף|here]].</span>
   For the הלכה when many non-דברים חריפים are cut in a row, see [[#Many Cut in a Row|here]].
## Spice Crusher/Grinder
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone crushes spices in a בן יומו/non-בן יומו spice grinder:</span>
### &lrm;בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, the spice gets the same dairy/meat (or אסור) status as the spice crusher. It is not a נ"ט בר נ"ט, it is viewed as a נ"ט (like a דבר חריף cut with a knife).[^12]</span>
### Non-בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר (here): There is no problem with using the spices for food of the opposite type as the crusher/grinder. (The בית יוסף holds like the שיטה over [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור#First שיטה|here]] that non-בן יומו doesn't doesn't make things אסור.)&lrm;[^12]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א, ש"ך, ט"ז, בית יוסף (elsewhere): There is no difference between non-בן יומו and בן יומו. (These שיטות hold like the שיטה over [[How a Knife Cutting a דבר חריף can אסור#Second שיטה|here]] that non-בן יומו is a problem by דבר חריף.)&lrm;[^13]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(There seems to be a סתירה in the בית יוסף/מחבר.&lrm;[^14])</span>

## &lrm;דבר חריף Cut with Dairy/Meat Knife and then Cut with Parve Knife
### Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone dices a דבר חריף with a dairy/meat knife. They then dice it again with a parve knife. They would like to use this parve knife to cut other דברים חריפים to use with the opposite of the original knife.</span>
   For example, someone dices a דבר חריף with a meat knife. They then cut the diced pieces with a parve. Later, the person wants to use this parve knife to cut a different דבר חריף with dairy.
### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מגן אברהם: The second knife gets the status of the of the original food. As such, one may not use it to cut דברים חריפים for food that is the opposite of the original food.[^15]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אבן העוזר: The second knife does not the get the status of the original food. As such, one may it to cut דברים חריפים for food that is the opposite of the original food.[^15]</span>
### Reasoning
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[שיטה of the אבן העוזר on דבר חריף and נ''ט בר נ''ט|here]].</span>

1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A דבר חריף can't pull out בליעות from a cold כלי just by resting on it.[^16] For more details, see [[סימן צה הלכות#On a Meat/Dairy Plate|here]].</span>
# Cutting a Regular Cold Food
## &lrm;לכתחילה
### Standard Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[סימן פט הלכות#Cutting Cold Food|here]].</span>
### Many Cut in a Row
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If many foods were cut in a row, one can eat them all with whatever they want. This is because there is no problem of בליעות (because it is not a דבר חריף) and the שמנונית is cleaned off when you cut the first few.[^17] For more details, see [[Many Foods Cut in a Row#Non-דבר חריף|here]].</span>
## &lrm;בדיעבד
### The Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you cut the opposite type of food, מסתמא, the knife would need נעיצה.</span>
### The Food
#### Clean Non-בן יומו Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the knife is clean and not a בן יומו and the food is not a דבר חריף, the food is totally fine.[^18]</span>
#### &lrm;סתם Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you cut the opposite type of food, or a parve food that you want to eat with the opposite type of food, if the food is dry, you need to just wash it off. If it is mushy, you need to scratch off the surface of what you cut (גרידה).&lrm;[^11] (<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to this. It would seem to me that this should have the same הלכות as [[סימן צא הלכות#Scrubbing Food|this]], but that is not explicitly said.</span>)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For more details, see [[Details about גרידה|here]].</span>
