# איסורי דאורייתא of בב״ח
1. Cooking dairy and meat together.[^1]
2. Getting הנאה from meat and dairy which has been cooked together.[^1]
3. Eating dairy and meat which has been cooked together.[^1]
## Definition of "Cooked Together"
### Count as "Cooked Together"
1. Literal cooking or roasting.
2. Frying. However, because there are some שיטות that say that it is not, one can be מקל in a case of הפסד גדול.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^2]
### Do not Count as "Cooked Together"
1. Pickling (kavush) milk and meat together.[^3]
2. Salting (melicha) milk and meat together.[^3]
3. Placing dairy/meat on top of its opposite when they are both hot (but not on the flame).[^2]
4. Smoking meat and milk together.[^4]
5. Cooking meat and milk together in hot springs.[^4]
6. Cooking meat and milk together in the sun. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">{need source}</span>
#### הלכה
1. אסור to eat midiraban.[^5]
2. I things have been combined together in any of these ways, they may no longer be cooked, even on their own, because they now contain בליעות of their meat/dairy opposite. (For example, if you salt meat and cheese together, the meat contains בליעות of cheese and the cheese contains בליעות of meat. Neither of them may be cooked, even on their own, because the person would be cooking meat and dairy together).[^2] ^16a5de
## Minimum Requirements to Be עובר on…
### איסור of Eating
1. There is no minimum amount. A person is עובר for eating or cooking any amount at all.[^6]
2. A person is עובר for eating in any fashion, even in ways which are not אסור regarding other איסורים, like swallowing without chewing. (Other איסורים, like eating treif, are מותר if swallowed.)[^7] 
### איסורים of Getting הנאה or Cooking
1. רמב"ם: There is no minimum amount. A person is always עובר.
2. Various other ראשונים and Achronim: Some say the mixture needs to be a kizayis. Others say that the person needs to get a perutah's worth of הנאה.<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">(It is not clear to me if these opinions argue with each other or agree.)</span>
# General איסורי דרבנן of בב״ח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Eating meat and dairy that has not been cooked together. As mentioned, meat and dairy that has been salted or pickled together is only אסור דרבנן.</span>

For further discussion, see [[Cooking or Getting הנאה from Foods Which Are Only אסור to Eat MIDIRABANAN|here]].</span>
# What Meat/Milk Count for בשר בחלב
## Not Milk/Meat at All
1. Almond milk.[^8]
2. Milk from a human (whether from a female[^9] or male[^10]).
3. Fish is parve.[^11] However, there is still an issue of סכנה to eat this with meat and potentially milk. For further discussion, see [[Fish with Milk and Meat|here]].
4. According to most, meat/milk from non-kosher animals (meaning, animals that are non-kosher, like a pig, not animals that are kosher but shechted incorrectly).[^11][^12][^13] (Obviously, one can't eat/drink the meat/milk anyway because these are non-kosher.) For further discussion, see [[Milk or Meat from Non-Kosher Animals with Its Kosher Opposite|here]].
### הלכה
1. מעיקר הדין, these are parve. However, there may be a problem of [[#מראית עין]].<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^9]
## Meat/Milk דרבנן, but not אסור to Cook
1. Bird meat (such as chicken and turkey).[^11]
2. Meat/milk of undomesticated kosher animals (like deer) according to most פוסקים.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^11][^14][^15] For further discussion, see [[Bird and Wild Animal Meat and Milk|here]].
### הלכה
1. אסור to eat together with their meat/dairy opposite. They are מותר to cook with their meat/dairy opposite.
## Meat/Milk דרבנן, but אסור to Cook
1. Some byproducts from cheesemaking. See [[Cheesemaking Byproducts|here]] for a discussion.
2. Milk in the udder of a dead animal.[^16]
3. Milk from a male animal.[^17]
4. Blood.[^4] (This is only referring to the איסור of בב״ח, not the סתם איסור of eating blood.)
5. Placenta.[^18]
6. Hide.[^18] However, for hide is the same as flesh underneath, some say that this is אסור דאורייתא.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^19]
7. Sinews.[^18]
8. Bones (without marrow. Marrow is בשר דאורייתא.)[^20]
9. Horns.[^18]
10. Hooves.[^18]
### הלכה
1. אסור to cook with milk/meat MIDIRABANAN (at least לכתחילה),[^21][^18] and certainly to eat.[^22][^18] (רע"א doesn't understand why this is different than chicken and milk for which we say that this is אסור to eat together but not אסור to cook together.[^23])
## Meat/Milk דאורייתא
1. Meat and milk from domesticated kosher animals.[^24] For a discussion about niveilah and cheilev from a kosher animal with milk, see [[Niveilah and Cheilev with Milk|here]]
2. Fetus.[^18]
3. Marrow.[^25]
4. Some byproducts from cheesemaking. See [[Cheesemaking Byproducts|here]] for a discussion.
### הלכה
1. This is considered full-fledged meat/milk. It is אסור midioraysa to cook or eat with its meat/milk opposite.[^18][^26]
## Disputed Cases
### Milk Drunk by a Now-dead Animal
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Perhaps should be made into its own section and then I should put a link here?</span>
#### Milk is Fluid (tzalul)
1. Milk has been drunk by an animal that has since died. The milk is still very fluid (it hasn't undergone much digestion, if at all).
##### שיטות
1. מחבר: This is a מחלוקת. It seems that he holds that it would be מותר לכתחילה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^27] (Presumably, there is a מראית עין problem, in which case this would be אסור לכתחילה but מותר בדיעבד,<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^28] but this is not explicitly discussed regarding the מחבר.)
2. רמ"א: This is a מחלוקת and it is not fine, even בדיעבד.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^29]
#### Milk is Congealed (קרוש)
1. The milk has undergone some degree of digestion and is now congealed.
##### שיטות
1. מחבר, רמ"א (as explained by the ש"ך):<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^30] מעיקר הדין, this is fine, לכתחילה. However, the רמ"א says that there is a מראית עין problem. בדיעבד, this is fine (as is true in general by מראית עין issues). (Presumably, the מחבר agrees.)
2. מהרש"ל, ר"ן, או"ה (all presented by the ש"ך):<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^31] There is a problem מעיקר הדין, even בדיעבד.
#### Milk Absorbed בליעות from Stomach, then Made into Cheese
1. A person salts the animal while there is some of this milk still in the animals stomach or he leaves the milk in the animal's stomach for 24 hours. He then mixes this milk with other milk used to make cheese. The potential issue is that this milk has acquired בליעות of meat from the stomach of the animal through melicha or כבישה.
##### Side Notes
1. לכתחילה a person should not leave the milk in the animal's stomach the length of time that it would take for it to cool down. This is because the milk in the stomach is full of acid and so the כבישה process occurs much faster.[^32]
2. While we are usually מחמיר to say that transfer occurred instantly when the meat has been salted, and even if it has been salted relatively lightly, if the milk has already been mixed with other milks we are more מקל because there we can rely on the שיטה of the מחבר, (explained shortly) who is מקל the moment the milk is drunk. Additionally, there is only a concern for an איסור דרבנן is this case[^33] (assuming that the milk was not cooked after, in which case it would be אסור דאורייתא, as discussed [[#^16a5de|above]][^34]). This is the שיטה of the רמ"א, as discussed <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">סימן צא סעיף ה (need to add link)</span>.
##### שיטות
1. מחבר (as explained by the ש"ך and ט"ז):[^35] One should not do this לכתחילה, but בדיעבד, this is okay. There is no difference about whether the milk is fluid or not.
2. רמ"א: Depends if the milk is fluid or not. If it is fluid, this is אסור, even בדיעבד (unless the amount of regular milk is 60x the amount of this stomach milk. For a discussion about a potential issue with bitul בששים and the response, see [[Bittul בששים by Milk Left in an Animal's Stomach|here]]).[^32]
   If it has congealed, it is fine, לכתחילה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^36]
For the reasoning behind these שיטות, see [[Using Milk in an Animal's Stomach to Make Cheese|here]].

# מראית עין
## Person Wants to Eat the Food
### לכתחילה
#### איסורי דאורייתא
1. A person wants to create and eat a food which appears like there is an איסור דאורייתא on eating the food.
##### שיטות
All opinions:[^37] This is אסור.
##### Examples
1. Catching fish blood in a cup to drink. (Fish blood is totally מותר, but it looks like animal blood which is אסור דאורייתא.)[^38]
2. Cooking meat with alternative milks to eat (like almond milk or Chalav אישה).[^9][^8]
#### איסורי דרבנן
1. A person wants to create and eat a food which appears like there is an איסור דרבנן on eating the food.
##### שיטות
1. רמ"א (פשוט פשט):<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^8] There is no issue of מראית עין by ISSUREI דרבנן. As such, this is perfectly fine.
2. רמ"א ([[מראית עין by ISSUREI DIRABANAN#^8bcaeb|לפי פתחי תשובה]]):<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^39] There is a problem if this is done in public, but not in private.
3. מהרש"ל,<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^38] ש"ך:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^40] There is an issue of מראית עין by איסורי דרבנן. As such, one must make it clear in some what that they are not violating an איסור.
4. ט"ז:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^41] One should make it clear in some what that they are not violating an איסור. However, if one has no way to do this, it is fine for them to create and eat the food.
##### Examples
1. Cooking chicken with alternative milks to eat (like almond milk). A way to indicate that one is not violating as איסור is by leaving almonds next to the mixture.[^8]
2. Mixing meat with alternative milks (without cooking them together) to eat.[^40]
### בדיעבד (דאורייתא and דרבנן)
1. A person has a food that looks like it is אסור to eat, whether דאורייתא or דרבנן, and they have no way to indicate that there is no איסור occurring here.
#### שיטות
1. מחבר,<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^9] ט"ז, רמ"א (לפי ט"ז):<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^41] One may eat the food.
#### Examples
1. A person accidentally mixed Chalav אישה into his pot of meat while it is cooking.[^9]
## Person Wants to Get הנאה or just Cook the Food
1. A person wants to cook or get הנאה from foods which appears like there is an איסור דאורייתא on doing this. (Whether there is an איסור דרבנן of בשר בחלב to eat together, or not.)
### שיטות
1. Various מפרשים (Brought down by the [[מראית עין by ISSUREI DIRABANAN#Answer 1|ש"ך's first answer]]):[^42] There is a problem of מראית עין.
2. ש"ך ([[מראית עין by ISSUREI DIRABANAN#Answer 2|Second answer]]), מחבר (According to this answer), רמ"א (According to this answer):[^42] There is no problem of מראית עין when it comes to cooking by ISSUREI דרבנן of בשר בחלב.
3. ש"ך ([[מראית עין by ISSUREI DIRABANAN#Answer 3|Third answer]]):[^42] There is a problem of cooking/getting הנאה when using alternative milks with meat. There is no problem when using non-kosher meat/milk with its kosher opposite. (It would seem that this answer would certainly agree with the LEVUSH ([[#^519575|below]]) that there is no problem of cooking chicken with meat.)
4. ט"ז, רמ"א (According to the ט"ז, and, seemingly, the PASHUT reading):[^43] There is a problem with any milk (including alternative milks and milk from non-kosher animals) and meat (including meat from non-kosher animals).
   (The ט"ז himself feels that there is no problem with meat/milk from non-kosher animals and its kosher opposite, but he doesn't want to be מקל against the רמ"א.)
5. Levush:[^44] There is no problem of cooking chicken with milk. ^519575
### Examples
1. Cooking chicken with milk.[^43]

1. There are 3 points to look at:
	1. The hardness of the shell (hard or soft).
	2. Development of the yolk (is there yolk and albumen, or just yolk).

# Previously Cooked Meat and Milk
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Should be moved earlier as one of the מראי מקומות are on סעיף 1</span>
1. If the meat and milk have been previously cooked separately, there is still an איסור to cook them together. We do not say אין בשול אחר בשול.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^50]
2. If they have previously been cooked together, it would seem one should be מחמיר. For more details, see [[בב״ח Previously Cooked Together|here]].
# Dried Meat
1. If meat has been dried to the point that they are hard like wood, one may store milk in them, but one shouldn't do this לכתחילה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^51]
2. However, if there is 60x the amount of milk as there was meat, then this is okay. Even though we usually don't allow one to be מבטל something on purpose, we are מקל here.[^52]
3. Dried meat which is still considered food (like beef jerky) is still totally considered meat.[^53]
# Coagulating Substance
## From a Kosher Animal
1. A דבר המעמיד from a kosher animal is מבוטל if the milk/cheese is 60x the amount of the דבר המעמיד (or if you can't taste it according to the ספרדים).[^54]
## From a Non-Kosher Animal
1. (This means an actual non-kosher animal, like a pig, or a kosher animal that hasn't been shechted properly.)
2. מהרש"ל: There is no difference between a דבר המעמיד from a non-kosher animal or a kosher animal.[^55]
3. מחבר, רמ"א, ש"ך: A דבר המעמיד from a non-kosher animal, is never מבוטל.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^56]
   This is because the דבר המעמיד from the kosher animal is kosher on its own and is only אסור when mixed with the milk/cheese. The דבר המעמיד from the non-kosher animal is actually אסור on its own.[^55]
## From both Kosher and Non-Kosher (זה וזה גורם)
### The Non-Kosher דבר המעמיד Can't Do it on Its Own
1. The non-kosher דבר המעמיד can be מבוטל, like we say by a דבר המעמיד [[#From a Kosher Animal|from a kosher animal]].[^57]
### The Non-Kosher דבר המעמיד Can Do it on Its Own
1. ט"ז, ש"ך, באר הגולה: This is not a case of זה וזה גורם. We view this the same as a regular case of a דבר המעמיד [[#From a Non-Kosher Animal|from a non-kosher animal]]. (The ש"ך, is נוטה like the ט"ז but ends off with צריך עיון.)[^58]
2. רמ"א: The non-kosher דבר המעמיד can be מבוטל, like we say by a דבר המעמיד [[#From a Kosher Animal|from a kosher animal]].[^59]
