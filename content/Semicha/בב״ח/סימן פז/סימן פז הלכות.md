# &lrm;איסורי דאורייתא of בב״ח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cooking dairy and meat together.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Getting הנאה from meat and dairy which has been cooked together.[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Eating dairy and meat which has been cooked together.[^1]</span>
## Definition of "Cooked Together"
### Count as "Cooked Together"
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Literal cooking or roasting. </span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Frying. However, because there are some שיטות that say that it is not, one can be מקל in a case of הפסד גדול.&lrm;[^2]</span>
### Do not Count as "Cooked Together"
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Pickling (כבוש) milk and meat together.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Salting (מליחה) milk and meat together.[^3]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Placing dairy/meat on top of its opposite when they are both hot (but not on the flame).[^2]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Smoking meat and milk together.[^4]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cooking meat and milk together in hot springs.[^4]</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cooking meat and milk together in the sun. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">{need source}</span></span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אסור to eat midiraban.[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">I things have been combined together in any of these ways, they may no longer be cooked, even on their own, because they now contain בליעות of their meat/dairy opposite. (For example, if you salt meat and cheese together, the meat contains בליעות of cheese and the cheese contains בליעות of meat. Neither of them may be cooked, even on their own, because the person would be cooking meat and dairy together).[^2]</span> ^16a5de
## Minimum Requirements to Be עובר on…
### &lrm;איסור of Eating
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is no minimum amount. A person is עובר for eating or cooking any amount at all.[^6]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person is עובר for eating in any fashion, even in ways which are not אסור regarding other איסורים, like swallowing without chewing. (Other איסורים, like eating treif, are מותר if swallowed.)[^7] </span>
### &lrm;איסורים of Getting הנאה or Cooking
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמב"ם: There is no minimum amount. A person is always עובר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Various other ראשונים and Achronim: Some say the mixture needs to be a kizayis. Others say that the person needs to get a perutah's worth of הנאה.<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">(It is not clear to me if these opinions argue with each other or agree.)</span></span>

# What Meat/Milk Count for בשר בחלב
## Not Milk/Meat at All
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Almond milk.[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Milk from a human (whether from a female[^9] or male[^10]).</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Fish is parve.[^11] However, there is still an issue of סכנה to eat this with meat and potentially milk. For further discussion, see [[Fish with Milk and Meat|here]].</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to most, meat/milk from non-kosher animals (meaning, animals that are non-kosher, like a pig, not animals that are kosher but shechted incorrectly).[^11][^12][^13] (Obviously, one can't eat/drink the meat/milk anyway because these are non-kosher.) For further discussion, see [[Milk or Meat from Non-Kosher Animals with Its Kosher Opposite|here]].</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מעיקר הדין, these are parve. However, there may be a problem of [[#&lrm;מראית עין]].&lrm;[^9]</span>
## Meat/Milk דרבנן, but not אסור to Cook
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Bird meat (such as chicken and turkey).[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Meat/milk of undomesticated kosher animals (like deer) according to most פוסקים.&lrm;[^11][^14][^15] For further discussion, see [[Bird and Wild Animal Meat and Milk|here]].</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אסור to eat together with their meat/dairy opposite. They are מותר to cook with their meat/dairy opposite.</span>
## Meat/Milk דרבנן, but אסור to Cook
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some byproducts from cheesemaking. See [[Cheesemaking Byproducts|here]] for a discussion.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Milk in the udder of a dead animal.[^16]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Milk from a male animal.[^17]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Blood.[^4] (This is only referring to the איסור of בב״ח, not the סתם איסור of eating blood.)</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Placenta.[^18]</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hide.[^18] However, for hide is the same as flesh underneath, some say that this is אסור דאורייתא.&lrm;[^19]</span>
7. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Sinews.[^18]</span>
8. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Bones (without marrow. Marrow is בשר דאורייתא.)&lrm;[^20]</span>
9. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Horns.[^18]</span>
10. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hooves.[^18]</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אסור to cook with milk/meat מדרבנן (at least לכתחילה),&lrm;[^21][^18] and certainly to eat.[^22][^18] (רע"א doesn't understand why this is different than chicken and milk for which we say that this is אסור to eat together but not אסור to cook together.[^23])</span>
## Meat/Milk דאורייתא
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Meat and milk from domesticated kosher animals.[^24] For a discussion about niveilah and חֵלֶב from a kosher animal with milk, see [[Niveilah and Cheilev with Milk|here]]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Fetus.[^18]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Marrow.[^25]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some byproducts from cheesemaking. See [[Cheesemaking Byproducts|here]] for a discussion.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is considered full-fledged meat/milk. It is אסור מדאורייתא to cook or eat with its meat/milk opposite.[^18][^26]</span>

### Milk Drunk by a Now-dead Animal

#### Milk is Fluid (צלול)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Milk has been drunk by an animal that has since died. The milk is still very fluid (it hasn't undergone much digestion, if at all).</span>
##### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: This is a מחלוקת. It seems that he holds that it would be מותר לכתחילה.&lrm;[^27] (Presumably, there is a מראית עין problem, in which case this would be אסור לכתחילה but מותר בדיעבד,&lrm;[^28] but this is not explicitly discussed regarding the מחבר.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: This is a מחלוקת and it is not fine, even בדיעבד.&lrm;[^29]</span>
#### Milk is Congealed (קרוש)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The milk has undergone some degree of digestion and is now congealed.</span>
##### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, רמ"א (as explained by the ש"ך):&lrm;[^30] מעיקר הדין, this is fine, לכתחילה. However, the רמ"א says that there is a מראית עין problem. בדיעבד, this is fine (as is true in general by מראית עין issues). (Presumably, the מחבר agrees.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל, ר"ן, או"ה (all presented by the ש"ך):&lrm;[^31] There is a problem מעיקר הדין, even בדיעבד.</span>
#### Milk Absorbed בליעות from Stomach, then Made into Cheese
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person salts the animal while there is some of this milk still in the animals stomach or he leaves the milk in the animal's stomach for 24 hours. He then mixes this milk with other milk used to make cheese. The potential issue is that this milk has acquired בליעות of meat from the stomach of the animal through מליחה or כבישה.</span>
##### Side Notes
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לכתחילה a person should not leave the milk in the animal's stomach the length of time that it would take for it to cool down. This is because the milk in the stomach is full of acid and so the כבישה process occurs much faster.[^32]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While we are usually מחמיר to say that transfer occurred instantly when the meat has been salted, and even if it has been salted relatively lightly, if the milk has already been mixed with other milks we are more מקל because there we can rely on the שיטה of the מחבר, (explained shortly) who is מקל the moment the milk is drunk. Additionally, there is only a concern for an איסור דרבנן is this case[^33] (assuming that the milk was not cooked after, in which case it would be אסור דאורייתא, as discussed [[#^16a5de|above]][^34]). This is the שיטה of the רמ"א, as discussed <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">סימן צא סעיף ה (need to add link)</span>.</span>
##### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר (as explained by the ש"ך and ט"ז):&lrm;[^35] One should not do this לכתחילה, but בדיעבד, this is okay. There is no difference about whether the milk is fluid or not.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: Depends if the milk is fluid or not. If it is fluid, this is אסור, even בדיעבד (unless the amount of regular milk is 60x the amount of this stomach milk. For a discussion about a potential issue with bitul בששים and the response, see [[Bittul בששים by Milk Left in an Animal's Stomach|here]]).[^32]</span>
   If it has congealed, it is fine, לכתחילה.&lrm;[^36]
For the reasoning behind these שיטות, see [[Using Milk in an Animal's Stomach to Make Cheese|here]].

# &lrm;מראית עין
## Person Wants to Eat the Food
### &lrm;לכתחילה
#### &lrm;איסורי דאורייתא
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person wants to create and eat a food which appears like there is an איסור דאורייתא on eating the food.</span>
##### &lrm;שיטות
All opinions:[^37] This is אסור.
##### Examples
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Catching fish blood in a cup to drink. (Fish blood is totally מותר, but it looks like animal blood which is אסור דאורייתא.)&lrm;[^38]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cooking meat with alternative milks to eat (like almond milk or Chalav אישה).&lrm;[^9][^8]</span>
#### &lrm;איסורי דרבנן
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person wants to create and eat a food which appears like there is an איסור דרבנן on eating the food.</span>
##### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (פשוט פשט):&lrm;[^8] There is no issue of מראית עין by אסורי דרבנן. As such, this is perfectly fine.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א ([[מראית עין by ISSUREI DIRABANAN#^8bcaeb|לפי פתחי תשובה]]):&lrm;[^39] There is a problem if this is done in public, but not in private.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל,&lrm;[^38] ש"ך:&lrm;[^40] There is an issue of מראית עין by איסורי דרבנן. As such, one must make it clear in some what that they are not violating an איסור.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז:&lrm;[^41] One should make it clear in some what that they are not violating an איסור. However, if one has no way to do this, it is fine for them to create and eat the food.</span>
##### Examples
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cooking chicken with alternative milks to eat (like almond milk). A way to indicate that one is not violating as איסור is by leaving almonds next to the mixture.[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Mixing meat with alternative milks (without cooking them together) to eat.[^40]</span>
### &lrm;בדיעבד (דאורייתא and דרבנן)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person has a food that looks like it is אסור to eat, whether דאורייתא or דרבנן, and they have no way to indicate that there is no איסור occurring here.</span>
#### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר,&lrm;[^9] ט"ז, רמ"א (לפי ט"ז):&lrm;[^41] One may eat the food.</span>
#### Examples
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person accidentally mixed Chalav אישה into his pot of meat while it is cooking.[^9]</span>
## Person Wants to Get הנאה or just Cook the Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person wants to cook or get הנאה from foods which appears like there is an איסור דאורייתא on doing this. (Whether there is an איסור דרבנן of בשר בחלב to eat together, or not.)</span>
### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Various מפרשים (Brought down by the [[מראית עין by ISSUREI DIRABANAN#Answer 1|ש"ך's first answer]]):[^42] There is a problem of מראית עין.</span>

4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז, רמ"א (According to the ט"ז, and, seemingly, the PASHUT reading):[^43] There is a problem with any milk (including alternative milks and milk from non-kosher animals) and meat (including meat from non-kosher animals).</span>

5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לבוש:&lrm;[^44] There is no problem of cooking chicken with milk.</span> ^519575

# Coagulating Substance
## From a Kosher Animal
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A דבר המעמיד from a kosher animal is מבוטל if the milk/cheese is 60x the amount of the דבר המעמיד (or if you can't taste it according to the ספרדים).&lrm;[^54]</span>
## From a Non-Kosher Animal
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(This means an actual non-kosher animal, like a pig, or a kosher animal that hasn't been shechted properly.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: There is no difference between a דבר המעמיד from a non-kosher animal or a kosher animal.[^55]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, רמ"א, ש"ך: A דבר המעמיד from a non-kosher animal, is never מבוטל.&lrm;[^56]</span>
   This is because the דבר המעמיד from the kosher animal is kosher on its own and is only אסור when mixed with the milk/cheese. The דבר המעמיד from the non-kosher animal is actually אסור on its own.[^55]
## From both Kosher and Non-Kosher (זה וזה גורם)
### The Non-Kosher דבר המעמיד Can't Do it on Its Own
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The non-kosher דבר המעמיד can be מבוטל, like we say by a דבר המעמיד [[#From a Kosher Animal|from a kosher animal]].[^57]</span>
### The Non-Kosher דבר המעמיד Can Do it on Its Own
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז, ש"ך, באר הגולה: This is not a case of זה וזה גורם. We view this the same as a regular case of a דבר המעמיד [[#From a Non-Kosher Animal|from a non-kosher animal]]. (The ש"ך, is נוטה like the ט"ז but ends off with צריך עיון.)&lrm;[^58]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: The non-kosher דבר המעמיד can be מבוטל, like we say by a דבר המעמיד [[#From a Kosher Animal|from a kosher animal]].[^59]</span>
