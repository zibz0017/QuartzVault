# Basic Cheesemaking Process
1. A person puts an acidic agent (like vinegar) into milk.
2. Curds start to form. The liquid becomes clearer and watery. This is called whey.
3. The milk is heated. This causes the curds to float to the top. The liquid becomes clearer.
4. The curds are then cut to release more whey that got trapped inside the curds.
5. The curds can either be gathered together at this point to form softer cheeses (like cottage or cream cheese) or cooked to get rid of liquid and form harder cheeses (like parmesan).
