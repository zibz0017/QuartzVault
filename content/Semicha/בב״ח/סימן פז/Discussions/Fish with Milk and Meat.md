1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לבוש (AT"Z), מחבר (according to the לבוש), Teshuvas חינוך, פרי מגדים, רבינו Bichai: אסור to eat fish with milk.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך, ט"ז, מחבר (according to the ש"ך and ט"ז), רמ"א, Other ACHRONIM: MUTTAR to eat fish with milk.</span>

# The Source of the Issue
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר in the בית יוסף in סימן 87 says that it is אסור to eat fish with milk and he says to see what he wrote in ORACH חיים סימן 173 סעיף 2. The problem is that over there, the מחבר says that one can't eat fish with meat.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">PESACHIM 76b sounds like it is saying that there is no issue with eating fish with milk.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Many people eat fish with milk and there doesn't appear to be a problem.</span>

1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There was a TAOS סופר. The בית יוסף in סימן 87 is supposed to say that it is אסור to eat fish with meat, not fish with milk.</span>
## The "אסור with Milk" Opinion
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בית יוסף simply means to reference the idea about how much we care about סכנה.
