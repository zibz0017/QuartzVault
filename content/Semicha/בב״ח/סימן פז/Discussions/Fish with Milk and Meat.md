# שיטות
1. לבוש (AT"Z), מחבר (according to the לבוש), Teshuvas חינוך, פרי מגדים, רבינו Bichai: אסור to eat fish with milk.
2. ש"ך, ט"ז, מחבר (according to the ש"ך and ט"ז), רמ"א, Other ACHRONIM: MUTTAR to eat fish with milk.
3. All opinions: אסור to eat fish with meat.
# The Source of the Issue
1. The מחבר in the בית יוסף in סימן 87 says that it is אסור to eat fish with milk and he says to see what he wrote in ORACH חיים סימן 173 סעיף 2. The problem is that over there, the מחבר says that one can't eat fish with meat.
2. PESACHIM 76b sounds like it is saying that there is no issue with eating fish with milk.
3. Many people eat fish with milk and there doesn't appear to be a problem.
# Responses
## The "MUTTAR with Milk" Opinion
1. There was a TAOS סופר. The בית יוסף in סימן 87 is supposed to say that it is אסור to eat fish with meat, not fish with milk.
## The "אסור with Milk" Opinion
1. The בית יוסף simply means to reference the idea about how much we care about סכנה. He is not referencing the הלכה he wrote there. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">{Opinion brought down by footnote in the טור. See there for the actual source I don't remember what it was.}</span>
