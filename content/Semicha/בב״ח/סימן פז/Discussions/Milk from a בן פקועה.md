# Basic ספק
1. The תורה says "לא sivashel gidi bachaleiv imo". A shechted animal can't be a mother and so is excluded from this pasuk. This is the reason that chalav שחוטה is not אסור midioraysa. (It is still אסור midirabanan.)
2. A בן פקועה is in an interesting middle-ground. It is considered to have already been shechted, in a sense, so the milk can be considered מותר דאורייתא. However, at the same time, a בן פקועה can be a mother. As such, it would seem that perhaps this would be an exception to the idea that the milk of a shechted animal doesn't count as chalav דאורייתא.
# Potential Proof
1. The 'גמ (bechoros ו) says that we know that milk is מותר because the תורה specifically says that בב״ח is אסור. That implies that milk by itself is fine.
2. How is this a good proof to all milk? If milk from a בן פקועה is אסור with meat, then perhaps the implication is that בן פקועה milk with meat is אסור but בן פקועה milk by itself is fine, and there is no implication to milk from a regular cow?
3. From the fact that the 'גמ says that the fact that the איסור of milk with meat implies that all milk is מותר, this must mean that milk from a בן פקועה is not אסור with meat.
