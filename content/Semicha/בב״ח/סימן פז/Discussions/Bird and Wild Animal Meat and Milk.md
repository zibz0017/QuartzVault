# Indications that This is Really דרבנן
## Many פוסקים
1. There is a vast amount of פוסקים who say that this is דרבנן.
2. In particular, the רמב"ם says that one who holds that bird meat is בשר דאורייתא is עובר on BAAL TOSEF.
## The SHIUREI DURO (the מהרא"י) Holds Its דרבנן
1. The מהרא"י (who the BACH and MAHARSHAL base their opinion on) only treats bird meat like בשר דאורייתא in regards to eating milchig right after bird meat.
2. Elsewhere the מהרא"י explicitly says one may eat eggs with milk because *bird meat is only אסור MIDIRABANAN*.
3. As such, it sounds like he only treats meat like בשר דאורייתא in that case he is talking about, namely, eating milk after eating bird meat. However, it seems that in general he holds that it is considered דרבנן.
### Potential Issue and Response
1. 'תוס makes it clear that the הלכה of not having milk after bird meat is predicated on the discussion about whether bird meat is considered meat דאורייתא. As such, the SHIUREI DURO (who PASKENS that one can't do that) would seem to hold that it is דאורייתא.
2. However, this is not compelling. One may hold that bird meat is only considered בשר דרבנן and still say that one should not eat milchig after eating bird meat.
