*Presented in פתחי Teshuvah ס"ק 6*
# Basic Background
1. There is rule that אין איסור CHAL על איסור.
2. However, if an איסור is considered a "davar mosif", then we do say that both אסורים do apply.
3. The 'גמ discusses what happens when you cook/eat/get הנאה from niveilah or חֵלֶב (foods that carry their own איסור) with milk.
# הלכה
## Eating
1. There is no איסור of בב״ח because there is already an איסור to eat this, so we say אין איסור Chal על איסור. (Obviously, you still can't eat it because there is still an איסור of Niveilah/חֵלֶב).
## Cooking
1. There is an איסור of בב״ח. There is no איסור of cooking niveilah/חֵלֶב, so אין איסור Chal על איסור does not apply.
## הנאה
### ראשונים
1. רמב"ם: If an איסור אכילה does not apply to a food, that איסור can't אסור the food for הנאה. In this case, because the food is not אסור to eat (in terms of בב״ח), it is not אסור to get הנאה because of בב״ח, either.
2. 'תוס: The איסור אכילה and איסור הנאה are not connected. Just because there is no איסור on the eating does not mean that there is no איסור on getting הנאה.
### אחרונים
1. דגול מרבבה: One does not lose out if they are מקל in a case of הפסד.
2. פרי מגדים: One may not receive הנאה in all cases.
# Potential Applications
1. Sometimes pet food can come with niveilah and milk. It would appear that this is אסור because one may not get הנאה from נבלה and milk (unless this is a case of הפסד מרובה, which it presumably is not).
2. Can one work in a non-kosher restaurant if they will have to cook cheeseburgers or something like that?
3. Can one do Uber eats if they will have to deliver cheeseburgers or foods like that? Is that considered getting הנאה from these foods?
