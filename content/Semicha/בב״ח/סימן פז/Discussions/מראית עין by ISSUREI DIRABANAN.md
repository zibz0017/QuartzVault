## Additional Concern Regarding Bird Meat and Milk
1. There is a שיטה in the 'גמ that it is מותר to eat bird meat with milk. As such, there is additional concern regarding eating bird meat and milk, as people seeing this may incorrectly learn that that the הלכה is like this opinion.
# Regarding Cooking and Getting הנאה
*Presented in the ט"ז סיף קטן 5 and the ש"ך סיף קטן 7*
## The סתירה
1. In סעיף 4, the מחבר says that one may not cook Chalav אישה with meat because of מראית עין.
2. The רמ"א in סעיף 4 makes KAL VACHOMER from this and says that there should also be a מראית עין problem when cooking or getting הנאה from non-kosher meat/milk with its kosher opposite.
3. However, the מחבר in סעיף 3 says that one may get הנאה and cook non-kosher meat/milk with its kosher opposite.

### Answer 3
1. The ש"ך gives another answer. He says that there is no problem of מראית עין when it comes to non-kosher meat/milk with its kosher opposite because these foods are clearly recognizable for what they are. As such, nobody will think that one is being עובר on cooking or getting הנאה from kosher meat and milk.
2. However, almond milk and Chalav אישה is not recognizable for what they are. As such, there is a problem of מראית עין because if someone sees someone cooking or eating these foods together, they will think that they are being עובר on בשר בחלב with regular meat and milk.
### Answer 4
1. The ט"ז says that חז"ל were only גוזר the איסור of מראית עין by בב״ח for things which are מותר to eat on there own. This is because this is more similar to the actual איסור דאורייתא of בב״ח, which is only on kosher milk and meat. חז"ל were not גוזר for cases that one of the foods was not מותר to eat.
2. As such, there is an איסור of מראית עין when it comes to almond milk/Chalav אישה with meat (because these foods are all מותר to eat). There is no איסור of מראית עין when it comes to non-kosher meat/milk with its kosher opposite because non-kosher meat/milk can't be eaten, anyway.
#### הלכה למעשה
1. The ט"ז does not feel comfortable being מקל against the רמ"א to permit cooking non-kosher meat/milk with its kosher opposite.
2. However, the ט"ז does say that one should not cook chicken and milk together, as these foods are both מותר. It is משמע from the דרכי משה that he agrees with this, as well. This is against the לבוש who says that there is no problem of מראית עין when it comes to cooking chicken because chicken is easily recognizably different than meat. The ט"ז says this because:
	1. There are times when the chicken is ground up and not recognizably different than meat.
	2. The person seeing this may not realize that that there is a difference in הלכה between chicken and milk (especially considering the fact that there is a מחלוקת TANAIM about whether there is a difference between chicken and meat).
	3. There is a TANA that says that there is no problem of eating chicken with milk. As such, we have to be extra cautious when it comes to making it seem that chicken and milk is okay.
3. When it comes to רפואה, one may cook any foods that have a מראית עין problem together, even if there is no איסור to eat these foods individually (which is the case that the ט"ז is most confident that there is a מראית עין problem).
