# Stoking the Flames under a גוי's Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A גוי's pot is on a flame. It currently does not contain meat or dairy. The Jew wants to stoke the flame for whatever reason (perhaps to warm up his home or something like that).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pot may contain בליעות of meat and milk. By stoking the flames, one may potentially be cooking meat and milk, which is אסור.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכה in all of these cases is the same. See [[#&lrm;הלכה in All Cases|below]].</span>
### Explanations

	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the גוי did cook the milk/meat opposite, did he cook it in the previous 24 hours?</span>

# Dishwater from Meat and Dairy Dishes
## &lrm;הג"ה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הג"ה proposes that one perhaps should not mix dishwater from meat and dairy dishes together and feed it to animals.</span>
## Problem
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As mentioned in סעיף 1, there is no problem with getting hanah from meat and milk that has not been cooked together as there is only an איסור דרבנן on such a food. As such, why does the רמ"א even suggest that there is potentially a problem?</span>
## Solutions
### &lrm;ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The case that the רמ"א is referring to is where the dishwater is hot.</span>
#### Problem
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">RA"E asks if this is the case, why does the רמ"א discuss the fact that this water was fed to animals? Even if the water is not fed to animals there is still a problem of cooking the meat and milk together.</span>
### &lrm;מנחת יעקב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This case is where the food is not hot, but despite this, the רמ"א considers whether one should avoid purposely mixing milk and meat together to get הנאה out of the resulting mixture.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכה in all of these cases is the same. See [[#&lrm;הלכה in All Cases|below]].</span>
### Explanations
#### For the ש"ך's Explanation
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בדי השלחן blibes shver. He mentions that there is an ערוך השלחן that discusses this, but he doesn't understand what the ערוך השלחן is saying.</span>
#### For the מנחת יעקב's PSHAT
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is simple to understand why at worst there is just a חומרה at worst according to the מנחת יעקב. As mentioned in סעיף 1, there is no איסור when it comes to getting הנאה from milk and meat that has not been cooked together.</span>
# Using the Container for Heating Water to Wash One's Head for Some other Purpose
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There was some container that was used to heat water to wash one's head. Someone wants to use this container to heat up water for some other purpose.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, this container contains ash from the top of the oven. This ash may contain bits of meat and milk that have fallen there. By heating up this container, one is cooking these pieces together.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For a discussion about why there is no problem with using the pot to heat water to wash one's head (presumably, heating the water to wash one's head is a form of הנאה, and so this should seemingly be אסור as well), see [[The Issur of Hannah From Meat and Milk Cooked Together|here]].</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכה in all of these cases is the same. See [[#&lrm;הלכה in All Cases|below]].</span>
### Explanations
#### &lrm;ערוך השלחן : First Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The little bits of בשר and חלב in this case have been burnt to the point that we consider them to be non-existent.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(This is not so simple to say because some say that ashes of meat/milk still cause problems. We have to say that this case is referring to where it has been burnt even beyond this point.)</span>
#### &lrm;ערוך השלחן : Second Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">These burnt pieces here are נותן טעם לפגם.</span>
# Using a Pot in an Oven of a Winter House
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There were pots that were put in oven and used in winter homes. A person wants to heat up this pot for some other purpose.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, sometimes small milk and meat pieces fall on these pots. By heating up this pot, the person may be cooking meat and milk.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכה in all of these cases is the same. See [[#&lrm;הלכה in All Cases|below]].</span>
### Explanation
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The דרכי משה says that there is no concern for this because there is no problem when it comes to meat and milk that we cannot see.</span>
# &lrm;הלכה in All Cases
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בדיעבד, this is certainly all fine and even לכתחילה, this is just a חומרה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the pots are metal, then there is no issue at all. This is because the בב״ח is cooked out of the pots the same way they were cooked in. As such, by the time that one is using the pots, the בב״ח has been cooked out and there is not issue.</span>
