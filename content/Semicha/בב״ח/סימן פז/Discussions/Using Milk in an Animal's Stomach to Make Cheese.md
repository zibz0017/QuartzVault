# מחבר
1. Even when the milk has started to become קרוש, it still absorbs the taste of meat from the walls and transfers that to the rest of the milk that it is mixed into.
2. As such, even if the milk is קרוש, one is not allowed to make cheese out of it.
# רמ"א
1. Once the milk start to digest (become קרוש), there is no concern to say that the meat taste from the stomach got into the milk and then goes into the rest of the milk because the milk in the stomach has already digested to the point that it is considered "pirsha בעלמא" and so it is difficult for the taste to get into the milk.
