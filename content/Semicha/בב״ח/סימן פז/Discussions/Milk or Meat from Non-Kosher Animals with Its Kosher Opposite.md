# איסור דרבנן of בשר בחלב in This Case
*Presented in the ט"ז ס"ק ב, ש"ך ס"ק ג*
## The שיטות
1. ב"ח: There is an איסור דרבנן of בשר בחלב.
2. שלחן ערוך, ט"ז, ש"ך: There is no איסור of בשר בחלב. (You still can't eat it because there is non-kosher animal there.)
### The לשון of the טור
1. The מחלוקת is based on the לשון of the טור. The טור discusses four cases:
	1. Milk from a kosher animal mixed with meat from a non-kosher animal.
	2. Meat from a kosher animal mixed with milk from a non-kosher animal.
	3. Milk mixed with meat from a bird.
	4. Milk mixed with meat from an undomesticated animal.
2. He says that the הלכה is that it is אסור to eat מדרבנן (meaning, there is an איסור of בשר בחלב), but מותר to get הנאה and cook together.
### Interpretations of the טור
#### The ב"ח
1. The ב"ח reads the טור to mean that all of the cases he discussed, including the cases of milk/meat from a kosher animal mixed with its kosher opposite from a non-kosher animal, have an איסור דרבנן of בשר בחלב.
2. The ב"ח reads it this way because the simple reading of the טור is that he is referring to all of the cases he discussed.
#### The SHULCHAN ערוך
1. The SHULCHAN ערוך reads the line of the איסור דרבנן as only referring to the cases of milk mixed with bird or undomesticated animal meat.
##### The SHULCHAN ערוך's Reasoning
1. The SHULCHAN ערוך says that the reason he reads the טור this way is because חז"ל wouldn't have ASSURED meat/milk of a kosher animal with its kosher opposite with a non-kosher animal because either way you can't eat it. It's not kosher.
##### The ב"ח's Response
1. The ב"ח responds that the there are some potential cases where there are NAFKA MINOS:
	1. If you hold we only say חתיכה NAASEH NIVEILAH (חנ"ן) {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to add link</span>} by בשר בחלב (like the Shulchan ערוך himself says in סימן 92 סעיף 4), there are 2 NAFKA MINOS that come out putting an איסור of בשר בחלב on this case if it falls into a kosher food:
		1. You need 60x the whole mixture (the kosher part and the non-kosher part). If we did not add an איסור of בשר בחלב, we only need 60x the non-kosher part.
		2. If either the kosher or non-kosher part is a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד, it is can't be מבוטל. If we did not add an איסור of בשר בחלב then there is only a problem if the non-kosher part is a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.
	2. Even according to those who say that there is חנ"ן by all אסורים (not just בשר בחלב like the רמ"א says in סימן 92 סעיף 4) there is still a NAFKA MINAH in a case where the kosher and non-kosher foods are not attached and the kosher food is not totally submerged in the non-kosher food. If we treat this as בשר בחלב, then we view the entire mixture as אסור. This makes the same NAFKA MINOS as said above if this falls into a kosher mixture (you need 60x the whole mixture, and if the kosher food is a DAVAR הראויה להתכבד, it can't be מבוטל).
	   If there is no added איסור of בשר בחלב, then we don't view it as a piece of איסור. As such, we only need 60x the non-kosher food, and it can be מבוטל, even if the kosher food is חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.
##### Attacks on the ב"ח
1. The ש"ך attacks the ב"ח and says that חז"ל don't have a reason to create an איסור. The reason חז"ל would create an איסור would be to dissuade people from eating this mixture because we don't want people to see someone eating this and think that it is מותר to eat. However, that is not relevant in this case because nobody is going to eat it because it can't eat it anyway. (The ש"ך adds that this is actually what the SHULCHAN ערוך was asking.)
2. The ט"ז attacks the ב"ח and says that in this case, those who say that we only say חנ"ן by בב״ח would not say חנ"ן in this case, even if there is an added איסור of בב״ח. The reason why there is חנ"ן by בב״ח in general according to this opinion is because we have two מותר things (kosher milk and kosher meat) and we end up with something אסור. The only way to explain this is if we say that these two מותר things transform into a new entity. However, in this case, one of the things is not מותר anyway (it is not kosher). As such, it is understandable why the mixture is אסור, even without invoking חנ"ן.
