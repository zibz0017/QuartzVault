# Presentation of מראה מקומות
## גליון מהרש"א
1. There seems to be a סתירה in the גליון מהרש"א. In סעיף א he brings in a שיטה that says that if the בב״ח was previously cooked together, it would appear that one may cook them together again (although he ends off with צריך עיון) but in סעיף ו he says that it is אסור to cook together, even if they have been previously been cooked together.
## פתחי תשובה
1. The פתחי תשובה talks about a case where someone lights a candle made from butter cooked in a בן יומו meat pot and seems to agree to the שיטה that says that there should be a problem of בשול in this case.
   It seems that this case is dealing with previously cooked בב״ח and there is problem of cooking it again.
# הלכה למעשה
1. It seems that one should be מחמיר, even if the foods have previously been cooked together.
