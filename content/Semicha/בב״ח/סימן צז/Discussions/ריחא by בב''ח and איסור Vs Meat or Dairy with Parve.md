# &lrm;הלכות
## &lrm;בב״ח Or איסור and התר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one puts meat and dairy or איסור and התר in the oven at the same time, the הלכה is that בדיעבד, the food may be eaten.</span>
## Meat or Dairy with Something Parve
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one puts meat/dairy in the oven at the same time as something parve, the הלכה is that the food may not be eaten with the opposite of the meat/dairy it was put with, even בדיעבד.</span>
# Reason for the Difference
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When it comes to בב״ח and איסור with התר, we are immediately in a בדיעבד situation. If we would be מקפיד on the ריחא, the food would not be able to be eaten at all.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When it comes to something meat/dairy with something parve, if we would be מקפיד on the ריחא, the food may still be eaten with something. It may still be eaten with the same type of food that it was cooked in the oven with (if it was cooked in the oven with meat it can be eaten with other meat and the same for dairy), or with something parve. As such, even once you cook it, we are still in a לכתחילה situation and so we are not מקל.</span>
