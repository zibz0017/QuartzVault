# Breads that the גזרה Applies to
1. The גזרה only applies to regular bread that a person where there is a concern that the person will eat it with dairy/meat.
2. The גזרה does not apply to things like sweet bread, for which there is no concern that one will eat with dairy/meat.

I don't know if that would apply to random pastries.)
# When One is Allowed to Eat Dairy/Meat Bread
1. For breads that the גזרה applies to, one may make and eat dairy/meat bread when:
	1. If the bread is somewhat small (exact amount is not said but it seems to be not more than a meal or two),
	2. or if the bread is made in a unique way.
# Exceptions to the Exceptions
1. These previous rules are only true when:
	1. This was done before it was baked. It does not help to cut bread into a unique shape after you bake it, for example.
	2. And this is only true for private use, not if you are selling the bread (or perhaps giving it out, but that is not particularly discussed).
