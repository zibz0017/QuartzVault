# Background
## Basic Concerns
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The dairy/meat may leak and flow under the bread. This would make the bread אסור because one can't have dairy/meat bread.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if it doesn't flow under the meat/dairy, בליעות may travel through the oven floor into the bread.</span>
## &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">הג"ה, ש"ך: If there is a ספק, one must be מחמיר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ב"ח: There must be a definite issue. Otherwise, the bread is fine.</span>
# Applications
## &lrm;ש"ך/הג"ה
### Ovens in the Times of the אחרונים
#### Flows under the Bread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the oven is slanted where the dairy/meat is on top and the bread is on bottom, we are in a מקום ספק. If the dairy/meat leaked, it would flow down to the bread and make it dairy/meat, which is אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, one can't eat bread that was in a such a situation. (It seems that you would not be able to tell if there was a flow after the fact.)</span>
#### Transfer of בליעות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the the floor of the oven is flat  to it was slanted and the bread was on top, there is potential concern that maybe the dairy/meat leaked and spread בליעות to the floor of the oven and those בליעות may spread through the oven floor to the bread.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is a ספק if בליעות transfer though a כלי (see סימן צב סעיף ה <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">need to add link</span>). In addition, there is a second ספק if the dairy/meat leaked out.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, if one does not see a leak, there is a ספק ספקה (maybe it leaked and maybe it didn't and even if it did, perhaps בליעות don't transfer accross the oven floor).</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if you see that there was a leak, then there is only one ספק. As such, in such a case, one would not be allowed to eat the bread.</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For a discussion for why we don't say that this is נ"ט בר נ"ט and so the bread will be מותר to eat with dairy/meat, at least בדיעבד, see [[נ''ט בר נ''ט During בשול|here]].</span>
### Modern Cooking
#### Differences
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In modern times, we don't put food directly on the oven. We put it on trays.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Food is cooked on racks, not on the oven floor.</span>
#### Applications
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is unlikely for food to flow from one food to another across the racks, unless the foods are right next to each other.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is cooking one above the other, then there is concern that the top food may drip into the bottom food.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Additionally, there is no concern that בליעות can travel from one food to another, even if there is a leak, because it can only travel across the oven or the racks. It can't travel from the oven or rack to the tray of the other food because that is תרי קדירות.</span>
## &lrm;ב"ח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">None of these are concerns. The only problem is where you actually see a flow under the bread.</span>
