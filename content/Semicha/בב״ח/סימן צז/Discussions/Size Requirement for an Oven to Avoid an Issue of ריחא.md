1. If an oven is "large" there is no issue of ריחא. A large oven is 12 עשרונים. There is a discussion about what this means:
	1. יד אליהו: This means the פשוט פשט what it sounds like; the whole cavity of the oven needs to be 12 עשרונים.
	2. פרי מגדים: This means that the bottom of the oven needs to be 12 עשרונים. The bottom of the oven means the bottom tefach. In other words, the oven needs to be big enough that there is a volume of 12 עשרונים within the bottom tefach of the oven.
