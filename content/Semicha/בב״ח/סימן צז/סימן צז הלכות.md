# Dairy/Meat Breads
## Made with Dairy/Meat
1. One may not make bread with dairy/meat.[^1]
### Exceptions
1. See [[When One may Eat Dairy or Meat Bread|here]].
## Made in an Oven with Covered Dairy/Meat
1. There is concern that the meat/dairy may leak out and either the leak or בליעות will travel to the bread, which would make the bread אסור. For details, see [[Bread Made in an Oven with Covered Dairy or Meat|here]].
# Dairy/Meat Bread Mixed with other Breads
1. If a bread that was made with dairy/meat was mixed with other breads, all of the breads become אסור, even if the אסור bread was only made by mistake.[^2]
# נ"ט בר נ"ט During בשול
1. There is a discussion about whether we say נ"ט בר נ"ט on food during בשול. For a discussion, see [[נ''ט בר נ''ט During בשול|here]].
# ריחה (Aroma)
## Basic הלכות
1. לכתחילה, one can't put open dairy and meat or open התר food and אסור food in the oven at the same time. בדיעבד, the foods are fine (except for the אסור food, obviously).[^3]
2. If one puts meat/dairy in the oven at the same time as parve food, one may not eat the parve food with the opposite (if the parve food was in the oven at the same time as the meat it may not be eaten with dairy and vice-versa).
3. For the reason behind these הלכות, see [[ריחא by בב''ח and איסור Vs Meat or Dairy with Parve|here]].
## Exceptions
1. If the food is covered,[^4]
2. or if the oven is large, there is no issue of ריחא. For a discussion of large is large, see [[Size Requirement for an Oven to Avoid an Issue of ריחא|here]].
