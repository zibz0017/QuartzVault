# Waiting after Eating Meat before Eating Dairy
## שיטות
1. [^1]מחבר,[^2] רמ"א:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> Six hours. The רמ"א says that this is the preferred מנהג.
2. רמ"א (first יש אומרים):[^1] Immediately, after bentching, kinuach and הדחה.
3. רמ"א (second יש אומרים):[^1] Bentch, one hour. (The order does not matter). For more details about this opinion, see [[Details about Opinion to Wait One Hour|here]].
	1. ש"ך:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3] According to this opinion, one doesn't need to do kinauch and hadacha (similar to what we say according to the six hour שיטה).
	2. ט"ז:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^4] According to this opinion, one always needs kinuach and hadacha.
For further discussion of this מחלוקת (and some other details), see [[Background on Machlokes on How Long to Wait After Meat Before Dairy#'גמ|here]].
## Reasons to Wait
1. See [[Reasons to Wait Between Meat and Milk|here]].
## One Later Finds Meat in One's Mouth
1. One must remove the meat and do kinuach and hadacha,[^5] but does not need to wait anew.[^6]
# Waiting After Eating Dairy before Eating Meat
## Time
1. In most cases, there is no length of time one needs to wait after eating dairy before eating meat.[^7]
## One's hands
### When One Needs to Wash
1. מחבר: If one has good lighting, they need to just check their hands to make sure that there is no dairy on them. If there isn't good lighting, one needs to wash their hands.[^8]
2. טור, רי"ף: One always needs to wash their hands.[^9]
3. פתחי תשובה: One should wash their hands if it is not difficult.[^10]
### Requirements when Washing
#### All Opinions
1. A person can't have הסח הדעת.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^11]
2. A person must dry their hands.[^11]
#### Usable Water
1. מחבר, ש"ך: One must use water.[^11]
2. מהרש"ל: One can use any liquid.[^11]
# Kinuach and Hadacha
1. This means that one eats something (although it cannot be a sticky food), swishes it around their mouth, swallows the food, and takes a drink.[^12] It does not matter if one kinuach and then hadacha or hadacha and then kinuach.[^13]
# Hard Cheeses
## שיטות
1. ש"ך:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^14] One needs to wait 6 hours for any cheese that has been aged 6 months.
2. ט"ז:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^15] One needs to wait 6 hours for cheese that has become wormy (apparently this is a step in some cheesemaking) or made from milk from an animals stomach.
3. (Practically, it seems that we are נוהג like the ש"ך. Very few cheeses would qualify according to the ט"ז.)
# תבשיל
## What is a תבשיל
1. There is a מחלוקת if liquid in meat/dairy is considered to be a תבשיל or the actual thing itself.[^16]
2. However, according to all opinions, a thick stew with mushed meat/dairy mixed in is considered to be the actual thing itself.[^16]
3. As such, מעיקר הדין, in such cases one would need to wait before eating dairy, if it is meat, and one can't eat it if it is dairy and one ate meat, according to all שיטות.
## Eating Dairy After Eating a תבשיל of Meat
1. If one wants to eat actual dairy after eating a תבשיל of meat, then:
	1. מחבר: One doesn't need to wait.[^17] (The בית יוסף in או"ח says that one should wait, so הלכה למעשה, ספרדים do wait.)
	2. רמ"א: One should wait the usual time that one waits after eating meat itself.[^18]
## Washing Hands Between the Two Foods
1. If one ate a food cooked with dairy without eating the dairy and then wants to eat meat (or the other way around) they should wash their hands between the two.[^17]
2. If, however, they are just eating a תבשיל of both of them:
	1. מחבר: They don't need to wash their hands.[^17]
	2. רמ"א: They do need to wash their hands.[^18]
## נ"ט בר נ"ט
1. If one wants to eat a food that was cooked in a meat/dairy בן יומו כלי and then eat a food of its opposite, they do not need to wait or wash their hands. For more details, see here. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">add link to נ"ט בר נ"ט</span>
# Fat From Meat/Chicken
1. The fat from meat and chicken has the same דין as the actual meat and chicken themselves.[^19]
# Meat/Dairy Equipment
1. רמ"א (פשוט פשט): One can eat something dairy right after eating a food made in the כלי of its opposite even without washing one's hands or doing kinuach and hadacha.
2. <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>ש"ך, <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>רמ"א (According to the ש"ך): One may eat something from a meat/dairy כלי that still has some ממשות and then right after, eat its opposite.[^20] For why the ש"ך says this, see [[Question on הג''ה סעיף סעיף ג|here]].
# Dirty Pots
1. The ש"ך says that if one cooks a dish in a pot that still has some ממשות on the bottom, they may not eat it with its opposite, but they don't need to wait. For more discussion of this opinion, see [[Dirty Pots (ש''ך ס''ק יט)|here]].

# Knives
## לכתחילה
### Cutting Cold Food
*Note: בן יומו is not mentioned. It seems that these הלכות are the same whether the knife is בן יומו or not.*
#### Cutting Many in a Row
1. See [[סימן צו הלכות#Many Cut in a Row|here]].
#### Standard Case
##### Cutting Dairy with a Meat Knife or Vice-versa
1. ש"ך: One cannot do this.[^22]
2. ט"ז: One can do this if they do נעיצה first. However, the מנהג is not to do this, even with נעיצה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^23]
##### Cutting Parve Food to Eat with Dairy with a Meat Knife or Vice-versa
1. מהרש"ל: One cannot do this, even if they first do נעיצה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^24] For further discussion of the מהרש"ל's שיטה, see [[מהרש''ל on Doing נעיצה on Knife between Dairy and Meat|here]].
2. ש"ך, פרי מגדים: One must first do נעיצה. However, the מנהג is not to do this, even with נעיצה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> בשעת הדחק, one can be be מקל if they do נעיצה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^25]
3. רמ"א: One must first do נעיצה. It is מותר לכתחילה to do this. However, the מנהג is to have separate knives for meat and dairy.[^26]
4. ט"ז: One can do this if they simply clean the knife first.[^27] For discussion about this opinion, see [[Questions on the ט''ז Regarding Knives|here]].
