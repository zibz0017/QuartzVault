# Waiting after Eating Meat before Eating Dairy
## &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">[^1]מחבר,&lrm;[^2] רמ"א:&lrm; Six hours. The רמ"א says that this is the preferred מנהג.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (first יש אומרים):&lrm;[^1] Immediately, after bentching, קנוח and הדחה.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (second יש אומרים):&lrm;[^1] Bentch, one hour. (The order does not matter). For more details about this opinion, see [[Details about Opinion to Wait One Hour|here]].</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך:&lrm;[^3] According to this opinion, one doesn't need to do kinauch and הדחה (similar to what we say according to the six hour שיטה).</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז:&lrm;[^4] According to this opinion, one always needs קנוח and הדחה.</span>
For further discussion of this מחלוקת (and some other details), see [[Background on מחלוקת on How Long to Wait After Meat Before Dairy#'גמ|here]].

# Waiting After Eating Dairy before Eating Meat
## Time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In most cases, there is no length of time one needs to wait after eating dairy before eating meat.[^7]</span>
## One's hands
### When One Needs to Wash
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: If one has good lighting, they need to just check their hands to make sure that there is no dairy on them. If there isn't good lighting, one needs to wash their hands.[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">טור, רי"ף: One always needs to wash their hands.[^9]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פתחי תשובה: One should wash their hands if it is not difficult.[^10]</span>
### Requirements when Washing
#### All Opinions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person can't have הסח הדעת.&lrm;[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person must dry their hands.[^11]</span>
#### Usable Water
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, ש"ך: One must use water.[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: One can use any liquid.[^11]</span>
# &lrm;קנוח and הדחה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This means that one eats something (although it cannot be a sticky food), swishes it around their mouth, swallows the food, and takes a drink.[^12] It does not matter if one קנוח and then הדחה or הדחה and then קנוח.&lrm;[^13]</span>
# Hard Cheeses
## &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך:&lrm;[^14] One needs to wait 6 hours for any cheese that has been aged 6 months.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז:&lrm;[^15] One needs to wait 6 hours for cheese that has become wormy (apparently this is a step in some cheesemaking) or made from milk from an animals stomach.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Practically, it seems that we are נוהג like the ש"ך. Very few cheeses would qualify according to the ט"ז.)</span>
# &lrm;תבשיל
## What is a תבשיל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת if liquid in meat/dairy is considered to be a תבשיל or the actual thing itself.[^16]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, according to all opinions, a thick stew with mushed meat/dairy mixed in is considered to be the actual thing itself.[^16]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, מעיקר הדין, in such cases one would need to wait before eating dairy, if it is meat, and one can't eat it if it is dairy and one ate meat, according to all שיטות.</span>
## Eating Dairy After Eating a תבשיל of Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one wants to eat actual dairy after eating a תבשיל of meat, then:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: One doesn't need to wait.[^17] (The בית יוסף in או"ח says that one should wait, so הלכה למעשה, ספרדים do wait.)</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: One should wait the usual time that one waits after eating meat itself.[^18]</span>
## Washing Hands Between the Two Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one ate a food cooked with dairy without eating the dairy and then wants to eat meat (or the other way around) they should wash their hands between the two.[^17]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If, however, they are just eating a תבשיל of both of them:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: They don't need to wash their hands.[^17]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: They do need to wash their hands.[^18]</span>

# Fat From Meat/Chicken
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The fat from meat and chicken has the same דין as the actual meat and chicken themselves.[^19]</span>
# Meat/Dairy Equipment
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (פשוט פשט): One can eat something dairy right after eating a food made in the כלי of its opposite even without washing one's hands or doing קנוח and הדחה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;ש"ך, &lrm;רמ"א (According to the ש"ך): One may eat something from a meat/dairy כלי that still has some ממשות and then right after, eat its opposite.[^20] For why the ש"ך says this, see [[Question on הג''ה סעיף סעיף ג|here]].</span>
# Dirty Pots
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ש"ך says that if one cooks a dish in a pot that still has some ממשות on the bottom, they may not eat it with its opposite, but they don't need to wait. For more discussion of this opinion, see [[Dirty Pots (ש''ך ס''ק יט)|here]].</span>

# Knives
## &lrm;לכתחילה
### Cutting Cold Food
*Note: בן יומו is not mentioned. It seems that these הלכות are the same whether the knife is בן יומו or not.*
#### Cutting Many in a Row
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[סימן צו הלכות#Many Cut in a Row|here]].</span>
#### Standard Case
##### Cutting Dairy with a Meat Knife or Vice-versa
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: One cannot do this.[^22]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: One can do this if they do נעיצה first. However, the מנהג is not to do this, even with נעיצה.&lrm;[^23]</span>
##### Cutting Parve Food to Eat with Dairy with a Meat Knife or Vice-versa
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: One cannot do this, even if they first do נעיצה.&lrm;[^24] For further discussion of the מהרש"ל's שיטה, see [[מהרש''ל on Doing נעיצה on Knife between Dairy and Meat|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך, פרי מגדים: One must first do נעיצה. However, the מנהג is not to do this, even with נעיצה.&lrm; בשעת הדחק, one can be be מקל if they do נעיצה.&lrm;[^25]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: One must first do נעיצה. It is מותר לכתחילה to do this. However, the מנהג is to have separate knives for meat and dairy.[^26]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: One can do this if they simply clean the knife first.[^27] For discussion about this opinion, see [[Questions on the ט''ז Regarding Knives|here]].</span>
