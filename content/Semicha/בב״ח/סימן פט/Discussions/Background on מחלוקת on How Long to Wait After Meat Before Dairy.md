1. There is a מחלוקת about how long one needs to wait after eating meat before eating dairy. The 'גמ says that one needs to wait "from one meal to the next". There are two interpretations of this:
	1. This means that one needs to end their meal and that is it. Once one ends their meat meal, they can begin a new meal of dairy. (They just may have to do קנוח and הדחה.)
	2. The average amount of time between two meals. This is generally around six hours.
# שעות זמניות

1. There is a discussion about whether this is שעות זמניות or not. According to most (if not all) opinions, this is not שעות זמניות.
# חולה
*Presented by פתחי תשובה ס"ק ג*
1. If a חולה needs to drink חלב דרבנן (like מי חלב), they only need to wait one hour after eating meat.
# הלכה למעשה how long One Should Wait
*Presented by רמ"א סעיף א, ש"ך ס"ק ח, ט"ז ס"ק ב, פתחי תשובה ס"ק ב*
1. It seems from the רמ"א, ש"ך, ט"ז, and מהרש"ל that one should wait 6 hours.
2. The פתחי תשובה says that one should not protest against those who wait 1 hour because they have who to rely upon. (However, to me, the לשון sounds like 1 hour is not the ideal.)
