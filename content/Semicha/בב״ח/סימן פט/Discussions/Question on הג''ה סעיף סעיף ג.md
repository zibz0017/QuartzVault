# הג"ה סעיף ג
1. One doesn't need to wait to eat dairy after eating parve food that has been cooked in a meat כלי.
# Question on the רמ"א
1. This is פשוט. The רמ"א even permits eating dairy *with* parve food that has been cooked in a meat כלי. (This is a case of נ"ט בר נ"ט.)
# Answers
1. ש"ך: The רמ"א is saying that even if there is some meat ממשות in the pan, one does not need to wait before eating dairy. This is even though the ממשות is more than 1/60 of the parve food.
   פתחי תשובה: The ש"ך means is only referring to a case where one can't taste the meat. Fat leaves a taste so if there is fat, one would have to wait.
2. בית מאיר, רע"א: The רמ"א is saying that even if a דבר חריף is cooked in a meat כלי, one does not need to wait before eating dairy.
3. בה"ט: The רמ"א actually holds that לכתחילה, one is not allowed to mix dairy with parve that has been cooked in a meat כלי. The רמ"א over here is saying that even לכתחילה one doesn't need to wait before eating dairy. (The בה"ט asks this as a question on the ש"ך. He is asking what is the ש"ך bothered by. Presumably, the בה"ט would ask this on all of the פוסקים that are trying to answer this question.)
   Rav Heinemann answered (as personally heard from him) that the ש"ך is really asking on the בית יוסף. The בית יוסף permits נ"ט בר נ"ט לכתחילה. The רמ"א here is just presenting the שיטה of the בית יוסף. The ש"ך is really asking why the בית יוסף would need to to mention that one doesn't need to wait because the מחבר permits eating dairy with parve cooked in a meat pot, לכתחילה.
