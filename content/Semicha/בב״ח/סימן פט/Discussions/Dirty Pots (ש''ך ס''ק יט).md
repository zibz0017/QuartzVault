# The ש"ך's question
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א says that one cannot eat dairy with something that has been cooked in a meat pot, even though there was no meat that was cooking in it.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This seems to go against our understanding of נ"ט בר נ"ט. We would say that this is fine.</span>
# &lrm;ש"ך's Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Really you are allowed to eat the dairy with the item cooked in the meat pot. When the רמ"א said that you can't eat the dairy with the food cooked in the meat pot, he was referring to a case where the pot wasn't cleaned well and there was some meat residue in the pot.</span>
# Question on the ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is difficult to understand why this is different than any other case of eating something that has been cooked in a pot with meat.</span>
# Answers
## &lrm;בית מאיר and רע"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">They give their own answer to the ש"ך's question. They say that the רמ"א was only saying that to include all cases, even cases of a דבר חריף for which we say that one can't cook dairy with something that has been cooked in a meat pot.</span>
## &lrm;פתחי תשובה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">He agrees to the ש"ך and says that if there is a small amount of meat in a dish, even if it is more than 1/60, one doesn't have to wait before eating dairy.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, this is only if there is no taste. If you can taste the ממשות then one can't be מקל.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Relatedly, one will taste fat if it is less than 1/60, so if there is fat, one can't be מקל.</span>
