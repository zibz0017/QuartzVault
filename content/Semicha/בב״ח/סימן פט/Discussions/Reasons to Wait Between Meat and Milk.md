# Reasons
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat gets stuck in one's teeth. After six hours, the meat is considered to no longer be meat.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The taste of meat sticks around in one's mouth for six hours. (According to this reason, meat found in the mouth after six hours is still considered meat.)</span>
# &lrm;הלכה
*Presented by ט"ז ס"ק א, ש"ך ס"ק ב, פתחי תשובה ס"ק א*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are מקפיד on חומרות of both reasons. As such, if someone chews the food but doesn't swallow, one must wait since the meat gets stuck in the teeth (like the first reason).</span>
   At the same time, if one finds food in their teeth even after six hours, they have to remove it (and perhaps do קנוח and הדחה) as according to the second שיטה, we do not consider the meat do be considered to have been digested (however the person doesn't need to wait dix hours anew).
   Similarly, if one's teeth are clean after eating, they still must wait because of the second reason.
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In fact, we are even מקפיד in cases where both reasons don't apply. For example, if someone chews up a תבשיל of meat (but not meat itself) but doesn't actually eat it, according to both reasons, the person doesn't have to wait. However, because of לא פלוג, the person should wait.</span>
# &lrm;שיטה of the עטרת זהב
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ב*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The עטרת זהב says that if one chews food and doesn't swallow, one must wait because of the meat taste.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This, seemingly, is against the simple understanding of the שיטות, as when a person chews but doesn't swallow, there is seemingly no taste in the person's mouth and the reason to wait is because it is stuck in the person's teeth.</span>
