# Meat/Milk That Falls into a Hot Pot of Its Opposite
## Asking a גוי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: If the food that fell in is removed quickly and a גוי tastes the food and doesn't taste the food that fell in, it is fine.[^1] (The גוי must be asked משיח לפי תומו.&lrm;[^2])</span>
   If the meat stays there for the amount of time that it takes to boil, we can't rely on the גוי's taste.[^1] For the reason, see [[Relying on a גוי for Food that Stays in the Pot Longer than it Takes to Cool Off|here]].
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: We can't ever rely on a גוי.&lrm;[^3]</span>
(The main source for these הלכות is found [[סימן צח הלכות|here]].)
## Status of the Food that Fell in
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food that fell in is always אסור if it is recognizable.[^1] (Practically, this is more relevant when the food that fell in is solid \[like a piece of meat or cheese\], as opposed to liquid \[like milk\] which mixes in.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is not recognizable, see [[Food that Fell in is not Recognizable|here]].</span>
## Status of the Food that Was in the Pot
### Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food that fell in is *mixed in to the pot before it dissolves*. For a discussion about what is considered mixed, see [[What is Considered Mixed|here]].</span>
#### Examples
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Meat/milk fell straight into liquid in a pot of its opposite.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Liquid milk/meat fell onto a solid food in a pot of its opposite and the pot was mixed immediately after the food fell in, or the pot was covered.[^5]</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food in the pot is מבטל the food that fell in (either the food that is in the pot is 60x the amount of the drop, or, according to the מחבר, if a גוי doesn't taste the drop, as discussed [[#Status of the Food that Fell in|above]]), the food in the pot is fine.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is not, then all of the food is אסור.</span>
### Case 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food that fell in landed on a piece of solid food and *was absorbed into that piece*.</span>
#### Example
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Milk falls directly onto a piece of meat in a pot and the pot is not mixed or covered.[^5]</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
##### Status of the Piece
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: If the piece is 60x the amount of the drop or a גוי doesn't taste drop in the food it fell on, everything is fine.[^5]</span>
   Otherwise, the entire piece is אסור. For a discussion of why this is, see [[Why Entire Piece is אסור|here]].
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: If the piece is 60x the amount of the drop everything is fine. Otherwise, the entire piece is אסור.&lrm;[^6]</span>
##### Status of the Rest of the Food
###### &lrm;רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the rest of the food in the pot is not 60x the amount of the *drop* the *rest of the food* is אסור.&lrm;[^6]</span>
###### &lrm;מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: *If the piece is mixed in*, you need 60x the amount *of the piece* in the rest of the pot. If there is not, the rest of the food is אסור.</span>
	   If the piece is not mixed in, the rest of the food is fine.[^7]
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: *Regardless of whether the piece is mixed in* you need 60x the amount *of the piece* in the rest of the pot. If there is not, the rest of the food is אסור.&lrm;[^8]</span>
For a discussion of the underlying reason for these שיטות, see [[Food that is Partially Submerged in a Pot (מחלוקת רש''י, ר''י)|here]]. ^04f188
### Case 3
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A drop falls into a pot of food but we don't know which piece of food in the pot it fell onto.</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: One mixes the pot. If the מותר food is 60x the amount of the אסור food, or if a גוי tastes that food and doesn't taste the איסור, everything is fine. This is even if someone mixes the pot after some time.[^9]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: One *immediately* mixes the pot. If the מותר food is 60x the amount of the אסור food, everything is fine.&lrm;[^10]</span>
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not 100% certain that the sources for this are correct. Need to double check.</span>
For further discussion of the מחלוקת between the מחבר and רמ"א, see [[מחלוקת מחבר רמ''א about Purposely Being מבטל a ספק|here]].

# &lrm;חנ"ן
## All Opinions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We do not say חנ"ן by two dry foods. For example, if a בב״ח cheerio gets mixed up with another cheerio and then both cheerios fall into something else, we only need to be מבטל that one אסור cheerio, not both of them.[^11]</span> ^e58d34
## &lrm;מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנ"ן only applies to בב״ח.&lrm;[^12]</span>
## &lrm;רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנ"ן applies to all איסורים. However, there are a few exceptions by שאר איסורים and one exception by בב״ח.&lrm;[^13]</span>
### Exceptions by שאר איסורים
#### Not Stuck together and Submerged in Sauce
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the איסור food is not stuck to the מותר food *and* the מותר food is submerged in מותר sauce, we it does not become חנ"ן.&lrm;[^13]</span>
   In other words, it *will* become חנ"ן if either:
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The איסור food is stuck to the מותר food, or</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">it is *not* submerged in מותר sauce,</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This only applies to שאר איסורים.&lrm;[^13]</span>
#### Two Liquids
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If אסור liquid and מותר liquid mix, we will not say חנ"ן *in a case of הפסד מרובה*. As such, if this mixture falls into a מותר food, the מותר food only needs to be 60x the אסור food *in a case of הפסד מרובה*.&lrm;[^13]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This only applies to שאר איסורים.&lrm;[^13]</span>
#### Two Dry Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[#^e58d34|above]]. As noted above, this even applies to בב״ח, and presumably, the מחבר agrees to this exception as well.</span>
#### Reason for These Exceptions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[Reason for רמ''א's Exceptions to חנ''ן by שאר איסורים|here]].</span>
### Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The איסור food is not stuck to the מותר food and the מותר food is not submerged in מותר sauce.</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the איסור was בב״ח (meat and dairy are stuck together, or dairy/meat is in sauce and its opposite falls onto it), we say חנ"ן.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the איסור was some other איסור (מותר food is stuck to חֵלֶב, or מותר food is sitting in מותר sauce and some חֵלֶב falls onto it), we don't say חנ"ן.</span>
## &lrm;ספק חנ"ן
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[מחלוקת מחבר רמ''א about Purposely Being מבטל a ספק|here]].</span>
# &lrm;בב״ח Into a Parve Food in a כלי ראשון Removed from the Fire
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a food has איסור בלוע and it falls into a food that was removed from the fire, it does not emit the איסור into the food and so the rest of the food remains kosher.[^14]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This even applies to a piece of dairy/meat that is אסור because of בב״ח and it falls into a food. This is considered to be an איסור בלוע and so the rest of the food is fine.[^14] (In other words, this is a slight exception to חנ"ן. If actual אסור food would fall into the pot in this situation, the food would release בליעות and potentially make the rest of the food אסור. In this situation, it is not able to.)</span>
# Drop Falls onto the outside of a Pot
## The Pot is Hot
### Background
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For the basic background, see [[Background for the הלכות for When a Drop Falls onto the outside of a Pot|here]].</span>
### The Pot is Uncovered
#### Drop Falls above the Food
##### The Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The drop spreads throughout the upper part of the pot, up until 60x the size of the drop.[^15]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, one can't pour the food while it is hot out of the pot. One would have to wait until the food cools down to pour it out.[^15] (For details about whether the food is אסור if one does pour it out while it is hot, see [[Pouring Food Over the אסור Part of a Pot|here]].)</span>
##### The Food
###### &lrm;מן הדין
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is fine if it is more than 60x the drop (which it almost certainly is).</span>
###### &lrm;מנהג
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, או"ה: We treat the food as אסור. For why this is and for some exceptions, see [[The Food in the Pot When a Drop Lands on the Pot above the Food Line|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרא"י (ש"ד), מהרש"ל, ש"ך, ט"ז: We treat the food as מותר.&lrm;[^16]</span>
#### Drop Falls opposite the Food
##### The Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food in the pot is 60x the amount of the drop, the food is מותר.&lrm;[^15]</span>
##### The Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: The pot becomes אסור, and so one should immediately pour the food out.[^17]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז:&lrm; We are מספק about what happens to the בליעות from the drop. Either it doesn't go into the pot, or it spreads directly through the pot and into the food.</span>
   As such, the pot is מותר, but one should not use the pot because it looks like it is אסור.
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not sure what exactly the source is. Rabbi Hodkin said to focus on ס"ק יט, and ignore ס"ק יז</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חב"ד: We see from here the concept of that during בשול, the בליעות go straight from one thing to the next, skipping נ"ט בר נ"ט. We view the drop as going straight into the food. We don't view it as the בליעות going from the drop into the pot, and from the pot into the food. We view it as going straight into the food.[^18]</span>
   An application of this is if we have milk in a כלי, and that כלי is put into a hot בן יומו meat pot, the הלכה will be that we view the milk as going straight through the כלי, straight through the water, and straight into the meat pot.[^18]
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't think the חב"ד belongs here</span>
#### Exception
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the drop falls on the pot near the fire so that it is nearly instantly dried up, everything is מותר.&lrm;[^19]</span>
### The Pot is Covered
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A pot with food is covered and steam fills up the whole pot. A drop of the opposite type hits the side above the food or top of the pot.</span>
#### Steam Fills up the Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א, פרי מגדים: We always treat the drop as if it [[#Drop Falls opposite the Food|fell opposite the food]], even if it falls above the food. The basic idea is that the steam "connects" the inside of the pot where the drop hits to the food below.[^20]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: The steam doesn't do anything. It has the same הלכה as a standard case where a drop [[#Drop Falls above the Food|falls above the food]].[^21]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: If it is a potential איסור דאורייתא (there is meat in the pot and milk hits the outside), we treat it with the חומרות of both a drop hitting above the food and below the food.[^21]</span>
   If it is only a potential איסור דרבנן (for example, a drop of milk hits a pot of chicken), we treat it as if it [[#Drop Falls above the Food|fell above the food]]. For the ט"ז's reason, see [[Drop Hits a Steaming Pot According to the ט''ז|here]].
#### Steam Doesn't Fill up the Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">All opinions: We treat the drop as if it [[#Drop Falls above the Food|fell above the food]].[^21]</span>
## The Drop is Hot and the Pot is not
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">See סעיף ז</span>
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If food from a כלי ראשון was poured onto a cold כלי, then:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is a stream connecting them, this is considered ערוי כלי ראשון and so the pot would be אסור.&lrm;[^22] For the status of the food, see [[Reason why Food is מותר even though קליפה is more than 1 in 60#Liquid|here]]. For a discussion about why this is considered תתא גבר, see [[Why a Stream Falling on the Side of a Pot is Considered תתא גבר|here]].</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the stream is broken this is considered to be a כלי שיני and so everything is fine.[^22] If it stuck there (like if butter fell onto a meat pot), then it needs to be scraped off.[^23]</span>
# Putting a Pot Down on a Spill
## Hot Pot on a Cold or Hot Spill
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A hot pot with food is put on a spill of the opposite type. It does not make a difference if the spill is hot. (Neither the spill nor the pot are currently being heated by the fire. For the הלכה in that case, see [[#Pot or Spill is near the Fire|here]]).</span> ^a65054
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pot is אסור.&lrm;[^24]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food inside is מותר.&lrm;[^24]</span>
For the reason, see [[Reason Behind the הלכות When a Pot is Put on a Spill#Hot Pot on a Cold or Hot Spill|here]].
## Pot or Spill is near the Fire
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A pot with food is put on a spill of the opposite type. Either the pot or the spill (or both) are being heated by the fire.</span> ^6fc0b0
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pot and food are אסור.&lrm;[^24]</span>
For the reason for the הלכה for when the spill or pot is near the fire, see [[Reason Behind the הלכות When a Pot is Put on a Spill#Case 2|here]] and [[Reason Behind the הלכות When a Pot is Put on a Spill#Case 3|here]], respectively.
# &lrm;שיעור המדומה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is a כלי is submerged in a mixture of meat/dairy and other things, it only becomes partially meat/dairy, in the same ratio as the mixture it fell into.[^25]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For example, if a parve spoon fell into a chulent that is 5% meat, the spoon only becomes 5% meat. As such, if the spoon then falls into hot milk, you only need 60x 5% of the spoon. If the spoon is 5 oz, you need 5% of 60x5 oz, which end up being just 15 oz.</span>
# &lrm;הגלה on כלים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Filling up a כלי with water and heating it up does not work for הגלה. One has to dip the כלי into boiling water.[^26]</span>
# Steam (זיעה)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Steam from a dairy/meat dish that touches dishes or food of the opposite type can אסור them through and through.[^27]</span>
## Requirements
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food getting hit with steam is not 60x the amount *of the food emitting the steam* (note, that it is 60x the size *of the food emitting steam*, not 60x the size *of the steam*).[^28]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The steam needs to be יד סולדת בו.&lrm;[^28] However, if the steam is under יד סולדת בו and the top item is hot, it will become אסור כדי קליפה (like other cases of תתא גבר).&lrm;[^29]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A covered pot will not emit steam.[^28] However, it seems that לכתחילה, one should not put two foods in a situation that if they were to be uncovered, there would be a problem.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to some, the steam needs to be coming from a liquid. If it came from a solid (the example given is חֵלֶב that somewhat melts), everything is fine.[^30]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to Rav Moshe Feinstein, if there is a flame above the food (like if it is in a broiler), the steam dissipates and doesn't do anything.[^31]</span>
## Status of the Food Emitting the Steam
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">דגול מרבבה: &lrm;בליעות travel from the food that is hit by the steam back to the food that is emitting the steam and can אסור it.[^32]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">None of the other מפרשים on the page discuss the status of the bottom food, so it would seem that according to everyone else, the bottom food is fine.</span>

# Candles
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If איסור from a candle (like if the candle is made of חֵלֶב) falls onto a כלי, if the liquid fell from right by the flame, the כלי requires הגלה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it fell from a different part of the candle, then the [[#The Spill is Hot and the Pot is not|standard דינים of a hot liquid that falls onto a כלי apply]] (usually there won't be a continuous stream so the pot is fine and it just needs to have whatever came from the candle scraped off).</span>
# Ladles
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מהרי"ל says that if you dip a ladle (or any כלי) into a boiling כלי that is on the fire and leave it there for some time, the liquid in the ladle when you pull it out counts as a כלי ראשון (regarding kashering for פסח).&lrm;[^33]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For questions on this, see [[Ladles|here]].</span>
