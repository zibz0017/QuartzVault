# &lrm;שיטות
## &lrm;ר"י
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A food that is partially submerged in liquid is considered to be part of the liquid. As such, if something falls onto that piece, all of the food in the pot can combine together to be מבטל the thing that fell onto the piece.</span>
   If there is not enough to be מבטל, then all of the food in the pot becomes אסור.
## &lrm;רש"י
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about how to understand רש"י.</span>
### All Opinions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A food that is partially submerged in liquid is *not* considered to be part of the liquid. As such, if something falls onto that piece, only that piece can be מבטל the thing that fell onto the piece.</span>
   If there is not enough to be מבטל, then that piece becomes אסור.
### &lrm;רמ"א And ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This piece of food cannot make the rest of the pot אסור. Only pieces that are touching this now-אסור piece become אסור, כדי נטילה.</span>
### &lrm;ט"ז
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This piece of food can now make the rest of the food in the pot אסור. The איסור transfers from the top of the food to the bottom of the food and from the bottom of the food into the rest of the pot.</span>
# &lrm;מחלוקת מחבר רמ"א
## &lrm;מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר holds like רש"י and says you need 60x the piece or else the rest of the pot is אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת between the ש"ך and ט"ז how to understand this:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: This means that even if the food is not mixed, the איסור spreads from the piece to the rest of the food and so you need 60x the amount of איסור.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: The איסור would not travel from the piece to the rest of the food, so he understands the case to be that the piece was mixed into the rest of the food.</span>
## &lrm;רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The שיטה of the רמ"א is hard to understand. Why would the piece become אסור but then the rest of the food only needs to be 60x the amount of the drop?</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Additionally, according to the ש"ך that the piece needs to be mixed in to spread the איסור, why would the איסור spread in this case, as the רמ"א is clear that this case is even where the piece was not mixed in?</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת between the ש"ך and ט"ז about how to understand this:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: The רמ"א is מחמיר like רש"י and the ר"י. As such, according to רש"י that the piece is not considered to be part of the rest of the food, the piece will becomes אסור unless it is 60x the amount of the drop.</span>
	   According to the ר"י, the piece is considered to be part of the food and so the rest of the food will become אסור unless it is 60x the amount of the drop.
	   The רמ"א is מחמיר like both of these: the piece needs to be 60x the drop or else it is אסור, and the rest of the food needs to be 60x the drop or else it is אסור.
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: The רמ"א really holds like the ר"י. As such, the rest of the pot needs to be 60x the drop. The reason why the piece itself needs to be 60x the drop is  simply a that the רמ"א decided that one should be מחמיר (similar to what he says in סעיף ד).</span>
