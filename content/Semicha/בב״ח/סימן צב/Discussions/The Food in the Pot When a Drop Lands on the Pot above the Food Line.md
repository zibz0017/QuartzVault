# Background
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether the we treat the food in the pot as אסור.</span>
# Reason for Those Who Say it is אסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason for those who say it is אסור is because there is a concern that the food in the pot splashed up and touched the part of the pot that is אסור.</span>
# Exceptions: When Everyone Will Agree that the Food is מותר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is more than 60x the drop and the pot is new and has never been used for meat/milk yet (the opposite of whatever the drop was) or the pot is not a בן יומו.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is more than 3660x the amount of the original drop.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בשעת הדחק (like on ערב שבת, for guests, if there is a large loss of money, for the sake of a poor person), we do not treat the food as אסור. One may even pour the food out while it is hot if there are guests (just make sure to pour it out on the side away from the spot that the drop fell). In this case, we rely on the שיטה that says that there is no such thing as חנ"ן in כלים.</span>
