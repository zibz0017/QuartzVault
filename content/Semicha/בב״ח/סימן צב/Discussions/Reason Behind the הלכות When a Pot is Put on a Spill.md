# Hot Pot on a Cold or Hot Spill
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">[[סימן צב הלכות#^a65054|This case]].</span>
## Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The spill is only a כלי שיני, which is not hot enough to make בליעות transfer.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pot is hot, but we say תתא גבר, and the pot is on top. Because of this, the בליעות from the spill can only travel through the קליפה. As such, only the pot can become אסור, but the food inside is fine.</span>
# Case 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A pot is put on a spill that is being heated up by the fire.</span>
## Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because the spill is being heated up by the fire, it is considered a כלי ראשון. Because it is on bottom (and we say תתא גבר), we say that the בליעות from the spill travel up and through the pot and through the food, as well.</span>
# Case 3
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A pot is put on a spill and the pot is being heated up by the fire.</span>
## Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because the pot is being heated up by the fire, we don't say תתא גבר and the בליעות from the spill can only travel through the קליפה. We only say the התר of תתא גבר (meaning, that if only the top food is hot but not the bottom food, the בליעות from the bottom food only travels though the קליפה of the top) when the top food is not currently on the fire. When it is on the fire, then the בליעות from the bottom food travel completely though the top item.</span>
