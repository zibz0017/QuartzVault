# The שיטה of the מהרי"ל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מהרי"ל says that only when a ladle dipped into a כלי that is on a fire is left in the כלי, does the liquid that is removed with the ladle have the status of a כלי ראשון.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the ladle is removed quicky, the liquid in the ladle has a status of a כלי שיני.</span>
# Questions of the ט"ז
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why does the ladle need to wait there? Even if it is removed immediately, it should still be a כלי ראשון, the same way:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you dip something אסור into a pot of מותר stuff, the food in the pot becomes אסור.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When kashering things by dipping them into a pot, they becomes kosher immediately. They don't need to wait in the pot.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מהרי"ל agrees that if the כלי waits there it becomes a כלי ראשון. How long does it need to wait there and where does the מהרי"ל get this from?</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a story in the 'גמ that an amora was taking a bath on שבת and he told the amora he was with to pour from the main כלי where the water was warming up into a different כלי to make the water have the status of a כלי שיני and pour from there into the bath (which presumably had water).
   From the fact that the amora did not tell him to dip the כלי into the main כלי with the hot water is a raya against the מהרי"ל.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">תוס'&rlm says that the reason the something is considered a כלי שיני is because the walls of the כלי cool the liquid down. If you dip the כלי into the כלי ראשון, then the walls won't be cool, so why should this be considered a כלי שיני.</span>
