# How Much the בליעות Spread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When a drop falls onto the outside of a pot, there is a discussion about whether the בליעות of the drop spread, and how much they spread. The basic שיטות are: </span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות spread throughout the whole pot and everything in it.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות spread nearly up until 60x the size of the drop.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות don't spread at all. They stay where the drop hit the pot.</span>
# Repercussions
## Spread throughout the Whole Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the בליעות spread through the whole pot, then they will usually be מבוטל. In nearly all cases, if there is food in the pot (and probably even if there isn't food in the pot), the drop is less than 1/60 of the size of the pot and food (if there is).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, in this option, everything would be מותר.</span>
## Spread Nearly up until 60x the Drop
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the drop fell *opposite the food*, the בליעות would usually be מבוטל, because the food is almost certainly 60x the size of the drop.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the drop fell *above the food*, the בליעות will אסור a part of the pot. It will not אסור anything below the height of the food because בליעות that spread there would be מבוטל.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות will spread around the spot where the drop landed until it spreads to nearly 60x the size of the drop.</span>
## Don't Spread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות won't אסור anything. However, one should still not use the pot because one shouldn't use a pot that has איסור in it.</span>
# &lrm;הלכה למעשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are מחמיר like all of the שיטות (which, in practice, is really only the latter two. The first one doesn't add any חומרות).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, there may potentially be a חנ"ן problem. If there are meat בליעות in the pot, for example, and a drop of milk falls onto the pot above the food, the בליעות of the milk will spread through some of the top of the pot.</span>
   There are not בב״ח בליעות there. This is a חנ"ן problem. For more details about what happens, see [[חנ''ן in the Walls of the Pot|here]].
