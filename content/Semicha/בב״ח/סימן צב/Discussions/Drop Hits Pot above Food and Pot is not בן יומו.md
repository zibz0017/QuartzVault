# &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a drop hits a pot above the food line, if the pot is not a בן יומו, the drop spreads through the pot up to just under 60x the size of the drop but does not make the pot חנ"ן.</span>
# &lrm;ש"ך's Question
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">How can the pot possibly not be a בן יומו? The pot is literally cooking food now.</span>
# Answers
## Answer 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are unsure how far בליעות spread in a pot. If we say that the בליעות spread from the food up to the spot where the drop hit the pot, we would have to say that the בליעות from the drop spread down to the food.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If this would happen, the drop wouldn't אסור anything at all. This connection of בליעות would allow for the drop to become מבוטל by the food in the pot (assuming that the food is 60x the drop, which it presumably is).</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, the worst case scenario is if the בליעות from the food do not spread up until the drop.</span>
## Answer 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pot is new and the pot got hot before the food inside the pot did. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Not sure why it is relevant that the pot is new, unless the ש"ך just means that it is not a בן יומו.</span></span>
