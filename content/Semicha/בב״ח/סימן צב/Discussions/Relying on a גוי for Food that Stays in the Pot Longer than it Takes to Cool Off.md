# Reason One Can't Rely on a גוי in such a Situation
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Once the food stays in the pot for longer than it would take to cool off, there is concern that some of the food in the pot has gotten in to the food that fell in.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This food that was originally in the pot then becomes אסור through חנ"ן.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">After this, the food then leaves the food that fell in the pot and mixes with the rest of the food in the pot.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because this is the same food as the food that is in the pot, it is not able to be tasted. As such, even if the גוי can't taste it, that doesn't mean anything as it isn't possible to be tasted as it tastes the same as the rest of the food in the pot.</span>
