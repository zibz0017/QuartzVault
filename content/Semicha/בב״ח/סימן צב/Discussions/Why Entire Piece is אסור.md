# Question
1. If איסור falls onto hot מותר food, we say in סימן קה that the food is only כדי קליפה. Why is the whole piece of meat of here אסור?
# Answer 1
1. Milk is considered fatty, so it goes through the whole meat. For further discussion, see [[Is Milk Considered Fatty|here]].
# Answer 2
1. The food is sitting in liquid. The liquid allows the drop to spread through the whole food.
# Answer 3
1. The case is where the meat is fatty, so the drop goes through the whole meat.
# Answer 4
1. The drop is liquid and liquid spreads through the food.
