# Question
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If איסור falls onto hot מותר food, we say in סימן קה that the food is only כדי קליפה. Why is the whole piece of meat of here אסור?</span>
# Answer 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Milk is considered fatty, so it goes through the whole meat. For further discussion, see [[Is Milk Considered Fatty|here]].</span>
# Answer 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is sitting in liquid. The liquid allows the drop to spread through the whole food.</span>
# Answer 3
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The case is where the meat is fatty, so the drop goes through the whole meat.</span>
# Answer 4
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The drop is liquid and liquid spreads through the food.</span>
