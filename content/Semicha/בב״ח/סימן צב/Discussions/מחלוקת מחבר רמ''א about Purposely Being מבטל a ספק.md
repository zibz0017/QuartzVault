# Example
1. A drop of איסור fell into one of two pots of food, but we don't don't which pot. Neither pot has enough מותר food to be מבטל the איסור, but together, there is enough.
   Can one combine the pots together to be מבטל the איסור?
# All Opinions
1. If we have a שאלה on some food, but we don't know which food is the one with the potential issue, we don't say חנ"ן.
# מחבר
1. In addition, mixing the ספק food is not considered to be being מבטל an איסור, לכתחילה because all of the foods with the potential שאלה are considered to be במקום ספק.
# רמ"א
1. This is considered to be מבטל איסור לכתחילה because we technically can ascertain which one it is by asking a גוי to taste it (even though practically we don't trust a גוי). As such, one is not allowed to be מבטל it.
# Applications
1. In the case above:
	1. According to the מחבר, one may combine the two pots together and be מבטל the איסור.
	2. According to the רמ"א, one may not combine the two pots together.
2. If a drop of milk falls onto a piece of meat in a pot but we don't know which piece and the pot is not immediately mixed:
