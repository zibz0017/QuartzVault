# Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food that fell in is not recognizable because there are similar foods in the pot. For example, a non-kosher piece of meat falls into a soup, and there are other pieces of meat that look the same.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה on the Pieces
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pieces of meat follow the standard דין of רוב.</span>
### &lrm;חתיכה הראויה להתכבד
#### &lrm;בב״ח and non-Kosher
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the piece is אסור because of בב״ח, or because it is from a non-kosher animal, then if the piece is a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד, it can't be מבוטל. As such, all of the pieces of meat become אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is not a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד, it is מבוטל if there are two other pieces of meat in the pot.</span>
#### &lrm;איסור בלוע
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is only an איסור בלוע (the piece of meat has "swallowed" non-kosher), this is subject to whether we say חנ"ן or not (which is a מחלוקת between the מחבר and רמ"א) regarding if we say that this can't be מבוטל if this is a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If we don't say חנ"ן, the piece itself is bietzem fine, and so we won't say that the piece can't be מבוטל because the piece is חתיכה הראויה להתכבד. The only possible issue is that there are בליעות in the food. בליעות are not riuya להתכבד, and so that will be able to be מבוטל, as well.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If we do say חנ"ן, then the piece itself becomes אסור. As such, if the piece is a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד, the piece will not be מבוטל.</span>
