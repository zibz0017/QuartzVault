# רמ"א
1. If two hot pots with food touch each other, everything is fine. However, לכתחילה, one should be מקפיד and not let the pots touch each other.
# Basic Reasoning
1. There is a discussion about why the רמ"א says that one should be מקפיד לכתחילה. This is because:
	1. Even though we say that בליעות can't travel from כלי to כלי (without liquid), לכתחילה we are מקפיד on this, or
	2. We are concerned that a little bit of the food from one (or both) of the pot(s) will get into the other.
	   (Some ask on this reason that the רמ"א talks about the concern that food from one dish will go into the other (סימן צז סעיף א) and he says that they shouldn't even be put near each other, not that they just shouldn't touch each other.)
# הלכה למעשה
## One (or both) Pots Are Empty
1. פרי מגדים (and others): The concern is the first reason. As such, even if the pots are empty, if they are בן יומו, if בליעות transferred there would still be a problem. As such, even in this case, לכתחילה, one should be מקפיד.
2. Rav Moshe Feinstein: There is no concern with putting a pot on a stovetop grate (unless you see actual ממשות on it) because the only concern is that there is בליעות in the grate and one is only going to put pots and pans onto the grate so the only concern is שתי קדירות which is מותר לכתחילה (at least in this case). Even if liquid spills from the pot and onto the grate, the liquid dries so fast that it doesn't transfer the בליעות.
   Additionally, even according to those that are מחמיר לכתחילה, they are מתיר בדיעבד. For things like stovetop grates, for which it would be a tremendous טרחא to have to change grates for meat and dairy and so we can view this as a בדיעבד situation and so even according to these שיטות, one can be מקל.
