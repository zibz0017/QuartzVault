# Indications that Milk is Fatty
## &lrm;סתירה 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In סימן קה, סעיף ד we say that when a hot solid food of איסור touches a solid food of התר, we must remove כדי נטילה of the מותר piece.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, in סימן צב, סעיף ב we say that when a drop of milk lands on an individual piece of meat, if the meat isn't 60x the size of the drop, the whole piece becomes אסור.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why is that? Why don't we say that we only need to remove כדי נטילה of the meat?</span>
## &lrm;סתירה 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In סימן קה we say that food that when an אסור food is salted with a מותר food, בליעות of the אסור food only travels to the קליפה of the מותר food.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, in סימן פז, סעיף י we say that if there is milk in the stomach of an animal and the animal is salted, and then the milk from the stomach is mixed with other milk and used to make cheese, the cheese is אסור unless the מותר milk was 60x the amount of the אסור milk.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why don't we say that we only need 60x times the amount of *the קליפה* of the אסור milk, rather than 60x the amount of *all of* the אסור milk?</span>
## Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It appears that we need to say that the milk is considered fatty. בליעות can travel through something that is fatty.</span>
