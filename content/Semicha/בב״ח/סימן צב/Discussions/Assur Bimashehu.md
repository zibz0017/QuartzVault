*Presented by the ט"ז ס"ק 8*
# Basic הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some foods are not מבוטל when they are less than 1/60. They אסור any mixture no matter how little they are compared to the מותר food in the mixture.</span>
## Examples
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to Rabbi יהודה, if I have two of the same foods and one is אסור and the other is מותר, the אסור food will not be מבטל in the מותר food, no matter how much מותר food you have compared to the אסור food.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יין נסך makes any mixture that it is part of אסור.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חמץ on פסח makes any mixture that it is part of אסור.</span>
# Exception (According to רבינו תם)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a food becomes אסור using מין במינו (meaning, the מותר food is 60x or more than the אסור food, but it becomes אסור anyway because מין במינו is not מבטל), then it cannot אסור other foods using מין במינו.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, it can still אסור other foods. The מותר food needs to be 60x the amount of the now-אסור food.</span>
## Example
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Two pieces of the same type of meat touch each other. One of the pieces is מותר and one is אסור. The מותר piece is 60x the size or more of the אסור piece. The מותר piece becomes אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This now-אסור piece of meat becomes cooked with other (מותר) pieces of meat of the same type.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In this situation, because the now-אסור piece only became אסור through מין במינו, it cannot make the other pieces of meat אסור using מין במינו.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, if the other pieces of meat are 60x or more than this now-אסור piece of meat, they all remain מותר.</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if they are not 60x the amount of this now-אסור piece, they all become אסור.</span>
## Exception to the Exception
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The "exception" is only when the אסור food was removed. In the above example, the אסור piece of meat made the מותר piece of meat אסור and then it was removed. Only the newly-אסור piece of meat is mixed with other pieces.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the אסור food itself is mixed with מותר food, it always makes all of the food אסור, no matter how many times it is mixed with מותר food.</span>
### Example
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יין נסך is mixed with some מותר liquid (יין נסך makes food אסור במשהו). The liquid becomes אסור, no matter how many times the מותר liquid is the size of the יין נסך.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This mixture then falls into some other מותר liquid.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We don't say that we can't use the rule of משהו here, because in this case, the יין נסך is still here. As such, regardless of how much of the מותר liquid is compared to this אסור mixture, everything becomes אסור.</span>
