# Basic Rules
## How Hot Does a כלי ראשון Need to Be
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to most שיטות, a כלי ראשון needs to be יד סולדת בו to be able to transfer בליעות. (The מהרש"ל says that for a כלי ראשון on the fire, we are מחמיר even if it is hot but not יד סולדת בו but he is a דעת יחיד.)&lrm;[^1]</span>
## &lrm;בליעות From כלים
### &lrm;בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a בן יומו כלי is inserted into a food in a כלי ראשון, בליעות will come from the כלי and enter the food.[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is 60x the בליעות from the כלי, the food remains kosher.[^3]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For discussion about how much בליעות are in the כלי, see [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;שיעור המדומה|here]].</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For more details about what is considered בן יומו, see [[#When בן יומו Begins|here]].</span>
#### Inserting a Spoon (Or Some other כלי) into a כלי ראשון
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Only the amount of בליעות that was in the part of the כלי that was inserted into the food go into the food,[^2] no matter the material of the utensil (for further discussion of this topic, including exceptions, see [[Only Part of כלי is Inserted into Food|here]]).[^4] For example, if one only stuck the head of the spoon into a food, only the בליעות in the head of the spoon count.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one doesn't know how much of the utensil was put into the pot, he should assume a "normal amount" was put in (which is the head of the spoon).[^5]</span>
   If one does think that they know how much of the spoon was stuck into the food, there is a מחלוקת if we can rely on on the person's assessment:
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">נודה ביהודה: We can rely on the persons assessment.[^6]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פתחי תשובה: We cannot rely on the person's assessment, unless there is some sign on the utensil.[^6]</span>
### Non-בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the כלי is not a בן יומו the בליעות are נותן טעם לפגם and so they can't אסור anything. As such, the food and the כלי holding the food will be totally fine.</span>
## &lrm;חנ"ן In כלים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a כלי is a בן יומו, we פסקען that the בליעות in the כלי could become חנ"ן just like food. For example, if someone cooks meat in a בן יומו dairy pot, the בליעות in the pot become חנ"ן.&lrm;[^7]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the כלי is not a בן יומו, the בליעות do not become חנ"ן.</span>
# Applications
## Meat/Dairy בן יומו Utensil in a Pot of Food of Its opposite
### &lrm;בן יומו Utensil Used for the opposite Type of Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A בן יומו utensil is used for food of the opposite type in a כלי ראשון.</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
##### Utensil
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Since the utensil is not 60x the food, the utensil absorbs בליעות from the food and becomes אסור. It may not be used with anything.[^8] If it was used it for another food, the next food becomes אסור, as well (unless it is 60x the בליעות in the utensil).[^9]</span>
##### Food and Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As [[#Basic Rules#&lrm;בן יומו|discussed]], if the food is 60x the amount of the בליעות that come from the utensil, the food and pot are fine.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is not 60x the amount of the בליעות, the food and pot become אסור.&lrm;[^9] For discussion about using the אסור pot, see [[Using a Pot that has been Made אסור|here]].</span>
### Non-בן יומו כלי Used for opposite Type of Food
#### Utensil
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The utensil may not be used with milk or meat but may be used with parve. According to the רמ"א, even though it is מותר to use with parve, one still shouldn't. If you do use it with any food, the next food is fine.[^10]</span>
#### Food and Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As [[#Non-בן יומו-1|discussed]], a non-בן יומו בליעות can't אסור anything, so the food and כלי are perfectly fine. לכתחילה, one should not do this, though.</span>
### The Utensil Was Dipped Twice (Or mOre)
#### You Didn't Realize between the Dippings
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: You need 120x times the amount of בליעות from the utensil.[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: You still only need 60x the amount of the בליעות. The second dipping doesn't add anything.[^12]</span>
For a discussion of the שיטות and why dipping it more than twice doesn't make a difference, see [[Dipping the Utensil Twice (or More)|here]].
#### You Did Realize between the Dippings
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all שיטות, in this case you only need 60x. For why this is different than when you don't realize, see [[Dipping the Utensil Twice (or More)|here]].</span>
## Parve Food Cooked with Dairy/Meat and then in Opposite Pot
### Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One cooks parve food with meat/dairy. Then, they cook this food in a בן יומו pot of its opposite.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food and pot are אסור unless the pot is 60x the amount of בליעות in the food or the food is 60x the amount of בליעות in the pot.[^13]</span>
# When בן יומו Begins
## Removing a Pot from a Fire
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a pot with food is removed from the fire, בן יומו begins from when the food cools to lukewarm. (It seems that this is around 98.6$\degree$F.)[^14]</span>
## Cooking Something in a Pot
### Cooking Meat and Dairy
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The "בן יומו timer" begins from when the utensil was made אסור. Even if the the utensil had not been used for the thing that it is usually used for in over 24 hours, that doesn't matter.</span>
   For example, if a dairy spoon was used for milk at 6:00 PM, and it is then used for meat at 6:00 AM, the בן יומו timer begins at 6:00 AM, even though it will be over 24 hours from when it was used for milk.[^15]
### Basic Rule
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Every time there is a transfer of taste, the "בן יומו timer" begins for that taste.[^16]</span>
### General Application
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In all of these cases, we have to evaluate the original taste, and the new taste that occurs when we cook the parve thing in the pot.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it has been over 24 hours since the original taste, the taste is now not a בן יומו.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When we cook the thing in the pot, the taste from the pot may enter the food and then transfer back into the pot. There is a new "בן יומו timer" for this, which has to be evaluated.</span>
### Specific Cases
#### Meat/Dairy In Pot בן יומו from Parve; not בן יומו from opposite
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone cooked meat/dairy in a pot. While it was a בן יומו, they cooked something parve.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While it was a בן יומו of the parve thing, but *not* a בן יומו from the original meat/dairy, they cook the opposite of they cooked before.</span>
For example, someone cooks meat at 7:00 AM. They cook water at 7:00 PM. They cook milk at 8:00 AM.
##### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is fine. This is because:[^16]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The original taste is not בן יומו, so it can't affect the food.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The taste from the pot entered the parve thing and then went back into the pot. Even though that taste *is* a בן יומו, it is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא, so it can't אסור the food.</span>
#### Food in Pot בן יומו from התר; not בן יומו from איסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone cooked pig in a pot. While it was a בן יומו, they cooked something parve.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While it was a בן יומו of the parve thing, but *not* a בן יומו from the pig, they cook some other (מותר) food in the pot.</span>
##### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is אסור. This is because:[^16]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The original taste is not בן יומו, so it can't affect the food. But…</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to רמ"א, the איסור is transferred to the parve thing and becomes חנ"ן, because according to the רמ"א, there is חנ"ן created by all איסורים.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the מחבר, there is no חנ"ן created in this case because there is no חנ"ן by שאר איסורים.</span>
	   However, the taste being transferred from the pot to the parve food and from the parve food back to the pot is אסור taste. This is נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא. As such, since the "בן יומו timer" for this taste starts when the taste transfers, when you cook the the מותר food, it receives נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא, and this makes it אסור.
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In summary, according to everyone, the food is אסור. The only question is why.</span>
#### Food in Pot בן יומו from התר; not בן יומו from בב״ח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone cooked בב״ח in a pot, or they cook dairy/meat followed by the other while the pot is a בן יומו.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">They then cook something parve in the pot while it is a בן יומו.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While the pot is a בן יומו from the parve thing, but *not* from the meat or dairy, a person cooks something מותר in the pot.</span>
##### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is אסור. This is because:[^16]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The original taste is not בן יומו, so it can't affect the food. But…</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the בית יוסף, when the parve food is cooked in the pot and בב״ח taste is transferred to it, a new חנ"ן taste is created. In other words, according to the בית יוסף, we treat בליעות from בב״ח can create חנ"ן, just like when originally cooking dairy with meat, not like שאר איסורים.</span>
	   As such, there is now a new בן יומו timer from this brand new איסור. This makes the next food אסור quite simply because it is cooked in a בן יומו pot that was used for איסור.
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the ש"ך in the מחבר's opinion, cooking the parve food in the pot does not create a *new* חנ"ן. Only cooking meat with milk can do that. As such, it simply transfers the בב״ח taste from the pot to the water and then back again.</span>
	   However, as said before, this is considered נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא. As such, since the בן יומו timer for this נ"ט בר נ"ט taste starts when the taste is transferred, the מותר food is being cooked in a בן יומו pot that has נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא taste. As such, the food is אסור.
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the רמ"א, as said before, there is חנ"ן even by שאר איסורים. That would certainly apply to this case. As such, the מותר food is being cooked in a בן יומו pot that has been used for איסור. As such, the food is אסור.</span>
	5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In summary, according to everyone, the food is אסור. The only מחלוקת is why.</span>
# Can חנ"ן Create חנ"ן
## Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone puts dairy/meat into a food of its opposite type while it is in a כלי ראשון, and it is not מבוטל. The pot of food becomes אסור. It is חנ"ן.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some of that food falls into another food that is כלי ראשון and it is not מבוטל. That food also becomes אסור. However, does that food become חנ"ן?</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is relevant if food from that last pot falls into another pot. If we say that all of the food in that pot became חנ"ן, then the whole drop that fell into the next pot is איסור, and to make that next pot מותר, it would have to be מבטל the whole drop.</span>
   If it is not חנ"ן, then only part of the drop is איסור, and so you would only need enough to be מבטל that איסור.
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך:&lrm; חנ"ן can create more חנ"ן.&lrm;[^17]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בית יוסף:&lrm; חנ"ן can't create more חנ"ן.&lrm;[^17]</span>
# Using אסור כלים
## &lrm;בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is אסור to use בן יומו כלים.</span>
## Non-בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should not use a אסור dish, even if it is no longer a בן יומו.&lrm;[^18]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one does use such a dish:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: One should throw out money equal to the value of the food.[^19]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: There is no reason to do this. One doesn't need to do anything at all.[^20]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This הלכה does not belong here</span> מעיקר הדין, if one dips a non-בן יומו meat/dairy כלי into a hot food of its opposite, the כלי may be used for hot parve food. This is because the כלי doesn't have חנ"ן בליעות because non-בן יומו בליעות can't become חנ"ן. As such, when you stick the כלי into the parve food, the parve food doesn't get any חנ"ן בליעות from the כלי, because it doesn't have any.&lrm;[^18] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not sure if this source is correct. Perhaps סעיף ד is a better source</span></span>

# Hot כלים with Food
## Basic case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A hot כלי has אסור food on it, or the plate is dairy/meat and there is food of the opposite type put on it.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is totally dry, one needs to remove כדי קליפה of the food.[^26]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food has a drop of moisture, one needs to remove כדי נטילה of the food.[^26]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is totally wet, the whole food is אסור.&lrm;[^26]</span>
# Knives
## Cutting Hot Food with opposite Knife
### Status of the Food
#### If the Knife Wasn't Particularly Clean
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">At the very least, the קליפה must be removed.[^27] (Potentially, the entire food may be אסור.)</span>
#### &lrm;בן יומו Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone cuts hot food *in a כלי ראשון* with a בן יומו knife of the opposite type, the whole food becomes אסור unless it is 60x the בליעות in the knife.[^28] (The case discussed is a dairy knife cutting meat, but this would presumably apply even for a meat knife cutting dairy food.)</span>
   For why this is, see [[Why Cutting Hot Food with בן יומו opposite Knife Becomes Totally אסור|here]].
   For more details about what 60x the בליעות in the knife means, see [[בליעות Extracted from Knife to Food|here]].
#### Non-בן יומו Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is not בן יומו (or if you unsure if it is a בן יומו), you only need to remove the קליפה if the knife wasn't particularly clean.&lrm;[^29] For why the meat needs to have a קליפה removed even by a non-בן יומו knife, see [[Why a Non-בן יומו Knife can אסור כדי קליפה|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the knife was totally clean, you don't need to do anything at all.[^30]</span>
### Status of the Knife
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is the same as any other כלי used for its opposite type of food. This depends on if it is בן יומו or not, as discussed [[סימן צג הלכות#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]] and [[סימן צג הלכות#Cooking Meat/Dairy in a Non-בן יומו Pot of Its opposite|here]].</span>

# Specific Foods
## &lrm;בליעות From Cheese
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בליעות from cheese only travel a כדי קליפה in another food. As such, if hot cheese was put on hot meat (or the other way around), even if the meat is fatty or there was a little moisture, the meat only becomes אסור כדי קליפה&lrm;.[^32] However, if there was actual liquid, the cheese will אסור more than a קליפה.&lrm;[^33]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cheese absorbs בליעות like anything else. As such, in the previous case, if the meat was fatty or there was liquid, the cheese would be totally אסור, and if the there was not, the cheese would be אסור כדי נטילה.&lrm;[^34] (And according to the רמ"א that we treat everything like it is fatty, the cheese would always become totally אסור&lrm;[^35]</span>
## Honey
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Fat from honey is considered נותן טעם לפגם. As such, if one cooks honey in a בן יומו meat/dairy pot and pours it onto a plate of its opposite, the plate and honey do not become אסור, even if it is לא נפסק הקילוך.&lrm;[^36]</span>
