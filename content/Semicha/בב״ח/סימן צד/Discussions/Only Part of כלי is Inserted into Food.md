# Non-Metal Utensils
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, by non-metal utensils, if one end of a utensil is sitting in food, and the other end is not, if the end not in the food is not hot בליעות do not transfer from the food into that part out of the food or from that part into the food.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(The case where even the not submerged part is hot is not explicitly discussed by a non-metal utensil, but it would seem to be the same as by a metal utensil.)</span>
# Metal Utensil
## Whole Utensil is Hot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, if the whole utensil is hot בליעות transfer from the non-submerged part into the food and from the food into the non-submerged part.</span>
## Non-Submerged part is not Hot
### Utensil Has Been Inserted into Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רבינו Peretz, סמ"ק: &lrm;בליעות transfer from the non-submerged part into the food and from the food into the non-submerged part.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרי"ט: &lrm; בליעות transfer from the food into the non-submerged part but not from the non-submerged part into the food.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך, פתחי תשובה (and others): בליעות do not transfer between the non-submerged part and the food in either direction.</span>
### Utensil Has Been Heated Straight by the Fire
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ש"ך and פתחי תשובה say that in this case, בליעות transfer from the non-submerged part to the food and from the food into the non-submerged part. (It is not clear if anybody argues on this.)</span>
