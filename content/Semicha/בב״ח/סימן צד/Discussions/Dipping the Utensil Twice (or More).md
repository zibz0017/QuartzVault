# The מחלוקת
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ד-ו , ט"ז ס"ק ב*
## מחבר
1. The מחבר holds that we are concerned that not all of the בליעות left the utensil. Perhaps בליעות from the food in the pot entered the utensil and then mixed בליעות already there. This would create חנ"ן.
2. When you dip the utensil the second time, you are now putting חנ"ן בליעות into the pot.
3. As such, you can have the following worst case scenario:
	1. Just under 59/60 of the בליעות left the utensil and entered the food. The food in the pot needs to be just under 60x the בליעות in the utensil to be מבטל that.
	2. בליעות from the food enters the utensil. Because there is just over 1/60 בליעות left in the utensil, it is not מבטל. As such, the utensil gets fully infected with חנ"ן.
	3. Then you dip the utensil back into the food again. The new חנ"ן בליעות now enter the food. You need *another* 60x to be מבטל those בליעות.
	4. As such, the food in the pot needs to be just under 120x the amount of בליעות in the utensil.
## רמ"א
1. Some hold that בליעות can't become חנ"ן.
2. As such, when you dip the utensil the first time, the בליעות entering the utensil are not אסור.
3. Although we don't paskin like this שיטה, we can be מקל like this שיטה in this case.
# Why Dipping More than Twice Doesn't Make a Difference
1. According to the מחבר, חנ"ן is only by בב״ח, not by other איסורים.
2. Once the בליעות in the spoon become חנ"ן, they are no longer בב״ח; they are חנ"ן.
3. When you dip the utensil the second time and the utensil absorbs the בליעות from the pot (and puts its own בליעות into the pot), the בליעות entering the utensil don't become חנ"ן.
4. As such, when you dip it the 3rd time, the בליעות from the spoon aren't אסור, so you don't need to be מבטל those.
5. As said before, according to the רמ"א, we are not concerned that there are still בליעות in the utensil.
# Why Realizing between Makes a Different
1. Because you realized, at that point the בליעות are מבוטל (assuming that the food is 60x the amount of the בליעות in the utensil).
2. When you do the second dipping we view it as the entire pot is fully kosher, because the בליעות from the first dipping have already been מבוטל.
3. As such, once again the food you only need 60x the amount of the בליעות in the utensil.
