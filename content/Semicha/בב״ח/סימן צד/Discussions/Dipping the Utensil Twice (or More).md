## &lrm;מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר holds that we are concerned that not all of the בליעות left the utensil. Perhaps בליעות from the food in the pot entered the utensil and then mixed בליעות already there. This would create חנ"ן.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When you dip the utensil the second time, you are now putting חנ"ן בליעות into the pot.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, you can have the following worst case scenario:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Just under 59/60 of the בליעות left the utensil and entered the food. The food in the pot needs to be just under 60x the בליעות in the utensil to be מבטל that.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בליעות from the food enters the utensil. Because there is just over 1/60 בליעות left in the utensil, it is not מבטל. As such, the utensil gets fully infected with חנ"ן.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Then you dip the utensil back into the food again. The new חנ"ן בליעות now enter the food. You need *another* 60x to be מבטל those בליעות.</span>
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, the food in the pot needs to be just under 120x the amount of בליעות in the utensil.</span>
## &lrm;רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some hold that בליעות can't become חנ"ן.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, when you dip the utensil the first time, the בליעות entering the utensil are not אסור.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Although we don't paskin like this שיטה, we can be מקל like this שיטה in this case.</span>
# Why Dipping More than Twice Doesn't Make a Difference
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the מחבר, חנ"ן is only by בב״ח, not by other איסורים.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Once the בליעות in the spoon become חנ"ן, they are no longer בב״ח; they are חנ"ן.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When you dip the utensil the second time and the utensil absorbs the בליעות from the pot (and puts its own בליעות into the pot), the בליעות entering the utensil don't become חנ"ן.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, when you dip it the 3rd time, the בליעות from the spoon aren't אסור, so you don't need to be מבטל those.</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As said before, according to the רמ"א, we are not concerned that there are still בליעות in the utensil.</span>
# Why Realizing between Makes a Different
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because you realized, at that point the בליעות are מבוטל (assuming that the food is 60x the amount of the בליעות in the utensil).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When you do the second dipping we view it as the entire pot is fully kosher, because the בליעות from the first dipping have already been מבוטל.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, once again the food you only need 60x the amount of the בליעות in the utensil.</span>
