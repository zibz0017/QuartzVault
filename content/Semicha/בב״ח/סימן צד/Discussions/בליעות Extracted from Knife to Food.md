# How much בליעות Are Extracted
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We פסקען that בליעות are pulled into the food only from where the blade and food touched. (This is not a דין only relevant to knives. This is true about all כלים touching hot food: we פסקען that the בליעות are pulled only from where the utensil touched the food.)</span>
# How much Blade Was Used
## Person Claims to Know how much Blade Was Used
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether we can trust someone who tells us how much of the blade touched the food:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, רמ"א, ש"ך: We can trust the person.</span>
	   As such, if someone says that only some of the blade touched the food, they would only need 60x the part.
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז, מהרש"ל: We can *not* trust the person (as he presumably wasn't paying attention when he was cutting the food).</span>
	   As such, even if someone says that they know how much of the blade touched the food, they would require 60x the whole blade (but not the handle).
## Person Does not Know how much Blade Was Used
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all שיטות, we assume that the whole blade was used because a סתם person uses the whole blade.</span>
