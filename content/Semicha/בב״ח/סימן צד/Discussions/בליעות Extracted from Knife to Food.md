# How much בליעות Are Extracted
1. We פסקען that בליעות are pulled into the food only from where the blade and food touched. (This is not a דין only relevant to knives. This is true about all כלים touching hot food: we פסקען that the בליעות are pulled only from where the utensil touched the food.)
# How much Blade Was Used
## Person Claims to Know how much Blade Was Used
1. There is a מחלוקת about whether we can trust someone who tells us how much of the blade touched the food:
	1. מחבר, רמ"א, ש"ך: We can trust the person.
	   As such, if someone says that only some of the blade touched the food, they would only need 60x the part.
	2. ט"ז, מהרש"ל: We can *not* trust the person (as he presumably wasn't paying attention when he was cutting the food).
	   As such, even if someone says that they know how much of the blade touched the food, they would require 60x the whole blade (but not the handle).
## Person Does not Know how much Blade Was Used
1. According to all שיטות, we assume that the whole blade was used because a סתם person uses the whole blade.
