# Holding foods
## Used to Hold Dry Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may use the pot to hold dry foods because one is not getting hanna from the אסור בליעות in the pot when it is used for this.</span>
## Used to Hold Wet Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">באר הגולה, בית יוסף (according to the באר הגולה): One *may not* use the pot to hold wet foods.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך, B"Y (according to the ש"ך):  One *may* use the pot to hold wet foods.</span>
# Cooking with the pot
## &lrm;לכתחילה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, one may not cook with the pot, לכתחילה, in any case.</span>
