1. At first glance, the food should not become totally אסור, because we are dealing with two dry things. It should only be אסור כדי נטילה.
2. The reason that in this case we say that it becomes totally אסור is either because:
	1. The food is hot and there is דוחקא דסכינא. When we both of these factors, the בליעות to through the whole piece of food. בן יומו
	2. סתם knives have שמנונית. The שמנונית is fatty and so it travels through the whole food.
	3. The case discussed is where the meat is fatty. However, the פרי מגדים does not like this answer.
3. Additionally, according to the רמ"א, we treat all meat as fatty and so the בליעות will travel completely through.
