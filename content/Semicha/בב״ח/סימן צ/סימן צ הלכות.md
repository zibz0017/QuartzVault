# Udder
## Potential איסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">At worst, meat with milk that is still in the udder when the animal dies is an איסור מדרבנן, because this is חלב שחוטה, and חלב שחוטה is only אסור מדרבנן.</span>
## &lrm;לכתחילה

### &lrm;מעיקר הדין
#### Roasting it by Itself
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cut it open, drain out the milk, and roast it.[^1]</span>
#### Cooking it with other Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cut crisscrosses, stick it on a wall until there is no moisture of milk.[^1]</span>
### &lrm;מנהג
#### General Principles
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לכתחילה, never prepare udder with other meat, according to all שיטות, no matter how you prepared it (meaning, whether you roasted it, cooked it, etc. and whether you ripped and crisscrossed it, or stuck it on a wall).[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לכתחילה, never cook an udder, even by itself.</span>
#### Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Roasting without other meat on a spit.</span>
##### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: This is מותר לכתחילה with cutting crisscrosses (even without hanging on a wall).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: This is מותר לכתחילה with cutting crisscrosses and hanging on a wall.[^3]</span>
#### Case 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פשטידה.</span>
##### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מחבר:&lrm; מותר לכתחילה if crisscrossed and hung on a wall, even if it is prepared in a pan.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;רמ"א:&lrm; מותר לכתחילה if crisscrossed and hung on a wall only if it is not prepared in a pan.[^5]</span>
   However, if it was dried (for more details, see [[Drying Udder|here]]) this is מותר, even in a pan.[^6]
   For an additional הלכה, see [[פשטידה in an Oven with Meat|here]].
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;יש פירושים: &lrm;אסור unless dried, in which case, it would be מותר, even in a pan.[^6]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: Even if it was dried it is אסור in a pan.[^7]</span>
#### Case 3
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Frying without other meat, or</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Cooking (קדירה) without other meat.</span>
##### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר:&lrm; מותר לכתחילה if crisscrossed.[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א:&lrm; אסור, even if crisscrossed and hung.[^9] For why this is, see [[Why Cooking or Frying Udder is אסור and Cutting is מותר|here]].</span>
## &lrm;בדיעבד
*Note: There is no such thing as "מנהג בדיעבד". In a בדיעבד situation, the הלכה is as it says מעיקר הדין in a בדיעבד situation*
### Cooking it by Itself
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: If it was cooked (and certainly if roasted) without other meat, it may be eaten.[^10]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: If it was crisscrossed and hung, it may be eaten.[^3]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: If it was dried, it may be eaten.[^11]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: It is אסור, even if crisscrossed and hung.[^12]</span>
### Cooking it with other Meat
#### Without Any Preparation
##### All Cases
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The udder is אסור. For why this is, see [[Why is the Udder אסור When Cooked with Meat|here]].[^13]</span>
##### Udder is 1/59 of the Meat or less
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat is מותר.&lrm;[^13]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For why the ratio is 1/59 rather than the usual 1/60, see [[Ratio of 1 to 59|here]].</span>
##### Udder is More than 1/59 of the Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The meat is אסור.&lrm;[^13]</span>
#### With Preparation
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;רמ"א: If you cut crisscrosses into the meat and hung it on a wall, if you cooked it with other meat, you can be מקל in a case of הפסד מרובה.&lrm;[^14]</span>
   Additionally, If it was dried, it can be eaten.[^3]
### Roasting it by Itself
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: &lrm;מותר even without any preparation.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: &lrm;אסור if not prepared properly (crisscrossed and hung on a wall).[^15]</span> ^d03fcb
### Roasting it with other Meat
#### Without Any Preparation
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: Everything is מותר.&lrm;[^16] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not fully confident about this</span></span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;רמ"א: If one of the pieces are on top of the other one, the one on bottom is אסור.&lrm;[^3] (I assume that the case is that neither of the pieces were prepared.)</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: See [[#^d03fcb|above]].</span>
#### With Preparation
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">All opinions: Both pieces are מותר.&lrm;[^2]</span>
## Salting Udder with other Meat without Prior Preparation
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: It is a מחלוקת if one can salt the udder with other meat, לכתחילה. It is מותר בדיעבד.&lrm;[^17]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: It is אסור לכתחילה. It is מותר בדיעבד.&lrm;[^18]</span>
## Udder Fell into a Second Pot of Meat
### Udder Was 1/59 of the Meat in the First Pot or less
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the meat in the second pot is 59x the amount of the udder, the food is fine. Otherwise, it is not.[^19]</span>
### Udder Was More than 1/59 of the Meat in the First Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: If the meat in the second pot is 59x the amount of the udder, the food is fine. Otherwise, it is not.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: If the meat in the second pot is 60x the amount of the udder, the food is fine. Otherwise, it is not.</span>
## Cows These הלכות Apply to
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This applies to all cows, even young cows that don't produce milk yet.[^20]</span>
## Alternatives to Hanging on a Wall
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Tearing the udder and cutting crisscrosses all over it is more effective than hanging it on a wall.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Pressing it in a press is also more effective than hanging it on a wall.[^21]</span>
## Meat Utensils with an Udder and Vice-versa
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may cut a hot udder that has been properly prepared with a בן יומו meat knife. Similarly, one may use any בן יומו meat utensil with udder.[^23] For why this is, see [[Why Cooking or Frying Udder is אסור and Cutting is מותר|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The opposite is true as well, meaning, one may use בן יומו utensils that have been used with properly prepared udder with meat.[^23]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may cut a cold raw udder that has not been prepared with a בן יומו meat knife.[^24]</span>
## Finding Pockets of Milk
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the udder was properly prepared and roasted, if at that point you find pockets of milk, it is totally fine.[^25]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it has not been properly prepared, then it is אסור.&lrm;[^25]</span>
For further discussion of these הלכות, see [[Pockets of Milk|here]].
## Milk Was Left in the Udder before it Was Prepared
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the milk was left in the udder for 24 hours before it was prepared, the udder is fine.[^26] For more details and why this is, see [[Milk was left in Udder|here]].</span>
# Cow Stomach with milk
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone pours the milk out of the stomach and washes it out, it is considered to be a regular piece of meat.[^22]</span>
# Unorganized Notes
## &lrm;סעיף 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מדרבנן, an udder needs to be drained before it is cooked.[^27] This is not considered to be חלב שחוטה because the milk wasn't milked from the animal. As such, it really shouldn't be אסור at all. Despite this, חז"ל require one to get rid of the milk before using the udder.[^28]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is cut with crisscrosses and hung from a wall, it can even be cooked with other meat.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the udder is cooked by itself, even if one didn't drain it, it is מותר to eat.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one cooks the udder with other meat and didn't drain it, there needs to be 59x the amount of the udder.</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Whether there is 59x or not, the udder is asur either way.</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is because:</span> ^c3c474
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מראית עין.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is milk that is in grooves in the udder. The milk then gets the taste of meat. The milk gets cooked out, but we are concerned that the person won't cook it all the way and so we asur it. (ש"ך 5)</span>
7. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Technically, it should really just be the amount of milk that has drained out of the udder and into the meat. However, since this is hard to measure, we say treat it like the whole udder is asur. (ש"ך 4)</span>
8. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See ט"ז 2 for why it only needs to be 59x.</span>
9. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason for the difference between udder cooked by itself vs cooked with other meat is that the milk in the udder has taste of the udder and the udder has taste from the milk. As such, חז"ל were not גוזר on a case when the udder and milk are cooked by themselves. (בדי השלחן bringing down the Trumas Hadeshen bringing down the לבוש)</span>
10. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is no difference between a baby cow (that doesn't give milk) and a mother cow. (ש"ך 2)</span>
### Udder Falls into Second Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the udder falls into a second pot after falling into the first:</span>
#### If There Was \>59x the Meat in the First Pot
According to most opinions the udder in the second pot counts as part of the meat. As such, once again, you would only need 59x the udder. (ט"ז 4, ש"ך 5)
The reason for this is because the udder isn't really אסור. It is only אסור because of the the side reasons said [[#^c3c474|earlier]], but the udder is not actually בב״ח. As such, the same way it counted toward the התר side in the first pot, it also counts toward the התר in the second pot. (ש"ך 5)
#### If there Was \<59x the Meat in the First Pot
מחלוקת:
	- רמב"ם and Shulchan ערוך say it does not count as part of the meat. As such, you need 60x. (ט"ז 4)
	- רמ"א says that the udder does count as the meat and so you only need 59x.
### Basis for מחלוקת
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This מחלוקת is really about whether there is חנ"ן by isur דרבנן (need to rewrite it that way). (ט"ז 4)</span>
## &lrm;ט"ז ס"ק 4
### Reason You only Need 59x
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמב"ם: It is only an איסור דרבנן and so we treat it more מקל.</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Counter: There are many other איסורי דרבנן that require the full 60x.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רשב"א: The milk is not considered to be part of the meat and the milk that seeps out is מבוטל. The meat, however, is not considered a davar אסור. As such, it can count as part of the meat toward being mattir.</span>
   At the same time, the meat is considered a davar אסור when it comes to the second pot (if it falls into a second pot) because we don't know that all of the milk has seeped out.
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">I am confused by this רשב"א. According to this, why does the udder not count toward the איסור in the first pot? The same way we say when it comes to the second pot that there is a concern that there is still some milk in the udder that was not מבוטל and that makes the udder into חנ"ן and therefore count toward the איסור, we should say the same thing regarding the first pot: there perhaps is some milk in the udder that was not מבוטל and so this makes the udder a davar אסור and then it can go and count against the rest of the meat (and certainly not count with the rest of the meat to try and be mattir the food).</span>
   Perhaps since the udder is with the rest of the meat, we can't "single it out" and have the milk just target it, even though the milk is inside of the udder. However, according to this, why is the udder אסור after it leaves the pot?
## &lrm;סעיף 2
### &lrm;מנהג
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Don't cook with other meat.</span>
