# Cooking or Frying Udder
1. מעיקר הדין, it is actually מותר to cook udder without other meat if it has been prepared.
2. The reason why the רמ"א says this is אסור (לכתחילה) is because of a concern that one may come to cook an udder that hasn't been prepared with other meat, which is actually אסור, מעיקר הדין.
3. Similarly, if one roasts an udder by itself and then cooks it, it is אסור לכתחילה for the same reason, namely, we are concerned that the person will cook the udder with other meat without preparation.
# Cutting Hot Udder with Meat Knife
1. One is allowed to cut an udder that was properly prepared and is now hot from a כלי ראשון with a בן יומו meat knife.
2. This is because, as said above, מעיקר הדין, the udder is fine since it was prepared properly.
3. In contrast to the case above, since we are just talking about cutting it with a knife, there is no similar case regarding cutting it with a knife that we are concerned about that if we allow this, it may to something that is אסור. As such, חז"ל were not גוזר.
