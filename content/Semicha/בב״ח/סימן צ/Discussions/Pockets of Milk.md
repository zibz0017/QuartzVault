# &lrm;גזרה on חלב שחוטה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חז"ל were only גוזר on חלב שחוטה that has come out of the animal.</span>
# Applications
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone finds milk in the udder after getting properly prepared and roasted, the milk is fine because the milk hasn't come out of the animal, and so חז"ל weren't גוזר on that milk.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if the udder was not properly prepared, we consider the milk as if it has come out of the animal, and so we would treat it as אסור.</span>
