# נ"ט בר נ"ט
## נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא
### Cooking Food in a Pot
#### The Pot is a בן יומו
##### Eating the Food with Its opposite
1. Someone wants to cook a parve food in a בן יומו meat/dairy pot and then eat it with the opposite type of food.
###### שיטות
1. מחבר (according to Rav Ovadia): One may cook the food in the pot with intent to eat the food with the opposite. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need source</span> ^f31e0b
2. מחבר (according to the ש"ך): One may not initially cook it in the pot to eat it with its opposite. However, once one does, one may mix it with the opposite type of food and eat it.[^1]
3. רמ"א: One may not mix this with its opposite. However, once it has been mixed, it may be eaten.[^2]
4. פתחי תשובה, מהרש"ל, ש"ך: One may not mix this with its opposite. Additionally, even if one has already mixed it, the הלכה depends on how the parve food and the dairy/meat food that made the pot a בן יומו was prepared. For details, see [[Difference Between Roasting and Cooking by נ''ט בר נ''ט|here]].
#### The Pot is not a בן יומו
##### Non-דבר חריף
1. Someone cooks parve food in a non-בן יומו meat/dairy pot and they want to eat it with its opposite.
###### הלכה
1. If one plans on eating the food with meat/dairy, there is a discussion about whether one should not use the opposite type of pot. For details, see [[Food in non-בן יומו Pot One Plans to Eat with opposite|here]].
2. Once someone has already cooked the food in the pot, it is fine to eat it with its opposite according to all opinions.[^3]
##### דבר חריף
1. Someone has already cooked a דבר חריף in a non-בן יומו meat/dairy pot and mixed it with its opposite. For more details, see [[דבר חריף|here]]. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to this.</span>
###### הלכה
1. רמ"א: This is אסור to eat.[^2]
2. פתחי תשובה: If we have a ספק about whether the food is a דבר חריף, one can be מקל.&lrm;[^4]
### Putting it on a בן יומו Dish (עלו)
1. There are two different but similar cases that have the same הלכות:
	1. Someone puts a hot (straight out of a כלי ראשון) parve food on a בן יומו plate and they want to eat it with food that is the opposite type of the plate. It does not matter if the original pot was בן יומו or not.
	2. Someone cooks parve food in a meat/dairy pot and puts it (while it is still hot) on a בן יומו plate of the opposite type. It does not matter if the original pot was בן יומו or not.
	   (It should be noted that this case is specifically dealing with solids. If it is a liquid, this is subject to the הלכות of [[#Pouring Hot Water from a Meat/Dairy Dish onto Its opposite]].)
#### הלכה
1. רמ"א, (ט"ז): One may, לכתחילה, put the food on the plate, even if they plan on eating it with the opposite type of food.[^5] The ט"ז says that one who is מקל like the רמ"א is "לא הפסיד".&lrm;[^6]
2. מהרש"ל, או"ה: One may not put it on the plate if they plan on eating it with the opposite type of food. However, if one already put it on the plate, they may eat it with the opposite type of food.[^6]
### Dunking Dairy and Meat Dishes in Hot Water Together or Dairy/Meat Dish in Hot Water in opposite Pot
#### General Rule
1. This is only for cases in a כלי ראשון when both dishes were in the water at the same time. If the water was in a כלי שיני or the dishes were not put in at the same time (even if they were put in back to back), everything is fine according to most שיטות, even if there was actual ממשות. (The מהרש"ל is somewhat מחמיר on כלי שיני if there was actual ממשות).[^7]
#### Relevant Case
1. These הלכות would apply to a case where a בן יומו pot was used for parve and בן יומו utensil of the opposite type was used. For example, someone makes (parve) eggs in a בן יומו meat pot and uses and בן יומו dairy spatula.[^8]
#### Dirty Dishes
1. If the dishes were dirty, the water needs to be 60x the amount of the residue. If it is not, both dishes may become אסור. For details, see [[Dirty Dishes Washed Together|here]].
#### Clean Dishes
1. מחבר: If both are בן יומו, לכתחילה, one should not do this. בדיעבד, everything is מותר.&lrm;[^9] (Presumably, Rav Ovadia would say that the מחבר permits this לכתחילה, as discussed [[#^f31e0b|above]].)
   If one of them is not בן יומו, this is מותר לכתחילה.&lrm;[^9]
2. רמ"א: If both dishes are בן יומו, all of the dishes are אסור. If one of the dishes were not, all of the dishes are מותר.
   In either case, the water is אסור.&lrm;[^10]
   For why all of this is, see [[רמ''א on Clean Dishes Washed Together|here]].
### Pouring Hot Water from a Meat/Dairy Dish onto Its opposite
#### Both Dishes Were בן יומו
1. Someone pours from a hot כלי ראשון that is a בן יומו onto a בן יומו dish of the opposite type.
##### הלכה
1. רמ"א:&lrm;[^11] The dish becomes אסור. It is as if the person put the dish into the כלי ראשון.
2. ש"ך, ת"ח: In a case of הפסד מרובה or במקום צריך, one may use the dish.[^12] For the reason, see [[סתירה in רמ''א סעיף ב and סעיף ג#Application to Pouring|here]].
(The רמ"א is seemingly in סתירה to what he says in the ת"ח.&lrm;[^12])
3. According to all opinions, the top pot is fine.[^13]
#### One Dish Was not בן יומו
1. Same as the previous case but one of the כלים were not בן יומו.
##### הלכה
1. Everything is מותר, the same way that when we dunk כלים into hot water, if one of them is not a בן יומו, everything is מותר.&lrm;[^14]
### Pouring Hot Water onto Dairy and Meat Dishes
1. Someone pours כלי ראשון water from a parve כלי onto dairy and meat dishes.
#### הלכה
1. רמ"א: Everything is מותר.&lrm;[^10] For more details about the opinion of the רמ"א, see [[Hot Water onto Meat and Dairy Dishes#שיטה of the רמ"א|here]].
2. ש"ך, ט"ז: It depends on the details of the case. For more details, see [[Hot Water onto Meat and Dairy Dishes#שיטה of the ש"ך and ט"ז|here]].
3. ת"ח: In a case of הפסד מרובה or במקום צריך, one may use the dish.[^12] For the reason, see [[סתירה in רמ''א סעיף ב and סעיף ג#Application to Pouring|here]].
(The רמ"א is seemingly in סתירה to what he says in the ת"ח.&lrm;[^12])
### נ"ט בר נ"ט בשתי כלים
1. There are two basic cases:
	1. One cooks a parve food in a meat/dairy pot, and then cooks that food in a different pot.
	2. One cooks a parve food in a parve pot and uses a dairy/meat ladle.
#### הלכה
1. See [[נ''ט בר נ''ט בשתי כלים|here]].
## נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא
### Basic Case
1. Someone cooks a parve food in a בן יומו pot that was used for איסור.
2. Additionally, someone takes this parve food and cooks that in another pot. They then cook another parve food and cook it in that pot. The הלכה is the same no matter how many times someone keeps going with this.
### הלכה
1. The parve food becomes totally אסור. It makes the next food that it is put in אסור (if isn't מבוטל בששים). If that next food is put in another pot, that pot becomes אסור. And it keeps going from there.[^15]
# Salty Foods
## On Top or on Bottom
1. Being on top or on bottom doesn't make a difference regarding a transfer of בליעות when it comes to salt. The הלכה is the same whether the salty food is on top or on bottom.[^16]
## Timing
1. מחבר: When it comes to בב״ח, the salt only becomes potent after שיעור מליחה.&lrm;[^17]
   When it comes to blood with meat, the salt is immediately potent, but only lasts until שיעור מליחה.&lrm;[^17]
2. רמ"א: In both cases, the salt is immediately potent and only lasts until שיעור מליחה.&lrm;[^18]
   However, even after שיעור מליחה, one should be מחמיר unless it is a case of הפסד מרובה or צריך סעודת מצוה.&lrm;[^18]
# Fish Cooked in a Meat/Dairy בן יומו Pan
1. ט"ז, ש"ך: There is no problem of סכנה of eating fish cooked in a בן יומו dairy or meat pot.[^19]
2. מהרש"ל&lrm;,[^20] חכמת אדם (Chassidishe posek) <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need source</span>: One cannot cook fish in a בן יומו meat pan, because of סכנה.
3. (הלכה למעשה, Litvishe are מקל like the ש"ך and ט"ז. Chassidim are perhaps מחמיר.)
# Eggs in Their Shells
1. Eggs in their shells are the same as any other food. The shell doesn't provide any sort of protection.[^21]
# Meat/Dairy כלי Found Amongst the opposite Type
1. If one finds a meat/dairy כלי amongst כלים of the opposite type, they do not need to be concerned that they accidentally washed it with those dishes. The כלי is perfectly fine.[^22]
2. The reason is because we have a ספק ספיקא:[^23]
	1. Maybe you didn't was it with those opposite dishes.
	2. Even if you did, perhaps those dishes weren't a בן יומו.
# Something Disgusting Falls into the Pot
1. There is a מחלוקת between the מחבר, ש"ך, ט"ז, פתחי תשובה, and perhaps the רמ"א. For details see [[אפר (Ash)|here]].
## Applications
1. Soap can potentially be נותן טעם לפגם. As such, if meat and dairy dishes are washed together with soap, we can be מצטרף this into being מקל.
# Putting Foods Next to Each other
## Dairy next to Salt/Vinegar
1. One should not put a an open container of dairy food next to salt or vinegar if it will not dissolve into it because we are concerned that the dairy will fall into the salt and it will not be מבוטל since it is solid and the person will accidentally use the salt for meat.[^24]
2. One may put such dairy if it will dissolve.[^24]
3. For example, a solid cheese (like sliced American cheese) will not dissolve in either vinegar or salt.
   Soft cream cheese can dissolve in vinegar (because vinegar is a liquid) but won't dissolve in salt.
## Dairy/Meat Next to Each other
1. לכתחילה, one should not put open dairy/meat next to each other because one might fall into the other.[^25]
## Why Dairy next to Meat Vs Next to Salt Are Different
1. See [[Difference Between Dairy next to Salt Vs next to Meat|here]].
# Salt
## Next to Open Dairy
1. See [[#Dairy next to Salt/Vinegar|here]].
## On a Meat/Dairy Plate
1. Someone puts salt on a meat/dairy plate and they want it use it for the opposite.
### מעיקר הדין
1. There is no problem מעיקר הדין. This is because dry food, even if it is a דבר חריף, can't pull בליעות from a כלי.&lrm;[^26]
### תבו עליו ברכה
1. רמ"א: If one is מחמיר on this, they are תבו עליו ברכה.[^27]
2. פתחי תשובה: If the כלי is earthenware, one is תבו עליו ברכה. Otherwise, one is not.[^28]
3. ט"ז: This is totally מותר and one is not תבו עליו ברכה for being מחמיר.&lrm;[^29]
## Separate Dairy/Meat Salt Dishes
1. One should have separate salt dishes for meat and dairy because someone may stick their dirty hands into the salt dish and get dairy/meat ממשות into the dish, and they they will use it for the opposite.[^29]
