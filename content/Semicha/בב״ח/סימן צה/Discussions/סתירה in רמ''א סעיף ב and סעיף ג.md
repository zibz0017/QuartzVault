# &lrm;סתירה
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ה, יב, ט"ז ס"ק ח, סימן צד ט"ז ס"ק ז*
## &lrm;סעיף ב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one mixes the food cooked in the meat/dairy בן יומו pot with food of its opposite, they may eat it. (For example, someone cooks pasta in a meat בן יומו pot and then puts cheese on it.)</span>
## &lrm;סעיף ג
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one washes a בן יומו dish used for meat/dairy in the same hot water basin as another בן יומו dish used for its opposite, both dishes are אסור.</span>
## The סתירה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">From סעיף ב it sounds like בדיעבד, by נ"ט בר נ"ט, everything is fine. But in סעיף ג, it seems like the pots are אסור, even though they are נ"ט בר נ"ט. What's the difference?</span>
# Answers
## Answer 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When washing dishes, there is a concern that the dishes themselves touched. The בליעות aren't traveling from the dish to the liquid (in which case it would be נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא). They travel straight from one dish to the other. As such, there is no נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">By the food, we already have נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא. The בליעות traveled from the dairy/meat into the pot, and then from the pot into the next food.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It should be noted that the ש"ך does not bring down this answer, and the ט"ז, who originally brings down this answer, eventually says that the רמ"א does not hold of it.</span>
## Answer 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In סעיף ג, when the בליעות are leaving the dishes, they don't have a moment of התר. They are instantly mixed with בליעות of the opposite type in the water. This does not count as נ"ט בר נ"ט.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In סעיף ב, the בליעות leave the pot and enter the parve food. They are not mixed at this time with anything of the opposite type. As such, in this case we are more מקל.</span>
# Application to Pouring
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ש"ך points out that neither of these two reasons apply to a case where one pours from a כלי ראשון that is a בן יומו onto a בן יומו of the opposite type.</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The first reason certainly does not apply. The כלים don't touch.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The second reason doesn't apply, either. The בליעות from the כלי ראשון enter the water and there are no other בליעות there. As such, they are נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא and at least בדיעבד, everything will be fine.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, the ש"ך says that this is מותר. However, he does say that one should not rely on this except in a case of הפסד מרובה or במקום צריך.</span>
