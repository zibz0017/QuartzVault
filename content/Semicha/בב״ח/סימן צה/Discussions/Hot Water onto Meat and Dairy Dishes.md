# &lrm;שיטה of the רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In all cases, the dishes are מותר.</span>
## Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Dishes are hard and so the hot water can't pull out בליעות and put בליעות into them same time. As such, no בליעות enter the dishes and so they are מותר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if there is actual food, the same principle applies: the בליעות can't be pulled out of the food and then put into the dishes.</span>
# &lrm;שיטה of the ש"ך and ט"ז
## Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both dishes were בן יומו but clean.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Everything is fine. </span>
### Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We have a combination of reasons to be מקל:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the מחבר, the בליעות leave the dishes and enter the water. At that point, they are נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא and they can't אסור anything. (The רמ"א disagrees with this, as discussed [[סתירה in רמ''א סעיף ב and סעיף ג|here]].)</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The svarah of the רמ"א that dishes are too hard to absorb בליעות through ערוי, as discussed [[#&lrm;שיטה of the רמ"א|above]].</span>
## Case 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both dishes were dirty.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both dishes are אסור.</span>
### Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות from the meat and dairy mix in the water and become חנ"ן. They then infect the dishes and make them אסור.</span>
## Case 3
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One dish is בן יומו of actual איסור.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should be מחמיר on the dish that started out מותר.</span>
### Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In this case, there is no התר of נ"ט בר נ"ט because we are dealing with איסור (rather than בב״ח).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Despite this, it is not clear if the מותר plate becomes אסור. For this to happen, we need בליעות from the אסור *dish* and then go into the מותר *dish*. Perhaps ערוי doesn't have the power to pull בליעות out one dish and put it into a different dish. (In other words, the ש"ך can hear the [[#&lrm;שיטה of the רמ"א|reasoning of the רמ"א]], somewhat.)</span>
   As such, one should be מחמיר.
## Case 4
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One dish was a בן יומו. The other dish was dirty.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The clean dish is אסור. One should be מחמיר on the dirty dish.</span>
### Reason
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The clean dish is אסור for the reason discussed earlier. The בליעות from the food are pulled out and then put into the other dish, making it אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The dirty plate is more complicated. For the dirty plate to become אסור, we need בליעות from the food to go to the water, go into the other plate (making it אסור), pull בליעות from the now-אסור plate and into the water, and then put the בליעות back into the dirty plate. Somewhat similar to what we just said in [[#Case 3|case three]], it is not clear if ערוי has the power to do this.</span>
   As such, one should be מחמיר.
