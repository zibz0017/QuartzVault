# &lrm;מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone puts אפר (ash) into a בן יומו dairy/meat pot. A dish of the opposite type with ממשות falls into the pot (and the pot is a כלי ראשון).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכה is that the ash is נותן טעם לפגם and so the dish is fine.</span>
# &lrm;שיטות against the מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ש"ך and ט"ז say that there is no source for this הלכה. They bring cases numerous cases that seem to show that this is not a התר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א (סימן פז סעיף ו) says that one shouldn't take cook ash from on top of an oven because it may contain בב״ח. This would seem to go against the מחבר.</span>
# Defense of the פתחי תשובה for the מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The cases that were brought by the ש"ך and ט"ז are cases involving איסור. We aren't talking about making a food not אסור; we are talking about making dairy/meat not count as dairy/meat.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is "easier" to remove the dairy/meat status of food than make a food that is אסור no longer אסור.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(It would appear that this answer was not referring to the הלכה of the רמ"א.)</span>
