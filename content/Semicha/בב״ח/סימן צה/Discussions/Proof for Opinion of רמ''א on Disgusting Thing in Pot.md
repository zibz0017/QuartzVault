# סימן פז סעיף ו רמ"א שם
1. See [[Specific Cases with Potential Concern of Issur Basar Bichalav#Using the Container for Heating Water to Wash One's Head for Some other Purpose|here]].
# Proof from there
1. If ash is considered to be נותן טעם לפגם, then once you cook there shouldn't be a problem with using the pot. The בליעות in the pot have become פגום by the ash which is cooked in there. As such, if the רמ"א agreed that ash makes the בליעות פגום, why does he say that one should not use the pot?
2. It must be that the רמ"א holds that ash does not make the בליעות פגום.
# Own Thoughts
1. The ערוך השלחן there (see linked article) suggests that the reason רמ"א calls it a "חומרה בעלמא" because of this point, namely, he thinks that ash can be פגום the taste.
2. Clearly the ט"ז would disagree with this understanding of the רמ"א.
