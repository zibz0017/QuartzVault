# Proof from there
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If ash is considered to be נותן טעם לפגם, then once you cook there shouldn't be a problem with using the pot. The בליעות in the pot have become פגום by the ash which is cooked in there. As such, if the רמ"א agreed that ash makes the בליעות פגום, why does he say that one should not use the pot?</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It must be that the רמ"א holds that ash does not make the בליעות פגום.</span>
