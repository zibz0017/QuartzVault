# Basic Rules
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the dishes are בן יומו, בליעות leave from the dishes and can infect ממשות. This is not נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא. The ממשות become אסור and then may release בליעות that can אסור the dishes.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a dish is not a בן יומו, its בליעות can't make anything אסור.</span>
# Applications
## Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A בן יומו dairy/meat dish and a dirty dish of the opposite type are dunked in a כלי ראשון.</span>
   For example, a בן יומו dairy dish and a meat dish with meat residue are dunked in a כלי ראשון.
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both dishes becomes אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason is because the בליעות leave the בן יומו dish and infect the ממשות. Since this is not נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא, the בליעות make the ממשות אסור.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות from the ממשות are אסור and it now makes both dishes אסור.</span>
## Case 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One dish is dirty. The other dish is clean and not a בן יומו.</span>
   For example, a non-בן יומו dairy dish and meat dish with meat residue are dunked in a כלי ראשון.
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The clean dish becomes אסור (the dairy dish in the example) and the dirty dish remains מותר (the meat dish in the example).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason the clean dish becomes אסור is because the בליעות from the ממשות makes the non-בן יומו dish אסור.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, the בליעות from the non-בן יומו dish can't affect the ממשות. As such, the ממשות remain מותר and since they are the same type as the dish they are on (meaning, in the example, it is meat ממשות on a meat dish), there is no reason for the dish to become אסור.</span>
