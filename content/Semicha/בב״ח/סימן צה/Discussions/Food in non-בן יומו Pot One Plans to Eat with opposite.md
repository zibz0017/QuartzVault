*Presented by מחבר סעיף ב, הג"ה סעיף ב, The Laws of כשרות by Rabbi Forst (pg 162, footnote 44)*
# Case
1. A person plans on eating parve food with dairy/meat. Should he make sure not to use a non-בן יומו כלי of the opposite type?
# שיטות
## מחבר
1. According to Rav Ovadia's understanding of the מחבר, this is מותר לכתחילה. According to Rav Ovadia's understanding, even if the כלי was a בן יומו this would be מותר לכתחילה, as discussed [[סימן צה הלכות#^f31e0b|here]].
2. According to the ש"ך's understanding of the מחבר (that by a בן יומו כלי, one should make sure to not use the opposite type of כלי), this case is not explicitly discussed. Presumably, according to those who say that the רמ"א permits this לכתחילה, they would also say that the מחבר permits this לכתחילה, as well.
## רמ"א
1. There is a discussion about how to understand the רמ"א. The רמ"א says that if the food has been cooked in a non-בן יומו dairy/meat כלי, it is מותר לכתחילה to eat it with the opposite.
2. However, it is not clear about what he would say about whether one hasn't cooked it yet and one knows that they plan on eating with dairy/meat, if he should make sure not to use it with a pot of the opposite type or not.
3. There is a vague באור הגר"א (ס"ק י) which perhaps is saying that the רמ"א holds that one may initially use the meat/dairy non-בן יומו כלי, even though they plan on eating it with the opposite type of food.
4. There is an או"ה who somewhat implies that this is not okay.
5. Rav Moshe Feinstein says reads the גר"א this way (that the one may initially cook the food in the dairy/meat pot, even though they plan on eating with the opposite type of food) and so he says that this is fine לכתחילה.
6. The חכמת אדם (מח ב) reads the או"ה and the רמ"א himself to be saying that one should not do this and so he says that one should not do this.
7. Rabbi Forst says that he heard from Rav Elyashiv that one should be מחמיר like the חכמת אדם.
