# Basic Rule
1. According to the מהרש"ל, ש"ך, and פתחי תשובה, we need נ"ט בר נ"ט בר נ"ט, counting the liquid that the food cooks in counts as one of of the levels of נ"ט.
# Applications
## Case 1
1. Someone roasted meat on a skillet. They cleaned the skillet and then roasted parve food on it (while it was a בן יומו). They have mixed the parve food with dairy and now want to eat it.
### הלכה
1. This is אסור.
2. The reason is because we only have נ"ט בר נ"ט. The meat בליעות entered the skillet, and then they entered the parve food. That is only נ"ט בר נ"ט.
3. As such, *even though one has already mixed the parve food with the dairy* they may not eat it.
## Case 2
1. Someone cooked meat in water (or in any liquid) in a pot. They cleaned the pot and then roasted parve food directly on it (while it was a בן יומו). They have mixed the parve food with dairy and now want to eat it.
### הלכה
1. This is מותר.
2. The reason is because we have נ"ט בר נ"ט בר נ"ט. The meat בליעות have transferred to the water (or whatever liquid), then they entered the pot, and then they entered the parve food. That is נ"ט בר נ"ט בר נ"ט.
## Case 3
1. Someone roasted meat directly in a pot. They cleaned the pot and then cooked parve food in water (or any liquid, while it was a בן יומו). They have mixed the parve food with dairy and now want to eat it.
### הלכה
1. This is מותר.
2. The reason is because we have נ"ט בר נ"ט בר נ"ט. The meat בליעות have transferred to the pot, then they entered the water (or whatever liquid), and then they entered the parve food. That is נ"ט בר נ"ט בר נ"ט.
3. However, it should be noted that if the water (or whatever liquid) was mixed with the dairy, then it *may not* be eaten, because then we only have נ"ט בר נ"ט (meat to pot and pot to liquid).
   So if someone cooked the fish in a sauce, the even if the sauce was mixed already mixed with the dairy, one may not eat the food.
# דבר חריף
1. If something is a דבר חריף גמור, even in these cases of נ"ט בר נ"ט בר נ"ט, the בליעות transfer through. As such, the הלכה in [[#Case 2]] and [[#Case 3]] is that one may not eat the food, if we were dealing with a דבר חריף גמור.
