# Basic Rule
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the מהרש"ל, ש"ך, and פתחי תשובה, we only say נ"ט בר נ"ט if the בליעות passed through a liquid on the way into the כלי, or the way out. If we don't have this, this is not considered to be a נ"ט בר נ"ט.</span>
# Applications
## Case 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone roasted meat/dairy on a skillet. They cleaned the skillet and then roasted parve food on it (while it was a בן יומו). They have mixed the parve food with its opposite and now want to eat it.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason is because we don't have a liquid medium on the way in or the way out of the כלי. The בליעות go straight from the meat/dairy into the pan and from the pan into the parve food. As such, we do not view this the בליעות in the parve food as נ"ט בר נ"ט.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, *even though one has already mixed the parve food with the opposite type of food* it may not be eaten.</span>
## Case 2
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone cooked meat/dairy in water (or in any liquid) in a pot. They cleaned the pot and then roasted parve food directly on it (while it was a בן יומו). They have mixed the parve food with the opposite of the original food and now want to eat it.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is מותר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason is because we have we have a liquid medium on the way in. The meat/dairy בליעות have transferred to the water (or whatever liquid), then they entered the pot, and then they entered the parve food. That is a proper נ"ט בר נ"ט.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As discussed, according to all opinions, a food with נ"ט בר נ"ט בליעות may be eaten with its opposite if it has already been mixed together.</span>
## Case 3
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone roasted meat/dairy directly in a pot. They cleaned the pot and then cooked parve food in water (or any liquid, while it was a בן יומו). They have mixed the parve food with the opposite of the original food and now want to eat it.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is מותר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason is because we have the בליעות transferred through water on the way out of the כלי. The meat/dairy בליעות have transferred to the pot, then they entered the water (or whatever liquid), and then they entered the parve food. That is a proper נ"ט בר נ"ט.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, it should be noted that if the water (or whatever liquid) was mixed with the opposite food, then it *may not* be eaten, because the בליעות transferred directly to the liquid on the way out of the pot (meat to pot and pot to liquid).</span>
   So if someone cooked the fish in a sauce, the even if the sauce was mixed already mixed with the opposite of the original food, one may not eat the food.
