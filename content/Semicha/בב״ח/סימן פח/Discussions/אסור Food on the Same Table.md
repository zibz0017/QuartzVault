# Basic Issue
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The basic reason for these הלכות is that we are concerned that the person will eat some of the אסור food without thinking.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, if this we are dealing with food that the person is accustomed to eating, there is a concern. However, if we are dealing with food that the person doesn't normally eat, there is no concern.</span>
# Applications
## &lrm;אסור Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because a person doesn't normally eat אסור food, there is no issue with eating it on the same table.</span>
### &lrm;אסור Bread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because people are used to eating bread, we are more concerned about bread, and so we don't allow one to eat on the same table as אסור bread.</span>
## &lrm;בב״ח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because one is used to eating meat or dairy, we are concerned that the person may eat some of the meat/dairy, and so this is not allowed.</span>
## Making a נדר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one made a נדר not to eat certain foods, they would not be allowed to eat with that food on the table (without the [[סימן פח הלכות#&lrm;התירים|התירים discussed]]) because the person is used to eating these foods in general.</span>
## &lrm;מודר הנאה
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ב, ד and ט"ז ס"ק ג*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one was מודר הנאה from a person, then there is no concern that you will eat from this person's food because מסתמא, these people don't like each other.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if the people just don't like each other and they are eating meat and dairy, they would not be allowed to eat together.</span>
