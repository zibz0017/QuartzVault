# What Counts as a הקר
*Presented by הג"ה סעיף ב, ט"ז ס"ק ד, ש"ך ס"ק ו*
## שיטות
1. <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>מחבר, רמ"א, ש"ך: Something which is usually on the table and you are using it as part of the meal, it is not a הקר. (The example given by the הג"ה is bread.)
   However, if only one of those things are missing (it isn't usually on the table or it isn't being used) it works as a הקר.
2. ב"ח: If you use the item, even if it is not usually there, it does not work.
## Item Which is Sometimes on the Table
1. Even if the item is usually there, but not there at this time, that can count as a הקר.
   For example, if someone has a lamp on the table at night but not during the day, it counts as a הקר during the day, but not during the night.
## Height
1. A הקר needs to be "gavoa kitzas".
# מחלוקת about Who Needs a הקר
*מחבר סעיף ב, ט"ז ס"ק ב, ג, ש"ך ס"ק ד, ה*
1. <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>טור, מחבר, ט"ז: People who know each other, even if they are מקפיד on each, need a הקר.
2. <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>מהרש"ל: If people are מקפיד on each other, they do not need a הקר, unless they are brothers.
3. <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>טור (יש אומרים) (לפי תוס', רא"ש): People who don't know each other do not need a הקר. If they do recognize each other, a הקר does not work. (In other words, הקר is either not needed, or doesn't help.)
# מחלוקת about whether הקר Works
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ה*
1. תוס', רא"ש:<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> As explained above, הקר is either not needed, or doesn't help.
2. טור (יש אומרים):<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> הקר doesn't work for 2 people on the same bill.
3. מחבר, ת"ח, טור, מהרש"ל, ש"ך, other achronim: הקר does work.
