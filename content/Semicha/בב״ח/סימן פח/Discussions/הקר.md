# What Counts as a הקר
## &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מחבר, רמ"א, ש"ך: Something which is usually on the table and you are using it as part of the meal, is not a הקר. (The example given by the הג"ה is bread.)
   However, if it isn't usually on the table or it isn't being used, it does work.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ב"ח: If you use the item, even if it is not usually there, it does not work.[^2]</span>
## Item Which is Sometimes on the Table
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the item is usually there, but not there at this time, that can count as a הקר.
   For example, if someone has a lamp on the table at night but not during the day, it counts as a הקר during the day, but not during the night.[^3]</span>
## Item Which in an Unusual Spot on the Table
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If something is usually on the table but it is put in a spot that it is not usually in, this counts as a הקר.&lrm;[^4]</span>
## Height
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A הקר needs to be "גבוה קצת". (This was said in context of whether a lamp can be a הקר. It would seem that a tea light or something of similar height would not be tall enough.)[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if one person is eating on a tablecloth or placemat and the other person is not, (or both people are eating on their own tablecloths or placemats) this counts as a הקר, as well.[^6]</span>
# &lrm;מחלוקת About Who Needs a הקר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;טור, מחבר, ט"ז: People who know each other, even if they are מקפיד on each, need a הקר. Even though these people won't take food from each other, we make a לא פלוג.&lrm;[^7]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מהרש"ל: If people are מקפיד on each other, they do not need a הקר, unless they are brothers.[^8]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;טור (יש אומרים) (לפי תוס', רא"ש): People who don't know each other do not need a הקר. If they do recognize each other, a הקר does not work. (In other words, הקר is either not needed, or doesn't help.)[^9]</span>
# &lrm;מחלוקת About whether הקר Works
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">תוס', רא"ש, טור (יש אומרים):&lrm; As explained above, הקר is either not needed, or doesn't help.[^9]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, ת"ח, טור, מהרש"ל, ש"ך, other אחרונים:&lrm; הקר does work.[^10]</span>
