# What Counts as a הקר
*Presented by הג"ה סעיף ב, ט"ז ס"ק ד, ש"ך ס"ק ו*
## &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מחבר, רמ"א, ש"ך: Something which is usually on the table and you are using it as part of the meal, is not a הקר. (The example given by the הג"ה is bread.)</span>
   However, if it isn't usually on the table or it isn't being used, it does work.
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ב"ח: If you use the item, even if it is not usually there, it does not work.</span>
## Item Which is Sometimes on the Table
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the item is usually there, but not there at this time, that can count as a הקר.</span>
   For example, if someone has a lamp on the table at night but not during the day, it counts as a הקר during the day, but not during the night.
## Height
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A הקר needs to be "gavoa kitzas".</span>
# &lrm;מחלוקת about Who Needs a הקר
*מחבר סעיף ב, ט"ז ס"ק ב, ג, ש"ך ס"ק ד, ה*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;טור, מחבר, ט"ז: People who know each other, even if they are מקפיד on each, need a הקר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;מהרש"ל: If people are מקפיד on each other, they do not need a הקר, unless they are brothers.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;טור (יש אומרים) (לפי תוס', רא"ש): People who don't know each other do not need a הקר. If they do recognize each other, a הקר does not work. (In other words, הקר is either not needed, or doesn't help.)</span>
# &lrm;מחלוקת about whether הקר Works
*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ה*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">תוס', רא"ש:&lrm; As explained above, הקר is either not needed, or doesn't help.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">טור (יש אומרים):&lrm; הקר doesn't work for 2 people on the same bill.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, ת"ח, טור, מהרש"ל, ש"ך, other achronim: הקר does work.</span>
