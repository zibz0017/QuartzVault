# Eating Meat/Dairy on the Same Table as Someone Eating the opposite
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not do this.[^1]</span>
## &lrm;התירים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is no problem for food on a serving table.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is a הקר, this is fine. For further discussion about הקר, see [[הקר|here]].</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the people are out of arm's reach of each other, there is no issue.[^2]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there are other people eating at the table as well, there is no issue.[^3]</span>
## Eating Dairy with Someone Who is Fleishigs
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone is eating with someone who is fleishigs, but not currently eating meat. (It seems that it does not make a difference if he is eating something else, or even if he is not eating anything at all.)</span>
### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בית יעקב, בה"ט: This is אסור, unless the person eating the dairy knows that the other person is fleishigs. (The יד אפרים adds that according to this שיטה, if there is reason to assume that the fleishig person won't eat from the dairy, like if he just had a large meal so he is not hungry, or he is a חשוב person and so it is beneath him to take food from the other person, there is also no issue).[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יד אפרים, פרי מגדים: This is מותר. (The פרי מגדים implies that if the fleishig person is distracted, like if he is busy doing something else, there is a concern.)[^5]</span>
# Foods that Carry איסור (other than בב״ח) on the Same Table
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to most, one may do this, in most cases. However, אסור bread is a problem.[^6] For further discussion, see [[אסור Food on the Same Table|here]].</span>
# Sharing a כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not share a כלי with someone eating something that is אסור to them (for example, if you are eating dairy/meat, you can't share a כלי with someone else eating the opposite) because the food sticks to the כלי.&lrm;[^7]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For some additional points, see [[Sharing a כלי|here]].</span>
