# Unorganized Notes
## Sif 3
1. Can't eat tavshil of meat and milk together.
2. Sefardim: Can eat milk after tavshil of meat (and vice versa).
3. Ashkenazim: Can not.
4. Can eat milk with parve food cooked in fleishig pot. (shach 19)
5. Shach: Can eat milk after parve food cooked in meat pot even though meat pot has meat residue. (shach 19)
   Beis meir attacks this mima nafashach: if it is batul bishishim, then you should be allowed to eat it with milk. And if it is not, then you should have to wait. Beis Meir says that chidush of Rama is that one may eat milk with parve food cooked in meat pot, even if meat is davar charif.
6. Ashkenazim: treat tavshil of meat and milk like actual meat and milk (meaning, need to wait after tavshil of meat and need kinuach/hadacha after tavshil of milk).
7. Beis Yosef and Rema: A server doesn't need to wash hands between serving milk and meat.
   Shach: Server does need to. (Shach 21)
8. There is a machlokes about the status of meat juice. According to some, it is the same as meat. According to others, if it is thick it is like meat, and if it is thin, it is only like a tavshil of meat. (Practically, there doesn't seem to be a difference according to Ashkenazim.) (Taz 5)
## Sif 4
1. After eating meat/milk, you must clean the table from crumbs/take off the tablecloth before switching to the other one.
2. You can't use a knife used for meat (milk) for food that you will eat with milk (meat).
3. Rema and Shach: Midina, if you do neitzah, you can use the knife for the other one, lechatchila (a meat knife to be used for dairy and vice versa). However, Klal Yisroel have separate meat and milk knives and this is proper. If someone is in a situation that they only have one knife, they can use neitzah and use the knife.
   Mahashal: You can only do neitzah on a knife if it has been made assur.
   In short, it seems that one should have separate meat and milk knives. If someone is in a situation that they only have one knife then they may do neitzah to use the knife for the other one. (sif 4 and Shach 22)
4. One may use a meat (dairy) knife to cut bread that they will eat with dairy (meat). They just have to make sure the knife is clean (meaning, even without neitzah). As worst this is only nat bar nat (blios from the food into the knife and from the knife into the bread). And it isn't even that because blios only transfer with knives when there is a davar charif. (Taz 6)
