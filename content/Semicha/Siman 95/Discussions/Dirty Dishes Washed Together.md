# Basic Rules
1. If the dishes are בן יומו, בליעות leaves from the dishes and can infect the ממשות. This is not נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא. The ממשות may become אסור and then release בליעות that can אסור the dishes.
2. If a dish is not a בן יומו, it will not release בליעות.
# Applications
## Case 1
1. One dish is a בן יומו and the other one is dirty. (The הלכות is the same if both dishes were בן יומו.)
2. For example, the dairy dish is a בן יומו, and there is a bit of meat on the meat dish.
### הלכה
1. Both dishes becomes אסור.
2. The reason is because the בליעות leave the בן יומו dish and infect the ממשות. Since this is not נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא, the ממשות becomes אסור.
3. The בליעות from the ממשות is אסור and it now makes both dishes אסור.
## Case 2
1. One dish is dirty. The other dish is clean and not a בן יומו.
2. For example, the there is a bit of dairy on the dairy dish and the meat dish is clean and not a בן יומו.
### הלכה
1. The clean dish becomes אסור (the meat dish in the example) and the dirty dish remains מותר (the dairy dish in the example).
2. The reason the clean dish becomes אסור is because the בליעות from the ממשות makes the non-בן יומו dish אסור.
3. However, the בליעות from the non-בן יומו dish can't affect the ממשות. As such, the ממשות remain מותר and since they are the same type as the dish they are on (meaning, in the example, it is dairy ממשות on a dairy dish), there is no reason for the dish to become אסור.
