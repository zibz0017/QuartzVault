# נ"ט בר נ"ט
## נ"ט בר נ"ט דאיסורא
### Basic Case
1. Someone cooks a parve food in a בן יומו pot that was used for איסור.
2. Additionally, someone takes this parve food and cooks that in another pot. They then cook another parve food and cook it in that pot. The הלכה is the same no matter how many times someone keeps going with this.
### הלכה
1. The parve food becomes totally אסור. It makes the next food that it is put in אסור (if isn't מבוטל בששים). If that next food is put in another pot, that pot becomes אסור. And it keeps going from there.[^1]
## נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא
### The Pot is a בן יומו
#### Eating the Food with Its opposite
##### Case 1
1. Someone wants to eat a parve food with dairy/meat. They want to cook it *in a בן יומו* pot of its opposite.
###### שיטות
1. מחבר (according to the ש"ך), רמ"א: One *may not* do this.[^2]
2. מחבר (according to Rav Ovadia): One *may* do this. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need source</span>
##### Case 2
1. Someone has already cooked a parve food *in a בן יומו* dairy/meat pot. They *would like to* mix it with the opposite food and eat it.
###### שיטות
1. מחבר: One *may* do this.[^2]
2. רמ"א: One *may not* do this.[^2]
##### Case 3
1. Someone has already cooked a parve food *in a בן יומו* dairy/meat pot and they *have already* mixed it together with food of its opposite. They would like to eat it.
###### הלכה
1. מחבר, רמ"א: One *may* do this.[^2]
2. פתחי תשובה, מהרש"ל, ש"ך: *If one cooked it* in the pot, this is מותר. See [[Difference Between Roasting and Cooking by נ''ט בר נ''ט|here]] for the הלכה if one did not, and the reason for the difference.[^3][^4]
#### Putting the Food on a בן יומו Dish of Its opposite
##### Case 4
1. Someone *wants to* cook a parve food in a dairy/meat pot and then put it on a בן יומו dish of its opposite while it is hot.
###### הלכה
1. One *may not* do this.[^5]
##### Case 5
1. Someone *has already* cooked a parve food in a meat/dairy pot. They now want to put it on a בן יומו dish of it opposite while it is hot. (The הלכה is the same whether the dish is a בן יומו and/or the food is hot.)
###### הלכה
1. One *may* do this.[^5] However, if it is a liquid, this is subject to the הלכות of [[#Pouring Hot Water from a Meat/Dairy Dish onto Its opposite]]. (If it is solid then you obviously are not pouring it.)
#### Putting Food on a בן יומו Dish and Eating it with it opposite
##### Case 6
1. Someone wants to eat a parve food with meat/dairy. They *want to put it* while it's hot on a בן יומו plate of its opposite.
###### הלכה
1. One *may not* do this.[^6]
##### Case 7
1. Someone wants to eat a parve food with meat/dairy. They *have already put it* while it's hot on a בן יומו plate of its opposite.
###### הלכה
1. One *may* do this.[^6]
#### Dunking Dishes in Hot Water Together
##### General Rule
1. This is only for cases in a כלי ראשון when both dishes were in the water at the same time. If the water was in a כלי שיני or the dishes were not put in at the same time (even if they were put in back to back), everything is fine according to everyone.[^7]
##### Dirty Dishes
1. If the dishes were dirty, the water needs to be 60x the amount of the residue. If it is not, both dishes may become אסור. For details, see [[Dirty Dishes Washed Together|here]].
##### Clean Dishes
1. מחבר: Everything is מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^7]
2. רמ"א: If both dishes are בן יומו, all of the dishes are אסור. If one of the dishes were not, all of the dishes are מותר. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Perhaps should be divided and the הלכה about non-בן יומו should be moved to the non-בן יומו section?</span>
   In either case, the water is אסור.
   For why all of this is, see [[רמ''א on Clean Dishes Washed Together|here]].
#### Pouring Hot Water from a Meat/Dairy Dish onto Its opposite
##### Case 8
1. Someone pours from a hot כלי ראשון that is a בן יומו onto a בן יומו dish of the opposite type.
###### הלכה
1. רמ"א<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>:[^8] The dish becomes אסור. It is as if the person put the dish into the כלי ראשון.
2. ש"ך, ת"ח: In a case of הפסד מרובה or במקום צריך, one may use the dish. For the reason, see [[סתירה in רמ''א סעיף ב and סעיף ג#Application to Pouring|here]].
(The רמ"א is seemingly in סתירה to what he says in the ת"ח.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^9])
3. According to all opinions, the top pot is fine.[^10]
##### Case 9
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Perhaps should be moved to the non-בן יומו section?</span>
1. Same as the previous case but one of the כלים were not בן יומו.
###### הלכה
1. Everything is מותר, the same way that when we dunk כלים into hot water, if one of them is not a בן יומו, everything is מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^11]
#### Pouring Hot Water onto Dairy and Meat Dishes
##### Case 10
1. Someone pours כלי ראשון water from a parve כלי onto dairy and meat dishes.
###### הלכה
1. רמ"א: Everything is מותר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^7]
2. ש"ך: At best, one should be מחמיר.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^12]
For more details about the שיטה of the ש"ך and the מחלוקת in general, see [[Hot Water onto Meat and Dairy Dishes|here]].
### The Pot is not a בן יומו
#### Case 11
1. Someone *wants* to eat a parve food with dairy/meat. They want to cook it *in a non-בן יומו* pot of its opposite.
##### הלכה
1. If the food is not a דבר חריף, this is fine. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need source</span>
2. If it is a דבר חריף, and for some details about what is considered a דבר חריף, see [[דבר חריף|here]].
# Fish Cooked in a Meat/Dairy בן יומו Pan
1. There is no problem of סכנה by fish with meat/dairy בליעות<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>.[^13]
# Eggs in Their Shells
1. Eggs in their shells are the same as any other food. The shell doesn't provide any sort of protection.[^14]
# Something Disgusting Falls into the Pot
1. There is a מחלוקת between the מחבר, ש"ך, ט"ז, פתחי תשובה, and perhaps the רמ"א. For details see [[מחלוקת about Something Disgusting that Falls into a Pot|here]].
## Applications
1. Soap can potentially be נותן טעם לפגם. As such, if meat and dairy dishes are washed together, if there is soap there, we can be מצטרף this into being מקל.
# Fish in a בן יומו Meat Pan
1. he מהרש"ל[^15] and the Chochmas אדם (Chassidishe posek) <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need source</span> says that one cannot cook fish in a בן יומו meat pan, because of סכנה.
2. However, הלכה למעשה one may do this.[^16] (Perhaps not so for chassidim.)
