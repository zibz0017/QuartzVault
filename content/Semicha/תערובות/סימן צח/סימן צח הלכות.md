# Taste of חֵלֶב Vs Meat
1. חֵלֶב and meat have different tastes.[^1]
# Relying on a גוי for a Taste Being מבוטל
1. מחבר: If the food that fell in is removed quickly and a גוי tastes the food and doesn't taste the food that fell in, it is fine. For details about how to ask the גוי, see [[Asking the גוי משיח לפי תומו|here]].[^2]
   If a גוי is available and it the איסור can be tasted (excluding cases like מין במינו), one must ask a גוי.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3]
   If the גוי says that he tastes the איסור, the food is אסור, even if it is more than 60x the איסור.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3]
2. רמ"א: We can't ever rely on a גוי.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^4]
# בטל דאורייתא Vs דרבנן

## לח בלח
*It does not explicitly say that the case is referring to לח בלח, but I spoke to some people and that is the way they are learning it.*
### מין במינו
1. מדאוריתא: <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>בטל ברוב.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^5]
2. מדרבנן: <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>בטל בששים.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^5]
### מין בשאינו מינו
1. בטל בששים.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^5]
### Application to Cases of ספק
1. Some of the food falls out so we don't know the ratio of איסור to התר. For further discussion about what is considered to be a ספק, see [[What Counts as a Case of ספק|here]].
#### מין במינו
1. If you know that there was at least רוב התר, the food is מותר even if you don't know if there is 60x. Since there is רוב the food is מותר מדאוריתא. We only have a ספק if the food is מותר מדרבנן. As such we say ספק דרבנן לקולא.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^6]
2. If you don't even know if רוב was התר, the food is אסור because it is a ספק דאורייתא לחומרא.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^6]
#### מין בשאינו מינו
1. Since even מדאוריתא we need ששים, the food is אסור if we are unsure if there was ששים because this is ספק דאורייתא לחומרא.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^6]
#### מין במינו ושאינו מינו
##### Case
1. You have two kosher foods of different מינים in a pot. Then an אסור food falls in that is the same מין as one of the foods, but not the other.
##### הלכה
1. We only look at the מין במינו aspect and ignore the מין בשאינו מינו aspect. As such, we treat this case like מין במינו, so if we know that there was more of the התר food of the same מין as the אסור food but we don't know if there was ששים, the food is מותר.
   If we don't even know if there was more of the התר food of the same מין as the איסור food then the food is אסור.
   For example, one cooks cow meat and chicken in the same pot and some נבלה cow meat falls in. If we know that there was more kosher meat than נבלה meat and we are מספק if there was ששים, the food is מותר.
   If we don't even know if there was more kosher meat than נבלה meat, the food is אסור.
   We don't count the chicken in any of these cheshbonos.
# איסור Cooked with התר
1. If איסור falls into התר (or dairy/meat into its opposite) one needs 60x times the איסור (or dairy/meat) to be מבטל it. This is even if the איסור (dairy/meat) is removed.
2. One must remove איסור cooked with התר, even if it is מבוטל.
