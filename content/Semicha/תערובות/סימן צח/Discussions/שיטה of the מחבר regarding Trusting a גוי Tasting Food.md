# All Opinions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We can rely on an expert, even for איסורי דאורייתא, without asking him משיח לפי תומו. This is because a professional won't lie because it may hurt his reputation.</span>
# &lrm;שיטה Of the ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A regular גוי may be trusted if asked משיח לפי תומו, even for איסורי דאורייתא since we can fact-check wehether the גוי was lying. (For more details, see [[Asking the גוי משיח לפי תומו#&lrm;ש"ך|here]].)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In fact, if a גוי is available, the גוי must be asked about whether he can taste the איסור. We can't rely on the fact that there is 60x.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We listen to the גוי to אסור the food (even if the איסור is less than 1/60th) and the be מתיר (even if it is more than 1/60th).</span>
