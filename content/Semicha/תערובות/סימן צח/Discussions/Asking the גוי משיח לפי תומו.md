*Presented by מחבר סעיף א, ש"ך ס"ק ב, ט"ז ס"ק ב*
# הלכה
1. According to some, the מחבר holds that one may ask a regular גוי about the taste of a food, but only if the גוי is asked משיח לפי תומו.
# Question
1. מין בשאינו מינו is an איסור דאורייתא, and it is clear from Shas that one can't rely on a גוי, even using משיח לפי תומו.
# Answers
## ש"ך
1. We only said that we can't trust a גוי's testimony in cases where there is no way to check and see if the גוי is right. In this case, however, in theory, we can ask a non-Jewish chef (who we are are allowed to trust) about the food's taste. Since we can do that, we can rely on the גוי if he is asked משיח לפי תומו.
## ט"ז
*הלכה למעשה, the ט"ז seems to hold that one can't rely on a asking a גוי משיח לפי תומו for איסורי דאורייתא.*
1. The בית יוסף relies on the שיטות that hold that טעם כעיקר is only דרבנן (even though in general the בית יוסף doesn't hold of that). ^5def80
2. This is not a case of "halachic עדות".
