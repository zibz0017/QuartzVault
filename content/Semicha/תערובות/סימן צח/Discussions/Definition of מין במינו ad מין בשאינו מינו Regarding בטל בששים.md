# רמ"א
1. מין במינו is about the *name* of the food. Foods that have the same name are considered the same מין and foods that have different names are different מינים.
# ש"ך
1. מין במינו is about the *taste* of the food. Foods that have the same taste are considered the same מין and foods that have different tastes are different מינים.
2. The ש"ך brings numerous proofs against the רמ"א.
