# Where This התר Does not Apply
1. According to those that say that there is חנ"ן by שאר איסורים (see [[סימן צב הלכות#חנ"ן#רמ"א|here]]), if one of the איסורים fall in (creating a mixture with 1 part איסור and 59 parts התר) and then the other איסור falls in, the mixture has already become חנ"ן when the first איסור fell in. As such we would not say בטל בששים when the second איסור falls in, even though now there is 60x the amount of each איסור in the pot.
# Where This התר Does Apply
## לח בלח by הפסד מרובה
1. By a case of לח בלח, even according to the שיטה mentioned before, we do not say that this is חנ"ן in a case of הפסד מרובה.
## Fell in at the Same Time
1. If both איסורים fell in at the same time the food would not become חנ"ן at all, and both איסורים would therefore be able to be מבוטל.
## Milk and Meat
1. If one part of milk fell into 29 parts of parve food and one part meat fell into 30 parts of parve food and then these mixtures were combined together. The mixtures on their own don't become חנ"ן because milk and meat on their own can't make something into חנ"ן. Once they are mixed together, they are מבוטל.
## A Little More than a 60th
1. One part of one איסור falls into 60 parts of התר. This איסור is מבוטל. Then, another איסור which is slightly more than one part (it is one part and 1/60th, to be exact) falls into the mixture which is now 61 parts (60 parts התר and 1 part of the first איסור). In this case we would say that the first איסור can combine with the התר to be מבטל the second איסור.
