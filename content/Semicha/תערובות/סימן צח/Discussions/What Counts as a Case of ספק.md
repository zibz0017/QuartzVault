# הלכה
1. We only consider a case a ספק if some of the food falls out and so we don't know the amount of food that is there.
2. If some foods falls in and none falls out but we just don't know if it the התר is 60x the איסור that fell in (in dairy/meat is 60x more than the opposite type of food that fell in) this is not considered to be a ספק. As such, one can't be מקל, even by a ספק דרבנן.
