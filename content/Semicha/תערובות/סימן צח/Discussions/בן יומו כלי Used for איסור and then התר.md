# One Knows how Many בליעות Are in the כלי
## בב״ח
1. One has a non-בן יומו כלי that they use for איסור (or a non-בן יומו כלי that was used for meat and dairy and the person knows how much meat and dairy it was used for), and then it is used for some other food
### הלכה
1. בית יוסף: One needs 60x the איסור.
2. רמ"א: By cheres, one should be מחמיר by like the מרדכי who says that a כלי that can't be kashered (a כלי cheres) can itself become חנ"ן. As such, even by non-בן יומו כלים, one needs 60x the amount of the whole כלי. In a case of הפסד מרובה, one can be מקל.
The הלכה is the same according to the רמ"א and בית יוסף by other materials. ^110e1b
## שאר איסורים
1. One used a כלי for meat/dairy and then used it for the opposite. Then it was used for the same thing that it was used for originally. For example, a כלי was used for 1 kizayis of meat, then used for 1 kizayis of milk, and then it was used for more meat.
### הלכה
1. There are two aspects to this הלכה. First we need to ascertain if there is חנ"ן in the כלי. In addition, we need to discuss what the הלכה is about the שיטה of the מרדכי.
#### חנ"ן In the כלי
1. Most opinions: One needs 60x the meat and dairy (in the example given, one would need 120 kizaysim).
2. Some opinions: One would only need 60x the amount of the opposite (in the example given, one would only need 60x the milk, or 60 kizaysim). The reason for this opinion is that we don't say חנ"ן in כלים.
   We do not פסקען like this opinion.
#### שיטה of the מרדכי
1. בית יוסף: One needs 60x the איסור.
2. רמ"א: One should be מחמיר by cheres and so one needs 60x the amount of the whole כלי (like the מרדכי, discussed [[#^110e1b|above]]). In this case, we are not מקל, even in a case of הפסד מרובה.
   The הלכה is the same according to the רמ"א and בית יוסף by other materials.
# One Doesn't Know how Many בליעות Are in the כלי
## בב״ח
1. If the כלי was used for a lot of meat/dairy and the person doesn't know how much and then it was used for some amount of its opposite, and then it is used for some other food.
### הלכה
1. All opinions: one needs 60x the כלי.
## שאר איסורים
1. Similar to the previous case, a כלי was used for a lot of התר and the person doesn't know how much and it was then used for some amount of איסור, and then it was used for some other food.
### הלכה
1. מחבר: As discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#חנ"ן#מחבר|here]], the מחבר holds that we only say חנ"ן by בב״ח, not by other איסורים. As such, one would only need 60x the amount of the איסור.
2. רמ"א: As discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#חנ"ן#רמ"א|here]], the רמ"א hold that we say חנ"ן, even by שאר איסורים. As such, there would be no difference between שאר איסורים and בב״ח.
3. ט"ז: We do not say that there is חנ"ן in the בליעות in a כלי by שאר איסורים. (He agrees to the הלכה of the מחבר, but for a different reason than the מחבר.)
