# Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person sees a certain injury in the head of the animal that makes the animal a טרפה but the body has already been mixed with other animals in which case they would all be אסור because of חתיכה הראויה להתכבד unless the person can recognize which is the אסור animal.</span>
# &lrm;הלכה

1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר (and presumably the רמ"א): The person may see try and see which neck the head "fits" on (or some other similar methods) to find the אסור animal and pull that animal out, making the rest kosher.</span>
   In each situation the person has to use whatever clues are available to try and figure it out. For example, the person needs to use the fit of the neck and head, as well as matching the pattern of the skin if it is possible.
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ב"ח, או"ה: We need extremely clear signs. The examples given above are not good enough. (The ב"ח gives an example where there is fluid found in the brain, the person needs to look for this fluid in the spine.)</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: We can't ever rely on these types of signs.</span>
