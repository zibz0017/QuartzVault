# Basic הלכה
1. If the food hasn't been finished being prepared yet, the food cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד and so it can be מבוטל.
2. Even if the food will be prepared before it is eaten, once it has been מבוטל in the mixture, it does not get "reawakened".
3. There is a מחלוקת about what is considered "not prepared yet".
# Basic שיטות
1. According to some, if a food is missing a step in its preparation, it cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.
2. According to others, the whole idea of חתיכה הראויה להתכבד is about the size of the food once it is cooked, not about whether it would be served to someone honorable in its present state. As such, according to this opinion, even if the food is missing a step in its preparation, it can still be considered a  חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.
   However, if it is missing a major step, even this שיטה would agree that it cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.
# Examples
## Things that Disqualify the Food According to the First שיטה
1. The food is not cooked.
2. The food has not been salted.
3. The food has not been cut up into portion-sized pieces.
## Things that Disqualify the Food According to both שיטות
1. The feathers haven't been removed.
2. The hair hasn't been removed.
3. The skin hasn't been removed.
# How We פסקען
1. The מחבר brings down both שיטות of this מחלוקת.
2. The רמ"א says that we פסקען like the second שיטה.
