# Basic הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food hasn't been finished being prepared yet, the food cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד and so it can be מבוטל.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the food will be prepared before it is eaten, once it has been מבוטל in the mixture, it does not get "reawakened".</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about what is considered "not prepared yet".</span>
# Basic שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to some, if a food is missing a step in its preparation, it cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to others, the whole idea of חתיכה הראויה להתכבד is about the size of the food once it is cooked, not about whether it would be served to someone honorable in its present state. As such, according to this opinion, even if the food is missing a step in its preparation, it can still be considered a  חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.</span>
   However, if it is missing a major step, even this שיטה would agree that it cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.
# Examples
## Things that Disqualify the Food According to the First שיטה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is not cooked.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food has not been salted.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food has not been cut up into portion-sized pieces.</span>
## Things that Disqualify the Food According to both שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The feathers haven't been removed.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The hair hasn't been removed.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The skin hasn't been removed.</span>
# How We פסקען
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר brings down both שיטות of this מחלוקת.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א says that we פסקען like the second שיטה.</span>
