# Basic הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food hasn't been finished being prepared yet, the food cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד and so it can be מבוטל.&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the food will be prepared before it is eaten, once it has been מבוטל in the mixture, it does not get "reawakened".[^2]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about what is considered "not prepared yet".</span>
# Basic שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to some, if a food is missing a step in its preparation, it cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to others, the whole idea of חתיכה הראויה להתכבד is about a minimum size: the food must be large enough to serve before an honorable person not about whether it is honorable in its present state. As such, according to this opinion, even if the food is missing steps in its preparation, it can still be considered a  חתיכה הראויה להתכבד. Technically, according to this שיטה, even missing a major step would not preclude this from becoming a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.
   However, הלכה למעשה, the רמ"א (who holds like this שיטה) agrees that if it is missing a major step that it cannot be considered a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד.&lrm;[^3]</span>
# Examples
## Things that Disqualify the Food According to the First שיטה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is not cooked.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food has not been salted.[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food has not been cut up into portion-sized pieces.[^1]</span>
## Things that Disqualify the Food According to the רמ"א (And the First שיטה)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The feathers haven't been removed.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The hair hasn't been removed.[^4]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The skin hasn't been removed.[^5]</span>
# How We פסקען
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר brings down the first שיטה and ends off with "ויש חולקים".&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א has already been discussed.&lrm;[^4]</span>
## &lrm;דבר חשוב and דבר שבמנין
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the ש"ך, according to the רמ"א, just because something is missing preparation, it may still be a דבר שבמנין. For example, a sheep that has been slaughtered but has not been skinned or sheared is not a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד, but is a דבר שבמנין. Missing steps in its preparation does not make a difference regarding דבר שבמנין.
   However, we do not פסקען like this שיטה.&lrm;[^6]</span>
