# Meat and Honey
## Meat without Spices
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: Honey and meat without spices is considered נותן טעם לפגם.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: There is a מחלוקת about whether honey and meat is considered נותן טעם לפגם and so one should be מחמיר where there is no הפסד גדול.</span>
   There is a drink that comes from honey and the is נותן טעם לפגם from meat, so that would be totally מותר.
## Meat with Spices
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בית יוסף: This is not נותן טעם לפגם.</span>
# Meat and Oil
