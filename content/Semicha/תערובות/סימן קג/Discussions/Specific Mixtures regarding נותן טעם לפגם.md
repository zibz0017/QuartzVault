# Meat and Honey
## Meat without Spices
1. מחבר: Honey and meat without spices is considered נותן טעם לפגם.
2. רמ"א: There is a מחלוקת about whether honey and meat is considered נותן טעם לפגם and so one should be מחמיר where there is no הפסד גדול.
   There is a drink that comes from honey and the is נותן טעם לפגם from meat, so that would be totally מותר.
## Meat with Spices
1. בית יוסף: This is not נותן טעם לפגם.
# Meat and Oil
1. מחבר, רמ"א: It is considered נותן טעם לפגם.
2. ש"ך: It is not considered נותן טעם לפגם.
# Wine and Meat of Milk
1. This is considered נותן טעם לפגם.
