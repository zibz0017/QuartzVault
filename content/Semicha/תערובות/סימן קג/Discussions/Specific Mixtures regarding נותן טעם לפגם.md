# Meat and Honey
## Meat without Spices
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: Honey and meat without spices is considered נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: There is a מחלוקת about whether honey and meat is considered נותן טעם לפגם and so one should be מחמיר where there is no הפסד גדול.&lrm;[^2]
   There is a drink that comes from honey and the is נותן טעם לפגם from meat, so that would be totally מותר.&lrm;[^2]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: Meat with honey is not נותן טעם לפגם and so one can't be מקל, even in a case of הפסד גדול.&lrm;[^3]</span>
## Meat with Spices
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בית יוסף: This is not נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^3]</span>
# Meat and Oil
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, רמ"א: It is considered נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: It is not considered נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^5]</span>
# Wine and Meat or Milk
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is considered נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^5]</span>
