# &lrm;הלכה about an Unwashed Pot
## Food in Pot is not 60x the ממשות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A little bit of ממשות are left on a pot and after 24 hours, it used to cook another food (either there was a little איסור left in the pot and it is used for some התר food, or there is some meat/dairy ממשות and the pot is used for the opposite).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food in the pot is not 60x the ממשות that were left there.</span>
### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is מחלוקת about whether we consider the ממשות to be נותן טעם לפגם or not (even we usually do not say that food becomes נותן טעם לפגם after 24 hours).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the טור, מחבר, רמ"א, מהרש"ל, and ש"ך, we פסקען like those who say that it is not נותן טעם לפגם.</span>
## Food in Pot is 60x ממשות
### &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: If the food in the pot is 60x the ממשות in the pot, the food is מותר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: Because there are ממשות in the pot that are not נותן טעם לפגם, this keeps the בליעות in the pot from becoming פגום, as well. As such, the food needs to be 60x the ממשות and the בליעות.</span>
