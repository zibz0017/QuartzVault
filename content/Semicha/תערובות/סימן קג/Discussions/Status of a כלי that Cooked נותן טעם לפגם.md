# &lrm;אסור Food that is נותן טעם לפגם Fell in
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A food that is נותן טעם לפגם with whatever is cooking in a pot falls into a pot. (The food is fine as it is נותן טעם לפגם.) Within 24 hours, another food is cooked in this pot, and the flavor from the אסור food is not נותן טעם לפגם with this food.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The אסור flavor that has been absorbed into the כלי can אסור this next food that is being cooked.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For this next food to be מותר, it needs to be 60x the original איסור, or 60x the walls of the pot, whichever is less.
   The reason is because the איסור can't make the בליעות in the wall חנ"ן because it is נותן טעם לפגם regarding the בליעות that were in the walls. As such, the אסור בליעות are going to be capped by both the size of the original איסור, and by the size of the walls.[^2]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even though the בליעות start out as נותן טעם לפגם, because they end up tasting good, they אסור the food, as [[סימן קג הלכות#^c23a85|discussed]].[^3]</span>
# Ladle Used for נותן טעם לפגם and then for Another Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A כלי (like a ladle) was used for אסור food that is נותן טעם לפגם. It was then used for a food in a pot that these בליעות are נותן טעם לפגם with. (The food is fine as it is נותן טעם לפגם.) Within 24 hours, another food is used in the pot, and the flavor from the אסור food is not נותן טעם לפגם with this food.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is מותר.&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In this case, the flavor is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא. The flavor goes from the כלי into the food in the pot. This is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא because it is a case of נותן טעם לפגם and so the food is not אסור. As such, all steps (from the food into the כלי, from the כלי into the food, and from the food into the pot) are נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא. Now it can't אסור the next food.[^4]</span>
# Difference Regarding נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We only say that something is considered a "step down" in the potency of בליעות (in other words, נ"ט בר נ"ט), when there is a transfer of בליעות from food to כלי or כלי to food, but not food to food.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, when the איסור falls into the food (the first case), this is not considered נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא (from the אסור food to the מותר food in the pot and from the food in the pot to the walls of the כלי) because it is isn't considered נ"ט בר נ"ט at all (even though the food is מותר). The walls of the כלי are only considered a נ"ט.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When an אסור כלי is used (the second case), this is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא because there is a transfer of בליעות from the original food to the ladle, from the ladle to food and from food to a second pot. All of these are התר since the taste is נותן טעם לפגם in every step.</span>
# Ladle Used for *not* נותן טעם לפגם and then for Another Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Similar to the previous case, except the food in the original pot was not נותן טעם לפגם. A ladle is used with this אסור food and then it is used in a pot that the בליעות are נותן טעם לפגם with. The pot is then used for another food.</span>
## הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">From the fact that the רמ"א talked about a case where the original food was נותן טעם לפגם, it is clear that he would hold that if the food was not נותן טעם לפגם the food eventually cooked in the second pot would be אסור.&lrm;[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ט"ז disagrees and says that this case would be נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא, as well and so even in this case, the second food would be מותר.&lrm;[^5]</span>
