# &lrm;אסור Food that is נותן טעם לפגם Fell in
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A food that is נותן טעם לפגם with whatever is cooking in a pot falls into a pot. (The food is fine as it is נותן טעם לפגם.) Within 24 hours, another food is cooked in this pot, and the flavor from the אסור food is not נותן טעם לפגם with this food.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The אסור flavor that has been absorbed into the כלי can אסור this next food that is being cooked.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For the food being cooked in the pot to be מותר, it needs to be 60x the original איסור, or 60x the walls of the pot, whichever is less.</span>
   The reason is because the איסור can't make the בליעות in the wall חנ"ן because it is נותן טעם לפגם regarding the בליעות that were in the walls. As such, the אסור בליעות are going to be capped by the size of the original איסור, and by the size of the walls.
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Additionally, even though the בליעות start out as נותן טעם לפגם, because they end up tasting good, they אסור the food, as [[סימן קג הלכות#^c23a85|discussed]].</span>
# &lrm;כלי with אסור בליעות that Are נותן טעם לפגם Fell in
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A כלי (like a ladle) was used for אסור food. It was then used for a food in a pot that it the בליעות are נותן טעם לפגם with (The food is fine as it is נותן טעם לפגם.) Within 24 hours, another food is used in the pot, and the flavor from the אסור food is not נותן טעם לפגם with this food.</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The food is מותר.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In this case, the flavor is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא. The flavor goes from the כלי into the food in the pot. This is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא because it is a case of נותן טעם לפגם and so the food is not אסור. As such, both steps (from the כלי into the food and from the food into the pot) are נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא. Now it can't אסור the next food.</span>
# Difference Regarding נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We only say that something is considered a "step down" in the potency of בליעות (in other words, נ"ט בר נ"ט), when there is a transfer of בליעות from food to כלי or כלי to food, but not food to food.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, when the איסור falls into the food (the first case), this is not considered נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא because it is isn't considered נ"ט בר נ"ט at all (even though the food is מותר). The walls of the כלי are only considered a נ"ט.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When an אסור כלי is used (the second case), this is נ"ט בר נ"ט דהיתרא because there is a transfer of בליעות from a כלי to food and then from food to a כלי. Both of these are התר. The food is נותן טעם לפגם so that is התר and then we are transferring בליעות from a נותן טעם לפגם food to the walls of the כלי, which is also considered התר.</span>
