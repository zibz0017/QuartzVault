# &lrm;נותן טעם לפגם Basic הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If something אסור gives off a bad taste, it may not be able to אסור a mixture that it falls into, even if it is more than 1/60<sup>th</sup>. For the source, see [[Source for Why נותן טעם לפגם Cannot אסור a Mixture|here]].</span>
# Good on Its Own; Bad in a Mixture
## Standard case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This counts as נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^1]</span>
## &lrm;חשוב Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Examples of these are חתיכה הראויה להתכבד, בריה, and דבר שבמנין.&lrm;[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This does not count as נותן טעם לפגם and these cannot be מבוטל. This is because these foods are not ever מבוטל even if they don't give off taste, so if the food is fine, it won't be מבוטל.&lrm;[^3]</span>
# Requirements
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For details about bow bad the איסור needs to taste, see [[How Bad Does it have to Taste and Ratio of איסור to התר|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the mixture tastes good after the food was added at any point (it started off good and became bad or it started off bad and it became good), it does not count as נותן טעם לפגם and it can אסור the food (although if the food starts out bad, you may eat it before it becomes good).[^4]</span> ^c23a85
# Status of the כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Whether the כלי can אסור other foods depends on the exact parameters of the case. For details, see [[Status of a כלי that Cooked נותן טעם לפגם|here]].</span>
# Bad Taste Overpowered by other Tastes
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the bad taste is overpowered by other tastes, this is still considered נותן טעם לפגם and it מותר.&lrm;[^5]</span>
# Specific Examples
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Bad tasting איסור falls into a pot, but the dish still tastes fine because the taste of the איסור is overpowered by the taste of salt or spices in the pot. The הלכה is that this is still considered נותן טעם לפגם and is מותר.&lrm;[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is discussion about whether specific mixtures are considered נותן טעם לפגם. For details, see [[Specific Mixtures regarding נותן טעם לפגם|here]].</span>
# Non-בן יומו
## Basic הלכות of the Significance of Being Non-בן יומו
### &lrm;בליעות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בליעות in כלים that are not בן יומו are נותן טעם לפגם.&lrm;[^6]</span>
### Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Food is generally not נותן טעם לפגם even after 24 hours.[^6] However, according to some, ממשות on an unwashed pot may become נותן טעם לפגם. For details, see [[ממשות on an Unwashed Pot|here]].</span>

# &lrm;בליעות In a כלי Overnight
*Note: There is a מחלוקת about which בליעות count (only בליעות from בב״ח, or any איסור. For details about that discussion, see [[סימן צד הלכות#Cooking Something in a Pot|here]].*
## Background
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to some שיטות, &lrm;בליעות in כלים overnight become פגום. We do not פסקען like this שיטה, but we could rely on this in certain situations. There is a מחלוקת about when this applies.[^9]</span>
## &lrm;שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בית יוסף (according to the ש"ך): If a pot is used for בב״ח and left overnight, and in the morning it is used for something parve, the בן יומו timer does not reset. This pot is only considered בן יומו from when it was used for בב״ח.&lrm;[^9]</span>
   If the pot was used for בב״ח and then for something parve before the night, the בן יומו timer "resets" from when the parve food was cooked.[^9]
   Additionally, it is not clear what the בית יוסף holds about if someone cooks dairy/meat in a pot and left it overnight and then it was used for the opposite the next day.[^9]
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בית יוסף (according to the ת"ח), ת"ח: If a pot is used for בב״ח and then for something parve, whether it was left overnight before the parve thing was cooked or after, the בן יומו does not reset. בן יומו only counts from when the בב״ח was cooked.[^9]</span>
