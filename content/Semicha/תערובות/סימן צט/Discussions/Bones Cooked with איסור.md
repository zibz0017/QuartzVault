# &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the bones were already cooked with אסור meat, we no longer count the bones as התר if it falls into something else.</span>
# &lrm;בה"ט
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Implied question) By counting the bones as איסור, we are "double-counting" the איסור. If we started with 10 ounces of איסור, cooking it with the bones can't count make us have more איסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Answer) We are concerned that the meat was originally larger and it shrunk. For example, we may have started with 12 ounces of אסור meat, some of it was absorbed into the bone, and the meat shrunk to 10 ounces. In this case, though, there is 12 ounces of איסור because 2 ounces of meat were absorbed into the bones (assuming that the bones were at least 2 ounces).</span>
   As such, we פסקען that we treat the bones as איסור.
