# Enough התר Mixed to Be מבטל the איסור in One Shot
1. If התר that was 60x the amount of איסור was mixed with the איסור in one shot, according to all opinions, we obviously don't say חנ"ן here and so the איסור is מבוטל.
# Some התר Was Mixed with איסור and then More התר Was Mixed
## יבש ביבש
1. According to all opinions, we do not say that there is חנ"ן here and so the איסור would be מבוטל if you had 60x the איסור.
## לח בלח
### בב״ח
1. According to all opinions, there is חנ"ן here and so you would 60x the amount of the איסור and the first amount of התר.
### שאר איסורים
1. רמ"א: This is חנ"ן and so once again you would need 60x the amount of איסור and the first amount of התר. However, in a case of הפסד מרובה, we do not say חנ"ן so in such a case we would say you only need 60x the amount of איסור.
2. מחבר: This is not חנ"ן and so you would only need 60x the amount of the איסור.
# Alternate שיטה
1. We do not פסקען like this שיטה but it is brought down.
## Person Didn't Realize by איסור that Creates חנ"ן
1. The person accidentally mixed איסור with some התר (that usually creates חנ"ן), then accidentally added more התר (enough to be מבטל the איסור), and then at that point he finally realized.
### הלכה
1. According to this שיטה, we do not say חנ"ן. We allow all of the התר to go and be מבטל the איסור.
## Person Realized by איסור that Does not Create חנ"ן
1. The person accidentally mixed איסור with some התר (that usually does not create חנ"ן), he then realized what happened, and then he accidentally added more התר (enough to be מבטל the איסור).
### הלכה
1. According to this שיטה, we say חנ"ן here. Even though we are talking about the type of איסור that does not usually create חנ"ן, we knas the person shogeig ATU meizid.
