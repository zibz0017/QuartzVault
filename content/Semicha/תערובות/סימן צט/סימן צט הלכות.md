# What Counts toward the התר and the איסור
## Bones
### From איסור or התר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: The bones count toward התר (even from a non-kosher animal). This is because bones are not edible. However, they are able to absorb taste when they are cooked with food.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: Bones of an אסור animal don't count either way except in a case of הפסד (in which case they can count toward התר).[^2]
   Bones of a מותר animal count toward התר.&lrm;[^2]</span>
### Hardness and Dryness
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">או"ה, ש"ך: Only hard, dry bones can count toward התר. Moist, soft bones count toward איסור because they emit moisture.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ש"ך implies that the מחבר and potentially the הג"ה hold that all bones count toward התר.&lrm;[^3] The ט"ז implies that he himself agrees with this.[^4]</span>
### Have not Been Cooked yet
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the bones have already been cooked with the איסור meat, according to all opinions, we would count the bones as איסור as it has absorbed בליעות and now it is releasing them.[^5] For further discussion, see [[Bones Cooked with איסור|here]].</span>
## Marrow
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, the marrow from an אסור animal counts toward איסור.&lrm;[^6]</span>
## Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pot doesn't count toward the התר or the איסור.&lrm;[^6] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to ט"ז ס"ק ב and סעיף ד with the nosei כלים</span></span>
## &lrm;מותר Food that Absorbed איסור
### Basic הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If food that absorbed איסור becomes חנ"ן, the food counts toward the איסור. If not, it counts as part of the התר. Whether we say that the food becomes חנ"ן is subject to the מחלוקת here [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;חנ"ן|here]].[^7]</span>
### Application
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, if the food has absorbed איסור, the מחבר says that it is not חנ"ן (as discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;חנ"ן#מחבר|here]]) and so it counts toward התר, and the רמ"א says that it is חנ"ן (as discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;חנ"ן#רמ"א|here]]) and so it counts toward איסור.&lrm;[^7]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is dairy/meat and has absorbed its opposite according to all opinions this is חנ"ן and so the it counts toward איסור.&lrm;[^7]</span>
# &lrm;מבטל איסור לכתחילה
## &lrm;איסורי דאורייתא
### May One Do it
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not be מבטל an איסור לכתחילה. This means that one can't put איסור into 60x the amount of התר, or even if איסור and התר are already mixed, one may not add additional התר to be מבטל the איסור.&lrm;[^8]</span>
### &lrm;אסור דאורייתא or דרבנן
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רשב"א, ר"ן: &lrm;אסור דרבנן.&lrm;[^9]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ראב"ד: &lrm;אסור דאורייתא.&lrm;[^9]</span>
## &lrm;איסורי דרבנן
### May One Do it
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: It depends on the case. One may not add an איסור דרבנן to enough התר to be מבטל it לכתחילה. However, if איסור דרבנן has already mixed with התר, but not enough to be מבטל it, one may add more התר to be מבטל it.[^10]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א, ש"ך: One may not be מבטל an איסור דרבנן, even if it has already mixed with התר. There is no difference between איסורי דרבנן and איסורי דאורייתא.&lrm;[^11]</span>
## Both איסורי דאורייתא and דרבנן
### If One Did it Anyway
*Note: These הלכות are taking the assumption that there is no חנ"ן here. For further details about whether there is חנ"ן or not, see [[Applying חנ''ן by a מבטל איסור לכתחילה|here]]. Also, these הלכות are referring to cases where it was אסור to be מבטל. As noted, that will depend on whether the איסור was דאורייתא or דרבנן.*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it was done by accident, it is מותר to eat.[^12]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one was מבטל it on purpose then the owner of the food and who the person that he was מבטל it for can't eat it or sell it to a jew (for more details about selling it, see [[Selling איסור that has been Purposely מבוטל|here]]). Anyone else can eat it (including this person who was מבטל it, if he wasn't the owner and he didn't do it for himself).[^13]</span>
   The reason the רבנן made the food אסור for the person that the food was מבטל on behalf of is because we don't want people asking a non-Jew to be מבטל something on their behalf.[^14] ^84e5fb
# "Reawakening" איסור that Has Been מבוטל &lrm;(חוזר וניעור)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If איסור has been מבוטל in a mixture, if you add more of the same איסור to the mixture, it "reawakens" the מבוטל איסור and if there is enough איסור between what was originally there and what was added to not be מבוטל, the mixture becomes אסור.&lrm;[^15] There is a מחלוקת about where this applies:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א, מהרש"ל: This is true by all mixtures (לח בלח, יבש ביבש, מין במינו, מין בשאינו מינו, you realized before adding the new איסור or you didn't).[^16]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: One can be מקל by a mixture of יבש ביבש, מין במינו if one realized between. (The ש"ך himself feels that one can be מקל in this case even when it is not a case of הפסד מרובה, but he doesn't want to פסקען that way הלכה למעשה against the many שיטות who are against him.)[^17]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As noted [[סימן צח הלכות#Multiple איסורים|elsewhere]], multiple איסורים will be מבטל each other, not combine together. As such, if a different איסור was added, that will not reawaken this איסור.&lrm;[^18]
   See [[סימן קא הלכות#^c6558d|here]] for another case where we do not say חוזר וניעור.</span>
# &lrm;כלי That Has Absorbed איסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a כלי has absorbed איסור, if the person always uses the כלי for large amounts, the person may continue to use the כלי, לכתחילה, as there is no concern that the איסור will enter into the food that he is using in it (because it will be מבוטל).&lrm;[^19]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if the person sometimes uses it for smaller amounts, the person may not use it לכתחילה, even for larger amounts, because we are concerned that sometimes he may not use it for a large amount and there will be איסור in his food.[^19]</span>
