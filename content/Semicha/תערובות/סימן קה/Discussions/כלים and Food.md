1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">איסור was put onto a כלי so it absorbed בליעות. Later, מותר food was put on the כלי. How much of the מותר food becomes אסור is subject to מחלוקת and depends on the case, as will be discussed.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are a few main factors that determine the הלכה:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The fattiness of the foods.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Whether the מותר food was wet (or at least moist).</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Was the כלי hot when the מותר food was placed on it or just the food.</span>
# Wet or Dry
## Wet מותר Food is Put on the כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A כלי absorbed אסור בליעות and someone put wet מותר food onto the כלי when it is hot.</span>
### הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות have potential to transfer through the whole food and make the whole thing אסור, even if the איסור was lean.[^1]</span>
## Moist מותר Food is Put on the כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A כלי absorbed אסור בליעות and someone put slightly moist מותר food onto the כלי when it is hot.</span>
### הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: We view this the same as we view מותר food that absorbed בליעות and another מותר food with liquid was placed on it.
   According to the מחבר, if either food was fatty, the מותר food would have potential to become totally אסור. (If the איסור was fatty, the fatty בליעות would spread through the מותר food, and if the התר was fatty, it would make the בליעות in the כלי fatty and cause them to be able to spread through the מותר piece.) If neither food was fatty, the מותר food would become אסור כדי נטילה (like we say when two lean pieces touch each other).[^2]</span>

   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the רמ"א the מותר food would have potential to become totally אסור even if neither piece is fatty.[^2]</span>
   
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">The exact reasoning is not clear to me. The בדי השלחן ס"ק קיא here says that reason is because the we view the איסור as fatty (consistent with the regular שיטה of the רמ"א) and so the בליעות can transfer completely through the next piece (as we said according the מחבר when we know that the piece is fatty). Even though we usually say that we are בקיאין between lean and fatty pieces when they are absorbed in another piece (see [[בליעות Spreading from Piece to Piece#Both Pieces Are not Fatty|here]]), for some reason, we don't say that here.
   However, the פרי מגדים שפתי דעת ס"ק כג seems to say that the reason is because we view the *התר* as fatty and so it can be מפטם the אסור בליעות and cause them to spread totally through the מותר piece.</span></span> ^749f21
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: We do not say that the מותר food can make the בליעות fatty and cause them to spread through the מותר piece. As such, according to all opinions, if the איסור is fatty, then the food would become totally אסור, but if the איסור was lean, it would אסור the התר כדי נטילה. This is true regardless of whether the מותר piece was fatty or lean.[^3]</span>
## Totally Dry מותר Food is Put on the כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A כלי absorbed אסור בליעות and someone put totally dry מותר food onto the כלי.</span>
### הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the איסור was lean, the בליעות do not spread into the התר more than a קליפה.
   If the איסור was fatty, the ש"ך is unsure if it spreads through the whole food or just through the קליפה.&lrm;[^4] The מגן אברהם says that it only spreads through the קליפה.&lrm;[^5]
   (Because we are dealing with an איסור בלוע, the רמ"א is not מחמיר to treat all בליעות like they are fatty, as [[#^749f21|discussed]]. Additionally, we do not say that the מותר food can make the בליעות absorbed in the כלי become fatty and allow them to spread through the מותר food. We only say this idea when the בליעות would have spread כדי נטילה before adding in this factor. In this case, they would have only spread כדי קליפה.)[^4]</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It come out that we are more מחמיר in a case of lean אסור בליעות absorbed in כלים (they would אסור a food כדי קליפה) than a case where they are absorbed in food (they can't אסור another food at all). The reason for this is because a כלי does not have its own בליעות that it expunges so the בליעות that it had absorbed and are now leaving are more potent.[^6]</span>
# Only the Food is Hot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In addition to the above discussion, there is another potential "bottleneck" which can limit the spread of בליעות, namely, תתא גבר. This is subject to a מחלוקת:</span> ^324ce8
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א, או"ה, ש"ך: We say תתא גבר regarding בליעות being expunged from כלים, the same way we say תתא גבר when one puts one food on top of the other. As such, if one put a hot מותר food onto a cold אסור plate or a hot אסור ladle put into cold מותר food, in both cases the מותר food will become אסור כדי קליפה. This is regardless about whether any of the foods were fatty or lean or there was liquid or not.[^7]</span> 
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: The סברא of תתא גבר does not apply to a case with a כלי. There is always potency to אסור the whole food (assuming that this is a case that has potential to אסור the food completely, such as if there is liquid or the איסור was fatty). As such hot מותר food put on a cold אסור plate or a hot אסור ladle put into cold מותר food can totally אסור the food (unless we have 60x).[^8]</span>
