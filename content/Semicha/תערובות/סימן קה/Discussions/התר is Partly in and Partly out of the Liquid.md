# Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מותר food is partly submerged either in אסור liquid, or there is a piece of איסור in the liquid.</span>
# &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether we say that the איסור spreads to the part that is above the liquid.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">הלכה למעשה, we פסקען that if the איסור is fatty, it spreads through the whole התר. However if the איסור is not fatty, the part of the התר above the liquid remains מותר. This is even if the התר is fatty and even according to the רמ"א (who generally treats all cases as fatty. We are not going to be so מחמיר to say that this whole piece becomes אסור).</span>
