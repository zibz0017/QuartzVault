# &lrm;או"ה
## הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a מותר food is left in a כלי with אסור בליעות for 24 hours, it is considered כבוש and is אסור.</span>
## Question of the ש"ך and ט"ז
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בליעות left in a כלי for 24 hours become פגום and can't אסור anything. However, it takes 24 hours for something to become כבוש. As such, unless the בליעות enter the כלי at the exact same time as the מותר food (which is basically impossible), the בליעות will be פגום before the liquid will become כבוש.
   As such, how is it possible for a liquid to become כבוש in a כלי?</span>
# &lrm;ש"ך And ט"ז
## הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בדיעבד, the liquid is still מותר.</span>
## Reasoning
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are multiple reasons one can be מקל בדיעבד:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason just discussed [[#Question of the ש"ך and ט"ז|above]].</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to numerous שיטות,&lrm; בליעות in a כלי become נותן טעם לפגם after they have been there over night.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to numerous שיטות, &lrm;כבוש only occurs after 3 days.</span>
