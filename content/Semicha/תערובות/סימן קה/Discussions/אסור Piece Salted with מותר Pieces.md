# שיטות
## מחבר
1. בליעות from the אסור piece can get transferred to the מותר pieces. However, if the מותר pieces are far larger than the אסור piece, the אסור piece can't אסור so many of the מותר pieces.
2. For example, if the מותר pieces are all 59x the size of the אסור piece, the אסור piece only has the בליעות to אסור to אסור one of the מותר pieces.
3. As such, this would be similar to the case where the אסור piece touched one of the מותר pieces and we don't know which where we say that all of the מותר pieces are מותר, discussed [[סימן קה הלכות#Touched One Piece but We Do not Know Which|here]].
## רמ"א
1. In the above case, since we say that there is חנ"ן by שאר איסורים, even if the אסור piece only has the בליעות to אסור one of the מותר pieces, the בליעות from the מותר piece become נבלה themselves. If they are fatty, they would spread completely through the other מותר pieces (and we would say חנ"ן on each of those pieces, as well).
2. As such, according to the רמ"א, as long as the איסור was large enough to אסור the מותר pieces, even if it was only large enough to אסור one of them, if the מותר pieces are fatty, they all become אסור.
