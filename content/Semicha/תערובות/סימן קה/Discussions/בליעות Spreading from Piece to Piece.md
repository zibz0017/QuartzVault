# &lrm;איסור is Fatty
## Background
### מחבר
#### סעיף ז
1. When roasting multiple pieces of meat together, בליעות transfer from one piece that has received בליעות to another.
#### סעיף ט
1. When salting multiple pieces of meat together, each individual piece that is touching the אסור piece needs to be 60x the איסור.
### Question on מחבר
1. The ט"ז asks that we know that the הלכות by salting and roasting are the same. However, in סעיף ז the מחבר is saying that the בליעות spread while in סעיף ט he is saying that the בליעות do not.
### Answers
#### נקודות הכסף
1. The הלכות are actually the same. The מחבר is being מחמיר in both cases. In each סעיף he only writes one aspect of the חומרא.
#### פרי חדש, מנחת כהן
1. The הלכות are not the same.
## &lrm;הלכה למעשה Applications
### Basic Case
1. One אסור piece of meat and numerous מותר pieces of meat are in one vat. Some of the מותר pieces are touching the אסור piece and other מותר pieces are touching these מותר pieces that are touching the אסור piece.
2. The הלכה depends on whether the meat is salted or roasted and on which piece of meat we are referring to. This is subject to a מחלוקת.
### נקודות הכסף
1. The הלכות by salting and roasting are the same. We are doubly מחמיר:
	1. Regarding the pieces of meat that are touching the איסור we say that בליעות do not spread from piece to piece and so any individual piece that is not 60x the איסור becomes אסור.
	2. Regarding the pieces that are only indirectly touching the אסור piece, we say that בליעות do spread from piece to piece and so they can become אסור. If all of the מותר pieces together are 60x the אסור piece, all of the pieces that are indirectly touching the אסור piece remain מותר. Otherwise, they become אסור.
### פרי חדש, מנחת כהן
1. The הלכות of roasting and salting each contain a קולא and a חומרא.
#### Roasting
1. The בליעות transfer from piece to piece. As such:
	1. If all of the מותר pieces together are more than 60x the איסור, they all remain מותר, even the piece that is touching the איסור, directly. (This is a קולא.)
	2. If all of the pieces together are not 60x the איסור, they all become אסור, even the ones that are only indirectly touching the איסור. (This is a חומרא.)
#### Salting
1. The בליעות stay in the piece that they transfer to. As such:
	1. Any piece that is directly touching the איסור needs to be 60x the size of the איסור (individually) or it is אסור. (This is a חומרא.)
	2. Any piece that is only touching a piece that is touching a piece touching the איסור remains מותר, even if all of the pieces together aren't 60x the איסור. (This is a קולא.)
# &lrm;איסור Is not Fatty and התר is Fatty
1. There is discussion about whether the whole מותר piece becomes איסור and whether that מותר piece can אסור the next piece.
## Roasting
### Piece Itself
1. מחבר: The מותר piece becomes entirely אסור because the fat goes into the אסור piece and mixes with the אסור בליעות and then brings them back into the אסור piece.
2. רמ"א (own שיטה that all meat is treated as fatty): The אסור piece is viewed as fatty and so it spreads through the התר.
3. דרכי משה (in the שיטה that we don't treat all meat as fatty): If the איסור is חֵלֶב כחוש, this is a "middle level" of fat and so even though it doesn't have the strength to spread through the התר on its own, if the התר is fatty, that can assist it in spreading the התר through.
### אסור the next Piece
1. רמ"א (in his own שיטה that that we say חנ"ן by שאר איסורים): Because we say חנ"ן by שאר איסורים, the piece itself turns into איסור and so it can אסור the next piece.
2. מחבר (in his own שיטה that we don't say חנ"ן by שאר איסורים): The מחבר is unsure about whether the בליעות would spread from that מותר piece. We say that the fat from the מותר food can bring the אסור בליעות through the whole מותר food so perhaps we view these אסור בליעות as fatty now and so it will be able to travel to the next piece.
3. ש"ך (in the שיטה of the מחבר that we don't say חנ"ן by שאר איסורים): Even though the fat from the מותר piece can bring the בליעות from the אסור food through the whole מותר food. The fat from the מותר food can get the אסור בליעות directly from the אסור food to travel through the מותר food, but once these בליעות are in the מותר food, the fat doesn't have the potency to get them to go farther.
