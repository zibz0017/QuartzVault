# &lrm;איסור Is Fatty
## Background
### &lrm;מחבר
#### Roasting
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When roasting multiple pieces of meat together, בליעות transfer from one piece that has received בליעות to another.[^1]
   (This is even true if the piece had absorbed the אסור בליעות at one time and only later this piece that had absorbed the אסור בליעות was roasted with another piece, but that is not really relevant for this discussion.)</span>
#### Salting
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When salting multiple pieces of meat together, each individual piece that is touching the אסור piece needs to be 60x the איסור.&lrm;[^2]</span>
### Question on מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ט"ז asks that we know that the הלכות by salting and roasting are the same. However, in סעיף ז (by roasting) the מחבר is saying that the בליעות spread while in סעיף ט (by salting) he is saying that the בליעות do not spread (which is why each individual piece needs to be 60x the איסור and they do not combine together to be מבטל the איסור).[^3]</span>
### Answers
#### &lrm;נקודות הכסף
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכות are actually the same. The מחבר is being מחמיר in both cases. In each סעיף he only writes one aspect of the חומרא.&lrm;[^4]</span>
#### &lrm;פרי חדש, מנחת כהן
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכות are not the same. (They disagree with the ט"ז's assumption that salting and roasting have the same הלכות.)[^5]</span>
## &lrm;הלכה למעשה Applications
### Basic Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One אסור piece of meat and numerous מותר pieces of meat are in one vat. Some of the מותר pieces are touching the אסור piece and other מותר pieces are touching these מותר pieces that are touching the אסור piece.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכה depends on whether the meat is salted or roasted and on which piece of meat we are referring to. This is subject to a מחלוקת.</span>
### הלכה
#### &lrm;נקודות הכסף
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכות by salting and roasting are the same. We are doubly מחמיר:&lrm;[^4]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Regarding the pieces of meat that are touching the איסור we say that בליעות do not spread from piece to piece and so any individual piece that is not 60x the איסור becomes אסור.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Regarding the pieces that are only indirectly touching the אסור piece, we say that בליעות do spread from piece to piece and so they can become אסור. If all of the מותר pieces together are 60x the אסור piece, all of the pieces that are indirectly touching the אסור piece remain מותר. Otherwise, they become אסור.</span>
#### &lrm;פרי חדש, מנחת כהן
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכות of roasting and salting each contain a קולא and a חומרא.&lrm;[^5]</span>
##### Roasting
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות transfer from piece to piece. As such:[^5]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If all of the מותר pieces together are more than 60x the איסור, they all remain מותר, even the piece that is touching the איסור, directly. (This is a קולא.)</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If all of the pieces together are not 60x the איסור, they all become אסור, even the ones that are only indirectly touching the איסור. (This is a חומרא.)</span>
##### Salting
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The בליעות stay in the piece that they transfer to. As such:[^5]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Any piece that is directly touching the איסור needs to be 60x the size of the איסור (individually) or it is אסור. (This is a חומרא.)</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Any piece that is only touching a piece that is touching a piece touching the איסור remains מותר, even if all of the pieces together aren't 60x the איסור. (This is a קולא.)</span>
### Only top Piece is Hot
#### Dry
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a piece that has absorbed אסור בליעות is touching another piece and only the top piece is hot, if the pieces are dry בליעות do not transfer at all, even כדי קליפה.&lrm;[^6]
   However, if there is liquid, the piece become אסור כדי קליפה.&lrm;[^7]</span>
# &lrm;איסור Is not Fatty
## Cooking together
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This case is not explicitly discussed. It is clear that liquid makes the בליעות transfer but it is not clear how far.[^8]
   Presumably, this has the same הלכות as a כלי that absorbed אסור בליעות and is used with a מותר piece that has sauce, discussed [[כלים and Food#Moist (Or Wet) מותר Food is Put on the כלי|here]].
   However, one difference is that regarding בליעות absorbed in כלים, only a little bit of liquid counts as enough liquid to make the בליעות spread further. Regarding this case of בליעות absorbed in food, the piece that absorbed the בליעות needs to be cooked in significant liquid with the next piece.[^9]</span>
## Roasting or Salting together
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food that absorbed the אסור בליעות is being roasted (without liquid) or salted with the other מותר food, the הלכה depends on whether the מותר food is fatty or not, and whether you are salting or roasting, as discussed shortly.</span>
### &lrm;התר Is Fatty
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: The מחבר is unsure about whether the בליעות would spread from that מותר piece. We say that when it comes to roasting, the fat from the מותר food can bring the אסור בליעות through the whole מותר food so perhaps we view these אסור בליעות as fatty now and so it will be able to travel to the next piece.[^10]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל, פרישה, ב"ח: Even though the בליעות can spread through the whole מותר piece (by roasting), they can't spread to the next piece. This is true whether you are roasting it with the next piece or salting it.[^10] (It seems that it also does not matter how the בליעות got absorbed into the piece, either.)</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">דרכי משה: If the התר absorbed חֵלֶב כחוש, it will spread from the piece to the next piece.[^11]</span>
### Both Pieces Are not Fatty
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If both pieces are not fatty and the אסור piece is no longer here, בליעות from the מותר piece cannot transfer to another piece, even כדי קליפה. Even the רמ"א, who usually says that we treat all meat as fatty will not be מחמיר in this case.[^12]
   (The מהרש"ל is מחמיר where it was roasted with intense heat or was salted to treat all meat as fatty and so the בליעות will spread but the הלכה is not like him.[^13])</span>
2. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">The פרי מגדים משבצות זהב ס"ק יד seems to imply that if the איסור is still here, the בליעות will spread from piece to piece. However, in הג"ה סעיף ט with ש"ך ס"ק לו, the רמ"א seems to imply that only if the איסור is fatty do we say that the בליעות spread from piece to piece, and the case of the רמ"א is certainly where the איסור is still here. Additionally, או"ח סימן תס סעיף ה, the רמ"א discusses a case of מצה with a folded edge (so it may be חמץ) and we say that the other matzos in the oven are מותר (unless they touched the place where the מצה is folded over). This is because this is a case of an איסור כחוש and there is no liquid. Obviously, the איסור is still there in this case.</span>
# &lrm;כלים Absorbing בליעות from Piece that Absorbed אסור בליעות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In terms of absorbing אסור בליעות from a piece that had absorbed אסור בליעות, a כלי is just like a piece of food. As such, if a piece had absorbed fatty בליעות, it will אסור the כלי and if it had absorbed non-fatty בליעות, it won't אסור the כלי.&lrm;[^14]</span>
## Salting Bloody Meat on a כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether meat that has not yet been salted and still contains the blood is considered to be containing אסור בליעות or is אסור מחמת עצמו:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך:&lrm; אסור מחמת עצמו.&lrm;[^13]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: Contains אסור בליעות.&lrm;[^14]</span>
