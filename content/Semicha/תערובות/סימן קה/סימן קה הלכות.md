# כבוש (Pickling)
## Cases
1. איסור and התר are together in liquid.[^1]
2. מותר liquid (or something מותר sitting in liquid) in an אסור כלי.&lrm;[^2] (For further discussion about this case, see [[כבוש in an אסור כלי|here]].)
3. אסור liquid (or something אסור sitting in liquid) in a מותר כלי.&lrm;[^2]
## הלכה
### Length of Time
1. For regular liquid, the איסור and התר need to be together for 24 hours.[^1]
2. For briny (meaning, salty) liquid, the איסור and התר only need to be together of the amount of time it takes for the food to start cooking.[^1]
3. There is a מחלוקת about whether vinegar counts as a regular liquid or if it has the same potency as briny liquid: ^826d62
	1. מחבר, ב"ח, עטרת זהב: Vinegar is like briny liquid.[^3]
	2. או"ה, מהרש"ל, ש"ך: Vinegar is like a regular liquid.[^4]
### Status of the Food
#### Together for Enough Time
1. We treat this the same as if the food was cooked with איסור. As such, the food becomes totally אסור.&lrm;[^1] For what happens if the התר is partly in and partly out of the liquid, see [[התר is Partly in and Partly out of the Liquid|here]].
2. If the התר is 60x the איסור, it remains מותר.&lrm;[^2]
#### Together for less than the Required Time
1. If the foods are together for less than the required time, if it was regular liquid, the התר just needs to be washed off.[^1]
   For briny liquid (or vinegar, subject to the [[#^826d62|מחלוקת above]]):
	1. מחבר: The קליפה is אסור.&lrm;[^1] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This may be assuming that the liquid is not salt enough to make the food inedible due to saltiness, as this may אסור the whole food, depending on whether the איסור is fatty or lean. Also, this may only be for brine. It could be that for vinegar, nothing is אסור at all. See ביאור הגר"א ס"ק י.</span>
	2. רמ"א: The whole food becomes אסור (because we say that even low levels of salt can do מליחה and we treat most foods like fatty) unless it is an איסור דרבנן and the איסור is something which is definitely not fatty.[^5] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This is because of standard הלכות מליחה. This may be only according to the ש"ך. I need to check that out and add a link to there. Also is the דין the same for brine and for vinegar, or is this the הלכה just for brine (as there is obviously no מליחה when it comes to vinegar).</span>
### ספק כבוש

#### שאר איסורים
1. The food is אסור because of ספק דאורייתא לחומרה.&lrm;[^7]
#### בב״ח
1. בב״ח only becomes אסור דאורייתא when milk and meat are cooked together. כבוש does not count as cooking regarding turning milk and meat into בב״ח דאורייתא.
   As such, we assume that the there was no כבישה here because it is a ספק דרבנן and ספק דרבנן לקולא.&lrm;[^7]
# Transferring בליעות through Heat
1. There are numerous factors that affect how far בליעות transfer in food (and כלים) when transferring through heat (which will be discussed in more details below), such as:
	1. [[#Levels of Heat in כלים|How "far removed" it is from the heat source (כלי ראשון vs כלי שיני vs ערוי).]]
	2. How hot it is (יד סולדת בו or not).
	3. [[סימן קה הלכות#Positioning of the Foods|Positioning of the foods (is the hot food on top, on bottom, or are the foods side by side).]]
	4. Is the אסור food hot or just the מותר food.
	5. [[#Types of Foods|The consistency of the foods (fatty or lean, liquid or solid).]]
2. Each factor is like a "bottleneck", meaning that בליעות will only travel as far as the least potent factor. For example, if nearly all of the factors have the potential to transfer בליעות throughout the whole food, but one factor only has the potential to transfer the בליעות into the קליפה, the בליעות will only transfer to the קליפה.
## Underlying הלכות
### Levels of Heat in כלים
#### כלי ראשון
1. A כלי ראשון that is יד סולדת בו cooks food and transfers בליעות.&lrm;[^8]
2. If it is not יד סולדת בו, it does not cook. However, according to some, it can still transfer בליעות, and so one should be מחמיר.&lrm;[^9]
#### כלי שיני
##### שיטות
###### בשול
1. According to all שיטות,&lrm; כלי שיני can't cook things.[^8]
###### Transferring בליעות
1. 1st שיטה (including מהרש"ל and ט"ז): בליעות are transferred through the whole food.[^10]
   The מהרש"ל says that it can't be מפליט ומבליע כאחד. As such, if איסור and התר (or meat and dairy) are in liquid in a כלי שיני, the בליעות do not transfer because they would have to go out of one food, into the water, and then into the other food.[^11]
   However, if התר fell into a pot of איסור (or איסור into a pot of התר, or dairy/meat into a pot of its opposite) בליעות would transfer.[^11]
2. Others: בליעות are transferred into the קליפה.&lrm;[^8]
3. Others: בליעות are not transferred at all.[^8]
##### הלכה למעשה
1. מחבר: &lrm;לכתחילה, one should be מחמיר like the first שיטה, but בדיעבד, the foods just need to be washed off.[^8] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Presumably, the רמ"א agrees, as it mostly seems from סעיף ג.</span>
2. ש"ך: &lrm;כלי שיני will transfer בליעות through a כלי חרס (although one can be מקל in a case of הפסד).[^9]
   When it comes to food, the קליפה becomes אסור.&lrm;[^9]
3. ט"ז: &lrm;כלי שיני will transfer בליעות though the whole food (and make it אסור). In a case of הפסד גדול and something חשוב, one can be מקל and would only need to wash the food off.&lrm;[^11]
   However, כלי שיני will not be מפליט ומבליע כאחד.&lrm;[^11] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I'm not sure about this point.</span>
#### ערוי
##### From Hot into Cold
###### נפסק הקילוך
1. ש"ך: &lrm;עירוי שנפסק הקילוך can be מבליע כדי קליפה.&lrm;[^9]
###### שלא נפסק הקילוך
1. ש"ך: &lrm;ערוי שלא נפסק הקילוך is totally מבשל the food.[^9]
##### From Cold (or Hot) into Hot
1. If there is a stream connecting the two, if there is a bit of warmth that comes up from the bottom food, the top food is אסור.&lrm;[^12] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to this.</span>
#### דבר גוש
*For another location where this is discussed, see [[סימן צד הלכות#דבר גוש in a כלי שיני|here]].*
1. מהרש"ל, או"ה, ש"ך: Solid foods (as opposed to liquidy foods) do not become a כלי שיני when they go from one כלי to another.[^13]
   The reason why we are more מקל by a כלי שיני is because the walls cool the food down. However, this is only relevant to liquidy foods. This סברא does not apply to solid foods which do not spread out along the walls in the כלי. As such, they remain with the status of a כלי ראשון.&lrm;[^13]
2. רמ"א: There is no difference between a דבר גוש or more liquidy foods. They all have the status of a כלי שיני when they get transferred to the new כלי.&lrm;[^14]
##### Specific Examples
1. Pourable kind of rice (something like risotto) is not a דבר גוש.&lrm;[^13] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">There is another example at the end of ש"ך ס"ק ח, but I am not sure what it is.</span>
### Positioning of the Foods
#### Bottom Food is Hot (תתא גבר)
1. If the bottom food is hot, there is potential for the בליעות to transfer through both foods, completely.[^15]
2. If the top food is hot (and the bottom is not), there is only potential for בליעות to transfer through the קליפות of both foods.[^15]
#### Side by Side
##### One Food is Hot and One is not
1. רמ"א: For איסור and התר, the מותר food becomes אסור כדי קליפה (regardless of which one is hot). For meat and dairy, they both become אסור כדי קליפה.&lrm;[^16]
2. מהרש"ל: If the איסור is hot, the התר becomes totally אסור.&lrm;[^17]
   The ט"ז says that in a case where there isn't a הפסד, one should be מחמיר like the מהרש"ל.&lrm;[^17]
   Presumably, if we are dealing with meat and dairy, the hot food will only become אסור כדי קליפה and cold food will become totally אסור.&lrm;[^18]
   If the איסור is not hot (and the התר is hot) it is אסור כדי קליפה (like the רמ"א).[^18]
##### Both Foods Are Hot
1. Both foods becomes totally אסור.&lrm;[^19]
### Types of Foods
#### Fatty Foods and Liquids
1. Fatty foods and liquids have the potential to transfer בליעות through themselves or another food that they are touching. As such, the food itself and/or another food it is touching has potential to become totally אסור.&lrm;[^20]
2. There is a מחלוקת about whether we say that the בליעות spread through the whole food only if the איסור is fatty, or even if the איסור is lean and the התר is fatty:
	1. טור, מחבר, ש"ך: It does not matter if the מותר food or אסור food is fatty or liquid.[^21]
	2. ט"ז: The בליעות spread throughout the whole food only if the איסור is fatty.[^22] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to ט"ז ס"ק י for why he disagrees with the above שיטה.</span>
3. There is a מחלוקת about whether one has to remove the נטילה in a case where the fat would spread through the whole food but the food is 60x times the איסור:
	1. מהרא"י, מהרש"ל, מהר"ם מפאדווה: One does not have to remove the נטילה.&lrm;[^23]
	2. מחבר, רמ"א, others: One does have to remove the נטילה. Even though the בליעות spread throughout the whole food, they are more concentrated in this area.[^24]
#### Solid, Non-Fatty Foods
1. If both foods are solid, non-fatty foods, מעיקר הדין, they can't transfer בליעות beyond a נטילה. As such, these only have the potential to אסור a נטילה.&lrm;[^20]
2. The רמ"א is מחמיר to treat nearly all foods like they are fatty. As such, all foods have the potential to אסור another food, and become אסור, completely.[^25]
   The רמ"א does make exceptions for foods that can not be fatty and are only איסורי דרבנן.&lrm;[^25]
### Food is Itself אסור or Has Absorbed אסור בליעות
#### Food is Itself אסור
1. If the food itself is אסור, it has potential to spread בליעות throughout the food it touches.[^26]
#### Food Has Absorbed אסור בליעות
1. See [[בליעות Spreading from Piece to Piece|here]].

### Bottom Food is Hot (תתא גבר)
1. מחבר: If there is liquid or one of the foods are fatty, the whole food becomes אסור (by meat/dairy, both become אסור).[^20]
   If not, it is אסור כדי נטילה (by meat/dairy, both become אסור כדי נטילה).[^27]
2. רמ"א: In all cases, the whole food becomes אסור (because we treat all foods like they are fatty).[^25]
### Top Food is Hot
1. The food becomes אסור כדי קליפה (by meat/dairy, both become אסור כדי קליפה).[^19]
### All Cases
1. It does not matter if the התר or איסור (or dairy or meat) is on top/bottom.[^28]
### כלים and Food
#### Kind of בליעות Which Are Absorbed
1. Even though in regards to food how far an איסור בלוע spreads depends on the food that the בליעות came from (discussed [[#Food Has Absorbed אסור בליעות|above]]), when it comes to an איסור בלוע in כלים, it does not matter what kind of food the בליעות came from. All בליעות can אסור food even without liquid.[^29]
#### How בליעות Spread
1. רמ"א, או"ה, ש"ך: The same דינים apply to כלים and food. For example:[^30]
	1. Hot מותר food put on a cold אסור plate will become אסור כדי קליפה.
	2. A hot אסור ladle put into cold מותר food will אסור the food כדי קליפה.
2. מהרש"ל: The סברא of תתא גבר does not apply to a case with a כלי. There is always potency to אסור the whole food. As such:[^31]
	1. Hot מותר food put on a cold אסור plate will become totally אסור (unless we have 60x).
	2. A hot אסור ladle put into cold מותר food will totally אסור the food (unless we have 60x).

## Levels of Fattiness
1. If heavily salted meat touches other foods, it can pass בליעות to the next food. How far it can transfer בליעות depends on how fatty the meat is.
### מחבר
#### Lean Meat
1. בליעות transfer to the קליפה.&lrm;[^35]
#### Fat around the גיד הנשה
*This is treated as a "middle level" of fat since this is only אסור מדרבנן.&lrm;[^36]*
1. מעיקר הדין, fat, pipes, and membrane around the גיד הנשה can אסור up to a נטילה. However, if there isn't even a הפסד קצת, one should be מחמיר and treat it that it goes totally through the התר (and one would require 60x).[^37]
2. If the מותר piece of meat that this it is being salted with is fatty, the בליעות travel through the whole thing מעיקר הדין (and one would require 60x), and it makes אסור כדי קליפה (and according to some, כדי נטילה).&lrm;[^35]
#### Fatty Meat
1. Transfers בליעות all the way through. If the התר is 60x the איסור, the התר food stays מותר.&lrm;[^35]
### רמ"א
1. We treat all meat as fatty and so the בליעות transfer all the way through.[^38]
## Which Piece is Fatty
1. מהרש"ל, ב"ח, מחבר (לפי המהרש"ל): &lrm;How far בליעות transfer is entirely based on the status of the אסור piece.[^39]
   If the מותר piece is fatty and the אסור piece is lean, בליעות will only pass into the קליפה.&lrm;[^39]
2. ש"ך, גר"א, מחבר (לפי הש"ך, ביאור הגר"א): If both pieces are salty, בליעות will be transferred as far as the more potent one can carry בליעות.&lrm;[^40]
   If the מותר piece is fatty and the אסור piece is lean, the בליעות will transfer through the whole מותר piece.[^40]
## Multiple Pieces Salted together
1. One אסור piece is salted with multiple מותר pieces.
### Touched All of the Pieces
1. מחבר: Any piece that is 60x the size of the איסור remains מותר. Any piece that is less than 60x the size becomes אסור.&lrm;[^35]
2. רמ"א: If all of the מותר pieces together (meaning, the pieces touching the איסור directly, or even other pieces touching these pieces) are 60x the size of the אסור piece, they all remain מותר. If not, they all become אסור (the רמ"א is being more מקל than the מחבר).[^41]
   However, if חֵלֶב is attached to a piece, that piece needs to be 60x the איסור on its own or it becomes אסור.&lrm;[^42]
### It May Have Touched Any Number of מותר Pieces
1. There is one אסור piece that is salted with numerous מותר pieces. The אסור piece may have touched some of the מותר pieces but we don't know how many or which ones. ^245542

### Touched Under Half the Pieces but We Do not Know Which Ones
