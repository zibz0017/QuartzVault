# &lrm;כבוש (Pickling)
## Cases
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">איסור and התר are together in liquid.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מותר liquid (or something מותר sitting in liquid) in an אסור כלי.&lrm;[^2] (For further discussion about this case, see [[כבוש in an אסור כלי|here]].)</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אסור liquid (or something אסור sitting in liquid) in a מותר כלי.&lrm;[^2]</span>
## &lrm;הלכה
### Length of Time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For regular liquid, the איסור and התר need to be together for 24 hours.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For briny (meaning, salty) liquid, the איסור and התר only need to be together of the amount of time it takes for the food to start cooking.[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether vinegar counts as a regular liquid or if it has the same potency as briny liquid:</span> ^826d62
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, ב"ח, עטרת זהב: Vinegar is like briny liquid.[^3]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">או"ה, מהרש"ל, ש"ך: Vinegar is like a regular liquid.[^4]</span>
### Status of the Food
#### Together for Enough Time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We treat this the same as if the food was cooked with איסור. As such, the food becomes totally אסור.&lrm;[^1] For what happens if the התר is partly in and partly out of the liquid, see [[התר is Partly in and Partly out of the Liquid|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the התר is 60x the איסור, it remains מותר.&lrm;[^2]</span>
#### Together for less than the Required Time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the foods are together for less than the required time, if it was regular liquid, the התר just needs to be washed off.[^1]</span>
   For briny liquid (or vinegar, subject to the [[#^826d62|מחלוקת above]]):
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: The קליפה is אסור.&lrm;[^1] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This may be assuming that the liquid is not salt enough to make the food inedible due to saltiness, as this may אסור the whole food, depending on whether the איסור is fatty or lean. Also, this may only be for brine. It could be that for vinegar, nothing is אסור at all. See ביאור הגר"א ס"ק י.</span></span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: The whole food becomes אסור (because we say that even low levels of salt can do מליחה and we treat most foods like fatty) unless it is an איסור דרבנן and the איסור is something which is definitely not fatty.[^5] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This is because of standard הלכות מליחה. This may be only according to the ש"ך. I need to check that out and add a link to there. Also is the דין the same for brine and for vinegar, or is this the הלכה just for brine (as there is obviously no מליחה when it comes to vinegar).</span></span>

# Transferring בליעות through Heat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are numerous factors that affect how far בליעות transfer in food (and כלים) when transferring through heat (which will be discussed in more details below), such as:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">[[#Levels of Heat in כלים|How "far removed" it is from the heat source (כלי ראשון vs כלי שיני vs ערוי).]]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">How hot it is (יד סולדת בו or not).</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">[[סימן קה הלכות#Positioning of the Foods|Positioning of the foods (is the hot food on top, on bottom, or are the foods side by side).]]</span>
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Is the אסור food hot or just the מותר food.</span>
	5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">[[#Types of Foods|The consistency of the foods (fatty or lean, liquid or solid).]]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Each factor is like a "bottleneck", meaning that בליעות will only travel as far as the least potent factor. For example, if nearly all of the factors have the potential to transfer בליעות throughout the whole food, but one factor only has the potential to transfer the בליעות into the קליפה, the בליעות will only transfer to the קליפה.</span>
## Underlying הלכות
### Levels of Heat in כלים
#### &lrm;כלי ראשון
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A כלי ראשון that is יד סולדת בו cooks food and transfers בליעות.&lrm;[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is not יד סולדת בו, it does not cook. However, according to some, it can still transfer בליעות, and so one should be מחמיר.&lrm;[^9]</span>
#### &lrm;כלי שיני
##### &lrm;שיטות
###### &lrm;בשול
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all שיטות,&lrm; כלי שיני can't cook things.[^8]</span>
###### Transferring בליעות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">1st שיטה (including מהרש"ל and ט"ז): בליעות are transferred through the whole food.[^10]
   The מהרש"ל says that it can't be מפליט ומבליע כאחד. As such, if איסור and התר (or meat and dairy) are in liquid in a כלי שיני, the בליעות do not transfer because they would have to go out of one food, into the water, and then into the other food.[^11]
   However, if התר fell into a כלי שיני of איסור (or איסור into a כלי שיני of התר, or dairy/meat into a כלי שיני of its opposite), בליעות would transfer.[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Others: בליעות are transferred into the קליפה.&lrm;[^8]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Others: בליעות are not transferred at all.[^8]</span>
##### &lrm;הלכה למעשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: &lrm;לכתחילה, one should be מחמיר like the first שיטה, but בדיעבד, the foods just need to be washed off.[^8] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Presumably, the רמ"א agrees, as it mostly seems from סעיף ג.</span></span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: &lrm;כלי שיני will transfer בליעות through a כלי חרס (although one can be מקל in a case of הפסד).&lrm;[^9]
   When it comes to food, the קליפה becomes אסור.&lrm;[^9]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: &lrm;כלי שיני will transfer בליעות though the whole food (and make it אסור). In a case of הפסד גדול and something חשוב, one can be מקל and would only need to wash the food off.&lrm;[^11]</span>
   However, כלי שיני will not be מפליט ומבליע כאחד.&lrm;[^11] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I'm not sure about this point.</span>
#### &lrm;ערוי
##### From Hot into Cold
###### &lrm;נפסק הקילוך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: &lrm;עירוי שנפסק הקילוך can be מבליע כדי קליפה.&lrm;[^9]</span>
###### &lrm;שלא נפסק הקילוך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: &lrm;ערוי שלא נפסק הקילוך is totally מבשל the food.[^9]</span>
##### From Cold (or Hot) into Hot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is a stream connecting the two, if there is a bit of warmth that comes up from the bottom food, the top food is אסור.&lrm;[^12] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to this.</span></span>
#### &lrm;דבר גוש
*For another location where this is discussed, see [[סימן צד הלכות#&lrm;דבר גוש in a כלי שיני|here]].*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל, או"ה, ש"ך: Solid foods (as opposed to liquidy foods) do not become a כלי שיני when they go from one כלי to another.[^13]</span>
   The reason why we are more מקל by a כלי שיני is because the walls cool the food down. However, this is only relevant to liquidy foods. This סברא does not apply to solid foods which do not spread out along the walls in the כלי. As such, they remain with the status of a כלי ראשון.&lrm;[^13]
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: There is no difference between a דבר גוש or more liquidy foods. They all have the status of a כלי שיני when they get transferred to the new כלי.&lrm;[^14]</span>
##### Specific Examples
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Pourable kind of rice (something like risotto) is not a דבר גוש.&lrm;[^13] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">There is another example at the end of ש"ך ס"ק ח, but I am not sure what it is.</span></span>
### Positioning of the Foods
#### Bottom Food is Hot (תתא גבר)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the bottom food is hot, there is potential for the בליעות to transfer through both foods, completely.[^15]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the top food is hot (and the bottom is not), there is only potential for בליעות to transfer through the קליפות of both foods.[^15]</span>
#### Side by Side
##### One Food is Hot and One is not
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: For איסור and התר, the מותר food becomes אסור כדי קליפה (regardless of which one is hot). For meat and dairy, they both become אסור כדי קליפה.&lrm;[^16]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: If the איסור is hot, the התר becomes totally אסור.&lrm;[^17]</span>
   The ט"ז says that in a case where there isn't a הפסד, one should be מחמיר like the מהרש"ל.&lrm;[^17]
   Presumably, if we are dealing with meat and dairy, the hot food will only become אסור כדי קליפה and cold food will become totally אסור.&lrm;[^18]
   If the איסור is not hot (and the התר is hot) it is אסור כדי קליפה (like the רמ"א).&lrm;[^18]
##### Both Foods Are Hot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both foods becomes totally אסור.&lrm;[^19]</span>
### Types of Foods
#### Fatty Foods and Liquids
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Fatty foods and liquids have the potential to transfer בליעות through themselves or another food that they are touching. As such, the food itself and/or another food it is touching has potential to become totally אסור.&lrm;[^20]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether we say that the בליעות spread through the whole food only if the איסור is fatty or even if the איסור is lean and the התר is fatty:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">טור, מחבר, ש"ך: It does not matter if the מותר food or אסור food is fatty or liquid.[^21]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: The בליעות spread throughout the whole food only if the איסור is fatty.[^22] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to ט"ז ס"ק י for why he disagrees with the above שיטה.</span></span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about whether one has to remove the נטילה in a case where the fat would spread through the whole food but the food is 60x times the איסור:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרא"י, מהרש"ל, מהר"ם מפאדווה: One does not have to remove the נטילה.&lrm;[^23]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, רמ"א, others: One does have to remove the נטילה. Even though the בליעות spread throughout the whole food, they are more concentrated in this area.[^24]</span>
#### Solid, Non-Fatty Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If both foods are solid, non-fatty foods, מעיקר הדין, they can't transfer בליעות beyond a נטילה. As such, these only have the potential to אסור a נטילה.&lrm;[^20]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א is מחמיר to treat nearly all foods like they are fatty. As such, all foods have the potential to אסור another food, and become אסור, completely.[^25]
   The רמ"א makes exceptions for foods that can not be fatty and are only איסורי דרבנן.&lrm;[^25]</span>
### Food is Itself אסור or Has Absorbed אסור בליעות
#### Food is Itself אסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food itself is אסור, it has potential to spread בליעות throughout the food it touches.[^26]</span>
#### Food Has Absorbed אסור בליעות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[בליעות Spreading from Piece to Piece|here]].</span>

## Levels of Fattiness
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If heavily salted meat touches other foods, it can pass בליעות to the next food. How far it can transfer בליעות depends on how fatty the meat is.</span>
### &lrm;מחבר
#### Lean Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בליעות transfer to the קליפה.&lrm;[^35]</span>
#### Fat, Pipes, and Membranes around the גיד הנשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך: &lrm;מעיקר הדין, fat, pipes, and membrane around the גיד הנשה can אסור up to a נטילה.
   However, if there isn't even a הפסד קצת, one should be מחמיר and treat it that it goes totally through the התר (and one would require 60x).
   (This is considered semi-fatty.)[^36]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">גר"א: Fat and pipes around the גיד הנשה only אסור up to a קליפה and the membrane can either אסור כדי קליפה or perhaps nothing at all (the מחבר has different משמעות in different places).&lrm;[^37]</span>
#### Fatty Meat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Transfers בליעות all the way through. If the התר is 60x the איסור, the התר food stays מותר.&lrm;[^35]</span>
### &lrm;רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We treat all meat as fatty and so the בליעות transfer all the way through, even if the meat is only אסור מדרבנן.&lrm;[^38]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, for foods which are only אסור מדרבנן and it's not possible for them to be fatty, we do not treat these foods as fatty. As such, they can only אסור כדי קליפה.&lrm;[^39] </span>
## Which Piece is Fatty
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל, ב"ח, מחבר (לפי המהרש"ל): &lrm;How far בליעות transfer is entirely based on the status of the אסור piece.[^40]
   If the מותר piece is fatty and the אסור piece is lean, בליעות will only pass into the קליפה.&lrm;[^40]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ש"ך, גר"א, מחבר (לפי הש"ך, ביאור הגר"א): If both pieces are salty, בליעות will be transferred as far as the more potent one can carry בליעות.&lrm;[^41]
   If the מותר piece is fatty and the אסור piece is lean, the בליעות will transfer through the whole מותר piece.[^41]</span>

### It May Have Touched Any Number of מותר Pieces
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is one אסור piece that is salted with numerous מותר pieces. The אסור piece may have touched some of the מותר pieces but we don't know how many or which ones.</span> ^245542
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[אסור Piece Salted with מותר Pieces|here]].</span>
### Touched Less than Half the Pieces but We Do not Know Which Ones
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If most pieces were not touched and all of the התר combined is more than 60x the בליעות from the איסור, they all remain מותר (even if some of the pieces are ראויה להתכבד, since we are only dealing with בליעות).&lrm;[^47]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is because the אסור piece is בטל ברוב, and if the התר is 60x the איסור, even if one cooks it together there is no concern.[^48] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to add link to סימן קט where the this concern is discussed.</span></span>
## Which Piece is Salty and Positioning of the Pieces
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: The איסור piece needs to be salted for בליעות to spread to the מותר piece. If only the מותר piece is salted, בליעות will not transfer.[^49]
   In addition, if only the אסור piece is salty, it will only transfer בליעות if it is on bottom.[^50]</span> ^b246e2
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: Even if only the מותר piece is salted, the מותר piece can become totally אסור. However, in a case of הפסד, one can be מקל.&lrm;[^51]
   Additionally, it does not matter which piece is on top or on bottom. Either way, the בליעות will transfer (assuming that the אסור one is the one that is salty).
   This is because the whole idea of the positioning is only regarding heat, where one food can heat up the other or cool it down. There סברות do not apply to salt.[^52]</span>
## Salting Food in an אסור כלי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר:&lrm; לכתחילה, one should not salt food in a כלי that has absorbed איסור but בדיעבד, this is מותר.
   This is מותר either because we view this case as a case where the איסור is not salted and the התר is, which is מותר (see [[#^b246e2|above]]) or because salt simply can't extract בליעות from כלים if they are dry.
   We don't permit this לכתחילה, though, because we want to be "הרחק מן הכיעור" (create some separation from איסור).[^53]
   However, if the כלי has only absorbed wine or blood, one may salt food, even לכתחילה.&lrm;[^54]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: One may salt food in any כלי, even לכתחילה.&lrm;[^55]</span>
## Salt on a כלי (or Using Salt on a Dairy/Meat כלי for the opposite)
### Dry כלים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: One may use salt that has been put on a clean אסור כלי, or salt from a clean dairy/meat כלי for its opposite.[^56]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">הג"ה: If one does not use the salt (or doesn't use salt from a dairy/meat כלי for its opposite) one is תבוא עליו ברכה. This is because salt doesn't extract בליעות from כלים, but there is a concern that the כלי may have a little bit of food stuck to it.&lrm;[^57]</span>
3. For additional שיטות, see [[סימן צה הלכות#On a Meat/Dairy Plate|here]].
### Wet כלים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: This is still מותר לכתחילה. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">This is not explicitly discussed. However, the reasoning of the מחבר by dry כלים is that the איסור is not salted and the התר is salted, which, according to the מחבר, is מותר. As such, I don't see why that wouldn't apply to wet כלים as well.</span></span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">הג"ה: This is מותר בדיעבד.&lrm;[^58]</span>
## Salt that Has Absorbed Blood
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If salt that has absorbed blood falls into a food, there is a מחלוקת about  how much one need to be מבטל it:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, רמ"א, פרי מגדים: Even though one can still taste the salt (salt is a [[סימן צח הלכות#Foods that Have a Powerful Taste (עבידי לטעמא)|מלתא עבידי לטעמיה]]), it is still מבוטל בששים. This is because the blood is just an איסור בלוע in the salt and so the salt can't make the blood affect more than it naturally would be able to.[^59]
	   According to this שיטה, one would require 60x the amount of salt because we don't know how much blood was absorbed into the salt and so we view the salt as if it is entirely permeated with blood.[^60]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">טור: We view the salt as if it transformed into איסור. As such, one would need enough התר so that one would not be able to taste to salt (which would require a lot because salt is a מלתא עבידי לטעמיה).[^61]</span>
