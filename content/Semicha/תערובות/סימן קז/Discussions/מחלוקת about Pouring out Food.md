# &lrm;מחלוקת ריב"א, ספר התרומות, and רב Shimon
## Basic Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone has a pot of fish cooking in liquid. One finds out afterwards that one of the fish are אסור. The status of the fish depend on how one got the fish out of the pot.</span>
## Explained by Most שיטות
### Poured out Liquid First
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ריב"א: If one poured out the liquid first, all of the fish are אסור. This is because we are concerned that at some point, the אסור fish was under the water with only some of the fish and these fish weren't 60x the amount of איסור. These fish would then become אסור.
   Then, if one stirred the pot, or just poured the fish out of the pot, these אסור fish would come into contact with the מותר fish and make them אסור.
   As such, everything is אסור.
   </span> ^e2b78e
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ספר התרומות: If one poured out the liquid first, everything is מותר. This is because only some of the fish were under the water with the אסור fish. Presumably, it was not רוב of the fish. Since we have a ספק about which fish are אסור, they would be מבוטל ברוב because this is considered to be יבש ביבש.
   Additionally, these fish can't אסור the other fish that they touch for a variety of reasons:</span> ^095a15
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is an איסור בלוע and so the בליעות can't spread unless they are in liquid (as discussed <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">need to add link. It is in סימן קה סעיף ז. I don't know if this is in my notes.</span>). In this case, the person has poured out all of the liquid.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even once the fish are poured out into the bottom bowl, this liquid is considered a כלי שיני and so it can't אסור anything. (For further discussion about כלי שיני, see [[סימן קה הלכות#&lrm;כלי שיני|here]].)
	   As such, everything is מותר.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רב Shimon: We assume that the איסור is part of the רוב. As such, if by the time the liquid is less the 60x the size of the איסור, most of the food is above the water line, we assume that the איסור was above the water line and so it didn't אסור. According to this, everything would be מותר.
   <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to this שיטה.</span></span>
### Poured out a Little at a time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all שיטות, if you pour a little at a time, we are concerned that perhaps the אסור food was left in the pot with some liquid and fish. This liquid and fish would then become אסור. You are then pouring the fish and liquid onto the rest of the fish. This would make the rest of the fish אסור כדי קליפה (because ערוי can אסור the קליפה, as discussed [[סימן קה הלכות#&lrm;ערוי|here]]).</span>
## Explained by the רמ"א
### Poured out a Some and then Poured More
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some of the fish and liquid were poured. Then, the remaining liquid in the pot was poured out into the garbage. Then one pours out the remaining fish with the rest.</span>
#### הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ריב"א: Everything is אסור for the reasons discussed [[#^e2b78e|above]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ספר התרומות: Everything is מותר for the reasons discussed [[#^095a15|above]].</span>
### Poured out a Little at a time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">All opinions agree that by the case discussed [[#Poured out a Little at a time|above]], the הלכה is that everything is totally אסור unless there is ששים in the bottom against the איסור getting poured.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(The ש"ך asks on this why don't we say that the food will all become אסור כדי קליפה? Similarly, how will ששים help since we are only talking about an איסור in the קליפה?)</span>
## Explained by the בית יוסף and או"ה
### Poured out a Little at a Time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In the case discussed [[#Poured out a Little at a time|above]], all opinions agree that the food becomes אסור. The question is, how much of it becomes אסור:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ספר התרומות: The food becomes אסור כדי קליפה. This is because the liquid becomes אסור when it is less then 60x the איסור and then it is poured on to the rest of the fish.
	   (The או"ה holds that even if the fish are not poured out of the pot, they still become אסור. Since the fish is hot and moist, the בליעות can transfer, even though it is an איסור בלוע.)</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ריב"א: The food becomes entirely אסור.</span>
