# Basic Case and הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א says that if one dips a ladle into a food and pulls out some איסור with some התר and then puts it back into the food, the הלכה is that one needs 60x the איסור or the התר, whichever is greater.[^1]</span>
# Question of the ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why don't we also need 60x the ladle? The ladle definitely has some בליעות in it (as it was used to scoop up some of the התר) and we hold that there is חנ"ן by בליעות in כלים. As such, when one puts the ladle back into the pot, one should require 60x the בליעות in the ladle (which is presumably more than the amount of איסור or התר). Why does the רמ"א only say we need 60x the איסור or the התר?&lrm;[^2]</span>
# Own Thoughts
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why does the רמ"א say that we need either the איסור or the התר, whichever is greater? Why don't we need 60x against the whole התר and most of the איסור? For example, if I have one ounce of איסור and one ounce of התר on the ladle, why aren't we concerned that only some of the בליעות from the איסור entered into the התר and made it חנ"ן, but the איסור still has much of its בליעות remaining? In the above case, technically, the איסור may have expunged 1/59 of an ounce of בליעות into the התר, turning the התר into חנ"ן, while maintaining 58/59 of an ounce of איסור. One would then need 60x the full ounce of התר (which has turned into איסור because of חנ"ן) and the remaining בליעות in the התר?</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Asher Shechtman answered that these are two separate tastes and so they can't combine together (and in fact, presumably, you can use one to be מבטל the other one), as discussed [[סימן צח הלכות#Multiple איסורים|here]].</span>
