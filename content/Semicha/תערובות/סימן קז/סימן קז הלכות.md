# Removing Something with Potential איסור from a Pot of התר
## הלכות
### Not נותן טעם לפגם
#### Pouring from the Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When cooking multiple solids together and one may be אסור, one should not remove them until the water cools down (or one adds cold water). This is because we are concerned that if one of the pieces were אסור, as one removes the food, the איסור may be left behind without 60x and it will אסור the the food that is still in the pot with it.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to those who say that there is חנ"ן by שאר איסורים, there is a מחלוקת about if we say that the food is becomes אסור:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason for those who say that it is אסור say that when the food is being poured from the pot, we are concerned that the איסור was still in the pot with less than ששים, making everything in the pot into חנ"ן. Then when it is poured into the rest of the food, it makes the rest of the food אסור.&lrm;[^2]
	   However, even according to this שיטה the כלים are מותר after 24 hours. (This is because at that point there is only a ספק דרבנן, amongst other reasons. See תשובת מהרי"ל סימן קנא for these reasons.)[^3]
	   Additionally, according to this שיטה, if one pours out all of the liquid, the אסור food will אסור the food that it is touching since they are all moist and there isn't liquid in the pot to make all of the food in the pot combine to be מבטל the איסור. Then, these pieces can go and אסור more pieces. This keeps happening until everything in the pot is אסור.&lrm;[^4] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I need to come back to ט"ז ס"ק ב to understand the case and how it works that the foods are wet enough that they can become totally אסור, yet we don't say that we view this whole thing as one giant mass.</span></span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Those who are מקל say that we are not חושש that the איסור was left in the pot with less than ששים.&lrm;[^2]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We פסקען like this second שיטה.&lrm;[^5]</span>
#### Removing the איסור with a Ladle
##### Removing the איסור by Itself
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one removes the איסור with a ladle, the ladle becomes אסור.&lrm;[^6] This is because of a combination of factors:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are מחמיר and consider a ladle that scoops food from a כלי ראשון to be considered to be a כלי ראשון.&lrm;[^7]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are מחמיר and assume that בליעות from the איסור do not spread through the whole כלי, as discussed [[Background for the הלכות for When a Drop Falls onto the outside of a Pot|here]].[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone puts the ladle back into the pot, how much התר one would need to be מבטל the איסור depends on whether the כלי is a בן יומו or not, and what the ladle is made out of, as discussed [[בן יומו כלי Used for איסור and then התר|here]].[^9] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back and adjust this link. The link should be more targeted.</span></span>
##### Removing the איסור with Some התר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one removes the איסור with some התר in a ladle, according to the רמ"א, who says that there חנ"ן by שאר איסור (as discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;רמ"א|here]]), since we are מחמיר to treat the ladle like a כלי ראשון, the מותר food in the ladle would turn into איסור. As such, if one would return the ladle to the pot, one would need 60x the איסור or the התר (whichever one is greater).[^10]
   For the ש"ך's question on this הלכה, see [[Ladle with איסור and התר|here]].</span>
### נותן טעם לפגם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the piece of איסור is נותן טעם לפגם, there is no issue with the בליעות, so even if it is less than 1/60 of the התר, everything is מותר. (One just has to make sure to remove the piece of איסור before eating the food).&lrm;[^11]</span>
