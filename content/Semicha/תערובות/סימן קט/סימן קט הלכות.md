# Two Individual Things Mixed together (יבש ביבש)
## מין במינו
1. בטל ברוב.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^1] For whether one person can eat all of the pieces, see [[One Person Eating all Three Pieces|here]].
## מין בשאינו מינו
1. בטל בששים.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^2]
## Specific Cases
1. Granulated foods, such as flour count as individual things.[^3]
2. Individual foods, even if they are wet, such as individual drinks, count as individual things.[^4]
3. For individual foods within one pot, see [[Individual Foods in One Pot|here]].
4. Even if two of the foods are smaller than the other one (in which case the largest one may be the אסור one), we still say that there is בטל.
## איסורי הנאה
1. מחבר: No different than any other case.[^5]
2. Others: This is never מבוטל.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^5]
