# Two Individual Things Mixed together (יבש ביבש)
## &lrm;מין במינו
### &lrm;יבש ביבש
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בטל ברוב.&lrm;[^1] For whether one person can eat all of the pieces, see [[One Person Eating all Three Pieces|here]].</span>
## &lrm;מין בשאינו מינו
### &lrm;לח בלח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בטל בששים.&lrm;[^2]</span>
## Specific Cases
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Granulated foods, such as flour count as individual things.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Individual foods, even if they are wet, such as individual drinks, count as individual things.[^4]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For individual foods within one pot, see [[Individual Foods in One Pot|here]].</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if two of the foods are smaller than the other one (in which case the largest one may be the אסור one), we still say that there is בטל.</span>
## &lrm;איסורי הנאה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: No different than any other case.[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Others: This is never מבוטל.&lrm;[^5]</span>
