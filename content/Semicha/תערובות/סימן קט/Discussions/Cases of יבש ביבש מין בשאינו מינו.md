1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">At first glance, it is difficult to conceive of a ספק by יבש ביבש מין בשאינו מינו. If all of the pieces are distinct and not of the same מין, the אסור piece should be distinct and recognizable.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are two potential cases where this would not be true:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The pieces remain separate but they are all ground up. They are now no longer recognizable.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Someone has multiple pieces of different kosher animals (cow, lamb, etc.) and he knows one of them did not have a proper שחיטה, but he doesn't know which one it was.</span>
