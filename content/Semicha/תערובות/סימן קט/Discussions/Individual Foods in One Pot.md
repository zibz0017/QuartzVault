# Basic Case
1. There are pieces of food cooking in liquid in a pot. One of the pieces of food are אסור but we don't know which piece.
# Kashrus of the Food in the Pot
1. The food in the pot needs to be 60x the amount of the אסור food, like a classic case of בטל.
2. Additionally, the אסור piece needs to be מבוטל amongst the other pieces (if they are the same מין it needs two pieces against it and if they are different מינים it needs 60 against it). If there is not, the pieces would all be אסור but the rest of the food would be מותר.
