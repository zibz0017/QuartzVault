# What is the גיד הנשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The גיד הנשה is in the hip. It is 4 fingers-width in large oxen and 2 fingers-width in large sheep. סימן קא הלכות</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Additionally, we also אסור the fat around the גיד הנשה.</span>
# Taste of גיד הנשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">גיד הנשה has no taste. As such, the גיד הנשה itself can't אסור anything through its בליעות because it doesn't have בליעות.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, as noted above, the fat around the גיד הנשה is also אסור and that does have taste. This can אסור things through its בליעות.</span>
# &lrm;הלכה למעשה
## Being מבטל the גיד
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The גיד itself can't be מבוטל, just like by any other בריה.</span>
## &lrm;בליעות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The גיד itself has no טעם but the fat around it (which is also אסור), does.</span>
   As such, we need 60x the amount of the fat to be מבטל the בליעות.
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the רמ"א who says that there is חנ"ן by שאר איסורים (discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;חנ"ן#רמ"א|here]]), if the whole leg is in mixture, the fat is attached to the מותר meat of the leg. Since we are dealing with איסור דבוק the leg becomes חנ"ן, even though it is submerged in liquid (discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#Not Stuck together and Submerged in Sauce|here]]). As such, in such a case, according to the רמ"א, one would need 60x the fat around the גיד, as well as the leg meat.</span>
