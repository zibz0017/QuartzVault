# &lrm;בריה Has חשיבות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A בריה is considered חשוב on a מדאורייתא level regarding receiving malkus. Even though for most things one does not receive malkus if the thing they ate is less than a kizayis, that does not apply by בריה. We see this from the fact that the תורה made eating bugs אסור, even though they are less than a kizayis.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, the רבנן were מחמיר on a בריה regarding being מבוטל in התר.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because of this, in a case of ספק, one can be מקל.</span>
