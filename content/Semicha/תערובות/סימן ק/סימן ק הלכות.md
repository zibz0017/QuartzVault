# בריה
## בטל by בריה
1. מדרבנן, a בריה is never מבוטל, no matter how much התר it is mixed with.[^1] For why this is see [[Why בריה is not מבוטל|here]].
   (Presumably, this would not even allow one to get הנאה from the food it is mixed with if the בריה is אסור בהנה, as we say by [[סימן קא הלכות#חתיכה הראויה להתכבד|חתיכה הראויה להתכבד]]. I assume the reason that this is not mentioned here is because there aren't any בריות that are אסור בהנה.&lrm;[^2]) ^262127
2. For discussion about מין במינו vs מין בשאינו מינו, see [[מין במינו Versus מין בשאינו מינו|here]].
### טעם from a בריה
1. The בליעות from a בריה are מבוטל like anything else. They do not have the same status as the בריה itself.[^3] ^63379b
### ספק if there is a בריה
1. If there is a ספק if a בריה was mixed into food, we can be מקל (if there is 60x the "בריה") because the fact that בריה is not מבוטל is only מדרבנן (as discussed [[#^262127|above]].)[^4]
## Requirements to Be a בריה
1. For something to qualify as a בריה, it needs to:
	1. Something that was living, (things that are from something that is living, like גיד הנשה and חֵלֶב count as something that was living).[^5]
	2. Was always אסור from when it was created,[^6]
	3. If it was divided, it wouldn't maintain its name. (חֵלֶב, for example, would still be חֵלֶב, even if it was divided up, and, as such, is not a בריה).[^6]
	4. Is complete (not missing any part of it).[^6] ^f8fd58
## Specific Examples
### בריות
1. גיד הנשה.&lrm;[^6] For more details about גיד הנשה, see [[גיד הנשה|here]].
2. Egg with yolk.[^7]
3. אבר מין החי.&lrm;[^6]
### Not בריות
1. Whole wheat kernel (never alive).[^6]
2. Kosher bird that became a נבלה (wasn't originally אסור).[^6]
3. Ox that has been stoned (wasn't originally אסור).[^6]
4. חֵלֶב (as noted above, if it would be divided, it would maintain its name).[^6]
5. Bloody egg without yolk (never alive).[^8]
# Applications

## The בריה is Unable to Be Identified
### Large בריה
1. A בריה falls into a pot with liquid and solids but the person can't tell which piece is the אסור piece. However, the piece is large enough that the person can't accidentally eat it.
#### הלכה
1. All of the pieces in the pot become אסור no matter how many there are because we know that one of them is the בריה and so it can't be מבוטל.&lrm;[^9]
2. For the sauce to be eaten, the sauce and the other pieces need to be 60x the אסור piece. This is because there are only בליעות from the בריה in the sauce. The בליעות are מבוטל like anything else. They don't have the same status as the בריה itself.[^9] (If some of the pieces are only partially submerged, whether they can count against the אסור piece is presumably subject to the [[Food that is Partially Submerged in a Pot (מחלוקת רש''י, ר''י)|מחלוקת between רש"י and the ר"י]].)
   However, a strainer must be used to make sure that the person isn't getting any solids which may accidentally be the בריה.&lrm;[^10] ^e588a8
### Small בריה
1. A small בריה falls into a pot. The בריה is too small to be strained out or even detected on one's spoon. (Perhaps the food is thick and has food pieces that make detecting the בריה extremely difficult.)
#### הלכה
1. All of the food is אסור. The בריה is not מבוטל and it can be anywhere.[^11]
## The בריה is Able to Be Identified
1. A בריה falls into a pot with liquid and solids. The person knows which piece is the אסור piece.
### הלכה
1. Before the food may be eaten, the בריה must be removed.[^9]
2. If the pieces and sauce are 60x the אסור piece, everything may be eaten, as discussed [[#^e588a8|above]]. If they are not, everything is אסור.&lrm;[^9]
## The בריה Dissolved into the Food
1. A בריה fell into a pot with liquid. It then dissolved into the sauce.
### הלכה
1. If the pieces and sauce are 60x the אסור piece, everything may be eaten, as discussed [[#^e588a8|above]]. If they are not, everything is אסור. This is because once the בריה has dissolved, it is no longer a בריה anymore and so it is מבוטל like anything else.[^9]
## Finding Bugs when Checking Vegetables
1. A person washes vegetables and checks them for bugs. He finds three (or more) bugs.
### הלכה
1. The vegetables are all אסור. This is because we assume that there are more bugs there that the person didn't find and they can't be מבוטל because they are בריות.&lrm;[^12]
2. The liquid can potentially be drunk just like when בריות are lost in a mixture, as discussed [[#The בריה is Unable to Be Identified|above]].[^12]
