# &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a דבר שיש לו מתירין is put into another food of a different מין to enhance it in some way by, it cannot be מבוטל (despite the fact that we normally say that by מין בשאינו מינו, a דבר שיש לו מתירין can be מבוטל).</span>
# Reason
## Appearance
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food causes a noticeable change in the appearance, we say that the איסור becomes "part of" the התר, and so it is viewed as the same מין. As such, the איסור is a דבר שיש לו מתירין in a מין במינו mixture and so it is not מבוטל.</span>
## Taste
### Food is still בעין
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The איסור is still in the food and hasn't been absorbed.</span>
#### &lrm;הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If this food caused a noticeable change in taste, this is not מבוטל just like a regular piece of איסור (meaning, not a case of דבר שיש לו מתירין) that noticeably changes the taste.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food causes a noticeable change in taste but only in conjunction with other foods, we say that the איסור becomes "part of" the התר, as discussed above.</span>
### Food is not בעין
   1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the איסור has been pulled out or absorbed, it is subject to discussion, as discussed [[טעם from דבר שיש לו מתירין (אינו בעין)|here]].</span>
