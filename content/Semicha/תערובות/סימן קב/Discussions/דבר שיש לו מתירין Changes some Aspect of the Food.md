# הלכה
1. If a דבר שיש לו מתירין is put into another food of a different מין to enhance it in some way by, it cannot be מבוטל (despite the fact that we normally say that by מין בשאינו מינו, a דבר שיש לו מתירין can be מבוטל).
# Reason
## Appearance
1. If the food causes a noticeable change in the appearance, we say that the איסור becomes "part of" the התר, and so it is viewed as the same מין. As such, the איסור is a דבר שיש לו מתירין in a מין במינו mixture and so it is not מבוטל.
## Taste
### Food is still בעין
1. The איסור is still in the food and hasn't been absorbed.
#### הלכה
1. If this food caused a noticeable change in taste, this is not מבוטל just like a regular piece of איסור (meaning, not a case of דבר שיש לו מתירין) that noticeably changes the taste.
2. If the food causes a noticeable change in taste but only in conjunction with other foods, we say that the איסור becomes "part of" the התר, as discussed above.
### Food is not בעין
   1. If the איסור has been pulled out or absorbed, it is subject to discussion, as discussed [[טעם from דבר שיש לו מתירין (אינו בעין)|here]].
