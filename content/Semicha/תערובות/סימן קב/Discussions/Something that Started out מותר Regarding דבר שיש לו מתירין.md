# ט"ז ס"ק י
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The ט"ז explains the רמ"א that if something that is דבר שיש לו מתירין fell into a pot and was removed, this can be מבוטל. This is seemingly because the since we are only dealing with בליעות and the בליעות are untastable, it is viewed as if these בליעות don't exist (seemingly similar to what the ש"ך says in ס"ק ד about מין בשאינו מינו).
   However, when it comes to a food that falls into food of its own מין and melts there, according to the רמ"א, this would not be considered to be a דבר שיש לו מתירין and so would be מבוטל, as well. The סברא of the ט"ז would not explain this case because we are dealing with physical food, not just בליעות, and so we can't say that the food doesn't exist. It's physical food.
   (I am unsure if the סברא of the ט"ז would be able to explain the שיטה of the רמ"א about the where there are אסור בליעות in a dish that is mixed with other dishes which the רמ"א says is מותר, not just because the כלי is מבוטל, but also because the איסור in it is an איסור בלוע. I think that the ט"ז would not apply here since the בליעות still exist in the particular כלי. You can't say that they don't exist. You would have to use classic בטל to make these בליעות מבוטל and one can't do that by דבר שיש לו מתירין. See עזר לשלחן footnote 33. Also see באר היטב ס"ק ז. It is not clear to me from the באר היטב if he is saying that the ט"ז holds like the רמ"א or if the באר הלכה is giving פשט in how the ט"ז is explaining the רמ"א, but not that the ט"ז himself holds like the רמ"א.
   I am also confused because the ט"ז on the third wide line on page פה seems to say that מין במינו makes the בליעות more not exist. However, in מחבר סעיף א it seems that being מין בשאינו מינו makes the food more not exist. It seems that the difference is something along the lines that over here we are dealing with בליעות and over there we are dealing with being מבטל the food itself, but I don't know if that fully addresses everything.)</span>
