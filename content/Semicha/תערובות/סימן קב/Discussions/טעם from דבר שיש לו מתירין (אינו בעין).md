# Cases
## Food is Totally Removed
1. A דבר שיש לו מתירין fell into a food on the flame. Before the food was eaten, the איסור was removed (leaving behind the בליעות).
## Food is Absorbed
1. According to the רמ"א (according to the ש"ך and ט"ז) and the ט"ז (and others), if the איסור is absorbed into the mixture, it is also viewed as just טעם and so it can be מבוטל.
# שיטות
1. ט"ז, רמ"א: This can be מבוטל.
2. ש"ך: This is still considered בעין and so it cannot be מבוטל.
# Contrast between טעם and Change in Appearance
1. According to all שיטות, if the איסור changed the appearance of the mixture, even if the food has been absorbed into the mixture, we still consider the איסור as בעין and so it still can't be מבוטל.
2. As noted above, by טעם some say that the איסור is considered אינו בעין even though it still left a taste and so it can be מבוטל.
# Summary
|                          | Change in Taste | Change in Appearance |
| ------------------------ | --------------- | -------------------- |
| **Absorbed/Removed**     | מחלוקת          | Can't be מבוטל       |
| **Not Absorbed/Removed** | Can't be מבוטל  | Can't be מבוטל       |
