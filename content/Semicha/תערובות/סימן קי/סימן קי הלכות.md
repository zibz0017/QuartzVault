# דבר חשוב
## Basic הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even a משהו of a דבר חשוב can אסור a mixture.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the דבר חשוב is אסור בהנאה, it will אסור the mixture for הנאה. For what one can do with such a mixture see [[What One Can do with a Mixture with an איסור הנאה|here]].&lrm;[^2]</span>
## Types of Mixtures
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמב"ם&lrm; (פשוט פשט), &lrm;מחבר&lrm; (פשוט פשט), או"ה: This will only אסור a מין במינו mixture.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א,&lrm; רמב"ם (according to the רמ"א): This will אסור any mixture.[^4]</span>
## What Counts as a דבר חשוב
### Basic Rules
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חשיבות depends on the time and place.
   The מחבר gives examples of things that were חשוב in his time in certain places, such as certain nuts, fruits, and vegetables, homemade bread, and live animals.[^1] (Dead animals are not. However, they may be חתיכה הראויה להתכבד&lrm;.[^5] See [[סימן קא הלכות|here]] for further discussion).</span>

# דבר שבמנין
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רמ"א holds that there is another category that is not מבוטל, and that is a דבר שבמנין. These are things that are sold by individual units because of their חשיבות (although they are not חשוב enough to be considered a דבר חשוב). These have the same דינים as דבר חשוב.&lrm;[^6]
   For things which are sometimes sold by count and sometimes not, see [[Things Sometimes Sold by Count|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מחבר disagrees and holds that a דבר שבמנין is מבטל like anything else (unless, of course, it happens to be חשוב, in which case it is a דבר חשוב and is not מבטל for that reason).[^1]</span>
