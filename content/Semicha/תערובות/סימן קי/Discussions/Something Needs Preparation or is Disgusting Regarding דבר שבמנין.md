# Background
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about the הלכה in a case where something that is sold by count but is missing steps in its preparation or is disgusting if it counts as a דבר שבמנין.</span>
## שיטות
### ש"ך
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: It does not matter is something needs preparation or is disgusting. It can still count as a דבר שבמנין.&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר, ב"ח and מהרש"ל: If it needs preparation, it does not count as a דבר שבמנין (and presumably not if it is disgusting).[^1]</span>
### &lrm;פרי מגדים (And Presumably the ט"ז)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The פרי מגדים disagrees with the ש"ך and says that there is no proof that the רמ"א holds that something that needs preparation or is disgusting can count as a דבר שבמנין.
   Additionally, there is no proof that the מחבר holds that something that needs preparation or is disgusting cannot count as a דבר שבמנין.&lrm;[^2]</span>
# הלכה למעשה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For something to be considered a דבר שבמנין, the item can't require significant preparation (presumably just like [[Something Missing Steps in its Preparation Regarding חתיכה הראויה להתכבד|חתיכה הראויה להתכבד]])[^3] and presumably can't be disgusting.[^2]</span>
