<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*Presented by ש"ך ס"ק ב*</span>
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may sell food that has is אסור בהנאה that has not been מבוטל, provided that:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The person only sells it for the value of the מותר part, not the אסור part.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is no concern that the גוי will sell it to another Jew.</span>
