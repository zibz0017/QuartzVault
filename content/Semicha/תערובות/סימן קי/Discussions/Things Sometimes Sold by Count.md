1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some things are sometimes sold by count, and other times are not sold that way (for example, eggs were sometimes sold by the basketful). There is מחלוקת if this counts as a דבר שבמנין and is therefore not מבוטל or not.</span>
# שיטות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מסכת ביצה, ת"ח, טור, סמ"ג: Doesn't count as a דבר שבמנין.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל, או"ה, ב"ח, ש"ד: Counts as a דבר שבמנין.</span>
# הלכה למעשה
