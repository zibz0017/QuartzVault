# &lrm;איסור And התר Mix and then More התר is Added
## Cases
### Solid Absorbed איסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A מותר food absorbed איסור (the מותר food was not enough to be מבוטל the איסור) and was then mixed with another food</span>
### אסור Liquid Mixed with מותר Liquid
1. אסור liquid was mixed with מותר liquid (and there was not enough מותר liquid to be מבטל the איסור) and then more מותר liquid was added.
## הלכה
### Status of the Food in the Pot
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: You only need 60x the איסור. This is because the מחבר holds that we don't say that there is חנ"ן by שאר איסורים (see [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;חנ"ן#&lrm;מחבר|here]]) and so only the איסור is אסור.&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: You need 60x the original מותר food that the איסור was absorbed into or mixed with. This is because the רמ"א holds that we do say that there is חנ"ן by שאר איסורים (see [[סימן צב הלכות#&lrm;חנ"ן#&lrm;רמ"א|here]]) and so the whole food is אסור.&lrm;[^2]
   (If the case of the liquid mixing with אסור liquid involves הפסד מרובה, we do not say that it becomes חנ"ן, as discussed [[סימן צב הלכות#Two Liquids|here]].)[^3]</span>
### Status of the Food that Fell in (אפשר לסוחטו אסור)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to all opinions, if the piece that fell in is recognizable, even if the התר in the pot is more than 60x the food that fell in it is אסור. this is because we assume that the איסור in the food doesn't totally leave the food. There is always still some left.[^4]
   As such, by the solid that absorbed the איסור, the solid would be אסור. By the case of the אסור liquid, everything would be מותר because the liquid is not recognizable.[^4]
   For further discussion, see [[אפשר לסוחטו אסור|here]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the מותר food was a חתיכה הראויה להתכבד, if the case is where it absorbed איסור, it can be מבוטל, as discussed [[סימן קא הלכות#^c44783|here]]. If we are dealing with dairy/meat that absorbed its opposite, then it will not be מבוטל, as discussed [[Details about Requirements for חתיכה הראויה להתכבד#&lrm;בב״ח|here]].[^3] (Liquids cannot become חתיכה הראויה להתכבד so this discussion is only relevant to the case where a solid food absorbed איסור.)[^5] </span>
# &lrm;אסור Liquid Falls into Food and is מבוטל Later
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If אסור liquid falls into food and is not מבוטל because there is not enough התר and then more התר is added to make it that there is enough התר to be מבוטל the איסור, there is a מחלוקת if the food is מותר:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: The food is מותר. The איסור is now מבוטל because we do not say that the mixture became חנ"ן when the איסור was originally put in (similar to what we said above).[^6]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: This food is אסור. The איסור is now מבוטל because we do say that the mixture became חנ"ן when the איסור was originally put in (similar to what we said above).[^7]</span>
# Pouring out a Pot with a Piece of איסור
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*For further discussion about pouring a mixture of איסור and התר out of a pot, see <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">need to add link to the beginning of סימן קז</span>.*</span>
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One has a pot with התר and one puts in a piece of meat that has some חֵלֶב and the התר is more than 60x the איסור (the food is מותר as has been discussed many times before). There is a מחלוקת about how one should get the food out:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: If one starts pouring out the התר while the איסור remains in the pot, one will be left with less than 60x the איסור and that food will become אסור.
	   On the flip side, if you pour out the איסור right away, the מותר food that is poured out into the bowl with it becomes אסור because there isn't 60x the amount of איסור in the bowl.
	   Additionally, if one takes out the meat, the חֵלֶב will make the meat אסור. While the meat is in the pot, the בליעות spread throughout all the food in the pot and are מבוטל (because there is ששים). When you take the meat out while it is hot, the חֵלֶב can only affect the meat, so unless the meat is 60x the חֵלֶב, it becomes אסור.
	   As such, one should wait until the food cools down and then pour it out. The food in the pot and the meat will all be מותר.&lrm;[^8]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: Either way the meat is אסור because we hold of חנ"ן by שאר איסורים and even though it is in liquid, we say that there is חנ"ן by איסור דבוק.
	   As such, one should just take the meat with the חֵלֶב out of the pot, even while its hot as, either way, the meat is אסור and one shouldn't leave the חֵלֶב in the מותר food for nothing.[^9]
	   (Additionally, the whole pot of food is אסור unless the התר is 60x the whole piece of meat, not just the חֵלֶב.&lrm;[^10])</span>
