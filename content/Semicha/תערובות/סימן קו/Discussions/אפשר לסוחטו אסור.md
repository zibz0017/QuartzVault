# Basic Concept
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת about what happens when food that has absorbed איסור falls into enough התר to be מבטל it. Do we say that the איסור left the food and so the food is מותר or do we say that some of the איסור remained in the food and so it remains אסור.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is concept does not apply to התר that absorbed *dairy/meat* בליעות. In that situation, the food can lose those dairy/meat בליעות. We are only discussing a case where the food absorbed *איסור*.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This מחלוקת is only relevant according to those who say that we don't say חנ"ן by שאר איסורים. According to those who say that we hold that there is חנ"ן by שאר איסורים, the food is obviously אסור because the food has turned into נבלה. Even if you could remove the איסור, the התר itself has already turned in to איסור.</span>
# הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We פסקען like those שיטות who say that the food remains אסור.</span>
