# ריחא
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*Practically, in many cases there is also a problem of זיעה, as well. These cases assume that the foods were dry and so there is no זיעה issue.*</span>
## Basic Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One puts נבלה and מותר food or meat and dairy in an oven at the same time. (The foods are not touching each other.)[^1]</span>
### לכתחילה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is מותר לכתחילה if any one of the following conditions are met:</span>
#### מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The oven is 12 עשרונים and the door is open, whether wide open or just a little.[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both foods are lean. (This is according to the ש"ך's understanding of the מחבר. The רמ"א disagrees.)[^3]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One (or both) foods are covered, even with dough.[^4]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both foods are in pots that have walls, even if the oven is totally closed.[^5]</span> ^9c1abd
#### All Opinions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The oven is 12 עשרונים and the door is wide open.[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One (or both) foods are covered with a real covering.[^1] (Covering with dough does not work לכתחילה according to the רמ"א.&lrm;[^6]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The foods are not baked in the oven together. They are only baked there back to back.[^7]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Both foods are in pots that have walls if the oven is big or is a little open. (If there is a small opening that is big enough just for smoke to leave, that does not count as a little open.)[^8]
   (For the הלכה about what happens if the oven is totally closed, see [[Food in Pots and Oven is Totally Closed|here]].)</span>
### בדיעבד
#### מחבר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בדיעבד, &lrm;ריחא is unable to אסור things. As such, even if one did not satisfy the above requirements, once one does it, the foods are מותר.&lrm;[^9]</span>
#### רמ"א
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ריחא can אסור food even בדיעבד in 4 cases:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the oven is totally closed. (However, we are מקל in a case of הפסד מרובה).[^10]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If either food is a דבר חריף or the איסור is aromatic the food will become אסור unless the oven is wide open on the top.[^11]</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the food is something that can אסור even במשהו, the oven is completely closed, and the oven is small, even in a case of הפסד מרובה.&lrm;[^12]</span>
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the התר and איסור are baked under one cover.[^7] (It seems that this is viewed as an extreme case of a closed and small oven.[^13])</span>
## Additional בדיעבד Cases
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*Note: These cases are where the oven has not satisfied the parameters to make this לכתחילה, subject to the [[#לכתחילה|above מחלוקת]]. If these cases have satisfied the parameters to make this לכתחילה, then these would all be מותר לכתחילה, obviously.*&lrm;[^14]</span>
### Meat/Dairy With Something Parve
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one cooked something parve in the same oven as meat/dairy it can't be eaten with the opposite because this is not considered to be בדיעבד as one has an option to eat this food with the same type.[^7]</span>
#### Exception by Bread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a special התר for bread that has been baked in an oven with meat/dairy and one wants to eat it with a meal of its opposite.[^15]
   In fact, one may even buy food from a גוי that has been baked with איסור or with meat/dairy in a situation that one can't get other bread.&lrm;[^7]</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is because bread had a primary role in one's meal and so this is considered a בדיעבד situation.[^7]</span> ^21fcdb
### Buying Food from a גוי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not buy food from a גוי that has been baked with איסור. This is viewed as לכתחילה.&lrm;[^16]
   The only exception to this is bread in a situation that one can't get other bread, as [[#^21fcdb|discussed]].</span>
# &lrm;מרדה (Peel)
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*A peel is the shovel-like utensil used in pizza ovens in our times. Back then, it was clearly used in ovens for other foods.*</span>
## Dirty with איסור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While the peel is dirty with איסור, according to all שיטות anything baked using it will become אסור. (Whether it will be אסור כדי קליפה or כדי נטילה is subject to the usual הלכות of [[סימן קה הלכות#Types of Foods|התר cooked on top of איסור]].)
   However, after something is baked using the peel, we assume that this food "cleaned" off the איסור and the other foods made be fine (see below).
   If this food that cleaned the איסור off the peel was lost in a mixture of other foods, it is בטל ברוב.&lrm;[^17]</span>
## Clean
### בן יומו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Most שיטות: While the peel is a בן יומו from איסור, one may not use it.[^18]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">או"ה: If the peel is clean, one may use the peel.[^19]
   (The שיטה of the או"ה seems difficult to understand as the peel should still אסור at least the קליפה of food baked there, as discussed [[כלים and Food#Totally Dry מותר Food is Put on the כלי|here]].&lrm;[^20])</span>
### Non-בן יומו
#### Buying from a גוי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a גוי uses a peel that is not בן יומו, a Jew may buy the food.
   The reason for this is because the גוי is not going to listen to the Jew to use a new peel every time a Jew comes to buy food. As such, חז"ל were not גוזר on using a non-בן יומו כלי in this case since there is no other way.[^21] (Presumably the case is similar to the case discussed [[#Buying Food from a גוי|above]] that the food is only available from the גוי.)
   (The לבוש says that the reason חז"ל were not גוזר on the peel when it is not a בן יומו is because peels are not commonly used כלים. However, the ט"ז does not like this reason[^22] and the ש"ך doesn't even bring it down.)</span>
#### Jew Using Non-בן יומו Peel
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is מחלוקת about whether a Jew can use a non-בן יומו peel:[^23]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ט"ז: A Jew may use such a peel (it seems that once we are not גוזר on a גוי, we aren't גוזר on a Jew, either).</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פרי חדש: A Jew may not use such a peel.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(It should be noted that it seems that this מחלוקת is only about a peel, not about any other כלי that has non-בן יומו איסור.)</span>
# Bread on a Barrel of (אסור) Wine
## Basic Case
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Bread is placed on top of a barrel of אסור wine (יין נסך or סתם יינם&lrm;[^24]). The status of the bread will depend on whether the barrel was open or closed, whether the bread was hot, and what the bread was made out of.</span>
## Wheat Bread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the barrel was open and the bread was over the opening in such a way that the aroma was not able to escape except through the bread and the bread was hot, the bread will be אסור.&lrm;[^25]
   (If it was סתם יינם the bread will become אסור to eat. If it was יין נסך, the bread will even become אסור for הנאה.&lrm;[^24])</span> ^0749df
## Barley Bread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If *either* the barrel was open (even if the bread was cold) *or* the bread was hot (even if the barrel was closed) the bread will become אסור (either just to eat or even for הנאה, as discussed [[#^0749df|just above]]).[^26]</span>
# איסור בהנאה
## Smelling with One's Mouth
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can "smell" with one's mouth something that is אסור בהנאה.&lrm;[^27] While there is a מחלוקת about ריחא מילתא היא, &lrm;ריחא "smelled" through the mouth is certainly לאו מילתא היא according to all opinions.[^28]</span>
## Tasting
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not taste something that is אסור בהנאה, even if they spit it out.[^29]
   In fact, even if the food is only אסור באכילה (and מותר בהנאה), one may still not taste it, even if they spit it out, as a גזרה because of eating it.[^30] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to link back to the beginning of סימן צח where this is discussed further. Also, see פרי מגדים משבצות זהב ס"ק ט for further discussion.</span></span>
## Smelling Something that Absorbed איסור Smell
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If something absorbed aroma from something that is אסור בהנאה, it is מותר to smell, לכתחילה, if one intends to smell the thing itself and not the אסור smell.
   However, it is אסור to use this for בשמים for הבדלה.&lrm;[^31] (For a discussion about using pepper for הבדלה, see [[Using Pepper for הבדלה|here]]. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to ט"ז ס"ק י</span>)</span>
