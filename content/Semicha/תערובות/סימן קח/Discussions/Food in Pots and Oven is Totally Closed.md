2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מגן אברהם: This is מותר בדיעבד (without הפסד מרובה) even if the oven is totally closed and small.[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (according to the ש"ך): If the oven is small this is מותר only in a case of הפסד מרובה.&lrm;[^2] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">The ש"ך implies from ס"ק יח that the רמ"א makes a חלוק between large ovens and small ovens (the ש"ך implies that only according to the מהרש"ל there is no חלוק, implying that according to the רמ"א there is a חלוק), but the פרי מגדים doesn't sound like he is coming to argue on the ש"ך's understanding of the רמ"א. And if we do say that the ש"ך and פרי מגדים are arguing, what actually is the הלכה in large ovens according to the ש"ך's understanding of the רמ"א? Is it מותר לכתחילה or only מותר בדיעבד?</span></span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א (according to the גר"א and פרי מגדים): Whether the oven is big or small, this is מותר only in a case of הפסד מרובה.&lrm;[^3]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מהרש"ל: This is totally אסור in all cases.[^4]</span>
