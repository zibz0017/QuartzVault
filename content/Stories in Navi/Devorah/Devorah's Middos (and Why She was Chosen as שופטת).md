1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Devorah made the wicks for the Mishkan. Because of this, she was zoche to knowledge of Torah, nevuah, and to chosen as שופטת.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^1][^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Devorah did mitzvos with tremendous zerizus[^3][^4] and feeling.[^4] Because she was a neviah and she acted with zerizus, she was chosen as שופטת.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>[^3]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Devorah was a tremendous nevuah to the point that light would emanate from her when she would receive a nevuah, similar to Moshe Rabbeinu.[^5]</span>
