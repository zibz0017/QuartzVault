# Summary of מראי Mekomos
## גמרא ב ע"ב
1. An Eid Echud is believe for issurim.
## 'תוס
1. (Question) What is the source for Eid Echud?
2. (Answer) Eif Echud is learned out of Niddah. The תורה says that the woman is believed on Niddah because of the passuk of visafra lah.
3. (Question) Niddah is Ischazeik Issura. As such, if we learn out of Niddah, we would have to learn out to cases of ischazeik issura. However, that is not the halacha (at least according to the accepted opinion).
4. (Answer) The bleeding by Niddah is something that regularly stops after a short amount of time. When she stops, she will automatically become tahor and able to go to the mikva. As such, it is not considered to be ischazeik issura. In addition, is biyado to go to the mikva.
## Maharsha
### Question
1. What does 'תוס mean in his answer?
2. It sounds like it comes out Niddah is both biyado and not ischazeik. If that is the case, then how can we learn from Niddah to Eid Echud throughout shas? There is a special reason why the eid echud is believed in this case, namely, that it is biyado. Many (the vast majority, in fact) cases of eid echud do not have the power of biyado.
### Answer
1. There are really two aspects to Niddah. Niddah is composed of the woman not bleeding anymore, and the woman going to the mikva.
#### Bleeding
1. In terms of the bleeding, this is not considered to be ischazeik issura because bleeding regularly stops after a short amount of time, as 'תוס said.
2. In addition, the bleeding is not biyado. She can't control if she stops bleeding.
#### Mikva
1. The tumah that she has until she goes to the mikva is considered to be ischazeik issura.
2. However, this is also biyado.
#### Conclusion
1. As such, we can learn out from the bleeding to standard cases of eid echud (meaning, cases that are not ischazeik and are not biyado).
2. And we learn from the fact that she is believed on the fact that she went to the mikva to all cases of eid echud which is biyado and ischazeik.
## Maharam Shif
1. 'תוס in his answer was saying that the bleeding is not considered to be ischazeik issura because it is something that is going to go away shortly on its own.
2. (Question) What about the fact that she is also tamei in addition to the bleeding? The tumah is not going to go away on its own so it should be ischazeik issura.[^1]
3. (Answer) The mikva is biyado and so it is not considered to be ischazeik issura.
4. (Question) If that is so, then how do we learn out Eid Echud in cases which are not biyado from Eid Echud? This is a case of biyado without Ischazeik?
5. (Answer) This is a weakened biyado. The biyado is weakened by the fact that the woman may not have stopped bleeding.

### Second Question
1. How did [[#'תוס|our 'תוס]] learn out from niddah that eid echud is believed by issurim which are not biyado and not ischazeik? The tumah by niddah is ischazeik and biyado.
### Second Answer
1. Biyado functions as a anti-ischazeik. That is all it does. It simply tries to undermine the chazaka.
2. As such, if we say that in a case where the biyado is against the chazaka and we say that the biyado wins and the eid echud is believed, then we would certainly say that in a case where there is no chazaka and no biyado then the eid echud is believed. The "best case scenario" for the eid in a biyado vs chazaka case is that we consider it that biyado takes out the chazaka and basically turns it into a case of no biyado and no ischazeik.

### Question 2
1. The Maharik understands the later 'תוס to be saying that because niddah is ischazeik, if it were also a davar shebierva then the halacha would be that the woman would not be believed even though the mikva is ischazeik. In other words, an eid echud with biyado is not believes by a davar shebierva with ischazeik.
2. However, there are clear examples where this is not the cases. For example, if a man comes into בית דין and says that he had divorced his wife, he is believed.
3. This case sets up an eid echud (the man) with a biyado (he can write a get now and give it to his wife if he wants) against a davar shebierva (the woman is an אישת איש) and an ischazeik issura (the woman has been an אישת איש until now as far as we were aware).
4. The halacha is that the man is believed. As such, we see that an eid echud with biyado is believed even in a case where there is a davar shebierva and an ischazeik issura.

## Chidushei Rav Shmuel
### Question
1. According to [[#'תוס]], niddah has a chezkas tumah which is removed when she goes to the mikva.
2. How is her claim that she went to the mikva believed? Even though the mikva aspect is biyado, the bleeding is not. Her chezkas tuma should say that she is still bleeding, or that she did stop, but she started again.[^2]
3. We say these kinds of things all the time by chazaka. For example, if someone tamei went to the mikva, if the mikva was ספק 40 saah, we say that the mikva had less than 40 saah and the person remainse tamei (or we at least treat the mikva like it is less than 40 saah). Technically, the chazaka doesn't directly talk to the amount of water in the mikva. However, we still say that the chazaka does work this way.
4. As such, we can say that the same thing is true in our case. Even though the woman's chezkas tumah can't say that she didn't go to the mikva (because going to the mikva is biyada), it can talk to her bleeding and tell us that she either never stopped bleeding, or stopped but started again.
### Answer
1. We see from 'תוס that there is a chiluk between the "siba hamitaheres" (the "main thing" that makes her tahor and directly flies in the face of the chezkas tumah) and something which needs to be true to allow her to be tahor, but is only "background information".
2. Going to the mikva is the "siba hamitaheres" while waiting for 7 clean days is only background information. As such, the chazaka can't talk to it and say that there is a problem with the 7 clean days.
3. (According to this, it would seem that the 40 saah in the mikva is considered to be a shayla on the mikva and so this is considered to be the siba hamitaheres, not background information, and so the chazaka can talk to it and say that the mikva was not 40 saah.)

### Question
1. According to the Maharsha, we learn that eid echud with biyado is believed against ischazeik issura from the fact that the niddah is believed on having gone to the mikva (which is a case of ischazeik and biyado).
2. It seems that the case of niddah has a biyado working through a migu. Once the woman is believed on the fact that she had 7 clean days, then she can go to the mikva right now and she would be tahor.
3. As such, niddah can teach you about biyado working through migu, but how can we learn about biyado working through baalus from niddah?
### Answer
1. We have to say that from the fact that the תורה does not appear to give any caveats, it would seem that the niddah is believed on all shaylos involving her niddah status, even those that already happenned.
2. For example, if the woman touches taharos.
3. This can be used to answer the [[#Question 2|בית Haleivi's second question]] on the Maharik. The biyado in the case that the בית Haleivi is referring to is a biyado through the power of migu. That is a powerful biyado. As such, an eid echud with such a biyado is believed.
4. The case that we are dealing with only has a biyado tjat works through the power of baalus. That is a weak biyado and so an eid echud witth a biyado using baalus is not believed by a davar shebierva and ischazeik issura.[^3]
## Shaarei Chaim
1. When we say that an eid echud is believed by issurim, this does not mean that they are believed on the metzios. They just have the power to affect the דין.
## רמב"ן
1. According to 'תוס, the bleeding is considered to be biyado. Even though she obviously can't stop the bleeding, she can wait until she stops, at which point she will be able to go to the mikva.[^4]
