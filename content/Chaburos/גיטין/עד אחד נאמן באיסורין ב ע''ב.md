# Summary of מראי מקומות
## &lrm;גמרא ב ע"ב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">An Eid Echud is believe for אסורים.</span>
## 'תוס
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Question) What is the source for Eid Echud?</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Answer) Eif Echud is learned out of נדה. The תורה says that the woman is believed on נדה because of the פסוק of visafra lah.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Question) נדה is Ischazeik Issura. As such, if we learn out of נדה, we would have to learn out to cases of ischazeik issura. However, that is not the הלכה (at least according to the accepted opinion).</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Answer) The bleeding by נדה is something that regularly stops after a short amount of time. When she stops, she will automatically become tahor and able to go to the mikva. As such, it is not considered to be ischazeik issura. In addition, is biyado to go to the mikva.</span>
## Maharsha
### Question
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">What does 'תוס mean in his answer?</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It sounds like it comes out נדה is both biyado and not ischazeik. If that is the case, then how can we learn from נדה to Eid Echud throughout shas? There is a special reason why the eid echud is believed in this case, namely, that it is biyado. Many (the vast majority, in fact) cases of eid echud do not have the power of biyado.</span>
### Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are really two aspects to נדה. נדה is composed of the woman not bleeding anymore, and the woman going to the mikva.</span>
#### Bleeding
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In terms of the bleeding, this is not considered to be ischazeik issura because bleeding regularly stops after a short amount of time, as 'תוס said.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In addition, the bleeding is not biyado. She can't control if she stops bleeding.</span>
#### Mikva
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The tumah that she has until she goes to the mikva is considered to be ischazeik issura.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, this is also biyado.</span>
#### Conclusion
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, we can learn out from the bleeding to standard cases of eid echud (meaning, cases that are not ischazeik and are not biyado).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">And we learn from the fact that she is believed on the fact that she went to the mikva to all cases of eid echud which is biyado and ischazeik.</span>
## Maharam Shif
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">'תוס in his answer was saying that the bleeding is not considered to be ischazeik issura because it is something that is going to go away shortly on its own.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Question) What about the fact that she is also tamei in addition to the bleeding? The tumah is not going to go away on its own so it should be ischazeik issura.[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Answer) The mikva is biyado and so it is not considered to be ischazeik issura.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Question) If that is so, then how do we learn out Eid Echud in cases which are not biyado from Eid Echud? This is a case of biyado without Ischazeik?</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Answer) This is a weakened biyado. The biyado is weakened by the fact that the woman may not have stopped bleeding.</span>

### Second Question

### Second Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Biyado functions as a anti-ischazeik. That is all it does. It simply tries to undermine the חזקה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, if we say that in a case where the biyado is against the חזקה and we say that the biyado wins and the eid echud is believed, then we would certainly say that in a case where there is no חזקה and no biyado then the eid echud is believed. The "best case scenario" for the eid in a biyado vs חזקה case is that we consider it that biyado takes out the חזקה and basically turns it into a case of no biyado and no ischazeik.</span>

## &lrm;חידושי Rav Shmuel
### Question
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to [[#'תוס]], נדה has a chezkas tumah which is removed when she goes to the mikva.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">How is her claim that she went to the mikva believed? Even though the mikva aspect is biyado, the bleeding is not. Her chezkas tuma should say that she is still bleeding, or that she did stop, but she started again.[^2]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We say these kinds of things all the time by חזקה. For example, if someone tamei went to the mikva, if the mikva was ספק 40 saah, we say that the mikva had less than 40 saah and the person remainse tamei (or we at least treat the mikva like it is less than 40 saah). Technically, the חזקה doesn't directly talk to the amount of water in the mikva. However, we still say that the חזקה does work this way.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, we can say that the same thing is true in our case. Even though the woman's chezkas tumah can't say that she didn't go to the mikva (because going to the mikva is biyada), it can talk to her bleeding and tell us that she either never stopped bleeding, or stopped but started again.</span>
### Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see from 'תוס that there is a חילוק between the "siba hamitaheres" (the "main thing" that makes her tahor and directly flies in the face of the chezkas tumah) and something which needs to be true to allow her to be tahor, but is only "background information".</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Going to the mikva is the "siba hamitaheres" while waiting for 7 clean days is only background information. As such, the חזקה can't talk to it and say that there is a problem with the 7 clean days.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(According to this, it would seem that the 40 saah in the mikva is considered to be a shayla on the mikva and so this is considered to be the siba hamitaheres, not background information, and so the חזקה can talk to it and say that the mikva was not 40 saah.)</span>
