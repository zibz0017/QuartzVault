# Offer for Peace
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Before getting to ארץ ישראל,&lrm; יהושע sent messages to the inhabitants. He gave the people of ארץ ישראל the opportunity to run away or make peace with כלל ישראל.
   The גרגשי took this opportunity and ran away to Africa, while the גבעונים and רחב made peace.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This offer for peace or to run away didn't only apply to whole provinces at large. It seems that individuals were allowed to make peace.[^2]</span>
## Preference for Destruction
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem preferred that כלל ישראל would destroy the people of כנען rather than make peace or allow them to run away.[^3]
   (Presumably, this did not apply to רחב. She seemed to be a fully righteous convert.)</span>
