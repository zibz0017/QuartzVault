# Offer for Peace
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Before getting to ארץ ישראל,&lrm; יהושע sent messages to the inhabitants. He gave the people of ארץ ישראל the opportunity to run away or make peace with כלל ישראל.
   The גרגשי took this opportunity and ran away to Africa, while the גבעונים and רחב made peace.[^1]</span>
