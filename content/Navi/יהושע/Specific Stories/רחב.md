# Who She Was
## &lrm;אשה זונה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to nearly all מפרשים, the 'גמ, and the simple reading of the פסוק, רחב was a tremendous זונה.&lrm;[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to some, she may have (also) sold food. ("זונה" in the פסוק refers to מזון.)&lrm;[^2]</span>
## Gilgul
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to some, רחב was a gilgul of אישת potifar and יהושע was a gilgul of יוסף. &lrm;In her time, אישת potifar saw (through astology) that she was supposed to marry יוסף, which was part of why she pursued him so much. This was partially correct: a later gilgul of herself was going to end up marrying a later gilgul of יוסף.&lrm; (As an aside, אישת potifar's name was זליכא).&lrm;[^3]</span>
# Why Did פנחס and כלב Go to Her?
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This was to throw people off. It was well known that כלל ישראל hated זנות. By going to רחב, people thought that there was no way that these people were from כלל ישראל.&lrm;[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The leaders of ארץ כנען came to רחב and so she knew the secrets of what was going on (this is hinted to in the פסוק which says "ושמה רחב", somewhat implying that her name was of significance, namely, that she was well-known).[^5]</span>
# How the Spies Were Discovered
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The city was closed up and nobody could come in or out (during most times). They had come when the gates were closed (clearly they snuck in). This is implied by "אנשים באו".&lrm;[^6]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If they were travelling salesmen, they would have stayed near the gate so they could leave in the morning when the gates opened up (as they didn't stay open for long) so they could go to the next city and sell their wares. This is implied by "באו הנה" (they specifically came here and are clearly not rushing to leave).[^6] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't know why they couldn't be planning to sell their goods in Yericho the next day. As the פסוק is about to say, they came to Yericho at night and so they presumably hadn't had time to sell their wares yet.</span></span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">They came at night. This is not a time to travel even during good times, and certainly by dangerous times. This is implied by "בלילה".&lrm;[^6]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to summarize this</span> פנחס and כלב were recognized as Jews and they were going to רחב. This alone wouldn't have been a giveaway about the fact that they were spies for perhaps they were going to רחב for זנות. This is also why the fact that they were traveling at night wasn't a giveaway, either: if they were going to have זנות, it would be normal to travel at night.</span>
   There were two possibilities about what gave them away:
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">כלל ישראל was punished extremely severely for having זנות with the bnos Moav, and so כלל ישראל would have set up guards to make sure that that wouldn't happen again.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">People weren't having זנות at this time due to fear of כלל ישראל impending arrival. אישת יפת תואר is מותר in times of war, so these people must have been going to רחב to be מזנה (as that was now מותר).</span>
   פנחס and כלב had, in fact, gone to רחב to get people to think that they weren't Jewish. It turned out that this was actually what gave away their mission.[^7]
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">כלב and פנחס were seen entering רחב's house through the window from outside the wall (her house was built into the wall).</span>
   Clearly these people were Jewish because כנענים couldn't have tashmish because of their fear, and because it was night time and so nobody would recognize them if they went through the regular way. It must be that they were trying to avoid guards at the entrance of the city.
   Despite their efforts, כלב and פנחס were seen by sentinels on watch towers.

# What Happened to Her
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רחב ended up marrying יהושע and became the ancestor of numerous neviim.&lrm;[^8]</span>
   יהושע was allowed to marry her even though she was from the 7 nations either because:[^9]
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">That הלכה only applied once כלל ישראל crossed into ארץ ישראל, and רחב had already been מתגיירת.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She actually was not from the 7 nations, although she did live in כנען. (Although the ספרי says that she was from the 7 nations.)</span>
