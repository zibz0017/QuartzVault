# א א
## Why ספר יהושע Starts with the Word 'ויהי'
1. Why does ספר יהושע start with the word 'ויהי' and particularly with the letter 'ו' which is a letter that connects to the previous topic?
	1. רש"י, Metzudas Dovid (Addressing the letter 'ו'): <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>ספר יהושע is connected to the end of the תורה. The תורה ends off with the death of משה, and יהושע begins with talking about what happened after that.
	2. Radak: Many stories start with the word 'ויהי' even though they are not connected to the previous story.
## When יהושע Received This נבואה
1. שפתי zikeinim ב: This occurred 30 days after the death of משה as יהושע was not able to receive נבואה when he was in aveilus.
## משה Referred to as 'עבד Hashem'
1. Why is משה referred to as an עבד Hashem?
	1. מנחה Ketana (Addressing why משה is called Eved Hashem particularly over here): Hashem doesn't 'associate His name' with people when they are alive (with few exceptions). Now that משה had died, Hashem can 'associate His name' with him.
	2. Radak: Someone who does everything for the sake of Hashem (meaning, even all of the things about עולם Hazeh) is called an עבד Hashem.
	3. מלבי"ם: <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>משה had taught so many people during his life and because of him they were serving Hashem. Because of this, even though משה wasn't alive, he was still serving Hashem through these people that he had taught.
## יהושע Referred to as 'משרת משה'
1. Why is יהושע referred to as 'משרת משה'?
	1. רלב"ג, ביאורי הגר"א, מלבי"ם: The reason why יהושע was zoche to נבואה and to be the leader of כלל ישראל was because he was the משרת משה and it is greater to serve a great person than it is to learn from them ("גדול שמושה יותר מלמודה").
# א א-ב
## Death of משה Linked to יהושע Receiving נבואה ('אחרי מות משה… ויאמר ה' אל יהושע' and 'משה כבדי מת ועתה קום עבר')
1. The passukim seems to be linking the death of משה to יהושע receiving נבואה and crossing the Yarden.
	1. רלב"ג (On יהושע receiving נבואה): Hashem makes sure that כלל ישראל has a navi. When משה was alive, there was no need for יהושע to be able to receive נבואה and so he didn't receive נבואה. Only now that משה had died did יהושע begin receiving נבואה.
	   In addition, if משה led כלל ישראל into ארץ ישראל, they would have been able to defeat all of their enemies. However, כלל ישראל didn't deserve this, and, because of this, enemies were left in ארץ ישראל who caused problems for a long time. (The רלב"ג says more over [[#^2f44ab|here]].)
	2. מנחה Ketana (On יהושע receiving נבואה): משה davened to Hashem to make sure that כלל ישראל would not be left without a leader. משה's tefilos were answered and shortly after משה died יהושע became the leader.
	3. ביאורי הגר"א, רלב"ג, Metzudas Dovid, מלבי"ם (On יהושע leading כלל ישראל across the Yarden): <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>יהושע couldn't lead כלל ישראל across the Yarden during משה's lifetime because יהושע couldn't be the leader (משה was the leader) and  משה couldn't cross the Yarden (because of his עבירה).
	   Now that משה died neither of these reasons applied and so יהושע was able to lead כלל ישראל across. (The Metzudas Dovid only discusses the second point and the ביאורי הגר"א is not clear which point he is referring to.) ^2f44ab

# א ב
## Forgotten הלכות
1. Temura טז.: The 'גמ says that right before משה died, he asked יהושע if he had any questions. יהושע responded that he had already asked all of his questions to משה during the time they had spent together.
   This bothered משה (seemingly the fact that he was no longer needed). As a punishment, יהושע forgot many הלכות.
   כלל ישראל was furious with יהושע for causing this loss of תורה to the point that they wanted to kill יהושע.
   יהושע davened to Hashem to to teach him these הלכות again, however Hashem responded that the תורה was 'לא בשמים היא'.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> משה was the only person who was to learn the תורה from Hashem.
   As for the fact that כלל ישראל wanted to kill יהושע, Hashem told him to command them to start gearing up for war to distract them. (Presented by רש"י על יהושע א ב, שפתי zikeinim ד, ה)

## Borders of ארץ ישראל
1. The given borders of ארץ ישראל were from the Midbar and Levanon in the southeast, the Euphrates River in the northeast, and the Mediterranean River on the west.

### Land Conquered outside of ארץ ישראל
1. Sifri דברים נא: Any land conquered outside of ארץ ישראל gained the kedusha of ארץ ישראל. (Presented by רש"י, One פשט in Radak, מלבי"ם)
### Given Borders
1. מלבי"ם: While כלל ישראל was *allowed* to add to ארץ ישראל by conquering land outside of of the given borders, כלל ישראל was *required* to conquer the land in the given borders.
## Location of "Lebanon" (Levanon)
1. רש"י, ביאורי הגר"א, One פשט in Radak: The Levanon (generally translated as "Lebanon") is in the southeast of ארץ ישראל (nowhere near modern-day Lebanon).
2. One פשט in Radak: The Levanon in the passuk is referred to as 'halevanon hazeh'. This is a specific Levanon; there are others. (This place was called Levanon because of a certain type of tree that grew there.)

## Don't Stray to the "Right" or "Left"
1. רלב"ג, ביאורי הגר"א: Don't add anything to the תורה or take anything away.
## אַל Vs לא: Keeping One's Rebbi in One's Mind
1. Chomes Anuch: The word 'לא' should have been used, not the word 'אַל' (according to [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/share/73fe88ad833f), לא is a command not to do something (like לֹא תִרְצָח. Also the next passuk says לֹֽא־יָמ֡וּשׁ סֵ֩פֶר֩ הַתּוֹרָ֨ה הַזֶּ֜ה), which would make more sense here, and אַל is more like giving advice (like אַל-תִּירָא)). This is hinting that this is not referring to not leaving the תורה, it is referring to something else. It is referring to not letting the image of יהושע's rebbi, משה, out of his mind, and this will help him keep the תורה.
# א ח
## Learning תורה Helps with Shleimus and Success
1. רלב"ג: Learning תורה helps one understand the deeper purposes and understandings of מצוות, as well as help develop a person toward shleimus. If someone follows the תורה and learns it, he will get special hashgacha and be successful.
## יהושע Carried a תורה
1. רש"י (לפי שפתי Zikeinim ח, ט): Hashem to יהושע to follow the words of *this* תורה.<span style="visibility:hidden">l</span> יהושע was carrying a תורה. He did this because he was a king (who has a הלכה to always carry a תורה).
## Requirements of Learning תורה
### Command to Always Learn תורה
1. Radak, Maharal: One must must learn only תורה until they finish it. At that point they may learn secular knowledge. One must learn whenever they find time that they are not busy with earning a פרנסה.
2. Menachos 99b: One must learn at least a little bit of תורה by day and by night. Saying Shema by night and by day can count for this chiyuv. (There may be a שיטה in that 'גמ that argues on this and understands this passuk to mean more like the Radak and Maharal).
### Speaking the Words of תורה
1. מנחה ketana, Chomes Anuch: One must speak the תורה with one's mouth. (Does this literally mean that one must speak the words?).
### Knowing and Teaching תורה
1. מלבי"ם: One must know and teach תורה.
### Delving into תורה
1. (Radak?), ביאורי הגר"א, מלבי"ם, מנחה Ketana, רש"י: One must delve into the understanding of תורה.
   מנחה Ketana, מלבי"ם: One must delve with the purpose of fulfilling the מצוות. (Delving into the תורה may actually bring one to keep the מצוות, as discussed shortly.)
2. ביאורי הגר"א: Delving into the תורה brings one to keep the מצוות.
## Success with Intelligence and Luck
1. מלבי"ם, ביאורי הגר"א: By following the תורה, Hashem would give יהושע success even without seichel, and Hashem will give יהושע the seichel to be successful. (The Malbim may be saying that learning תורה can sharpen one's mind.)

# א יא
## Preparing 'צדה'
1. Why did they need to prepare food? They still had מָן until the 16th of Nissan.
	1. רש"י: <span style="visibility:hidden">l</span>צדה here refers to other needs other than food, including weapons.
	2. Chomes Anuch (one פשט), מנחה Ketana: They didn't need the food. יהושע was telling them to prepare food as a subtle hint that this was all they needed to prepare. They didn't need to prepare weapons as Hashem would fight for them.
	3. Chomes Anuch (one פשט), Radak, Metzudas Dovid: They had the מָן but this was referring to preparing other foods (the Metzudas Dovid says this is referring to fruits) which they had. (The Chomes Anuch says that this is because we try not to rely on nissim.)
	4. מלבי"ם: This refers to grinding up the מָן.
