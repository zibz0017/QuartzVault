### Chronology of the Spies
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רלב"ג (1st פשט), רש"י:&lrm; יהושע sent the spies on the 5th of Nissan and they crossed into ארץ ישראל on that day. Beginning that night (the 6th of Nissan) they began to hide in the mountains for 3 days (ב כב-כג) (6th, 7th, and 8th day). That night, they crossed the ירדן and went back to יהושע &lrm;(ג א) (9th night). The next day morning (9th day) יהושע traveled to next to the ירדן. They stayed there for a day (ג א) and then crossed the ירדן (ד יט) (10th day).
   ביאורי הגר"א: (Question on רש"י) Once some of the events were happening during משה's aveilus, how can we figure out when משה was nifter? (The גר"א seems to be assuming that once we are saying that some of the events occurred during משה's aveilus, it is not logical to start assuming which events occurred after his aveilus ended.)
   שפתי זקנים, (Defense for רש"י): It is reasonable to assume that the נבואה where Hashem told יהושע to cross the ירדן in 3 days was right after the aveilus for משה ended because it couldn't have been during the aveilus for משה because a person can't receive נבואה while in a state of aveilus, but presumably, Hashem wasn't going to have כלל ישראל sit around idly. As such, the נבואה presumably came on the 30th day from משה's passing, which was the 6th of Nissan (since מקצת היום ככולו).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רלב"ג (2nd פשט), ביאורי הגר"א:&lrm; יהושע sent the spies on the 6th of Nissan and the came to רחב on that day. That night (7th) they began to hide in the mountains until the third day (until the 9th). That night they crossed the ירדן and went back to יהושע &lrm;(ג א) (9th night). The next day morning יהושע traveled to next to the ירדן and stayed there for a day &lrm;(ג א) (9th day). The next day, they crossed (ד יט) (10th day).</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מלבי"ם: &lrm;יהושע spoke to בני גד בני ראובן and sent the spies before the נבואה from Hashem at the beginning of פרק א. (Seemingly agrees with רש"י). יהושע did this because he was afraid of the conquest.</span>

## How the Spies Were Discovered
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מלבי"ם: There were three signs that gave the spies away:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The city was closed up and nobody could come in or out (during most times). They had come when the gates were closed (clearly they snuck in). This is implied by "אנשים באו".</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If they were travelling salesmen, they would have stayed near the gate so they could leave in the morning when the gates opened up (as they didn't stay open for long) so they could go to the next city and sell their wares. This is implied by "באו הנה" (they specifically came here and are clearly not rushing to leave). <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't know why they couldn't be planning to sell their goods in יריחו the next day. As the פסוק is about to say, they came to יריחו at night and so they presumably hadn't had time to sell their wares yet.</span></span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">They came at night. This is not a time to travel even during good times, and certainly by dangerous times. This is implied by "בלילה".</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מנחה קטנה:&lrm; פנחס and כלב were recognized as Jews and they were going to רחב. This alone wouldn't have been a giveaway about the fact that they were spies for perhaps they were going to רחב for זנות. This is also why the fact that they were traveling at night wasn't a giveaway, either: if they were going to have זנות, it would be normal to travel at night.</span>
   There were two possibilities about what gave them away:
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">כלל ישראל was punished extremely severely for having זנות with the bnos Moav, and so כלל ישראל would have set up guards to make sure that that wouldn't happen again.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">People weren't having זנות at this time due to fear of כלל ישראל impending arrival. אישת יפת תואר is מותר in times of war, so these people must have been going to רחב to be מזנה (as that was now מותר). This is somewhat implied in the next פסוק where the king's messengers tell רחב to bring out the men who "came to her, came to her house". This is the wrong order. It should put coming to her house before coming to her. The פסוק is saying that their "mission" was to be מזנה with her (in this context "הבאים" means "be מזנה with".)
   פנחס and כלב had, in fact, gone to רחב to get people to think that they weren't Jewish. It turned out that this was actually what gave away their mission.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מנחה קטנה (Additional פשט): כלב and פנחס were seen entering רחב's house through the window from outside the wall (her house was built into the wall).
   Clearly these people were Jewish because כנענים couldn't have tashmish because of their fear, and because it was night time and so nobody would recognize them if they went through the regular way. It must be that they were trying to avoid guards at the entrance of the city.
   Despite their efforts, כלב and פנחס were seen by sentinels on watch towers.
   The מנחה קטנה derives this from the fact that the פסוק used the wrong order ("came to her, came to her house"). The פסוק is saying that the people were telling רחב "bring out the people *who came to you*. We know they are guilty because *they came* directly *to your house* and not through the city".</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ראשון לציון: When men go to a זונה, they do not go with someone else. (פנחס and כלב thought that the people would assume that one of them was the master and the other was his assistant and so this would not be suspicious.)</span>

# ב יז
## Putting תנאים on the שבועה after Climbing out of רחב's House
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אברבנאל: There is a הלכה that when one is trapped and they are forced to make a שבועה, it does not count. As such, the spies made the שבועה a second time after they got out, because that made the שבועה binding. 
   This also explains why they are seemingly repeating the תנאים that they had already made when they were in her house.
   Additionally, when the פסוק says "נְקִיִּ֣ם אֲנַ֔חְנוּ מִשְּׁבֻעָתֵ֥ךְ הַזֶּ֖ה אֲשֶׁ֥ר הִשְׁבַּעְתָּֽנוּ", it does not mean "we are free from the שבועה *if you violate the following*" and then explain what the conditions are. Rather, the פסוק means, "we are free from the שבועה, *period*" because they had made it when they were forced. They then proceeded to basically repeat the שבועה they had made, now that they were free, to make it binding.</span>
