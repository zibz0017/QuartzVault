### Chronology of the Spies
1. רלב"ג (1st פשט), רש"י: &lrm; יהושע sent the spies on the 5th of Nissan.
   and they crossed into ארץ ישראל on that day. Beginning that night (the 6th of Nissan) they began to hide in the mountains for 3 days (ב כב-כג) (6th, 7th, and 8th day). That night, they crossed the Yarden and went back to יהושע &lrm;(ג א) (9th night). The next day morning (9th day) יהושע traveled to next to the Yarden. They stayed there for a day (ג א) and then crossed the Yarden (ד יט) (10th day).
   ביאורי הגר"א: (Question on רש"י) Once some of the events were happening during משה's aveilus, how can we figure out when משה was nifter? (The גר"א seems to be assuming that once we are saying that some of the events occurred during משה's aveilus, it is not logical to start assuming which events occurred after his aveilus ended.)
   שפתי Zikeinim, (Defense for רש"י): It is reasonable to assume that the נבואה where Hashem told יהושע to cross the Yarden in 3 days was right after the aveilus for משה ended because it couldn't have been during the aveilus for משה because a person can't receive נבואה while in a state of aveilus, but presumably, Hashem wasn't going to have כלל ישראל sit around idly. As such, the נבואה presumably came on the 30th day from משה's passing, which was the 6th of Nissan (since מקצת היום ככולו).

## "אנשים"
1. רד"ק: These spies were "אנשים", people on a very high מדרגה, as opposed to the original spies in the times of משה. (This must have been an extraordinary level, because even though they failed, the original spies were people on a tremendous level, as well.)
## The Spies Were "חרש"
1. רש"י (1st פשט), תרגום (according to רש"י), רד"ק (4th פשט): They acted like deaf-mutes so the כנענים would speak freely in front of them.
   שפתי Zikeinim (Question of this פשט): The next word in the פסוק is "לאמר" which means that they are supposed to say something over. If they are deaf-mutes, then they obviously not going to be speaking.
2. רש"י (2nd פשט), רד"ק (3rd פשט): They acted like pottery salesmen ("חרש" is hinting to "חרס" which is a potter). "לאמר" means that they would go around announcing that they have pottery for sale. (It seems that the words after "לאמר" are not the words that they said. It seems that the way to read the פסוק is something like "act like potters and announce (that you are potters. Meanwhile) go around and spy out the land".)
3. רד"ק (2nd פשט), תרגום (according to the רד"ק), מלבי"ם: They were sent in secret ("חרש" literally means deaf-mute, so in this context in means "silence") so כלל ישראל wouldn't realize that they had been sent. יהושע did this because he was concerned that כלל ישראל would think the spies were a sign that יהושע lacked confidence in Hashem, and this would get them nervous and start doubting. ## The Purpose of the Spys' Mission
### Why יהושע Sent Spies
1. רלב"ג, רד"ק: יהושע sent spies so that they would give כלל ישראל (and יהושע, according to the רלב"ג) confidence reporting how fearful the nations were of them.
   (The רלב"ג contrasts this with spying on how strong the nations and their defenses were. This seems interesting as that could also have been used to build confidence in כלל ישראל. It seems that specifically gauging the mood of the כנענים had a stronger (positive) impact on כלל ישראל's confidence.)
   (This is why the spies left immediately after speaking to רחב and they didn't go spy out the rest of the land like יהושע had told them: because their purpose was to gauge what all the people were thinking, and they had gotten that from רחב.) ^929a7d
2. מלבי"ם, מנחה קטנה:  &lrm;יהושע sent the spies because that was what his rebbi משה רבינו did. (See [[#What about how They Failed so Bad by משה?|below]] for why יהושע wasn't concerned that they would fail as they had by משה.)
3. חומת אנך: As said [[#^534e37|above]], יהושע sent the spies to find the weaknesses of the magical mazikim that the people of shechem had set up to defend the city.
### Why יהושע Wasn't Concerned about how They Failed so Bad by משה
1. ביאורי הגר"א (says the fourth reason given by the מלבי"ם here), מלבי"ם: &lrm;יהושע sent the spies because that was what his rebbi משה רבינו did. However, there were 4 reasons for why יהושע was more confident in this group:
	1. By משה, the people sent the spies, so when they came back, the people were more inclined to believe them. Over here, יהושע sent them, not the people.
	2. משה sent the spies from far away, and so it wasn't clear that they were going to enter. As such, they went with a mindset of trying to figure out if they could do it or not. יהושע sent these spies right by the border. They were definitely going to enter. As such, the spies went in with a mindset of trying to find the best way to do that. <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I'm not precisely sure that this is what he is saying.</span>
	3. משה sent the spies with the mission to see whether the land was good or not and how strong the people were. יהושע simply sent them on a mission to find the easiest way to conquer the land. (This is partly why משה needed to send leaders of every shevet, because only they know what their shevet really needs. When trying to find the weakness of the land, leaders were not needed and one person from each shevet was not needed, so that was what יהושע sent.)

## How the Spies Were Discovered
1. מלבי"ם: There were three signs that gave the spies away:
	1. The city was closed up and nobody could come in or out (during most times). They had come when the gates were closed (clearly they snuck in). This is implied by "אנשים באו".
	2. If they were travelling salesmen, they would have stayed near the gate so they could leave in the morning when the gates opened up (as they didn't stay open for long) so they could go to the next city and sell their wares. This is implied by "באו הנה" (they specifically came here and are clearly not rushing to leave). <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't know why they couldn't be planning to sell their goods in Yericho the next day. As the פסוק is about to say, they came to Yericho at night and so they presumably hadn't had time to sell their wares yet.</span>
	3. They came at night. This is not a time to travel even during good times, and certainly by dangerous times. This is implied by "בלילה".
2. מנחה קטנה:&lrm; פנחס and כלב were recognized as Jews and they were going to רחב. This alone wouldn't have been a giveaway about the fact that they were spies for perhaps they were going to רחב for זנות. This is also why the fact that they were traveling at night wasn't a giveaway, either: if they were going to have זנות, it would be normal to travel at night.
   There were two possibilities about what gave them away:
	1. כלל ישראל was punished extremely severely for having זנות with the bnos Moav, and so כלל ישראל would have set up guards to make sure that that wouldn't happen again.
	2. People weren't having זנות at this time due to fear of כלל ישראל impending arrival. אישת יפת תואר is מותר in times of war, so these people must have been going to רחב to be מזנה (as that was now מותר). This is somewhat implied in the next פסוק where the king's messengers tell רחב to bring out the men who "came to her, came to her house". This is the wrong order. It should put coming to her house before coming to her. The פסוק is saying that their "mission" was to be מזנה with her (in this context "הבאים" means "be מזנה with".)
   פנחס and כלב had, in fact, gone to רחב to get people to think that they weren't Jewish. It turned out that this was actually what gave away their mission.
3. מנחה קטנה (Additional פשט): כלב and פנחס were seen entering רחב's house through the window from outside the wall (her house was built into the wall).
   Clearly these people were Jewish because כנענים couldn't have tashmish because of their fear, and because it was night time and so nobody would recognize them if they went through the regular way. It must be that they were trying to avoid guards at the entrance of the city.
   Despite their efforts, כלב and פנחס were seen by sentinels on watch towers.
   The מנחה קטנה derives this from the fact that the פסוק used the wrong order ("came to her, came to her house"). The פסוק is saying that the people were telling רחב "bring out the people *who came to you*. We know they are guilty because *they came* directly *to your house* and not through the city".
