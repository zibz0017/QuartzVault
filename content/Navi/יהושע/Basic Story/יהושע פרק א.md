# &lrm;א א
## Why ספר יהושע Starts with the Word 'ויהי'
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why does ספר יהושע start with the word 'ויהי' and particularly with the letter 'ו' which is a letter that connects to the previous topic?</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רש"י, מצודת דוד (Addressing the letter 'ו'): &lrm;ספר יהושע is connected to the end of the תורה. The תורה ends off with the death of משה, and יהושע begins with talking about what happened after that.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רד"ק: Many stories start with the word 'ויהי' even though they are not connected to the previous story.</span>
## When יהושע Received This נבואה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">שפתי זקנים ב: This occurred 30 days after the death of משה as יהושע was not able to receive נבואה when he was in aveilus.</span>
## &lrm;משה Referred to as 'עבד Hashem'
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why is משה referred to as an עבד Hashem?</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מנחה קטנה (Addressing why משה is called עבד Hashem particularly over here): Hashem doesn't 'associate His name' with people when they are alive (with few exceptions). Now that משה had died, Hashem can 'associate His name' with him.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רד"ק: Someone who does everything for the sake of Hashem (meaning, even all of the things about עולם הזה) is called an עבד Hashem.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מלבי"ם: &lrm;משה had taught so many people during his life and because of him they were serving Hashem. Because of this, even though משה wasn't alive, he was still serving Hashem through these people that he had taught.</span>
## &lrm;יהושע Referred to as 'משרת משה'
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why is יהושע referred to as 'משרת משה'?</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רלב"ג, ביאורי הגר"א, מלבי"ם: The reason why יהושע was זוכה to נבואה and to be the leader of כלל ישראל was because he was the משרת משה and it is greater to serve a great person than it is to learn from them ("גדול שמושה יותר מלמודה").</span>
# &lrm;א א-ב
## Death of משה Linked to יהושע Receiving נבואה ('אחרי מות משה… ויאמר ה' אל יהושע' and 'משה כבדי מת ועתה קום עבר')
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The פסוקים seems to be linking the death of משה to יהושע receiving נבואה and crossing the ירדן.</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רלב"ג (On יהושע receiving נבואה): Hashem makes sure that כלל ישראל has a נביא. When משה was alive, there was no need for יהושע to be able to receive נבואה and so he didn't receive נבואה. Only now that משה had died did יהושע begin receiving נבואה.</span>
	   In addition, if משה led כלל ישראל into ארץ ישראל, they would have been able to defeat all of their enemies. However, כלל ישראל didn't deserve this, and, because of this, enemies were left in ארץ ישראל who caused problems for a long time. (The רלב"ג says more over [[#^2f44ab|here]].)
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מנחה קטנה (On יהושע receiving נבואה): משה davened to Hashem to make sure that כלל ישראל would not be left without a leader. משה's תפילות were answered and shortly after משה died יהושע became the leader.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ביאורי הגר"א, רלב"ג, מצודת דוד, מלבי"ם (On יהושע leading כלל ישראל across the ירדן): &lrm;יהושע couldn't lead כלל ישראל across the ירדן during משה's lifetime because יהושע couldn't be the leader (משה was the leader) and משה couldn't cross the ירדן (because of his עבירה).</span>
	   Now that משה died neither of these reasons applied and so יהושע was able to lead כלל ישראל across. (The מצודת דוד only discusses the second point and the ביאורי הגר"א is not clear which point he is referring to.) ^2f44ab

# &lrm;א ב
## Forgotten הלכות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">.תמורה טז: The 'גמ says that right before משה died, he asked יהושע if he had any questions. יהושע responded that he had already asked all of his questions to משה during the time they had spent together.</span>
   This bothered משה (seemingly the fact that he was no longer needed). As a punishment, יהושע forgot many הלכות.
   כלל ישראל was furious with יהושע for causing this loss of תורה to the point that they wanted to kill יהושע.
   יהושע davened to Hashem to to teach him these הלכות again, however Hashem responded that the תורה was 'לא בשמים היא'.&lrm; משה was the only person who was to learn the תורה from Hashem.
   As for the fact that כלל ישראל wanted to kill יהושע, Hashem told him to command them to start gearing up for war to distract them. (Presented by רש"י על יהושע א ב, שפתי זקנים ד, ה)

## Borders of ארץ ישראל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The given borders of ארץ ישראל were from the מדבר and Levanon in the southeast, the Euphrates River in the northeast, and the Mediterranean River on the west.</span>

### Land Conquered outside of ארץ ישראל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ספרי דברים נא: Any land conquered outside of ארץ ישראל gained the קדושה of ארץ ישראל. (Presented by רש"י, One פשט in רד"ק, מלבי"ם)</span>
### Given Borders
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מלבי"ם: While כלל ישראל was *allowed* to add to ארץ ישראל by conquering land outside of of the given borders, כלל ישראל was *required* to conquer the land in the given borders.</span>
## Location of "Lebanon" (Levanon)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רש"י, ביאורי הגר"א, One פשט in רד"ק: The Levanon (generally translated as "Lebanon") is in the southeast of ארץ ישראל (nowhere near modern-day Lebanon).</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One פשט in רד"ק: The Levanon in the פסוק is referred to as 'halevanon הזה'. This is a specific Levanon; there are others. (This place was called Levanon because of a certain type of tree that grew there.)</span>

## &lrm;אַל Vs לא: Keeping One's Rebbi in One's Mind
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חומת אנך: The word 'לא' should have been used, not the word 'אַל' (according to [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/share/73fe88ad833f)), לא is a command not to do something (like לֹא תִרְצָח. Also the next פסוק says לֹֽא־יָמ֡וּשׁ סֵ֩פֶר֩ הַתּוֹרָ֨ה הַזֶּ֜ה), which would make more sense here, and אַל is more like giving advice (like אַל-תִּירָא)). This is hinting that this is not referring to not leaving the תורה, it is referring to something else. It is referring to not letting the image of יהושע's rebbi, משה, out of his mind, and this will help him keep the תורה.</span>
# &lrm;א ח
## Learning תורה Helps with שלמות and Success
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רלב"ג: Learning תורה helps one understand the deeper purposes and understandings of מצוות, as well as help develop a person toward שלמות. If someone follows the תורה and learns it, he will get special השגחה and be successful.</span>
## &lrm;יהושע Carried a תורה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רש"י (לפי שפתי זקנים ח, ט): Hashem to יהושע to follow the words of *this* תורה.&lrm; יהושע was carrying a תורה. He did this because he was a king (who has a הלכה to always carry a תורה). (Also see [[#&lrm;יהושע Status as a King|here]].)</span>

1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רד"ק, מהר"ל: One must must learn only תורה until they finish it. At that point they may learn secular knowledge. One must learn whenever they find time that they are not busy with earning a פרנסה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Menachos 99b: One must learn at least a little bit of תורה by day and by night. Saying Shema by night and by day can count for this chiyuv. (There may be a שיטה in that 'גמ that argues on this and understands this פסוק to mean more like the רד"ק and מהר"ל).</span>
### Speaking the Words of תורה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מנחה קטנה, חומת אנך: One must speak the תורה with one's mouth. (Does this literally mean that one must speak the words?).</span>
### Knowing and Teaching תורה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מלבי"ם: One must know and teach תורה.</span>
### Delving into תורה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(רד"ק?), ביאורי הגר"א, מלבי"ם, מנחה קטנה, רש"י: One must delve into the understanding of תורה.</span>

# &lrm;א יא
## Preparing 'צדה'
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Why did they need to prepare food? They still had מָן until the 16th of Nissan.</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רש"י: &lrm;צדה here refers to other needs other than food, including weapons.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חומת אנך (one פשט), מנחה קטנה: They didn't need the food. יהושע was telling them to prepare food as a subtle hint that this was all they needed to prepare. They didn't need to prepare weapons as Hashem would fight for them.</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חומת אנך (one פשט), רד"ק, מצודת דוד: They had the מָן but this was referring to preparing other foods (the מצודת דוד says this is referring to fruits) which they had. (The חומת אנך says that this is because we try not to rely on נסים.)</span>
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מלבי"ם: This refers to grinding up the מָן.</span>

# &lrm;א יד
## Strength of בני גד and בני ראובן
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Miyuchas Limahari KRA: בני גד and בני ראובן were so strong that in a single cut, they could slice from someone's head all the way down to halfway through their body. This is פשט in the פסוק וטרף זרוע רף קדקד (דברים לג כ), which is explained to mean that they could cut from the head to opposite the forearm (around halfway down the body).</span>
# &lrm;א יג-יד
## Why בני גד and בני ראובן Had to Go with כלל ישראל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מנחה קטנה: &lrm;כלל ישראל was promised that they would win supernaturally without fighting, so why did בני גד and בני ראובן have to go with them (and leave their wife and kids behind for years)?</span>
   This was because they promised משה that they would and so יהושע couldn't be mochel that.
   Also, this was so that the rest of כלל ישראל wouldn't be jealous that בני גד and בני ראובן already had their inheritance and the rest of of כלל ישראל did not.
   This is hinted to in the פסוק which says "ועזרתם אותם", which generically refers to help, because בני גד and בני ראובן weren't really going to be doing anything.

### Listening to יהושע
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רלב"ג (One פשט), רד"ק: &lrm;בני גד בני ראובן said that they wouldn't listen to יהושע if he told them to do an עבירה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רלב"ג (One פשט), מלבי"ם:&lrm; בני גד בני ראובן said that they would only listen to יהושע if he would properly lead the nation.</span>
(This may be based on the discussion about what is meant by "רק חזק ואמץ", discussed [[#&lrm;חזק ואמץ Mentioned Three times|above]].)
### &lrm;יהושע Status as a King
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מצודת דוד, מנחה קטנה, רלב"ג: &lrm;בני גד and בני ראובן were going to be chayav misah if they did not listen to יהושע because יהושע had the status of a king and a נביא. יהושע wouldn't be able to be mochel because a king is not allowed to be mochel on his כבוד. (Also see [[#&lrm;יהושע Carried a תורה|here]].)</span>
   (מצודת דוד learns that יהושע couldn't be mochel from the words רק חזק ואמץ. &lrm;מנחה קטנה gets this from this from the fact it says יומת (which is more impersonal, "he shall be killed") as opposed to תמות ("You shall kill him", somewhat implying it is up to יהושע to decide)).
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חומת אנך, מלבי"ם: (Double לשון of כל איש אשר ימרה את פיך and ולא ישמע אל דבריך) Whether they actively go against your word or even just passively don't listen to you.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חומת אנך: &lrm;(יומת, perhaps vs תמות) The person shall be killed without being warned.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מלבי"ם: &lrm;(לכל אשר תצונו) The person shall be killed, even if they don't listen to little things that יהושע says.</span>
