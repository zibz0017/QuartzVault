# אלקנה
## Came from "הָרָמָתַיִם צוֹפִים"
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The simple understanding is that this refers to two tall mountains close to each other from which one could look from one to the other. ("הָרָמָתַיִם צוֹפִים" literally means "two heights looking".)[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה was from the 200 נביאים in his generation.[^2] ("מָתַיִם" \[from "הָרָמָתַיִם"] is similar to "מָאתַיִם" which means "200" and "צוֹפִים" can mean "seers".[^3] Additionally, the "ר" from "הָרָמָתַיִם" also hints to the 200 seers.[^4]
   This is actually referring to a place called "הרמתה", as we know from elsewhere that אלקנה came from הרמתה. The strange לשון tells us to be דורש.&lrm;[^4])</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה was a descendant of קרח's children. קרח's children were at the "height of the world". Hashem placed them in the "highest part" of גיהנם (the least bad part). (The פסוק is saying that אלקנה came from people who were "at the heights \[of the world]". "רָמָתַיִם" means "heights".)[^5] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not sure why the 'גמ refers to the highest part of גיהנם as the "heights of the world". I guess according to this פשט, they must have had children already or else how could אלקנה have come from them.</span></span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">An alternative method of getting the same idea is that the פסוק is telling us that אלקנה came from נביאים, namely, the children of קרח. (The פסוק is read as "מן הרמתים *מן* צופים", meaning, "he came from the heights \[referring to where he physically lived]; he came from seers \[meaning, נביאים]".[^6]</span>
## Geneology
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה was from שבט לוי and a descendant of קרח.&lrm;[^7]</span>
## The Greatness of אלקנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה was a נביא. (His נבואות were not needed for future generations so they were not recorded. There were many נביאים )[^2]</span>
2. See [[#Causing Others to Be עולה רגל]].
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה was זוכה to be the father of שמואל.
   (It is indicated that this required a person of greatness from the words "ויהי איש אחד". The word "אחד" tells us that אלקנה was a person uniquely suited to be the father of שמואל.)[^8]</span>
# &lrm;אלקנה Going to שִלֹה
## Why אלקנה Was Going to שִלֹה
### &lrm;עולה רגל Vs סתם Going
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה would consistently go to שִלֹה. Either this was because:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">He was עולה רגל.&lrm;[^9]
	   (Even though the פסוקים seem to say that he only went once a year, that means that he went with his whole family \[including women and children] once per year. He, himself, as well as the men in his household went every year.[^10])</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">He went once a year to bring קרבנות.&lrm;[^11]</span>
	   
	<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not sure about what the רד"ק holds. The רד"ק says he went every year, but his לשון seems to imply that it was to be עולה רגל and he also says that he was bringing קרבנות for the מועד. However, this may have been קרבן שלמים.</span></span>
### Daven
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה went to שִלֹה to daven. ("להשתחות" always means to daven.[^12]) Davening at the place of the hashraas hashchina is greater.[^10]</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(There is a מחלוקת about whether תפילה is equal to קרבנות or whether it is greater. There is proof from this פסוק that תפילה is greater because the פסוק first says "להשתחות" (which refers to davening, as just discussed) and then says "ולזבוח".&lrm;[^13]</span>
## Causing Others to Be עולה רגל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה would encourage his town to be be עולה.&lrm;[^14] Similarly, he may have lead his whole town up to be עולה רגל. They would wait for him to go. (This is, in fact, why it is referred to as "ערו", meaning *his* town.)[^15]
   </span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אלקנה would go by a different way every time he would be עולה רגל so different people would see him and be inspired and עולה רגל, as well.[^16] He eventually got the whole כלל ישראל to be עולה רגל.&lrm;[^17]
   (This is derived from the extra words "מעירו מימים ימימה". This is learned to mean that, while he would "come from his city every year" other things about his trip would not be the same \[i.e. the path he would take][^18] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">There is an alternative way this is indicated. See שפתי זקנים ג</span></span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is, in fact, one reason why אלקנה did not live in שִלֹה: He wanted to inspire people to be עולה רגל. The זכות of being מזכה the רבים is so great that it was worth not living in שִלֹה.&lrm;[^19] (Presumably, since אלקנה was a לוי, he would have done the עבודה. The מנחה קטנה may be saying that it was even worth giving that up for this זכות).</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because אלקנה inspired כלל ישראל, he was זוכה to שמואל who would inspire כלל ישראל.&lrm;[^20]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is difficult the understand how to פשט that אלקנה only went every year rather than every רגל fits with these פשטים that he went by a different way or that he led his city.&lrm;[^18] Perhaps פשט is that אלקנה may have influenced people to סתם go up to שִלֹה presumably to benefit from the positive influence there. The אברבנאל says that even according to the שיטה that אלקנה was being עולה רגל, women are not חייב. The reason why אלקנה brought them was to inspire them by seeing שִלֹה.&lrm;[^21] Perhaps the same is true for other people that אלקנה would try to inspire.[^22]</span>
### Why Others Were not Going
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The children of עלי were כהנים in שִלֹה and they bothered the people who would come.
   The נביא is praising אלקנה going up, regardless.[^23]</span>
# &lrm;חנה And פננה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנה was the "main wife".[^24] In fact, חנה gave over פננה to get married to אלקנה to have children in the hopes that she, too, would be זוכה to have children.[^25] She was correct in that this was part of the זכות that allowed to her to have children.[^26]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנה was married to אלקנה for 10 years at which point אלקנה married פננה (as one is supposed to marry a new wife if one does not have children after 10 years). פננה had 10 children in 8 years. At that point, חנה got pregnant and had שמואל, meaning, חנה was married 19 years before having שמואל.&lrm;[^26]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנה was naturally able to have children (as opposed to many of the אמהות who were עקרות). Hashem kept חנה from having children בדרך נס.&lrm;[^21]
   Part of why חנה did not have children was because Hashem values the תפילות of צדיקים (just like by שרה, &lrm;רבקא, and רחל).[^27]
   Another part was that Hashem saw that פננה was less loved than חנה so Hashem gave her children to increase אלקנה's love for her.[^21]</span>
## &lrm;פננה Taunting חנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פננה would taunt חנה about the fact that she did not have children.[^28]</span>
### &lrm;פננה's Intent
  1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חז"ל tell us that פננה only had proper intentions in mind. She taunted חנה so that she would daven harder.[^29] In fact, פננה was a complete צדקת and it was difficult for her to hurt חנה.&lrm;[^30] There are numerous indications to this:</span>
	  1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The פסוקים imply that פננה would only do this when they would go to שִלֹה. If פננה was just trying to taunt חנה, why wouldn't she always do this? This was clearly because תפילות are more powerful in the place of the hashraas hashchina.[^31]</span>
	  2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The פסוק says that she did this "because חנה didn't have children". That is obviously not a reason to taunt someone.[^32] (I think that this שיטה is saying that the proper way for the נביא to have written this would have been to say "פננה taunted חנה *about* her kids *because*…".[^22])</span>
	  3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The נביא refers to פננה as the co-wife of חנה. Additionally, she was the wife of אלקנה. Neither of these would be true about a רשע.&lrm;[^33]</span>
	  4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem would not cause something good to come out from something evil.[^33] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">There seem to be many examples to the contrary. Wasn't it, somehow, good for כלל ישראל that we were enslaved by פרעה? In fact, isn't every bad thing somehow the best thing that could have happened?</span></span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some learn that פננה really had bad intentions. She may have been jealous of the fact that אלקנה loved חנה more and showed her more affection.[^34] She would say that she was trying to help motivate חנה, but that was really not her goal.[^35]</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(These שיטות may really just mean that פננה only had a דקי דקות of this bad מידה but that clouded her judgement and caused her to try and provoke חנה to daven harder when that was really not the right thing to do. However, the לשון of the בן יהוידע seems that פננה acted with more premeditation to me.[^22])</span>
### What Ended up Happening
   1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">פננה was correct; the extra power of חנה's תפילות led to her having a child.[^36]
      However, this was still improper. פננה was severely punished. 8 of her 10 children died, and if not for חנה specifically davening on behalf of the last 2, they may have died, as well.[^26]</span>
#### Why פננה Was Punished
   1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the שיטות that say that פננה had bad intentions, it makes sense why she was punished.</span>
   2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to those who say she had proper intentions, perhaps there may have been a דקי דקות of bad מדות mixed in there and she was punished for that. (I am unsure if according to these שיטות, this דקי דקות caused פננה to make the wrong decision and so she was punished for that, or if she actually made the right decision, but since she had slightly improper intention, she was punished for that.)</span>
   3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Chaim Shmuelevitz says that when one hurts someone else, the natural consequence of this is that they will be hurt back, even if they did it with fully pure intentions. למשל, if one sticks their hand in a fire, regardless of how pure the person's intentions were, the person will be burned. (We certainly see from here how bad it is to hurt someone when we don't have pure intentions.)[^30]</span>
# Did חנה Eat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">There is discussion amongst the מפרשים about whether חנה actually ate, or whether the time for eating had passed but she had not eaten. The basic discussion centers around פסוק ז which, says that she didn't eat, פסוק טו which says that she didn't drink, and פסוק ט which seems to either say that *she* ate and drank or that *people* ate and drank.</span></span>
# &lrm;עלי הכהן's Rise is Greatness
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">מפרשים here say that עלי became the כהן גדול and/or the שופט on this day. This is implied by the word ישב in פסוק ט which implies that he sat the, but not before. I am not sure how this implies that he became the שופט, but it implied he became the כהן גדול because only the כהן גדול was allowed to sit in the עזרה.</span></span>
# &lrm;חנה Going to Daven
## Why She Particularly Davened now
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Until now, she had relied on the תפילות of אלקנה, who was a big צדיק. However, אלקנה had given up that she would ever have children. This motivated חנה to daven herself.[^37] (She obviously davened before this. This means that this made her daven even harder than she had been.[^38])</span> ^aa149c
## Why Her תפילות Were Accepted
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She davened from a place of anguish[^39] (partially caused by פננה, as discussed [[#What Ended up Happening|above]]).</span> ^f26d43
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She had complete כוונה on the fact that only Hashem could help her, similar to what we [[#^aa149c|mentioned previously]].[^40]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She cried, and the gates of tears are never closed.[^41]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She took a נדר upon herself.[^42]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to some, she was davening right next to the part of the wall of the משכן surrounding the קדש הקדשים.&lrm;[^43] (Even according to those who disagree and say she was davening opposite the היכל&lrm;[^44] presumably agree that there is כח to davening there, as well.)</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She ate from the קרבן שלמים that אלקנה brought. Eating from a קרבן makes one more קדוש and more likely for their תפילות to be answered.[^45]</span>
7. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנה davened to Hashem to remember her זכותים,&lrm;[^46] particularly the fact that she was מקפיד on the מצוות of חלה, &lrm;נדה, &lrm;and שבת candles.[^47]</span>
## &lrm;חנה's תפילה
### Disrespect to Hashem
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנה's תפילה seemed to be extremely biting. However, the 'גמ says that one who is in tremendous pain is not held responsible for this.[^48] (As noted [[#^f26d43|above]], the fact that she davened from a place of such terrible pain was actually part of the reason why her תפילות were accepted.)</span>
### Threatening to Become a סוטה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to one opinion in the 'גמ, &lrm;חנה may have "threatened" to get אלקנה to warn her about secluding with a man, and then secluding with him (but obviously not actually have relations) and then be forced to drink the מי סוטה. Since she was innocent, she would then have a child.[^49]
   (The פני יהושע understands this to mean that she wasn't actually going to do this as this would involve violating numerous איסורים \[such as יחוד]. She was arguing that if she was somewhat promiscuous, she would be able to put herself in a situation that she would be able to "force" Hashem to give her a child, as such, she shouldn't lose out because she is properly צניעות and wouldn't do this.[^50])</span>
### Referring to Hashem as "ה' צבקות"
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is the first time it is mentioned that someone in תנ"ך referred to Hashem as "ה' צבקות". &lrm;חנה was saying with this phrase that Hashem has the entire world with everything in it at his disposal. Certainly he can give her just one son.[^51]
   According to those who say that אלקנה was in שִלֹה to be עולה רגל, &lrm;חנה was noting the masses of Jews who were there being עולה רגל and in Hashem's dominion.[^52]</span>
### What חנה Asked for ("זרע אנשים")
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">During her תפילה, &lrm;חנה mentioned the words "זרע אנשים". It seems that this was a combination of a תפילה and נבואה.&lrm;[^53] The מדרש and גמ'&rlm; learn these words to mean that she was davening for/having a נבואה that her son would be:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A צדיק.&lrm;[^54]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Wise.[^54]</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A נביא.&lrm;[^55]</span>
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As great as משה and אהרן.&lrm;[^55]</span>
	5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Anoint two kings (דוד and שאול).[^56]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Others learn that she was actually davening for a son who would be pretty average in basically every regard.[^57]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The simple reading of the פסוק is that she davened for a son,[^58] but the מדרש learns that she davened for sons and daughters.[^55]
   The מלבי"ם learns פשט that for this first child, she was davening that he would be a son but that Hashem should give her other sons and daughters, as well.[^46] (Perhaps she was saying that the נדר that he would be a נזיר and given to Hashem all of his life would only apply to this first child.[^22])</span>
### What חנה Promised
#### "נתתיו לה' כל ימי חייו"
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;"><span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">There is further discussion amongst the מפרשים about whether the line that חנה would give her child to Hashem was a part of the תפילה (she was davening that the child should be fitting to be given to Hashem) or that this was part of the נדר. This is somewhat centered around the question that אלקנה was a לוי and so the child would "belong to Hashem" anyway.</span></span>
#### "מורה לא יעלה על ראשו"
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת in the משנה about whether חנה was promising that שמואל would be a נזיר all of his life ("מורה" would mean "razor". חנה was saying that שמואל would never cut his hair, a classic הלכה of being a נזיר) or she was davening that he should only have the fear of Hashem and not man ("מורה" should be read as "מורא" and the פסוק would mean "fear \[of man] should never be upon him \[only fear of Hashem].[^59]</span>
### Lengthy תפילה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חנה davened for a long time. The מדרש says that one is not supposed to daven for a long time and because of this, חנה accidentally caused שמואל to die young.
   She promised that שמואל would serve Hashem "עד עולם". However, regarding לויים this terminology is actually used to mean 50 years (לויים serve Hashem until they are 50, and the לשון used there is "עד עולם"). As such, שמואל served in the משכן for 50 years, and then he died. (He died at 52 years old because he didn't serve in the משכן until he was 2.)[^60]</span>
# Conversation between חנה and עלי
## Why עלי Thought חנה Was Drunk
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are a variety of reasons עלי suspected that חנה was drunk. There was a variety of reasons for this:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She was whispering her תפילות and in those days, people davened out loud.[^61]</span>
		1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מהרש"א asks on this פשט that the גמ'&rlm; says that we learn from חנה that one is supposed to daven quietly.[^62] If חנה was davening as one was supposed to, why did עלי think that she was drunk?[^63]
		   (For an answer, see below.)</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One davens the standard davening quietly. However, one's additional תפילות one does out loud. חנה, however, davened for a long time (clearly finishing the regular תפילות) and never raised her voice.
	   (This would answer the מהרש"א's question on רש"י, above.)[^64]</span>
		1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that even in those days, they at least had some basic structure to davening (not necessarily an exact נוסח but at least a structure).[^22]</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">People would daven at least part of their תפילות out loud, but חנה davened for a long time and only davened quietly the whole time.[^65]
	   (This may really be the same as the previous answer.)</span>
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She was davening for an exceedingly long time.[^63]</span>
	5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are two categories of davening: One davens directly to Hashem to fulfill his request, or one davens that the request is fulfilled through מלאכים and other intermediaries.
	   If one davens directly to Hashem, they rely on Hashem's רחמנות to give them what they want. In that case, their תפילה is relatively short (they don't need to justify why they deserve what they ask for. They are relying on Hashem's mercy and חסד) and it is also quiet (since Hashem knows people's thoughts).
	   When one davens for help through the מלאכים, one davens for a long time (they need to justify why they deserve what they ask for) and out loud (the מלאכים don't know one's thoughts).
	   עלי didn't understand why חנה was davening quietly (as if she was davening to Hashem) but for a long time (as if she was davening for help through מלאכים)[^66]</span>
	   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are a few points I found interesting from this:[^22]</span>
		1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">These two forms of davening. I am unsure how that applies to our own davening. Which type are we doing?</span>
		2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that one actually davens to the מלאכים for help. I thought that one is only allowed to daven to Hashem.</span>
		3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that מלאכים don't know what people are thinking.</span>
		4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">I am unsure why one can't just whisper their תפילות to the מלאכים. Does one need to get the attention of the מלאכים when one is davening to them?</span>
