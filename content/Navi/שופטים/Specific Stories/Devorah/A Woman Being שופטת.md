# Issue with a Woman Being שופטת
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A woman is not allowed to be a judge.[^1]</span>
# Did She Actually Judge the People?
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Numerous מפרשים explicitly say she judged the people,[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8] and/or she had tremendous knowledge of תורה&lrm;[^9][^10] (in addition to the fact that the simple reading of the נביא is that she judged the people).</span>
# Why Devorah Was Allowed
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There were very few talmidei חכמים around at that time[^11] (and so it presumably was a הוראת שעה. See next reason.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It was a הוראת שעה&lrm;[^12] (presumably because there were few talmidei חכמים. See previous reason.)</span>
