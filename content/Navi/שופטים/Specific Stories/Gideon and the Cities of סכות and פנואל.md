# Basic Storyline With Minimal Peirush
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Gideon and his small force of 300 men[^1] spearheaded a counterattack against Midian. The attack was highly successful and in miraculous fashion, Gideon's force, with some assistance, had killed 120,000 of the original 135,000 man army[^2] and were chasing down the Midiani kings, זבח and צלמנע,&lrm;[^3] who were with the rest of the army.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Gideon's force was exhausted and so Gideon asked the towns of סכות and פנואל for food. However, they rudely refused. They minimized Gideon's accomplishment so far and doubted that he would be able to capture זבח and צלמנע.&lrm;[^5]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Gideon responded to the people of סכות that after defeating the Midiani army and capturing their kings, he would whip them with desert thorns[^6][^7] (or throw them onto a bed of thorns and tramp them[^6]) as punishment. He said similarly to פנואל but he also added that he would destroy the fortified tower that was there, as well.[^8]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">After Gideon defeated the Midiani force, he returned to סכות whipped the people.[^9] (The פסוק is not clear who from the town he whipped. Will be discussed later.)</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Gideon then returned to פנואל, destroyed the tower and killed the people of the town.[^10]</span>
# Storyline as Discussed by the מפרשים
## Who From the Cities Responded to Gideon
### מנחה קטנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Only the leaders responded in סכות. In פנואל, the whole town responded.[^11] ([[#^bbeff9|The רלב"ג]] may agree to this, as well.)</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(This may be alluded to in the פסוק. By סכות the פסוק says "ויאמר **שרי** סכות" which sounds like only the leaders answered. Perhaps people in the town were unaware of the request, or they really were willing to help but the leaders didn't allow it, or they at least would not have responded with the scorn that the leaders had responded with. By פנואל it says "ויענו אותו **אנשי** פנואל" which sounds like the people as a collective group had responded.)</span> ^781938
### מלבי"ם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The leaders and the townspeople of both towns responded.[^12] (The מלבי"ם may learn that the response was not derisive.)</span>
## The Response from the Cities
### מנחה קטנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The people made it clear that they did not believe in Hashem's ability to make Gideon win after Hashem had explicitly promised Gideon that he would be able to capture זבח and צלמנע. This lack of belief in Hashem was כפירה and so they were חייב מיתה. (Simply not helping Gideon out would not have made them חייב מיתה.)[^16]</span> ^b492d7
### מלבי"ם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The people of the cities should have helped out because of the מצוה of tzedaka. The leaders of the towns who were in charge of the communal funds should have used some of these funds to help Gideon's army fight a war against the enemies of כלל ישראל.[^13]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The leaders didn't want to help Gideon's army because they were afraid that if he would lose, Midian would punish them for helping Gideon's army.[^14]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The leaders of סכות were not held responsible because this was a reasonable concern. The leaders of פנואל was held responsible for this because they had a fortified tower so they didn't have reason to worry about reprisals from Midian.[^15]</span> ^09a427
## Punishment for the People of סכות
### מנחה קטנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Only the leaders of the town were killed, not just whipped or thrown onto thorns.[^11] (To be able to read this into the פסוק, it would seem that they were whipped or crushed against the thorns to death.[^17])</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[#^b492d7|here]] for why they received this punishment.</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As [[#^781938|mentioned]], the רלב"ג [[#^bbeff9|here]] may agree to this.</span> ^fc9bb0
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In addition, because these people were leaders, they were held to higher standard.[^18]</span>
### מלבי"ם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Only the townspeople were whipped as they had little reason not to help because of the מצוה of tzedaka.[^12] (Using communal funds to supply the army would have elicited reprisals. Private people giving food to hungry people, even if they were the army, was unlikely to cause a response.[^12] However, because there was a small chance that something would happen, the people of סכות were held less responsible.[^19])</span>
## Why Gideon Destroyed the Tower in פנואל
### רד"ק and מצודת דוד
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The people in פנואל felt confident because they had this fortified tower.[^20][^21] This was a major factor in why they responded as scornfully as they did.[^20]</span>
### מנחה קטנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[#^bbeff9|here]].</span>
### מלבי"ם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[#^09a427|here]]. Because the leaders and the people of the town should have relied upon the strength of their tower to help Gideon and chose not to, Gideon destroyed the tower.[^15]</span>
## Why Gideon Killed the People of פנואל
### רד"ק and מצודת דוד
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Gideon killed the people who fought against him when he came to destroy the tower.[^20][^22] (It would appear that there were a lot of people who fought against him as the פסוק makes it seem like Gideon killed a high percentage of the town.)</span> ^f85451
### מנחה קטנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The entire town of פנואל had refused Gideon's request. This is also why he destroyed the tower.[^23][^11]</span>
   <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(It seems that the רלב"ג agrees to the מנחה קטנה [[#^781938|here]] that only the leaders had responded in סכות but the whole town had responded in פנואל. Also, see [[#^b492d7|here]] for why they were חייב מיתה. According to this opinion, the leaders of סכות who had responded in the same way were also killed, as [[#^fc9bb0|mentioned]].)</span> ^bbeff9
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Additionally, the people of פנואל should have learned from Gideon's response that the response of סכות was seriously incorrect.[^18]</span>
### מלבי"ם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The people of פנואל didn't have to worry about reprisals because of their tower. As such, the עבירה of the leaders, as well as the people of the city, was more egregious and so they were killed.[^19]</span>
