It is better to use the median if there are a few outlier numbers that make the mean number non-representative of the data set.

Here is an example of a set of salaries in a company: 40k, 50k, 60k, 80k, 1m.

The median of this set is 60k. This is number is within the cluster of the data. One can make the statement that a typical worker at this company earns around 60k.

The mean is 244k. This is far higher than most of the numbers of the set. It is not reasonable to say that a typical worker at this company earns 244k.
