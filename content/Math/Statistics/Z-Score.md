# Definition
The z-score is simply the measure of how many [[Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD), Variance, and Standard Deviation|standard deviations]] a point is from the mean.
# Formula for Converting Values Into Z-Scores
$$\frac{value-mean}{standard \space deviation}$$
## Example 1
Let's say we have a set of data with a standard deviation of 2 and a mean of 5. And let's also say that we want to calculate the z-score of a few points in our set. The points we want to calculate are 10, 0, 3, and -1. The way we would calculate these is as follows:
$$\frac{10-5}{2} \Rightarrow \frac{5}{2} = \textbf{2.5}$$
$$\frac{0-5}{2} \Rightarrow \frac{-5}{2} = \textbf{-2.5}$$
$$\frac{3-5}{2} \Rightarrow \frac{-2}{2} = \textbf{-1}$$
$$\frac{-1-5}{2} \Rightarrow \frac{-6}{2} = \textbf{-3}$$
