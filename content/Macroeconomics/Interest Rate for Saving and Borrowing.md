1. Saving and borrowing follows the law of supply and demand, just like anything else.[^1]
2. When the interest rate is high, people save more and borrow less. When the interest rate is low, people save less and borrow more.[^1]
3. The interest rate is determined by the intersection between the saving curve and the borrowing curve.[^1]
4. If there is a shift in one of the curves, that shifts the point of intersection.[^1]
