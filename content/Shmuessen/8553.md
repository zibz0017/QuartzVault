# Shmuz 1 and 2
## Sefer Hachinuch Mitzva 263
A cohen hedyot is not allowed to become tamei to anyone except for the 7 close relatives.

Tumah is something bad and so the cohanim are supposed to avoid it.

In reality, the cohanim should even avoid becoming tamei from the 7 close relatives as well. However, דרכיה דרכי נועם וכל נתיבותיה שלום so the Torah doesn't demand something so difficult for the cohen hedyot and so the Torah allows him to be metamei to his closest relatives.
## Sefer Hachinuch Mitzva 270
A cohen gadol can't become tamei to anyone at all, as he is on a very high spiritual level.

This is less difficult for the cohen gadol because he has such a love of Hashem that this dominates his life. As such, his relationships don't mean so much to him and so when one of his close relatives die, he can move past it relatively easily.
## Sefer Hachinuch Mitzva 376
A nazir is not allowed to become tamei to anyone, even the 7 relatives.

A cohen hedyot never made the decision to become a cohen. He was a cohen from birth. In addition, the cohen spends time with his relatives and becomes close to them. As such, it is too difficult for him not to be allowed to go to their levayos and דרכיה דרכי נועם וכל נתיבותיה שלום.

However, the nazir made the decision on his own. In addition, because of the halachos of nzair, he can't go to parties and spend time the same way with his relatives. His focus, like that of the cohen gadol, is much more on Hashem, and so it is less difficult for him not to go to the levayos of even his close relatives.
## במדבר ו ב רש"י ד"ה כי יפלא
If someone sees a sotah being afflicted by the curse, he should take upon himself nezirus. (Rebbi understood this that this should serve as a wakeup call to the person. He should realize that this is what comes from gashmius and he should use this as inspiration to try and separate himself from it as much as he can.)
## פרקי אבות א יב רמב"ם ד"ה מתלמידיו של אהרן
Aharon loved peace and loved people.

If someone would do an aveirah, Aharon would befriend them. They would feel bad that someone like Aharon was their friend when they were doing aveiros and so it would get them to stop.

## Shmuess 1
On one hand, the Sefer Hachinuch seems to imply that a cohen gadol is aloof and has little to do with people of this world.

On the other, we know that Aharon, the first cohen gadol, was well known for caring about others (and as we see from the Alter, these were legitimate feelings). It is clear from the medrashim and Pirkei Avos how much he cared about strangers, let alone his wife, and his sons who he learned Torah with.

It seems that we have to say that both of these points are true. It seems that someone can be close to people. At the same time, they can maintain this focus that it is all about Hashem.

## Shmuess 2
As we see from Rashi (who is presenting a gemara and a Medrash), the situation of a nazir may be where one is galvanized in a powerful moment and makes a decision. It may be that the person was not on a particularly high level before making this decision.

However, what happens if a close relative of the nazir dies only a few days later? It doesn't seem that the nazir has already reached this spiritual plain as described in the Chinuch.

It seems that we see the power of making a real decision to grow and change oneself. Just this decision alone can be expected to make a significant impact.

The standard length of nezirus is 30 days, so it seems that the Chinuch's description of the nazir reaching this lofty level would have to be within a 30 day period (a probably sometime relatively early within the 30 days.
