<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem told משה to count כלל ישראל but not to count לוי amongst the rest of the בני ישראל.
This was because it was not appropriate for the "army of Hashem" to be counted with everyone else.
Also, Hashem saw that everyone who was counted from 20 years of age and up would end up dying in the מדבר and Hashem didn't want the people who were "His", meaning, they weren't involved in the עגל, to end up dying in the מדבר.</span>
# Shmuessen
## Shmuess 1
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It sounds that simply the reason that they were not involved in the עגל is not enough. There was also this factor that they were "Hashem's".
We see how sometimes Hashem can include other people in a גזרה, even if some specific people are not involved in the reason behind the גזרה. In this situation, it seems that even though לוי was not involved in the עגל, they would have been punished (if not for this additional point).</span>
## Shmuess 2
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We also see that sometimes not doing an עבירה can make someone part of "Hashem's entourage". Sometimes we think that the only way we can get such an honor is by doing something grand. We see that this isn't the case.
Over here, לוי fought against the rest of כלל ישראל. Yet, what makes them "Hashem's entourage" is the fact that they didn't commit this עבירה.</span>
