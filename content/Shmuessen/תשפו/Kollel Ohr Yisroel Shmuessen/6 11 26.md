משה defended כלל ישראל from destruction on numerous occasions.
On one of these times, Hashem offered משה the possibility that Hashem would destroy כלל ישראל and rebuild כלל ישראל through משה.
It seems that according to most (if not all) מפרשים, Hashem didn't want כלל ישראל to be destroyed and the proper thing was for משה to daven on their behalf.
