# &lrm;שמות רש"י שם
The taskmasters over the Jewish slaves were other Jews. These Jews had Egyptians over them and if the slaves were not able to deliver the quota of bricks, they would get beaten by the Egyptians above them.
These taskmasters didn't beat the slaves underneath them to get them to work harder and deliver the quota of bricks. They took the beatings from the Egyptians.
Because they did this, they were זוכה to become the סנהדרין.
# Shmuess 1
We see what is really important for being a גדול in תורה. We think that intellectual abilities are what really matter.
From here we see that it is actually one's מדות that determine if one will be a גדול in תורה or not. Even if one does not have the tremendous intellectual properties, it seems that Hashem will grant them the תורה knowledge anyway.
(Presumably, these people did have some degree of intellect on their own, as it would be a major חידוש to say that these people were actually totally devoid of intelligence and they still became members of סנהדרין, but it was clearly nowhere near enough for them to have become members of סנהדרין.)
# Shmuess 2
We see that תורה knowledge is different than other forms of knowledge. תורה knowledge is a gift and even someone with less intelligence is able to be granted the knowledge of a גדול בישראל.
# Discussions about the Shmuess
## Point 1
People asked that perhaps these people were זוכה to extra intelligence. That was their reward for saving their fellow members of כלל ישראל.
Rabbi Hodkin resonded that this was adding into רש"י. רש"י just says that they were זוכה to this position. He does not say that they were זוכה to extra intelligence and because of that they were able to learn better and so after many years they became גדולים in תורה.
(Personally, I didn't think it was adding that much into the מדרש.)
## Point 2
All that רש"י says is that these people did this tremendous chesed and so they were זוכה to this reward. It does not seem to say that this is the only way that one can become a גדול, or that this is a requirement to be a גדול. Perhaps someone who is simply born with that high level of intelligence can become a גדול in תורה without doing any particular act of chesed, and perhaps even if they have bad מדות (even if it is not true, it doesn't seem that you can prove that from this רש"י).
