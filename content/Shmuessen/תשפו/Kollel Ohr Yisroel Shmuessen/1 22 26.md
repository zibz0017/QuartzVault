# Shmuess 1
## Tehilim א א רש"י שם
A person is praiseworthy for not "going in the path" of reshaim (which means keeping a solid distance).
## Shmuess
It seems that this is referring to everyone, even צדיקים.
We would think that this is easy for a צדיק to do, so they shouldn't be rewarded for this.
We see that even צדיקים get rewarded for this, so we certainly see how much of a reward regular people like us get for keeping our distance from rishaim.
# Shmuess 2 and 3
## Tehilim קכב א רש"י שם
People were saying about Dovid Hamelech "when is this old man going to die so Shlomo can rule and we can build the בית Hamikdash?"
## Shmuess
We see how easy it is to underappreciate great people, even someone like Dovid Hamelech.
It would certainly seem that we may feel the same way about ourselves. We may underappreciate ourselves and what we ourselves do.
## Shmuess
We also see how wild the "frum yetzer harah" can be.
The people who want the בית hamikdash are calling Dovid hamelech "this old man" and hoping that he dies.
