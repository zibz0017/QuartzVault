# מגילת RUS ג יג
RUS goes to Boaz at night and tells him to redeem her (something like Yibum). He takes a shivua that either he or a different relative will. He then tells her to spend the night there.
# רש"י שם
He swore to her either because she didn't believe him, or, an alternative פשט, Boaz took a shivua that he wouldn't live with her until they were married to fight his Yetzer harah. (Both of these פשטים are from מדרשים.)
# רלב"ג שם
Boaz told her to stay because she would be worried about people speaking about her if they see her going around at night. He told her to stay so she could avoid the anxiety of avoiding people, or the embarrassment of being caught.
# Shmuess 1
Presumably, Boaz took a shivua because he had real concern that he would be nichshal. This is even though he was the גדול hador, and even though she was presumably going to become מותר the next day (as ploni almoni was presumably not going to take her, as he didn't).
We see how powerful the yetzer harah is in this area.
We also see that one can't feel "too frum" or whatever about doing what one can to try to limit the nisayon.
# Shmuess 2
We see how important caring for other people is. As just stated, Boaz was at some degree of risk of being nichshal.
Still, because of her discomfort, he felt that it wasn't proper to send her out.
We see how much we need to care about about a person's feelings that we even need to withstand nisyonos to help someone. (Obviously, how to apply this is tricky.)
# Dicussions about the Shmuess
## Shmuess 1
People pointed out that there are many examples throughout תנ"ך and Shas of great people struggling in this area.
The response was that this is another ציור to this.
Additionally, the second point (how one has to be do what one can to limit the nisayon) is a חידוש.
## Shmuess 2
The רלב"ג himself says like the first פשט in רש"י.
The response was that presumably, even though that was the פשט given by the רלב"ג, he doesn't disagree with what פשט from the מדרש. As such, presumably his פשט would fit with that מדרש, as well.
(This isn't so clear to me. The רלב"ג argues on מדרשים, and even if he agrees, perhaps he is only giving this פשט for the first פשט in רש"י.)
