# &lrm;בראשית מח ב רש"י שם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יעקב sat up in his bed when יוסף came in with Menashe and אפרים. He did this to give כבוד to יוסף because he was a king.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We learn from here that one should give כבוד to a king.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Similarly, משה gave כבוד to פרעה, and אליהו gave כבוד to a king <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">need to find out which</span> as well.</span>
# Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It appears that according to רש"י, יעקב only afforded יוסף this כבוד because he was a king. Not because יוסף was a צדיק, or because he was the progenitor of יעקב legacy, or because he loved, or any other reason.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The first thing we see is that we see how calculated יעקב was. Maybe יעקב did this for any of the other reasons mentioned. The fact that רש"י can learn something from this shows us that יעקב was being calculated and so we can learn this from here.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Second, many times parents have a hard time seeing their kids as anything other than their little children, no matter how far they have gone in life. Clearly, יעקב saw right through this and appreciated יוסף for what he did.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Thirdly, we see an angle into דרך ארץ. How can we learn from what יעקב did for יוסף to משה giving כבוד to פרעה or אליהו giving כבוד to <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">whoever it was. He was a רשע</span>. We see that כבוד למלכות is a לא פלוג to all kings. Simply the fact that the person is a king makes it that they are worthy of כבוד, regardless of how much of a רשע they are.</span>
   This would seemingly apply to giving כבוד to any צלם אלקים. Even though they may be tremendous , that doesn't take away from the כבוד they deserve for being a צלם אלקים.
