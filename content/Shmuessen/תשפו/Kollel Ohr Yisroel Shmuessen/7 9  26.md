# לא.-לב:
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">How far does kibud av vaeim go? We see this from the story of Dama ben Nesina who didn't wake up his father to sell a jewel.
There is another story about someone who was sitting amongst the Roman nobility and his mother came and ripped his clothing and spit in front of him and he didn't do anything his mother. (תוס'&rlm there says that his mother was not insane.)
There is a story of Rebbi Tarfon who would get onto all fours to help his mother get in and out of bed.
There is a story about someone who's father through his son's wallet into the sea and the son didn't shame the father.</span>
# Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Most, if not all, of these stories are dealing with great people. It is easier to present the shmuess on one of these stories but it is really about all of them.)
Of course Rebbi Tarfon appreciated the מצוה that he was doing and was honored that he was lowering himself to be able to do. Yet, it clearly was difficult as the 'גמ is praising him for doing this.
Even someone like Rebbi Tarfon who had clarity in what this world is about still had difficulty with this. We are still flesh and blood and so he felt the בזיון. (Presumably, he felt this to a large degree because the 'גמ is implying that this was a big deal that he did this. If it was just a דקי דקות, it wouldn't be such a חידוש.)
We see that there is no way to fully escape these types of feelings. One can't fully escape these natural human feelings.</span>
