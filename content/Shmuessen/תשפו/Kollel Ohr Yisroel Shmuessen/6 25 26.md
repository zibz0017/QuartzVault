# יהושע ג א רלב"ג
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">כלל ישראל arrived at the ירדן at night but didn't cross until the next morning. One of the reasons for this was so that כלל ישראל would be able to work on themselves over that night. This would make them more ראוי for the נס and so they would be able to appreciate how bizarre the נס is and it would affect them more.</span>
# Shmuess
## First Point
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see that being on a higher מדרגה can affect one's ability to appreciate Hashem's involvement in a נס.
(Perhaps one can certainly make the argument that for everyday life, where Hashem's presence is not so obvious, there will certainly be an increase in awareness the higher of a מדרגה that one is on.)</span>
## Second Point
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even for things as wild as the splitting of the ירדן, one will not automatically appreciate it to the maximum degree possible.</span>
## Third Point
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see the difference that even one more night can make. This generation saw all of the נסים that happened in the מדבר and they were led by משה רבינו. Despite this, Hashem wanted them to wait one more night for them to grow some more and apparently, this was able to make a difference.</span>
