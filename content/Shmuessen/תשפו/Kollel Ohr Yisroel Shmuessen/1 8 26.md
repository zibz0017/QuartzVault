# בראשית מו ד רש"י שם
Before going down to מצרים, Hashem promised יעקב that יעקב would be buried in ארץ ישראל.
# בראשית מז לא
When it comes time for יעקב to die, he tells יוסף to bury him in ארץ ישראל, and then he makes יוסף swear. מצוה
# בראשית מח ז רש"י שם
יעקב told יוסף the reason that he buried רחל on the road because יעקב was concerned that יוסף might not follow through and bury יעקב in ארץ ישראל because יוסף may have been bothered by the fact that יעקב seemingly didn't treat רחל's burial properly.
# Shmuess
Why is יעקב go through all of this השתדלות?&lrm; יעקב was already promised by Hashem that he would make sure that יעקב would be buried in ארץ ישראל.
It seems that we see from here that when it comes to doing a מצוה, someone should make sure to do their utmost, even in a situation that one has a havtacha from Hashem.
We somewhat see a similar concept by the story of the last descendant of דוד המלך who was locked up and the רבנן of the time went through great lengths to make sure that there would be future progeny of מלכות בית דוד, even though Hashem promised that there would always be lineage from דוד.
# Discussions on the Shmuess
## Point 1
People pointed out that רש"י on בראשית נ ו perhaps implies that the reason יעקב made יוסף swear was so that יוסף would be able to further pressure פרעה to let יוסף bury יעקב in ארץ ישראל, not because יעקב did not trust יוסף.
However, there is still the other מראה מקום. In addition, it is not clear that רש"י is saying that this is why יעקב did it, or perhaps he is just saying that this is what happened (פרעה felt pressure because יוסף swore, but not that that was the reason that יעקב made him swear).
## Point 2
You can't prove from here that there is a difference between the השתדלות in regard to completing מצוות vs doing other things. However, it does seem that יעקב went a bit overboard, so it does seem מסתבר that the פשט is that when it comes to doing מצוות, there is a different level of השתדלות that is required.
