# 
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Yosi makes a קל וחומר.&lrm; אדם was commanded on one מצוה, and because of the this one עבירה that he did, death was brought to this world. In other words, this one עבירה is the reason that every single person has died.
We know that Hashem's מידה of kindness is hundreds of times greater than Hashem's מידה of punishment.
As such, if a person refrains from doing a single עבירה, or one does a single מצוה, how much more so are they going to get rewarded.</span>
# Alter
## Addional Tziurim about the מדרש
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מדרשים also say that not only was death brought to this world, but the ground was cursed (people had to work for a living) and the whole world changed for the negative.
Additionally, קין's עבירה additionally caused the ground to be cursed, but that only occurred because if was "piggybacking" off of אדם's עבירה.
Despite this, the ground was still quite "blessed" compared to now. Before the mabul, nobody would die young, you would plant vegetables and they would grow on their own. Additionally, they would grow fully cooked. People were pregnant either for only 1 day or 3 days (it is a מחלוקת). There weren't miscarriages. Kids were born largely functional. They were able to carry out tasks, like lighting a candle, from when they were born. Lions and leopards were like ants compared to people (I'm not exactly sure what this means).
(Rabbi Hodkin thought that the Alter was working with the assumption that the world got worse after the mabul partially because of אדם's עבירה similarly to how קין's עבירה made the world worse, partially because it piggybacked on אדם's עבירה.)
Additionally, the difference between Gan Eden and the world after אדם was for forced to leave was so vast, it was the same difference between us and the world that was just described in the times of the mabul.
As mentioned, this is after the curse because of אדם's עבירה. We can't even imagine what the world was like in the times of אדם.)</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Yosi is making a קל וחומר from the one מצוה that Hashem commanded to אדם, and us. This seems difficult to understand. אדם was directly created by Hashem, he was a giant, malachim wanted to sing shira to him. We are just regular people.</span>
