# דעת Zikeinim
1. יעקב was afraid that Esav might kill him. This was because Esav was able to do some מצוות that יעקב was not able to do, such as:
	1. Kibud AV.
	2. Living in ארץ ישראל.
# Shmuess
1. יעקב had so many other zechusim. יעקב learned in yeshiva Shem Vaeiver and kept all of the מצוות when he was by Lavan. So even though he didn't have these מצוות, why was he concerned?
2. It seems that we see that we really have no understanding of how Hashem gives reward in this world. It is entirely possible that certain מצוות have far more power than other מצוות.
3. This is even though we are dealing with a rasha like Eisav and a צדיק like יעקב.
# Questions on the Shmuess
1. People asked that perhaps this is only because Eisav was tremendously exemplary in the מצוה of kibud AV.
2. People responded that the דעת Zikeinim also mentioned living in ארץ ישראל (for which we have no source that Esav did this particularly well, whatever that would mean).
