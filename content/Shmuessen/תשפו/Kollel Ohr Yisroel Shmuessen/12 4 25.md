# &lrm;דעת זקנים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יעקב was afraid that Esav might kill him. This was because Esav was able to do some מצוות that יעקב was not able to do, such as:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Kibud AV.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Living in ארץ ישראל.</span>
# Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יעקב had so many other zechusim. יעקב learned in ישיבה Shem Vaeiver and kept all of the מצוות when he was by לבן. So even though he didn't have these מצוות, why was he concerned?</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that we see that we really have no understanding of how Hashem gives reward in this world. It is entirely possible that certain מצוות have far more power than other מצוות.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is even though we are dealing with a רשע like עשו and a צדיק like יעקב.</span>
# Questions on the Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">People asked that perhaps this is only because עשו was tremendously exemplary in the מצוה of kibud AV.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">People responded that the דעת זקנים also mentioned living in ארץ ישראל (for which we have no source that Esav did this particularly well, whatever that would mean).</span>
