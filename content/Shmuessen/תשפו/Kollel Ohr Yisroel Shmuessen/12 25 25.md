# בראשית יא כח רש"י שם
The passuk says that Haran died "על pinei Terach". The simple understanding is that Haran died during the lifetime of his father. רש"י says that this means that Haran died because of his father, Terach.
Terach what אברם did to the avoda zaros to Nimrod, and they sentenced אברם to be thrown into the furnace.
Haran said to himself that if אברם survives, he will stick with אברם. If אברם dies, he won't.
אברם survived, Nimrod asked Haran which side he was on, he said he was with אברם, they threw him into the fire, and he died.
# בראשית רבה יג עם Eitz יוסף שם
Nimrod's people thought that Haran would survive the fire, and when they saw that he was dying, they tried to pull him out to save him.
# Shmuess
It doesn't seem that Terach was responsible for this. It appears that Terach was trying to get אברם in trouble, but that is it. There is no mention in the מדרש, the מפרשים on the מדרש, or the מפרשים on the passuk, that discuss anything about Terach trying to do anything to Haran. It is not really even clear why they even asked Haran which side he was on.
In addition, according to the one פשט in the Eitz יוסף, they thought that Haran would survive and even tried to save him.
Despite this, the תורה still puts the blame on Terach.
We see how far our responsibility goes. We are even responsible for seemingly distant and largely indirect ramifications from our actions.
