# בראשית יא כח רש"י שם
The פסוק says that חרן died "על פני תרח". The simple understanding is that חרן died during the lifetime of his father. רש"י says that this means that חרן died because of his father, תרח.
תרח what אברם did to the עבודה זרות to Nimrod, and they sentenced אברם to be thrown into the furnace.
חרן said to himself that if אברם survives, he will stick with אברם. If אברם dies, he won't.
אברם survived, Nimrod asked חרן which side he was on, he said he was with אברם, they threw him into the fire, and he died.
# בראשית רבה יג עם עץ יוסף שם
Nimrod's people thought that חרן would survive the fire, and when they saw that he was dying, they tried to pull him out to save him.
# Shmuess
It doesn't seem that תרח was responsible for this. It appears that תרח was trying to get אברם in trouble, but that is it. There is no mention in the מדרש, the מפרשים on the מדרש, or the מפרשים on the פסוק, that discuss anything about תרח trying to do anything to חרן. It is not really even clear why they even asked חרן which side he was on.
In addition, according to the one פשט in the עץ יוסף, they thought that חרן would survive and even tried to save him.
Despite this, the תורה still puts the blame on תרח.
We see how far our responsibility goes. We are even responsible for seemingly distant and largely indirect ramifications from our actions.
