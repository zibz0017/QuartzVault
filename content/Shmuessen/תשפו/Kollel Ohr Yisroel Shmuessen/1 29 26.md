Hashem didn't lead כלל ישראל through the פלשתים because כלל ישראל might be frightened by war and run back to מצרים.
# Shmuess 1
Presumably, after seeing all of the נסים, &lrm;כלל ישראל would have trust in Hashem and משה and would not return, especially considering that they were slaves in מצרים. This was not the case. In fact, כלל ישראל was not punished for having this feeling.
Similarly, we do see many times that כלל ישראל talked about returning. We even see this by the end of the trip in the מדבר that כלל ישראל still thought about returning to מצרים.
By the war with עמלק, &lrm;כלל ישראל thought about returning and the only reason why they didn't was because they didn't know the way back, or they knew that עמלק would chase them down. There were other times that כלל ישראל thought about going back and the whole reason they didn't was because of things like this.
We see how difficult loyalty to a leader, and loyalty to Hashem, is. Even after the whole experience in the מדבר, there was still doubt and questioning of Hashem and משה. We see that עבודת Hashem can sometimes be something that we barely make it through and the only reason why we made it through is because Hashem orchestrated the circumstances to make sure that we properly serve him.
