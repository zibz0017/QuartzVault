# &lrm;בראשית רבה צ ב (מא לח) עם מהרז"ו שם
In two places, Hashem tells כלל ישראל that he will be greater than us, no matter how much we grow.
גאווה could get a person to think that he could potentially be greater than Hashem.
# Shmuess 1
We see the greatness that people can reach. Even though this person is being blinded by גאווה, it would seem that there must be some basis for what this person is thinking. Even with גאווה, the person presumably won't feel this unless there is some basis.
We are talking about the DOR Deah and we are talking about someone who has grown a tremendous amount. As such, it seems that this is a reasonable "hava amina".
Even though this is in truth, incorrect, the fact that this is a reasonable "haavah amina", the "maskana" can't be that far from the "hava amina".
# Shmuess 2
We also see the power of גאווה. We are talking about a great person, yet he could think that he is in some way equal to Hashem.
