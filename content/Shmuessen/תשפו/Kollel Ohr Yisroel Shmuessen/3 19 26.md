# &lrm;ברכות פרק א משנה ה with רע"ב
One is חייב to mention yetzias מצרים at night. Rav Elazar בן azaria said that he was like 70 years old and he was yet the הלכה was not like him. Rav Elazar בן Azaria held that one does not mention yetzias מצרים at night and the רבנן held that one does, and we פסקען like the רבנן.
# &lrm;ברכות כח.
Rav Elazar בן Azaria was appointed as nasi. Hashem made a neis that his beard turned white and he looked like he was 70 years old. (This is פשט in the משנה when Rav Elazar בן Azaria said "I am like 70 years old".)
# Likutei Sichos : 2nd night of פסח, תשח"י (The Lubavitcher Rebbe)
## Question
As the 'גמ says, Rav Elazar בן Azaria wasn't actually 70 years old. What does the fact that he looks 70 have anything to do with the fact that the הלכה should be like him?
## Answer
Physical reality can the spiritual reality.
The אריז"ל says that Rav Elazar בן Azaria was actually a גלגל of Shmuel הנביא. Shmuel lived until he was 52 years old. Rav Elazar בן Azaria was 18 years old. Together, that makes 70 years.
Rav Elazar בן Azaria was saying that despite the fact that he had greatness from Shmuel, as well as his own greatness, the הלכה was not like him.
We see a powerful idea that we can have positive characteristics from previous גלגלים. Out current strengths stack on top of what we have gained from previous גלגלים.
This is particularly relevant in our times because they say that we are all גלגלים.
(The Rebbe said that we only gain the positive attributes from the previous גלגלים, not the negative ones. That has been wiped away by תשובה, and perhaps punishments that we have received.)
