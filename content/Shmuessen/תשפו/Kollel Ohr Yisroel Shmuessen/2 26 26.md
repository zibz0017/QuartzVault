# מגילה יב
1. Vashti and Achashveirosh both made parties because they were both trying to get everyone to be מזנה. She wanted people to look at her beauty.
# מגילת אסתר ב א רלב"ג שם
1. After Achashveirosh got rid of Vashti, he regretted because she was beautiful, and because perhaps she wasn't wrong by not coming because perhaps she didn't come to the mens' party because of (some slight degree of) צניעות.
2. The advisors really understood that it was about the beauty, so they advised him to gather all the women so he could find a replacement.
# Shmuess
## Question
1. From the 'גמ, it is clear that Vashti and Achashveirosh were both major prutzim. It is strongly implied that Vashti would have come to the party if not for her pimples or tail. What is going on here?
## Answer
1. We see how we can think absolutely irrational thoughts.
2. In this situation, it seems that Achashveirosh was depressed because he missed Vashti, and so he started thinking more negative thoughts, even though they were irrational.
3. His advisors realized this and so their solution only addressed the point about her beauty, not about the fact that perhaps she was being somewhat צניעות.
# Discussion about the Shmuess
1. Perhaps the word צניעות should be read a little לאו דוקא. Perhaps she was just embarrassed to come without clothing.
   A potential response is that it was clear that she was willing to do this if not for the tail or pimples. It seems clear that she was not embarrassed.
