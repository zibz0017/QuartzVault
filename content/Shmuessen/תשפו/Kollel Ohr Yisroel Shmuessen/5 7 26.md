# בראשית רבתי על בראשית ז א
The cat and mouse were fighting on the תבה, and the cat scratched the mouse. The mouse came to נח and asked him to heal him. נח told the mouse to get a whisker from the cat and he would sew the mouse's lip. The mouse got the whisker and נח sewed him up.
For this reason, נח is considered a צדיק.
