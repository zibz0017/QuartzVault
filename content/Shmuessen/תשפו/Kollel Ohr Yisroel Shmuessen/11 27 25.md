# &lrm;בראשית כג כה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יעקב thought he was marrying רחל, but לבן tricked him and instead he married לאה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יעקב had made certain signs with רחל, but when רחל saw that לבן was putting לאה there and she realized that לאה was going to be embarrassed, she gave לאה the signs.</span>
# Shmuess 1
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">What changed? רחל and יעקב had made a plan in case of this exact event. The point of these signs was for the exact scenario that לאה is put instead of רחל.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Clearly, for whatever reason, they thought in advance that this was the right thing, but in the moment, רחל realized that this was not correct.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רש"י is a little משמע that רחל made the decision from seeing לאה going and realizing what is going to happen to her.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that we see that even for tremendous גדולים like יעקב and רחל, and especially considering that רחל of course loved her sister, still, in the moment, רחל was able to דהר it better.</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This better דהר was able to get her to change what she thought the דעת תורה was.</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We also see that sometimes, emotions can actually enhance one's understanding of a situation, rather than work against one's intellect.</span>
