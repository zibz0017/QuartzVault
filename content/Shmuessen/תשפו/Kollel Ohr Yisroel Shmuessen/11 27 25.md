# בראשית כג כה
1. Yaakov thought he was marrying Rachel, but Lavan tricked him and instead he married Leah.
2. Yaakov had made certain signs with Rachel, but when Rachel saw that Lavan was putting Leah there and she realized that Leah was going to be embarrassed, she gave Leah the signs.
# Shmuess 1
1. What changed? Rachel and Yaakov had made a plan in case of this exact event. The point of these signs was for the exact scenario that Leah is put instead of Rachel.
2. Clearly, for whatever reason, they thought in advance that this was the right thing, but in the moment, Rachel realized that this was not correct.
3. רש"י is a little mashma that Rachel made the decision from seeing Leah going and realizing what is going to happen to her.
4. It seems that we see that even for tremendous גדולים like Yaakov and Rachel, and especially considering that Rachel of course loved her sister, still, in the moment, Rachel was able to daher it better.
5. This better daher was able to get her to change what she thought the daas תורה was.
6. We also see that sometimes, emotions can actually enhance one's understanding of a situation, rather than work against one's intellect.
