# &lrm;אסתר רבה ט ד (ה, יד) עם עץ יוסף שם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Haman came to מרדכי after making the tree and he found him teaching children. Haman locked up all of the kids and said that they, along with מרדכי, would all be killed the next day.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מרדכי refused food and so the kids followed his example and fasted, as well.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">At that time, מרדכי and all of the children davened to Hashem to be saved. These תפילות went up to Shamayim and were able to get them saved. That night was "בלילה ההוא".</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason מרדכי chose children was because their mouths had pure mouths who that didn't have any sin.</span>
# Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that מרדכי chose children as opposed to tremendous תלמידי חכמים. The reason is because the children had pure mouths. Presumably, תלמיד  חכמים would get a better understanding of the תורה but these children clearly have something that these גדולים don't have.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We gain an understanding of how learning תורה works. I could have heard that the entire goal of תורה is to understand the תורה to the greatest degree one can. Obviously, one certainly should, but we see that תורה is really about creating a connection with Hashem.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is similar to some degree to the way we look at davening: we understand that putting in effort is the main point of davening, not just going through the motions of saying the words. We see that learning is somewhat similar. It also is about this connection.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is similar to other ideas about learning that we know such as how one's understanding of תורה is based on how hard they try and not how intelligent they are, and similar ideas.</span>
