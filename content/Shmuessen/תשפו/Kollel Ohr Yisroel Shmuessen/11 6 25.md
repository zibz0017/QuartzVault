# &lrm;בראשית כב כ רש"י שם
When אברהם was coming back from the akeida, he thought that if he actually had shechted יצחק, there wouldn't be any children. As such, he started pursuing a shidduch for יצחק.
# &lrm;משכיל לדוד
אברהם didn't pursue a shidduch before because he wanted to make sure that יצחק married the best person possible. The only צדיקים were ענר, אשכל, and ממרא, but they didn't have yichus.
Hashem put this thought in אברהם's heart because רבקא wasn't born yet and Hashem wanted יצחק to marry רבקא. Hashem then orchestrated this to happen to get אברהם to pursue the shidduch.
# Shmuess 
Obviously, אברהם always knew that יצחק could die. This point of creating progeny was of utmost importance, as it was about the future of כלל ישראל. And obviously, אברהם appreciated this to the utmost level.
Additionally, יצחק wasn't actually in any danger. Hashem never planned on killing יצחק.
We see the power of a ציור חושי. Even אברהם didn't appreciate to the utmost extent that he should make sure to pursue a marriage for יצחק.
Additionally, אברהם had a good reason to not marry יצחק earlier. This got him to change his פסק.
We see that this can even get someone like אברהם to change their פסק.
# Discussion
Was אברהם incorrect in either of his psakim?
It seems that אברהם was correct both times. He didn't appreciate this before to the same extent. Now, because of this event, he appreciates this more. Because of his newfound appreciation, he paskens differently.
It seems that פסק is not based on some objective truth. It goes by one's appreciation and understanding of the צדדים. (Obviously we are referring to people on the proper level. We don't mean that anyone can just go by what they feel).
