# בראשית כב כ רש"י שם
When Avraham was coming back from the akeida, he thought that if he actually had shechted Yitzchak, there wouldn't be any children. As such, he started pursuing a shidduch for Yitzchak.
# משכיל לדוד
Avraham didn't pursue a shidduch before because he wanted to make sure that Yitzchak married the best person possible. The only צדיקים were ענר, אשכל, and ממרא, but they didn't have yichus.
Hashem put this thought in Avraham's heart because Rivka wasn't born yet and Hashem wanted Yitzchak to marry Rivka. Hashem then orchestrated this to happen to get Avraham to pursue the shidduch.
# Shmuess 
Obviously, Avraham always knew that Yitzchak could die. This point of creating progeny was of utmost importance, as it was about the future of כלל ישראל. And obviously, Avraham appreciated this to the utmost level.
Additionally, Yitzchak wasn't actually in any danger. Hashem never planned on killing Yitzchak.
We see the power of a ציור חושי. Even Avraham didn't appreciate to the utmost extent that he should make sure to pursue a marriage for Yitzchak.
Additionally, Avraham had a good reason to not marry Yitzchak earlier. This got him to change his psak.
We see that this can even get someone like Avraham to change their psak.
# Discussion
Was Avraham incorrect in either of his psakim?
It seems that Avraham was correct both times. He didn't appreciate this before to the same extent. Now, because of this event, he appreciates this more. Because of his newfound appreciation, he paskens differently.
It seems that psak is not based on some objective truth. It goes by one's appreciation and understanding of the tzdadim. (Obviously we are referring to people on the proper level. We don't mean that anyone can just go by what they feel).
