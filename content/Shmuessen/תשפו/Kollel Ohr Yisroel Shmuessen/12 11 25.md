# סוטה י:
1. It is better that a person throws themselves into fire than embarrass someone.
2. This is seen from יהודה and Tamar. יהודה (unknowingly) had biah with Tamar. He later found out that Tamar had biah, and she was חייב מיתה. She was willing to allow herself to get killed rather than embarrass him.
## Shmuess
1. יהודה was a tremendous גדול. All we see from here is that where the person being embarrassed is a tremendous גדול it is better that one kill themselves than embarrass them. How do we see from here that this applies to all people?
