# Shmuess 1
## Mechilta
When the תורה says ואלה המשפטים (with an extra ו) the תורה is linking the mishpatim with the aseres hadibros to tell us that the same way that the aseres hedibros came from Hashem, these interpersonal הלכות also came from Hashem.
## Shmuess
What is the alternative if it did not come from Hashem? It came from משה. Why should that make such a difference? The "ethics and morals" of משה should be quite fine.
Apparently, that is not the case. There is a hige difference between the תורה coming from משה רבינו and from Hashem. (This is intellectually obvious, but a good ציור.) It can be an important to sometimes think about that the תורה actually came from Hashem (not from the גדולים who one is learning).
# Shmuess
## מדרש Xx עם עץ יוסף שם
The מדרש says that when you are Moser nefesh for something, there is a certain attribution of that thing to the person.
The עץ יוסף says that this means that משה davened on behalf of כלל ישראל on their behalf.
## Shmuess
This is what it means that משה was Moser Nefesh? That he davened to Hashem? this doesn't sound like so much mesiras nefesh.
We see how much a person's davening (on the highest madreigos) affects them. משה's pushed himself so hard during his davening that it was considered that he risked his life.
# Shmuess 3
##
A slave goes free after some amount of time, but he may stay longer if he puts a hole in his ear.
## Kiddushin כב
We make the hole in his ear because Hashem told כלל ישראל that they were his slaves and now Hashem freed them. This person didn't "hear" what Hashem said. We do this by the doorpost because it is a reminder of Hashem passing over the Jewish houses in מצרים (where Hashem made כלל ישראל free).
## Shmuess
This is clearly not an עבירה. So what is going on here? It seems like it is a problem, but it is not clear what the problem is.
It seems that there is something called "חרות" which is important (although how exactly to define it based on יהדות is difficult).
