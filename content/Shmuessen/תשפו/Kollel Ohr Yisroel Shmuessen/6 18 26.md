# במדבר טז ד רש"י שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">משה fell on his face when קרח challenged him.
This was because משה felt that he wouldn't be able to defend כלל ישראל from this situation after having defended כלל ישראל from so many other terrible עבירות that they had done.</span>
# במדבר טז ד רבינו בחיי שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Only משה fell down and not אהרן. This was because משה felt the terrible slight to אהרן's כבוד (as it was a fight about the כהונה) and that overwhelmed him and so he fell down.</span>
# משכיל לדוד על רש"י
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">משה fell on his face by the מרגלים, as well. However, that was to try and get people to realize how bad the situation was and do תשובה. That is not relevant here as there is only a handful of people fighting with משה. As such, רש"י learned פשט that משה fell down due to his understanding of the terrible significance of this complaint.</span>
# Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see that even the greatest people such as משה רבינו have emotions and have emotional responses to situations to the point that they can be overwhelmed by their emotions.
Not only is this for national emergencies (as it appears based on רש"י's פשט), but even for more localized issues, like the attack on אהרן's כבוד can cause such a powerful response.
It is clear from the משכיל לדוד that this is certainly a חידוש (as he is trying to understand why רש"י said this פשט about being emotionally overwhelmed).</span>
