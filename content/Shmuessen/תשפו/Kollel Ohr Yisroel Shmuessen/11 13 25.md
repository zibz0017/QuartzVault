This story with Eliezer and רבקא (with Eliezer davening for something very specific and רבקא showing up and immediately fulfilling his tefillah and she was from the family of אברהם) made it clear to לבן and Besuel that "this was from Hashem".
חז"ל say that we see from here that shidduchim are arranged by Hashem. (It is not exactly clear what it means, but the פשוט פשט is that there is a higher level of hasgacha pratis with shidduchim.)
# Shmuess
How does רבינו בחיי see this from here? This was a marriage of tremendous historical importance. In addition, we are talking about people on a very high מדרגה. For these reasons, perhaps Hashem gave particular attention to this marriage, but that would not necessarily apply to all other marriages?
It seems that we need to say that while these factors are certainly important, there is a factor that far outweighs this that we have in common with יצחק. This would appear to be gadlus haadam. Hashem considers everyone so important that He gets involved in their marriages with nearly the same level of hashgacha as He did with יצחק and רבקא.
We also see the importance of marriage in general that Hashem gets involved in the marriage of regular people nearly to the same extent that he got involved in the marriage of יצחק and רבקא.
