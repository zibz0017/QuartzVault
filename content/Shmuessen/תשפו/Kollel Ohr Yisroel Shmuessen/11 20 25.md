# &lrm;בראשית כה כב with רש"י שם
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The kids fought in רבקא's womb.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This was so painful that she didn't want to carry the pregnancy anymore.</span>
# &lrm;בראשית רבה סג ו
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רבקא was in terrible pain and she asked other women if this was normal.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">She said if this is what pregnancy is like, I don't want it anymore, even if that means that I won't have the shevatim.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In fact, רבקא was destined to have the shevatim, but because she said this, she lost it.</span>
# &lrm;מזרחי כה כב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Clearly the women responded to רבקא that it was not a normal pain.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because רבקא was experiencing an abnormal pain, this made her feel despondent.</span>
# Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how being alone in pain can add a lot to the pain and make it much worse.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are talking about רבקא who obviously really wanted children and she certainly wanted to be the mother of the shevatim. Obviously, this was extremely painful.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The fact that she was alone in this made it too difficult, even for someone like רבקא.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">On the flip side, we also see that being with someone in pain can give them a tremendous amount of relief, even if the actual physical pain remains the same.</span>
