# בראשית כה כב with רש"י שם
1. The kids fought in רבקא's womb.
2. This was so painful that she didn't want to carry the pregnancy anymore.
# בראשית רבה סג ו
1. רבקא was in terrible pain and she asked other women if this was normal.
2. She said if this is what pregnancy is like, I don't want it anymore, even if that means that I won't have the shevatim.
3. In fact, רבקא was destined to have the shevatim, but because she said this, she lost it.
# מזרחי כה כב
1. Clearly the women responded to רבקא that it was not a normal pain.
2. Because רבקא was experiencing an abnormal pain, this made her feel despondent.
# Shmuess
1. We see how being alone in pain can add a lot to the pain and make it much worse.
2. We are talking about רבקא who obviously really wanted children and she certainly wanted to be the mother of the shevatim. Obviously, this was extremely painful.
3. The fact that she was alone in this made it too difficult, even for someone like רבקא.
4. On the flip side, we also see that being with someone in pain can give them a tremendous amount of relief, even if the actual physical pain remains the same.
