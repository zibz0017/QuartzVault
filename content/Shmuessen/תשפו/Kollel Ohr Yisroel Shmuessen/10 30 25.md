# Basic Story with מפרשים
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to find out which specific מראי mekomos</span>
1. לוט goes to סדום.
2. רש"י says that this was a bad decision. He was separating himself from אברם and his G-d.
3. רש"י says that אברם didn't have נבואה the for much of the time that לוט was with him. However, he started off as a "kosher person".
4. The שפתי חכמים says that לוט was a צדיק, but he was negatively influenced by the people of מצרים regarding זנות.
# Shmuess
1. What happened in מצרים? Pharoah took שרה against her will. אברם must have been davening for her. We know that שרה was davening. Quite possibly, לוט was davening as well (as he was still a kosher at first). We also know that there was a tremendous neis and Pharoah couldn't touch her. אברם and שרה were certainly feeling tremendous gratitude to Hashem over this.
2. It would certainly seem that לוט was disgusted by what happened in מצרים.
3. We see from here that one can be mushpa even if the person finds the thing disgusting.
# Discussion
## Question
1. It could be that לוט's negative influence didn't come from Pharoah. It came from the other things that he saw there.
## Answer
1. This is still a חידוש that he would be able to dissociate Pharoah's act (which is the "main story" about what happened in מצרים) with the other זנות that he may have seen. It would seem that Pharoah's act should overall leave a negative taste in his mouth about זנות.
