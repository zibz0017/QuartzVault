# Basic Story with מפרשים
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to find out which specific מראי מקומות</span>
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לוט goes to סדום.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רש"י says that this was a bad decision. He was separating himself from אברם and his G-d.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רש"י says that אברם didn't have נבואה the for much of the time that לוט was with him. However, he started off as a "kosher person".</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The שפתי חכמים says that לוט was a צדיק, but he was negatively influenced by the people of מצרים regarding זנות.</span>
# Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">What happened in מצרים? פרעה took שרה against her will. אברם must have been davening for her. We know that שרה was davening. Quite possibly, לוט was davening as well (as he was still a kosher at first). We also know that there was a tremendous neis and פרעה couldn't touch her. אברם and שרה were certainly feeling tremendous gratitude to Hashem over this.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It would certainly seem that לוט was disgusted by what happened in מצרים.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see from here that one can be mushpa even if the person finds the thing disgusting.</span>
# Discussion
## Question
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It could be that לוט's negative influence didn't come from פרעה. It came from the other things that he saw there.</span>
## Answer
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is still a חידוש that he would be able to dissociate פרעה's act (which is the "main story" about what happened in מצרים) with the other זנות that he may have seen. It would seem that פרעה's act should overall leave a negative taste in his mouth about זנות.</span>
