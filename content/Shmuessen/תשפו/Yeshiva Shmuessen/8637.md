# שמות רבה לא ב and סנהדרין קי.
קרח's wealth led to his downfall.
## יפי תואר (on the מדרש)
This was because the wealth caused him to have gaavah.
## מהרש"א (on the גמ')
This was because the wealth caused him to be jealous of the positions of משה and Aharon and feel that he was deserving of their positions.
# Shmuess
We see a tremendous insight into how wealth can affect people.
קרח was a tremendous צדיק before this episode.
כלל ישראל is currently in the מדבר where there isn't anything one can buy with money, anyway.
The maalos of משה and Aharon were obviously ruchni, not gashmi. Money certainly wasn't going to help with those maalos. In fact, one can argue that having more money would make one less attached to ruchnios maalos and so *less* fitting for the roles of משה and Aharon.
Despite all of these things, it seems that the wealth still affected קרח in this way.
It is not muchrach that the יפי תואר agrees to the מהרש"א's interpretation. He could feel that the money gave קרח the gall to be able to start up with משה, not that it made him feel more fit for the position, but it may be reasonable to assume that he agrees to the מהרש"א (and we generally don't assume that מפרשים are arguing unless we have reason to do so).
(I didn't understand what rebbi was using the יפי תואר for. He may have just been bringing him in as an additional מראה מקום that agrees to this concept. Rebbi pointed out during the shmuess that it wasn't muchrach that he agreed to the מהרש"א and then I don't think he came back to it later to explain how it tied in.
