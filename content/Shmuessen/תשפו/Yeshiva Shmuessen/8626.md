מוצאימוצאי# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;בראשית מז ח-י רש"י שם
יעקב told פרעה that his life was difficult and that was why he looked old.
Hashem punished יעקב for this speech of כפירת טוב. Hashem had saved יעקב from עשו and לבן, returned דינה from Shichem, and reunited יעקב with יוסף.
## Shmuess
Obviously, יעקב understood this and he certainly appreciated all the good that Hashem had done for him.
However, the fact that יעקב said this created a הרגשה in him of כפירת טוב and יעקב was punished for that.

## &lrm;שמות רבה מב ג (לב ז-ח) עם יפי תואר שם
כלל ישראל was כופר טוב. They said that Hashem really took them out of מצרים for Himself, not for the benefit of כלל ישראל.
Hashem had put his name with כלל ישראל because of the greatness of the אבות. It was a Chillul Hashem for the nation that had Hashem's name to be in galus. Because of this, Hashem had to save כלל ישראל.
# Shmuess 3
## Yalkut Shemoni Iyov Remez תתקח
Hashem punished Iyov tremendously. He lost all of his children and all of his wealth. He then was physically afflicted with terrible suffering.
Iyov remained strong through most of it. However, at some point, Iyov complained to Hashem.
Hashem responded sharply to Iyov.
Did Iyov think that he was greater than אדם who, because he did one עבירה, was punished with bringing death to the world?
Did he think he was greater than יצחק, who, because he misplaced his love for עשו, was punished with being blinded?
Did he think he was greater than משה, who, because he said שמעו נא המורים, he lost the opportunity to go into ארץ ישראל?
