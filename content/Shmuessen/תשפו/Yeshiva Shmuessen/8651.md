# מדרש תנחומא פרשת אמור אות ט
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem has a desire for people and things that have been started up with or chased.
Whether it is a צדיק being chased, or even a רשע being chased by a צדיק, Hashem has a certain feeling for such a person.
We see this with many people from the תורה:</span>
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem desired אברהם who was chased by the נמרוד.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem desired יצחק who was harassed by the פלשתים.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem desired יעקב who was chased by עשו.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem desired יוסף who harassed by his brothers.</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem desired משה who was attacked by פרעה.</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem desired דוד who was chased by שאול.</span>

<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(There were a few additional examples presented by the מדרש.)
This is, in fact, part of the reason for the choice of animals for קרבנות. Hashem chose prey animals (like cows, sheep, and goats) as they are chased by predators.</span>
# <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">&lrm;יפי תואר on a Similar מדרש Found in the מדרש רבה but it Was not on the sheet</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This obviously does not mean that if these great צדיקים were not chased or harassed that Hashem would not have loved them because, obviously, Hashem would have loved them, regardless, because of their צדקות. However, these people reached an even higher tier of being loved by Hashem because of this factor that they were harassed and chased.</span>
# Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">What is פשט in this מדרש? What is it about being chased that makes one be loved by Hashem so much?
It does not seem that it is about accepting יסורים with love because, as mentioned in the מדרש, even if a רשע is being chased Hashem still has extra feeling toward this person. Obviously this person is not accepting his יסורים with love.
It seems that פשט simply is that being in a situation that engenders Hashem's רחמנות gets Hashem to feel for this person.
Even for great צדיקים, simply the fact that they were in situations where they needed Hashem's רחמנות causes Hashem to have feelings for this person that their צדקות on its own would not be able to engender.
פשט for this seems to be that a person can't ever really do enough to really "earn" Hashem's love. A person owes Hashem more than he could ever pay Hashem. Really, Hashem's love for a person is an act of חסד that a person can't ever truly earn. As such, a major part of Hashem's love for a person can simply be for reasons that he did not earn (like in this case, for being harassed or chased) simply because that is *always* true; a person *never* really earns Hashem's love anyway.
What is tremendously surprising is that we see that this is even true for some of the greatest people in history. Even for them, they did not truly earn Hashem's love, and, as such, being is certain situations that could engender Hashem's love also played a pivotal component in their closeness to Hashem.
We see two things from here:</span>
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see this idea that being chased or harassed can cause Hashem to have special feelings for a person.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see that no matter how great a person is (even if they are as great as משה or the אבות) they can't do enough to really earn Hashem's love. Hashem's love is a great gift for everyone.</span>
