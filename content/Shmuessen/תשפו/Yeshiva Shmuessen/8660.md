# Shmuess 2
## בראשית ל ד חזקוני
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בלהה and זלפה were full-fledged wives of יעקב. (They just used to be their maidservants.)</span>
## בראשית לא לג חזקוני
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The way the tents of the אמהות were set up was that יעקב had to walk through the tents of רחל and לאה to get to the tents of בלהה and זלפה that way יעקב couldn't be with them without "asking permission" from רחל and לאה.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how sensitive one has to be when dealing with others.
בלהה and זלפה were full-fledged wives and רחל and לאה were אמהות and so had tremendous עניוות. Yet, because they had been given over as maids for רחל and לאה,&lrm; יעקב made sure to set things up in a way to avoid "stepping on the toes" of רחל and לאה.</span>

<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(Presumably רחל and לאה did not ask for this, and presumably, יעקב must have gotten permission from בלהה and זלפה to do this or this would have infringed on their כבוד.)</span>
