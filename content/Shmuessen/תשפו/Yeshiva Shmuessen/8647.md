# &lrm;ויקרא יג ו (with Basic Explanation from מפרשים)
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Sometimes when it comes to צרעת, a person may be in a state of ספק. A כהן will check the צרעת and if it grew since the previous week, it turns out that it was צרעת and the person is, and was, a מצורע. If it shrunk, the person is, and was, טהור. The person, needs to טובל himself and his garments garments.</span>
## &lrm;בכור שור, חזקוני שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The person needs to טובל himself because he was in a state of ספק טומאה and so he won't be careful.
(There is a footnote on the בכור שור that rebbi saw from whoever published the ספר that the concern is that the person will touch קדשים or something like that while in the state of ספק and it may come out that he was really טמא.)</span>
# Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While it does make sense that a person who is in a state of ספק טומאה will be less careful than a person who is ודאי טמא, it still seems incredible that this person won't be careful.
First of all, even if this isn't actual צרעת, this is still a supernatural occurrence. One would imagine that this would cause a person to wake up and start doing better.
In addition, צרעת turns one's life upside down. One has to quarantine for an indefinite amount of time. This person is certainly frightened by the thought that the כהן will inspect the נגע and declare him טמא. One would imagine that this person would do anything to get more זכותים so that Hashem doesn't make him a מצורע.
We see that despite these motivations, a person can still quite possibly not be careful. We see that even a נס can have surprisingly little lasting effect. We also see that even though someone may know something, getting one to feel that way and act on it is something very different, even when there can be strong negative repercussions because of it.
In addition, the solution that we tell this person is to act like they are really טמא. We do that by making this person go to the מקוה. In other words, even though the fact that this is an open miracle, and that there are strong repercussions for one who is not careful is not enough to keep this person acting in line, acting like one is actually טמא can perhaps accomplish this.
Not only this, but, presumably, the person knows that reason for why he is going to the מקוה. It's not like he thinks he is actually טמא. He knows he isn't actually. Yet, even though he knows it, this still can have such a powerful effect.
We see how strong acting something out is, even if the person knows it is just an act.</span>
# Own Thoughts
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rebbi (and the person who wrote the footnotes for the בכור שור) are learning that what the בכור שור (and presumable the חזקוני) mean when they say that the person won't be careful is that they may touch קדשים while they are in a state of ספק and it may come out that they were actually טמא the whole time.
Perhaps that is not what the בכור שור or חזקוני are saying. Perhaps they mean that the person won't be careful from becoming טמא. We are concerned that the person may have done this, and so we make him go to the מקוה after this whole process is complete.</span>
