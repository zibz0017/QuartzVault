מוצאימוצאי# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;מכות יא:
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One has to do hataras נדרים on a nidui. We see this from יהודה. יהודה guaranteed to יעקב that he would make sure to bring Binyomin safely back from מצרים, and if he did not, he would forfeit his share of עולם הבא.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even though יהודה did successfully do this, he never was mattir נדר. Because of this, he was not allowed into עולם הבא for many years.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Eventually, משה davened on his behalf. He asked Hashem to let יהודה into עולם הבא because he helped ראובן do תשובה for his עבירה (with moving the יעקב's bed). ראובן was inspired to admit to his עבירה after he saw יהודה publicly admit to his עבירה with Tamar.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">משה's תפילות were successful and יהודה was allowed to enter עולם הבא</span>
## &lrm;תוס' ד"ה מי גרם שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מדרש (see below) says that by mechiras יוסף (which was before יהודה's עבירה with Tamar), ראובן wasn't there because he was at home, fasting over his עבירה with יעקב's bed.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that ראובן began the תשובה process, but he hadn't publicly admitted to his עבירה. After taking inspiration from יהודה's action, ראובן also publicly admitted to his עבירה.</span>
## &lrm;בראשית רבה פד יט (לז כט)
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">During mechiras יוסף, ראובן was at home, fasting and mourning over his עבירה with יעקב's bed.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">ראובן was the first person in history to do תשובה with such sincerity.[^1] Because of this, Hashem rewarded ראובן with having Hoshea descend from him who would help כלל ישראל do תשובה.</span>
## &lrm;סוטה י:
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because יוסף was מקדש שם שמים in private (referring to the the story with אשת פוטיפר), Hashem rewarded him by adding a letter of Hashem's name to יוסף's name. There is a פסוק which refers to יוסף as יהוסף, which is יוסף with an extra "ה".</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because יהודה was מקדש שם שמים in public  (referring to the יהודה admitting by the story with Tamar), Hashem rewarded him by having his entire name filled with letters of Hashem's name (the name יהודה has the entire name of Hashem in it. It seems that when לאה was naming יהודה, Hashem inspired her to give this name to יהודה, because Hashem knew he would do this tremendous act later in his life). In addition, because יהודה saved the life of Tamar and the two unborn children, Hashem saved the life of 3 of יהודה's descendants, חנניה, מישאל, and עזריה.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">משה was trying to help יהודה get into עולם הבא. The זכות that משה invokes is not the simply the act of יהודה admitting to his עבירה with Tamar. משה invokes the positive impact that it had on ראובן.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The act of admitting to the עבירה with Tamar was a powerful זכות. We see from the 'גמ in סוטה, the זכות from this was, in a way, more powerful than יוסף's act of refraining from אשת פוטיפר.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Not only that, but ראובן already did a high degree of תשובה on his own without inspiration from יהודה. We see from בראשית רבה that ראובן was already doing תשובה on his own, and this act of תשובה was so sincere that it was regarded as being the first act of תשובה the history, and ראובן received the tremendous reward of having a descendant like Hoshea.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יהודה's act certainly inspired ראובן to do more, but he had already done a tremendous amount on his own.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Despite all this, the fact that it helped ראובן in whatever way it did is the זכות that משה invoked to get יהודה into עולם הבא.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see from here the tremendous power of helping others with their spiritual growth.</span>
## Applications
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rebbi pointed out that if the בני הישיבה would be mechaeik themselves in a variety of ways, they would perhaps be able to positively impact the atmosphere of the ישיבה and help others grow. This would be a tremendous זכות to the person. Rebbi gave some examples (although he explicitly said that one should not be מדייק from what he did say vs what he didn't say. These were just some things that he happened to think of) such as coming on time to seder, not bateling by seder, not taking a coffee break immediately after mussar seder, and trying to maintain a positive attitude about ישיבה, even in the face of real (or perceived) grievances against the ישיבה.</span>
# Shmuess 2
## &lrm;בראשית מט לא רמב"ן שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When יעקב was asking his sons to bury him in the מערת המכפלה, he mentioned that יצחק was buried there to emphasize how special the place was to further invigorate his sons to make sure that they buried him there.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are talking about the shevatim, and this is יעקב's dying wish. We would surely imagine that they would try their utmost to fulfill it.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In addition, they probably already knew who was buried in the מערת המכפלה.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see that hearing something one more time, no matter who you are and in what situation you are in, can help galvanize one further to try and accomplish something.</span>
# Footnotes

[^1]: The מדרש actually says that ראובן was the first person do to תשובה in history, entirely. However, there were some people who had done תשובה earlier, like אדם. As such, rebbi understood the מדרש to be saying that this תשובה was done with a uniquely high degree of sincerity.
