# מדרש תנחומא הישן פרשת יתרו יא
משה sent יתרו away before הר Sinai because יתרו didn't undergo the same suffering as כלל ישראל did. כלל ישראל underwent shibud מצרים and that earned them the תורה. יתרו didn't undergo that, so he was not allowed to stay for קבלת התורה.
# Shmuess
It seems that the simple understanding of this מדרש is that כלל ישראל would feel that this is not fair. They put in all of their efforts to earn the תורה, and then יתרו, who didn't go through the suffering that they went through, gets to be by הר Sinai, as well.
However, it would seem reasonable that כלל ישראל would be so enamored and focused on the events at הר Sinai that they wouldn't even care about the fact that יתרו was there, as well.
In addition, we are talking about one individual person.
In addition, יתרו wasn't even receiving the תורה. He was simply going to observe קבלת התורה.
In addition, the עֵרֶב רַב came with כלל ישראל and they were allowed to be by הר Sinai even though they didn't go through the shibud. The difference between the עֵרֶב רַב and יתרו is that they were megaeir and יתרו was not. However, once there are going to be hundreds (if not thousands) of people who did not go through the shibud who are going to be there anyway, how much of a difference does one more person make?
In addition, יתרו would have presumably gained tremendously from הר Sinai. By sending him away, he is missing out on these gains.
We see how sensitive people are. Despite all of these reasons, כלל ישראל would have felt יתרו's presence and it wasn't fair to tarnish their reward after all of the suffering they went through to earn it.
