מוצאימוצאימוצאימוצאימוצאימוצאי# Shmuess 1
## שמות רבה י ב (ז כז)
The frogs by makas tzefardaia went exactly up to the border of מצרים. This was important because מצרים and its neighbor, Kush, had a dispute about where exactly the border was. The frogs were able to solve the dispute.
## שמות רבה יג ד (י ב)
The locusts from makas arbeh went exactly up to the border of מצרים so everyone could know exactly where the border went.
## Shmuess
*Side note: Rabbi Grunblatt noted that מפרשים ask why the border of מצרים needed to be displayed twice.*
The primary point of the makkos is quite straightforward: these were supposed to show the world, and כלל ישראל in particular, that there is a Hashem who runs the world. These makkos were obviously of all-time importance. Why is there even a thought about some border dispute between מצרים and Kush?
We see the importance of Emes and Shalom. Even during these important events, emes and shalom were important things to take care of as well.
(The לשון of the מדרשים sounded like the frogs and locusts established the border for these reasons.)
# Shmuess 2 and 3
## שמות רבה א כא (ב ג) עם Maharzu שם
According to one opinion, Yocheved used a cheaper wood when building the תבה for משה because of the concept that "צדיקים value their money more than their bodies".
(Basically, this concept means that צדיקים don't like to spend extra money because it may cause them to steal, either because they may run out of money, or by treating money with respect and not wasting it, they train themselves not to act flippantly with the money of others.)
According to this opinion, משה was only going to be saved through a neis, anyway, and once Yocheved was relying on a neis, this concept of valuing money for the reasons stated became her concern.
## Shmuess 2
According to this opinion, even though Yocheved was relying on a neis anyway, using stronger wood would have helped משה survive longer, על פי דרך הטבע.
Additionally, we know that משה was a special baby. He was born with a bris and the house lit up when he was born.
In addition, presumably the price of getting more expensive wood wasn't that much money. We are talking about a relatively small box. It would seem that this would do little to prevent Yocheved from theoretically coming to steal some day.
(Rebbi didn't mention this, but it seems reasonable to assume that the whole concern that Yocheved would potentially come to steal is already farfetched.)
Additionally, even though Yocheved was relying on a neis, we do know that there is a concept that one should minimize a neis as much as one can. (The Maharzu actually says that as the פשט for the שיטה that argues on this opinion in this מדרש.)
We see the importance that emes plays. If there is a slight concern that one may come to steal, even in such a situation, one needs to be cognizant of it and do whatever they can to minimize it.
## Shmuess 3
We also see how calculated גדולים were. Even in such a situation, Yocheved is attempting to figure out what the ratzon Hashem is about which type of wood she should use.
# Shmuess 4
## בראשית רבה עח יד (לג יד) עם רש"י על בראשית לג יד
Esav wanted to travel with יעקב, but יעקב told him to travel on ahead and יעקב would catch up with him at Seir. However, יעקב never did go to Seir.
יעקב was the paradigm of truth. He certainly wouldn't tell Esav a straight-up falsehood like that.
It seems that יעקב did say a straight lie. There is a פסוק in Ovadia that says that after משיח comes, יעקב will go to הר Seir (to judge Esav). So, יעקב will (eventually) go to Seir.
יעקב mislead Esav because he was afraid that Esav would have a change of heart and ambush יעקב.
## <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Some 'גמ. Need to Find out which</span>
Rebbi עקיבא's תלמידים mislead a group of bandits in a similar way as יעקב did with Esav. The תלמידים told the thieves that they were going to a certain city that they had no intention of going to.
When they later asked their rebbi if they did the correct thing, Rebbi עקיבא learned from יעקב that this was indeed correct.
## Nachlas יעקב על רש"י על בראשית לג יד
יעקב technically didn't lie. He is (eventually) going to go to Seir. These תלמידים didn't go to that place. It seems that יעקב was technically allowed to say a straight lie. However, because he was the paradigm of truth, he wouldn't have done that. He only said what he said because it wasn't a straight-up falsehood.
The תלמידים of Rebbi עקיבא, while presumably very חשוב, were obviously not on the level of יעקב and so for them it was okay to say a straight-up falsehood.
## Shmuess
Once again, we see the tremendous importance of emes. Even though יעקב was allowed to lie in this sitiution, he wouldn't have done it if he would have had to give a straight lie.
# Shmuess 5 and 6
## שמות לו ה רבינו בחיי שם
The פסוק is telling us about the greatness of the workers for the משכן who worked with tremendous אמונה. Whenever a donation would come in, they would stop what they were doing and report it to משה רבינו.
We also see the greatness of משה that he didn't use the money for other purposes.
## Shmuess 5
פשט in the first part of the רבינו בחיי seems to be that the workers didn't want there to be untracked donations that were in their possessions as this opens up a nissayon for them. To avoid this situation, they would repeatedly stop what they were doing and go report the donation to משה. Obviously that is rather annoying. They could have made it easier on themselves and give משה a full report at the end of the day, or something like that. However, that would open them up to a nissayon of gezel, and because of the importance of emes, they didn;t want to even be tempted with that.
## Shmuess 6
The פשוט reading of the רבינו בחיי is that we are praising משה for not taking the money. However, this is  to praiseworthy. If he took the money, he would be a thief. People who are far less than משה wouldn't straight-up steal donations.
It seems that פשט is that משה would have used the money for usages that are tangential to the needs of the משכן. Perhaps he would save the money for karbanos, or for a repair fund for the משכן, or things of that nature.
However, משה did not do this. He made sure to only use the money for the משכן because that was what the people were donating the money for.
From the fact that משה is getting praised for this shows us that this is something which is not an easy thing to see through.
Many times, we have many svaros about how donation money should be spent and how it really makes sense to use it for this or that. We see how difficult it can be to use proper discernment when it comes to properly judging how to use donated funds.
