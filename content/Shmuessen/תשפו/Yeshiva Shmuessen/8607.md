# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;ספר החינוך
We blow שופר on Rosh Hashana because people are physical and need something to jumpstart them.
## Shmuess
This seems to be true for at least most people (if not all people), even big צדיקים. Even though they obviously appreciate the gravity of the moment, there is still more for them to tap into.
We see the power of the physical nature of a person and how it can hold someone back.
In addition, we see that we need to take advantage of the moment. The שופר and the whole atmosphere around Elul and certainly the ימים נוראים can help a person make strides that they may not be able to match at a different point in the year.
# Shmuess 2
## &lrm;ויקרא יג רבינו בחיי
Hashem gave people צרעת because they did עבירות. However, Hashem brought the צרעת in stages to give people a chance to do תשובה. They would first receive צרעת on their house. If they continued doing עבירות, they would receive צרעת on their clothing. If they continued doing עבירות they would receive צרעת on their body. And even on their body, there were varying degrees of severity. A person would start at the lesser degrees, and only if they would keep sinning would the level of affliction become progressively worse.
In addition, צרעת was a supernatural phenomenon. It only occurred inside of ארץ ישראל.
## Shmuess
First of all, we see Hashem's tremendous love for כלל ישראל. Hashem set up this whole system and acts with such patience as this person keeps sinning again and again and again. Still, Hashem keeps reminding this person in stages to try and get him to do תשובה.
In addition, we see how stubborn a person can be. Despite seeing these supernatural signs, and despite the fact that they keep getting worse, and despite the fact that this person knows why he is receiving them (its pretty explicit in the תורה why one receives צרעת), he continues to sin. We see that תשובה is hard and nothing outside can guarantee that we will do it. It will only happen if we make it happen.
