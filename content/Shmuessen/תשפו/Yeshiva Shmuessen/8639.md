# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;שמות רבה לז ד (כח א) עם יפי תואר שם
משה רבינו wanted to be the כהן גדול in addition to being the leader.
Hashem didn't allow this because there would be a drop lacking in משה's teaching of the תורה and the world would be lost.
## Shmuess
We see the tremendous value of תורה. We see that even משה רבינו, to some small degree, did not appreciate the full value of תורה.
This would have only caused a small change in משה's ability to teach. This is clear because the מדרש explicitly says it would have only caused a small change, and because משה obviously would not have asked for the כהונה if this would have caused a major change. As such, משה asked for the כהונה.
Yet, the truth was that this slight lack of תורה would have caused the destruction of the whole world. This clearly shows the tremendous חשיבות of תורה.
In addition, משה himself didn't realize this (which is why he made his request). We see that appreciating the greatness of תורה is something that anyone can underappreciate.
# Shmuess 2
## &lrm;ויקרא רבה לא ה (כד ב)
תורה is called "strength" because it is something that if one put effort into, other people will follow what he says.
We see this from משה רבינו. Hashem told משה that because he worked so hard in תורה, &lrm;כלל ישראל is going to listen to what he teaches.
## Shmuess
There are lots of reasons I could have thought that כלל ישראל would listen to משה. We know that משה was was of the wisest people who ever lived. משה was the only person to receive the תורה directly from Hashem. Those would seemingly be good reasons that the people would listen to משה.
We see the supernatural nature of the תורה. The fact that משה put in effort comes across to כלל ישראל and that is what makes them listen to him, over all of his other מעלות.
(Rebbi said that there are certainly numerous סברות that can be given for why this is and he suspected that presumably, a few of them were at play.)
