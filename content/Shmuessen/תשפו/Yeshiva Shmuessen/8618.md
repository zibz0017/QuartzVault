מוצאי# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;בראשית רבה סה יז (כז טז) עם יפי תואר שם
When יעקב was going to "steal" the ברכות, רבקא carried the food for יעקב to the door of יצחק room. She told him that she did this because of her love for יעקב, and she would have gone with him all the way into the room if she could.
## Shmuess
We see multiple lessons about חזוק.
First, we see how doing something small for someone, even something that they can easily do on their own, can provide חזוק. Obviously יעקב was fully capable of carrying the food from the kitchen to יצחק's doorway. But still, רבקא carried it for him to show how much she was with him in this.
Second, we see how that even though it is obvious that she is carrying this to provide חזוק, she still spells out that she did it because of her love for him and that she would keep going with him if she could. This is all obvious, but she clearly spelled it out because it could make a difference.
Third, we see that everyone needs חזוק. Even יעקב needed חזוק.
# Shmuess 2
## &lrm;דברים לא ז
משה told יהושע before all of כלל ישראל that יהושע was going to lead them into ארץ ישראל.
## &lrm;ספורנו שם
This was even though משה himself was not going to do this.
## &lrm;דברים לא יד
Hashem told משה to bring יהושע with him to the אוהל מועד so Hashem could talk directly with יהושע.
## &lrm;רלב"ג שם
Hashem did this in order to strengthen יהושע's resolve that he was going to be the one who led כלל ישראל into ארץ ישראל. This was to make sure that he would stay true to the תורה and מצוות.
Hashem needed to do this because even though Hashem already told this to יהושע at הר חורב, perhaps that was assuming that יהושע would grow to the proper level to be the leader and perhaps he had not done so. Or, perhaps, יהושע had done an עבירה since הר חורב and so for that reason he couldn't be the leader. To this, Hashem himslef had to give יהושע חזוק that he would be the leader.
## Shmuess
There was obviously no indication that something had happened. In addition, הר חורב was only a few months earlier. It isn't likely that something happened in that small amount of time.
In addition, right before Hashem told משה to bring יהושע into the אוהל מועד, משה had told כלל ישראל that יהושע would be the one who led them. Obviously, he is not making this up. This came from Hashem. What was יהושע concerned about?
It would seem that he had some illogical thought that משה had said this on his own without Hashem, and perhaps יהושע really had done something, or not grown enough, to be the leader.
Obviously this is totally illogical. There is no way that משה gathered the entire כלל ישראל together to tell them that יהושע would be their leader without asking Hashem before doing this.
Furthermore, it seems that משה needed to address that fact that יהושע would be the one to bring כלל ישראל into ארץ ישראל even though משה himself was not.
This is also obviously illogical. There was never going to be anyone as great as משה who would come along. Would that mean that nobody would ever be able to bring כלל ישראל into ארץ ישראל? Would Hashem be going back on his word to bring כלל ישראל into ארץ ישראל? Obviously not. That would mean that *someone* who was not as great as משה was going to be the one to do it.
Additionally, it wasn't enough for Hashem to tell משה to tell יהושע, Hashem Himself had to tell יהושע.
Also, we are talking about a great person like יהושע, not a random individual.
It also seems from the רלב"ג that this could have made a difference, that perhaps יהושע would not have led כלל ישראל properly if he did not hear this from Hashem.
We see how everybody needs חזוק.

## &lrm;במדבר רבה יט (כא, לד)
When יעקב was preparing to fight against Esav he was afraid that perhaps he had done some עבירה that he didn't realize.
Similarly, משה was afraid when he was about to fight against עוג. He was afraid because עוג had lived for a very long time, and so it seemed that he had some sort of זכות on his side.
This was because he told אברהם that לוט was captured, and even though he had bad intentions (he hoped that אברהם would be killed), Hashem gave him tremendous reward that he lived for a long time. (This point is something that is discussed by numerous מדרשים. Some say that he had actually been living since the mabul.)
Hashem therefor told משה that he should not be afraid.
## &lrm;במדבר כא לד, תרגום יונתן שם
עוג made fun of אברהם that he was like a tree that doesn't bear fruit (or something like that) because he doesn't have any children. Hashem therefore kept עוג alive so he would be able to see that אברהם would in fact become a father of a whole nation of people and would even be killed by one of his descendants.
## Shmuess
יעקב was doing the proper thing by being afraid. Being afraid allowed him to be able to focus better on his reliance on Hashem, daven better, and all that. (אשרי אדם מפחד תמיד)
Seemingly, משה was trying to do similar (from the fact that the מדרש seems to be comparing the two).
However, Hashem needed to calm משה down a bit. It seems that he may have accidentally overdone it and was perhaps feeling some sort of חלישות הדעת. (It seems that this was not משה's fault as it doesn't appear that משה was held to task for this.)
Why was משה worried? Obviously, he was משה and so he had tremendous zechusim.
Additionally, the reason עוג lived so long was not a particularly great זכות. He may have lived a long time but either this was because he had selfishly told אברהם that לוט was captured (hoping that he would die), or because he had poked fun at אברהם.
As such, why did משה need Hashem to help mitigate his fear?
Once again, it seems that people need חזוק, and even משה רבינו in this situation needed Hashem to calm him down a little bit.
As a side point, we also see that, at times, a person can make themselves afraid to try and connect with Hashem, but this can accidentally be taken too far, even for great צדיקים.
