מוצאי# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;בראשית ט כ רש"י ד"ה ויחל, ספורנו ד"ה ויחל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The first thing that נח planted after getting off of the תבה was grapes.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This was not proper. נח made himself "חולין" and this led to extremely bad results (the story which followed this).</span>
## Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This obviously was not a halachic violation. There obviously is no הלכה about what one should plant when they get off of a תבה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מסתמא, נח had good intentions when he was planting the grapes. He probably wanted to use the grapes for kiddush or some other higher purpose.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Yet, because נח didn't have this proper sensitivity for the negative aspects of wine, it led to חם doing what he did and כנען getting cursed.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see that even things that are not straight out הלכה are really important. Having sensitivities is extremely important.</span>

## &lrm;דברים כא יב רמב"ן
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">By an אישת יפת תואר, the person brings the woman into his house for 30 days and lets her cry for her family and country. After doing this, amongst other things, the person may live with her.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason why she is given 30 days is because it isn't proper to live with a woman while she is in a state of mourning. As such, she is given time to mourn so she could somewhat feel better, and then the person can live with her.</span>
## Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason for this whole thing of אישת יפת תואר is because we are concerned that the person may do an עבירה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, we don't allow this person to marry her immediately because of this idea that it isn't proper to live with a woman in such a state.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Not only that, but the רמב"ן says that we give her this time to feel *somewhat* better. This isn't even making her completely better.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see the importance of sensitivities. We are  taking a risk that this person may do the עבירה, just for the sake of making her feel somewhat better when he lives with her, because it isn't proper to live with her when she is in such a state of grief.</span>
# Shmuess 3
## &lrm;שמות לח ח רש"י ד"ה במראות הצובאות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Women donated their mirrors to the משכן.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">משה was disgusted by the mirrors because they were used for תאוה. Hashem, however, told משה that these were the most precious thing to him because the women had used these mirrors in מצרים to "seduce" their husbands so they would live together and create future generations.</span>
## Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how difficult it is to properly weight these types of things. Even משה רבינו was able to make a mistake to the point that he was totally off: he found these mirrors disgusting, but in truth, they were the thing that Hashem found most precious.</span>
# Shmuess 4
## &lrm;בראשית נ יז רבינו בחיי ד"ה ויבך יוסף
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We don't find in the פסוק that יוסף forgave his ברכות. He cried when they asked for forgiveness from him and he comforted them and they seemed to find favor with יוסף, but he never seemed to fully forgive them.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because of this, the brothers were not able to do a full תשובה because one can't ever do a full תשובה when one hurts another unless they receive mechila from the person they hurt.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Because of this lack of full תשובה, this resulted in the עשרה הרוגי לפימלכות.</span>
## Shmuess
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While יוסף didn't fully forgive them, they were able to find grace with יוסף. It seems that this is a harsh punishment for this.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see the harsh results that can occur when the exact parameters are not followed.</span>
# Shmuess 5
## &lrm;הלכות שכנים פרק יד מגיד משנה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are many general הלכות given in the תורה which are about acting with proper מדות and in a דרך yashar. Many times these הלכות are not explicit because they are too all encompassing to be written down in specifics.</span>
## &lrm;אמונה ובטחון פרק ג (from the חזון איש)
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There are many situations that it can be extremely difficult to know what to do. For many of these situations the תורה tells us what is right, regardless of what out intuitions are.</span>
### &lrm;משל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For example, there are מחנכים in a certain town, and a new group of better מחנכים show up, and people like this new group better. However, because people begin sending their kids to this new group, the old mechnachim lose פרנסה.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Is one supposed to say לשון hara about the new group?</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the new group is improperly taking kids from the old group, it is a מצוה to speak about them and defend the old group.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it is correct for the new group to take the kids, one would violate the איסור of לשון hara by speaking about the new group (in addition to the fact that they may wrongly cause students not to go to the new group).</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can certainly hear two sides to the discussion.</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The answer to this happens to be an explicit 'גמ which says that when it comes to teaching תורה, there is no concept of being the "incumbent", or anything like that. Anyone can come in a "steal" the talmidim.</span>
7. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For other businesses, then there is such a concept. Depending on some details, in non-תורה related situations, one may not come in and "steal" one's business.</span>
## Shmuess
*Note: This wasn't actually a shmuess*
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The relationship between הלכה and one's intuitions and sense of right and wrong is very tricky. At times, one's sense of yashrus is considered important and one is supposed to follow it.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">At other times, one is supposed to subvert one's sense of right and wrong.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The only real way to know what to do is to go learn from someone older and wiser.</span>
