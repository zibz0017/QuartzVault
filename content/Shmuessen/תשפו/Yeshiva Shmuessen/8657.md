# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;בראשית לט ו רש"י
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יוסף was punished for not feeling the pain of his father after יוסף was sold to מצרים.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see a few things from here. We see how far one is responsible for feeling someone else's pain. Even though יוסף had just been sold to מצרים by his family and, presumably, was dealing with that, he is still held to task for not feeling the pain of his father enough.
We also see that one is supposed to feel someone else's pain even if they don't know about it and it doesn't do anything for them.
We also see how difficult it is to feel someone else's pain.</span>
# Shmuess 2
## &lrm;נפש החיים הקדמת בן המחבר
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Chaim Volozhiner would give his son, Rav Itzeleh Volozhiner, constant מוסר that he needs to feel the pain of others more. Rav Chaim would say that this is the main purpose of a person: to help others in whatever way one can.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*This wasn't actually a* real *shmuess*.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how difficult it is to feel the pains of others. Even Rav Itzeleh Volozhiner needed constant מוסר about this מידה.</span>
# Shmuess 3
## &lrm;חובות הלבבות שער חשבון הנפש פרק ג
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person needs to make an accounting of how much they care about this world and all of the pleasures that it offers and stack that against the way they the next world.
(The חובות הלבבות gives many examples of how much a person cares about this world and how his friends are only people who can help him achieve more pleasures in this world.)</span>
## &lrm;רבינו בחיי הקדמה פרשת וישב
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">משלי discusses how big of an עבירה it is to steal from a poor person.
A poor person is particularly mentioned because it is more common to steal from poor people as nobody likes him and so he doesn't have anyone who is going to defend him.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how polarized the different parts of us are.
On what hand, we know from various מאמרים in חז"ל (such as ספר אהבת חסד, from the Alter) about how much people love doing חסד.
On the other hand people, we see these מאמרים about how much people only love this world and that is all they care about.</span>
