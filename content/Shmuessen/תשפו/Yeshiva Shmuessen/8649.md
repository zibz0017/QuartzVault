# ויקרא יט ד רבינו בחיי
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The תורה ends off with the words "אני ה' אלוקיכם" by a few פסוקים to teach us that if one doesn't do מצוות they don't believe in Hashem (or at least, they are at risk of not believing in Hashem).
This is discussed in a 'גמ in :עבודה זרה יז which says that one who only learns תורה and doesn't do מצוות doesn't believe in Hashem.</span>
# עבודה זרה יז.
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Two תנאים were arrested by the Romans. One told the other that he, the other tana, would live, while he, himself, would not.
He knew this because the other tana engaged in learning תורה and חסד while he, himself, had only learned תורה and one who only engages on תורה is as if they don't have a G-d.
The 'גמ points out that this tana had actually engaged in acts of חסד but it was not to the proper level of someone on his level.</span>
# Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*I wrote this shmuess down considerably later so this may not be accurate.*</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to רבינו בחיי, it seems to be that this tana was saying that despite all of the תורה that he learned, there was something lacking (on a tiny level) in his אמונה because he didn't do מצוות (to the degree that he was supposed to).
It seems that every way of connecting to Hashem is unique and powerful and even תורה can't fully make up for the אמונה and בטחון that is fostered by doing other מצוות.</span>
