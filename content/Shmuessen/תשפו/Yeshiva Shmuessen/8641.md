# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;שמות פרק יא רלב"ג תועליות תועלת ב
Hashem told משה to speak strongly to פרעה. We see from here משה's strength and courage that he was willing to do this.
## Shmuess
Why did this require strength and bravery? It would seem that for someone on the מדרגה of משה, their בטחון and אמונה should be enough for them. For someone like משה, he should appreciate that only Hashem is in charge and פרעה can't do anything at all unless Hashem let's happen. As such, why does this require bravery?
We see how powerful appearances can be. Because the situation looks like פרעה is in control and speaking forcefully to him can cause him to get angry, בטחון alone was not enough (although, presumably, it was a major contributing factor). He needed this added factor of his strength and bravery.
# Shmuess 2
## &lrm;ספר החינוך מצוה ערה
A כהן with a מום is not allowed to serve because people won't have confidence that they will receive כפרה.
Likewise, the כהן who is going to get כפרה for people should be good looking so that people are more confident in his success.
## Shmuess
This is obviously a totally illogical thought. כפרה is not based on one's appearance; it is entirely based on the level of שלמות that one does the עבודה and on their מדרגה.
Despite this, people are naturally going to feel this way. And it is so ingrained into people that the תורה actually incorporates this into the הלכות.
We see how strongly ingrained into people being affected by superficial appearances is, even when it is totally irrelevant.
# Shmuess 3
## &lrm;ספר החינוך מצוה שפד
When going out to war (referred to as "in times of trouble"), trumpets must be blown.
One reason is because a person, by nature, needs to be "woken up" to things. Something like a trumpet blast is able to do this.
## Shmuess
We see how deeply permeated our physical nature is. Even though the person is going out to war, he is still "asleep" (that is the language of the ספר החינוך) without a physical wake-up call.
