# Shmuess 1
## ויקרא טו נד-נו רבינו בחיי
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חז"ל tell us that Hashem would first put צרעת on a person's house, then put it on a person's clothing, and lastly (if the person still hadn't done תשובה), Hashem would put the צרעת on the person themselves.
However, the תורה writes it in the reverse order: first the תורה writes about צרעת on a person's body, then it talks about צרעת on the person's clothing, and lastly it talks about צרעת on the person' possessions.
The תורה did this because of דרכיה דרכי נועם. People don't want to hear about punishments that increase in pain. People want to think that it is only going to get easier from this point.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We obviously aren't dealing with the greatest of individuals. We are dealing with a person who sins repeatedly, despite being punished repeatedly.
Despite this, Hashem somewhat "distorted" the תורה (by somewhat implying that צרעת occurs in the reverse order of what it actually does).
(I don't remember if Rebbi also pointed out that this doesn't seem to make much of a difference anyway. The person would still be receiving the same punishments, albeit, in a different order, and it is quite possible that the person knows the actual truth about the order of the punishments, anyway, so this is basically just a game. We are pretending the the punishments get easier even though we all know the truth that they do not.)
We see how much Hashem cares about each person, even the רשעים
The ראש ישיבה thought that this realization is necessary for a person to be able to accept the תוכחה and do תשובה. (Rabbi Grunblatt wasn't sure if this point was muchrach.)
</span>
# Shmuess 2
## מדרש שוחר טוב תהלים נג י
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">דוד went to go kill נבל&lrm; (נבל had been מורד במלכות against דוד after דוד had been anointed but before he was established as the king). אביגיל gave דוד מוסר and convinced him that this was not the correct thing to do. She then told דוד that if he didn't want to listen to her מוסר, he should give himself מוסר.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This מדרש is difficult to understand. Was אביגיל trying to play some sort of game of pretend or something? What was she saying?
It seems that תוכחה is the type of thing that only one can really give themselves. Even though אביגיל told דוד not to go, it would be his own choice and his own work to internalize what she was saying.
This can be a useful way of trying to get oneself to accept תוכחה. One can acknowledge the fact that they, themselves need to internalize the message for it to have a positive impact on them.
(Additionally, once again, we see how hard it is to accept תוכחה. &lrm;דוד was known as a paradigm for accepting תוכחה, and yet, even he needed to use תחבולות to be able to accept the תוכחה.)</span>
