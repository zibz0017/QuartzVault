## בראשית רבה מא מד Presented by רבינו בחיי על ויקרא כו א-ב

Hashem tells us "'אני ה" by many מצוות to inform us that we will be getting tremendous schar from keeping the מצוות.
We see this from a קל וחומר from פרעה. &lrm;פרעה told "יוסף, "אני פרעה after יוסף interpreted his dreams and gave פרעה the solution to what to do about the upcoming famine, and proceeded to take off his ring and gave it to יוסף.
If this is the reward for listening to a human being, we see the how unimaginably large reward awaits us for doing any of these מצוות that Hashem said "'אני ה" by. On the other side, we see how powerfully negative it is not to keep these מצוות that Hashem says "'אני ה" by.
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I think one of the examples of such a מצוה is שבת. I don't know if rebbi said other examples of such מצוות.</span>
## Shmuess
### Question
There seems to be two major problems with this קל וחומר.
First of all, יוסף solved a problem that was not able to be solved by anyone else. He did something uniquely special. The מצוות that we do are done by many people all the time. There certainly doesn't seem to be anything uniquely special about the מצוות that are done by most people.
Additionally, the task that יוסף accomplished was of unique importance. יוסף saved an entire country, and perhaps, the entire world, from destruction. What is accomplished by the מצוות that are done by random people?
### Answer
It seems that we need to get a proper understanding of ourselves and what we accomplish.
The answer to the first question seems to be that each one of us has our own world. We are the only person who can do anything in our own world. Just as יוסף was uniquely capable in his situation, whenever we do one of these מצוות that it says "'אני ה" by (and presumably by any other מצוה, as well), we are also uniquely capable. Every person is the only person who can do that מצוה that can have a unique effect on their own world.
Similarly, the effects of each of these מצוות that we do create whole worlds and have even larger effects than what was accomplished by יוסף.
