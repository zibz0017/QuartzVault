# Shmuess 1
## במדבר יא יז רבינו בחיי ד"ה ואצלתי מן
Hashem took some of His שפע from Moshe Rabbeinu and gave it to some of the zikainim who would lead with Moshe. Moshe's greatness was heightened through this as Moshe now became more like Hashem. Hashem has His "beis din" that receives שפע from Hashem, and now Moshe has his "beis din" (70 zikeinim) that receives שפע from Moshe.
## Shmuess
For us, this seems to be lowering Moshe's status. Moshe used to be head and shoulders above everyone else. Now he has "competition".

We see how the Torah views things. Greatness is not measured against others. It is measured by how similar one is to Hashem.

(In the context of the topic, the idea was not not compare oneself to others as this is not what the Torah considers to be important.)
