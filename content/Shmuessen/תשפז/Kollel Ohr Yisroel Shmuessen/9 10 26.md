# עבודה זרה כח
Rebbi Elazar בן Dordia was with every זונה in the world. One time, he traveled far to a specific זונה. Before he was with her, she passed gas, and the זונה said the same way the gas isn't going to go back to the place it came, Elazar isn't going to be able to do תשובה.
This shook him up and he immediately left. He went many physical entities (like mountains and rivers) and asked them to intercede for תשובה on his behalf, however, they all said that they could not do this.
At this point, he realized that he would have to be responsible for himself.
He started crying and feeling tremendous charata, to the point that it actually killed him.
The 'גמ says that he was able to acquire עולם הבא in a moment.
## מהרש"א
He was afraid that he would have to die to accomplish the תשובה that he required.
## מאירי
We see from this 'גמ that one shouldn't worry that they can't do תשובה, because, with death, one will always be able to do תשובה.
# Point 1
What does the מאירי mean that you shouldn't worry about תשובה that you just need to die? That doesn't seem to be very comforting?
We see how important תשובה is. תשובה is so important that the idea of dying is small compared to the idea of not having accomplished תשובה.
