# בראשית א כא רש"י שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem put אדם to sleep when creating חוה so he wouldn't see from where he took the piece so he wouldn't be disgusted by her.</span>
# Shmuess
## First Point
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">אדם was tremendously great. There are חז"ל about his greatness.
We see that even, to some degree, someone like אדם is affected by physical things.</span>
## Second Point
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It's not like this would have made a difference in some practical way. He would have still married her and all that. Still Hashem doesn't want that. Hashem wants a husband and wife to be as close as possible, even if that doesn't lead to מצוות.</span>
## Third Point
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see the kindness of Hashem that Hashem is so involved that he cares about this little tiny thing.</span>
