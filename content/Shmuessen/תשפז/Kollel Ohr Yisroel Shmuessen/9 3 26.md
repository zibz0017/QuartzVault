# או"ח שלחן ערוך סימן תרכב
We lein the pasrhios of arayos and Yonah on יום Kippur.
# משנה ברורה שם
This is because arayos is something very powerful.
# באר היטב שם, תוס'&rlm; על מגילה לא.
The women dress nicely because it is יום טוב and so we give a warning so that people don't stumble.
# Shmuess
It doesn't seem that the women are being not צניעות. It seems that they are acting appropriately.
In addition, we are talking about a person on יום kippur. You would imagine that the person would be on a high מדרגה and on guard, especially after davening so much.
Apparently, we see that a person is always susceptible. The יצר הרע is very powerful, especially for these things.
Sometimes, after יום kippur, the יצר הרע can get us down when we make mistakes. He can tell us that we are fake and that our יום kippur was a waste.
We can use this to realize that the יצר הרע is powerful to a point beyond belief to push off this depression that he tries to get us in.
Obviously, one has to be careful not to use this to start doing עבירות.
