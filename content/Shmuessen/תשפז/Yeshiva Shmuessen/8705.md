
# דברים כט ח, טו, יז-יט, כח ספורנו שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">כלל ישראל was standing before Hashem to make a ברית. If they would accept the תורה and מצוות, Hashem would do good for them, and if not, they would be punished.
משה told כלל ישראל that there may be some who think that they can "fool Hashem" and make the ברית but really not accept it in their heart. They think that perhaps Hashem won't punish them because they didn't really accept the ברית.&lrm; משה was warning them that this would not be the case. They would be held to task for violating the ברית.</span>
# Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that משה is warning כלל ישראל not to take this ברית insincerely as they will still be held accountable. It seems that this is a real concern.
We see that people can convince themselves that they can "blend in with the crowd" and Hashem won't notice them and Hashem will give them ברכה like everyone else, even though they didn't really accept the ברית. It seems that פשט is that to our own senses and experiences, when dealing with a person in such a situation, one would be able to be lost in the crowd. This seems to be able to cause a person to be able to believe that about Hashem in some sense.
This is different than most cases where a person is נכשל in some עבירה. Over there, the person is faced with some sort of תאוה that they can indulge in right now. This person may block out the fact that Hashem can see him and there will be a price to pay. Over here, there is no תאוה in front of him. There is just the fact that Hashem is going to do good for him. But why would he possibly think that Hashem is going to do good for him? That is absurd. Yet, the person is able to convince himself that Hashem "won't notice" and just include him in the ברכה.
It is משמע that a way to deal with this is to put everyone "before Hashem", almost like a physical representation of the fact that Hashem can see everyone and so you are not going to be able to slip through the cracks.
Additionally, the person is convincing himself of two opposite things. It sounds like this person believes that he will be let off the hook because he never really accepted it in his heart. That means that he thinks that Hashem knows what he is thinking. So it comes out that this person both convinces himself that Hashem knows what he is thinking and that Hashem *doesn't* know what he is thinking at the same time.
Furthermore, it should be pointed out that we are dealing with the generation right after the דור דעה who were obviously tremendously great. There was even a concern for them.</span>
<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am not sure if rebbi mentioned the fact that משה may have specifically mentioned that he is talking to everyone, including the leaders and צדיקים (as discussed in פסוק ט with the ספורנו there).
Additionally, I am not sure if rebbi really focused on the fact that it seems that a person may have such a thought to rebel against Hashem and not accept this ברית because of the השפעה of the surrounding people (as discussed in פסוק יז with the ספורנו there), and so we see the power of influence.</span>
