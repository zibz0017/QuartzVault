# &lrm;דברים כט טז בכור שור and מדרש תנחומא ג
כלל ישראל will see the disgusting things that the people of ארץ ישראל are doing (referring to עבודה זרה) and they should make sure not to do them.
There things are referred to as disgusting because the גויים find the disgusting. They serve the idols and nothing that they "daven" for happens. They end up throwing them out and getting a new one.
The gold and silver idols they don't throw out. When they see that they aren't working, they stop worshipping them, but they keep them for the precious metals. Additionally, they leave their gold and silver idols inside because they are afraid that they will get stolen. See how the idols are so powerless that they are not even able to prevent themselves from being stolen, much less prevent someone from stealing something else.
# Shmuess
It is clear that these פסוקים are trying to inspire us not to serve עבודה זרה. It seems that the fact that the גויים find this disgusting will affect us and help us resist the pull to do עבודה זרה.
Why does it matter that the גויים throw out their idols and, at least on some level, realize that this is worthless.
Furthermore, they get other idols. It's not like they actually really get it. They don't end up serving Hashem.
Furthermore, this is only some גויים, not all of them. There are פסוקים in נביא about how the גויים cling to their idols. Presumably, the way to answer this סתירה is that some גויים will throw out their idols but others do not.
So why does this make a difference to כלל ישראל? How can this affect what people from כלל ישראל will choose to do?

Apparently we see the power of השפעה. Despite all of these things, we can still take inspiration.
Additionally, we see that we can choose what to focus on and use that to give ourselves inspiration. כלל ישראל can take positive השפעה from the fact that some גויים throw out their idols, even though others do not, and even though these גויים themselves get new idols.

# Additional Point from Rebbi
Rebbi felt that regarding the point that כלל ישראל is supposed to take inspiration from the fact that the גויים hide their gold and silver idols was clear from the מדרש תנחומא, not as much from the בכור שור.
