# Shmuess 1
## דברים כג כג-כד רמב"ן שם
 <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The תורה says that if a person doesn't take נדרים, he won't commit sins (presumably, specifically the sin of violating a נדר).
 At first glance, the פסוק seems rather obvious. What the פסוק is teaching us is that we have a person who feels inspired by something to bring a קרבן and so he makes a נדר to bring it. He does this because he wants to give himself extra motivation to go and bring the קרבן. He totally plans to actually carry through with his נדר. However, something comes up and interferes. He would really want to bring the קרבן, but he is unable to because of an אונס.
 The person may think that he had originally made the נדר with proper intentions, he had done so to try and give himself extra motivation to bring the קרבן, he had no reason to suspect that something would go awry, and now he is in a state of אונס. He didn't do anything wrong.
 the תורה is teaching us that a person has to think about the future and realize what might occur.</span>
## Shmuess
### Point 1
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The obvious point that we see is that a person needs to really think about the ramification of one's actions and how it can effect the future.</span>
### Point 2
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how tricky the יצר הרע can be. He can make us think that we are doing a big מצוה, but, in reality, he is setting us up to do עבירות.</span>
# Shmuess 2
## בבא בתרא
Those who rule over their יצר make a "חשבון about the world". They weigh the loss of doing a מצוה against its reward and the gain from doing an עבירה against its punishment.
## מסילת ישרים
Only those who rule over their יצר are able to give us this advise.
## Explanation of מראה מקום
The מסילת ישרים seems difficult to understand. Why is it that only someone who has ruled over their יצר is able to give us this advice? This doesn't seem like difficult advice to understand. It just seems difficult to implement.
And if there is something that is theoretically difficult to understand about this, why does it matter that someone who rules over the יצר tells us? How is this going to help us understand?
It seems that on some level, we are not able to understand the advice. The יצר הרע gets in the way and makes this seemingly obvious advice, on some level, incomprehensible. (I am not 100% sure what Rebbi meant by this but I think he may have meant that on some level, the יצר הרע gets us to believe that the benefit from doing an עבירה justifies the punishment and the loss of doing a מצוה is not justified by the reward.)
The מסילת ישרים gives us advice on how to do this. The מסילת ישרים tells us that if someone who has conquered his יצר tells this to us, we would be able to understand it. It seems that when we come face to face with a person who has conquered his יצר and gone through this world properly, we automatically can't help it but believe the advice he is telling us. He is living proof that this works.
## Shmuess
We see that the יצר הרע can blind us to even the most obvious advice (at least on some level).
It seems that a way of defeating him with this tactic is presenting the truth in such a real sense that there is no way to deny it.
# Shmuess 3
## במדבר רבה כ יז (כב לו)
When בלעם came to בלק to curse כלל ישראל, &lrm;בלק offered him 1 sheep and one cattle. This paltry gift greatly upset בלעם and בלעם "got בלק back" by making him bring 7 sheep and 7 cattle for קרבנות.
The reason בלק gave such a small gift was because either he did not want to share the כבוד with בלעם or he was stingy and didn't want to give בלעם too much of his money. Despite the small gift, בלעם stayed because of his hatred of כלל ישראל so he strongly desired to curse them, anyway. (Rebbi said that this was clear from the מפרשים.)
## Shmuess
בלק hired בלעם because he was afraid that his country would be destroyed by כלל ישראל. If that would happen, obviously בלק would lose his wealth and become a fugitive at best, or get captured and killed at worst. Why would he possibly take a risk on this? Why wouldn't he just cough up the cash and/or give בלעם the כבוד he wants. בלק was taking a huge risk that בלעם wouldn't go through with cursing כלל ישראל. He didn't know that בלעם's hatred of כלל ישראל would get him to stay.
We see that sometimes, the יצר הרע can be so powerful, that it can actually undermine itself. בלק's desire for money and כבוד almost caused him to totally destroy himself.
# Shmuess 4
*The ראש ישיבה would use this מדרש from פרשת בו on a פסוק in בהעלותך. Rebbi wasn't sure but guessed that perhaps the ראש ישיבה did this because by פרשת בו, &lrm;כלל ישראל didn't have any food at all so their complaints were more justified. In פרשת בהעלותך, they had מן. People pointed out that the point of the shmuess is the same, regardless. Rebbi seemed to have thought of the same thing, as well.*
## במדבר יא ה
כלל ישראל complained to משה that they were tired of the מן and they remembered the food that they had in מצרים.
## מדרש רבה בו
The מצריים would force the בני ישראל to sit and watch them cook up meat and eat it while not letting the בני ישראל partake as a form of torture. The בני ישראל would only have bread to eat.
## Shmuess
We see how the יצר הרע can totally corrupt one's memories. The מצריים used the meat to torture כלל ישראל. Somehow, the יצר הרע got כלל ישראל to view the experience as positive when it came to complaining about the מן.
# Shmuess 5
*I am not sure if rebbi added anything more than what the מראה מקום said.*
## שבת לא:&rlm;
Sometimes the יצר הרע can get one to do עבירות without any sort of tricks. He can get people to do עבירות even though they know how things are going to end, and even without distracting them or making them forget about the consequences.
## Shmuess
We see the tremendous power of the יצר that even though people are fully aware of the consequences, they still can get taken by the יצר הרע.
# Additional Points from Rebbi
Rebbi pointed out that while all of this can make one depressed, one can use this to realize the שכר one gets for fighting and defeating the יצר.
Rebbi also mentioned the מדרש that when משיח comes, Hashem will slaughter the יצר הרע. The יצר הרע will appear to the רשעים like a strand of hair and like a mountain to צדיקים. The רשעים will not believe that they could not defeat such a weak foe and the צדיקים won't be able to believe that they defeated something so powerful.
One of the understandings of the מדרש is that the צדיקים take everything one step at a time and they are consistent about it. They don't realize it but after a whole lifetime of work, they end up having climbed a mountain.
