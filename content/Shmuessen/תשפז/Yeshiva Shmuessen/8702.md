# &lrm;ילקוט שמעוני תתסג (On דברים יא יג)
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The פסוק says "אנכי מצוה אתכם היום".
A person may feel a sense of hopelessness about keeping the תורה. He may feel that the תורה is too large. There is no way that he can keep the whole thing. The תורה is telling us to keep the perspective that he is a day worker ("היום").
למשל, a king hires people to fill a bottomless pit. The fool comes and sees how futile it is and says that this is impossible and leaves. The wise person sees this and says that it doesn't matter to him. Either way he is getting paid.</span>
# Shmuess
## Point 1
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Presumably, the person is not upset by the fact that he can't keep מצוות that are impossible for him to do and תורה that is impossible for him to learn. A person without arms isn't distraught that he can't put on תפילין.
It seems that you have to say that the person is referring to תורה and מצוות that are within his reach.
This פסוק is talking to the דור דעה and it seems to say that they feel that what they are going to do is only going to be a small drop compared to what they feel like they are able to do. It seems that they feel that they have this within reach because of the great potential that their נשמה has. Unfortunately, the נשמה is buried under layers and layers of corporeality, but the potential is still there.
And we have the same נשמה. Our נשמה is buried under far more layers of physicality, but nonetheless, we still have this potential. Our נשמה has the potential to keep and learn the תורה to the level that would make the accomplishments of the דור דעה seem like almost nothing compared to it.</span>
## Point 2
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see that this סברא of feeling that the תורה is too large to keep is a powerful סברא that can have real negative ramifications, even for the דור דעה. We have to put effort into holding onto this סברא that we are a day worker and we "get paid" regardless of whether we complete the job or not.
Rebbi pointed out that on the flip side, we can't just have this perspective. A person also needs to have a mentality of achrayus and attempting to accomplish. If a person doesn't maintain this mindset, they are not going to put in their all. A person needs to balance both of these mindsets.</span>
# Own Thoughts
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">I am not sure why rebbi is sure that it is clear from hear that the heights of תורה are in our reach. the משל is about a person trying to fill a bottomless pit. That certainly seems to be beyond his reach and it still makes the person give up hope.</span>
