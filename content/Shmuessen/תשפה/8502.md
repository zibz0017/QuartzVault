# Shmuess 1
## ספרי פרשת בלק
The תורה talks about the Kohen גדול and the daughter of the kohen who is מזנה and gets killed, back-to-back. The תורה does this because the kohen גדול goes to the women before she gets killed and tells her that if only had behaved properly she could have had a child who would have been like him (meaning, a future kohen גדול), and because she did what she did, she won't be זוכה to that.
## Shmuess
It seems that the reason that this is done is to try and get this woman to feel some remorse for her עבירה.
However, there are a lot of factors to consider.
This clearly isn't about trying to change this woman around as her life is about to be over, regardless of what she feels.
She committed a terrible act. She isn't someone that we would expect to have particular sympathy about.
The kohen גדול has a tremendous amount of responsibilities. He has to take the time out to come to this person.
It's not like the woman doesn't know this, or can't even be told this by someone else. The whole reason for the kohen גדול to come is to provide a physical ציור to the words. It's not just some abstract idea that he child could have been a kohen גדול, her child could have been like this man in front of her. However, even without this ציור, it is still a powerful message.
We see from here how important the הרגשה of every person is. Even this woman who did such a terrible עבירה, we go through such lengths to try to get her to feel some more charata.
# Shmuess 2

## <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Unknown מדרש or 'גמ. I Didn't Write down the source</span>
There was a city that didn't receive rain for some time.
There was a man in this city who had gotten divorced. One day, during this drought, he saw his divorcee and he saw that she didn't have any money. He took pity on her and gave her money.
Someone saw this man give his divorcee money and thought that the money was for something inappropriate. He told the גדול of the city about this (as he thought that this may have been part of the reason for the drought).
The גדול brought this and asked this person what he was doing. The man told the גדול the truth: he was giving her money because she was in a bad situation and he took pity on her.
The גדול turned to Hashem and said, "this man took pity on his estranged wife, we, who are your children, should certainly be helped".
Hashem then gave them rain.
## Shmuess
We see how every person could make a huge impact. This person was presumably a regular person (and it is somewhat implied from the story that someone thought that he was engaging in inappropriate behavior). It is reasonable to assume that there were many great צדיקים who were davening and doing other great things to try and get Hashem to end the drought. (This story occurred during the times of the תנאים).
Yet, despite this, it was this person's מצוה that was the זכות that brought the rain.
We never know how things work and even regular people can make extraordinary impacts, even when the actions of great גדולים do not.

## &lrm;שמות יד יג אבן עזרא ד"ה התיצבו
One of the reasons that everyone who came out of מצרים had to die was because they weren't able to be soldiers due to the how they were brought up in an environment where they were trod on and beaten down in מצרים. They were not going to be able to defeat the כנענים, and, in fact, they would have lost to the עמלקים if not for משה's תפילות and the fact that עמלק only sent a small force.
## Shmuess
כלל ישראל was in the מדבר for forty years, constantly experiencing נסים. Shouldn't that be enough to knock this "slave mentality" out of them?
We see how powerful the feelings and mentalities we develop in our youth are.
