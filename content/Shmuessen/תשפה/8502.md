# שמות יד יג אבן Ezra ד"ה התיצבו
One of the reasons that everyone who came out of מצרים had to die was because they weren't able to be soldiers due to the how they were brought up in an environment where they were trod on and beaten down in מצרים. They were not going to be able to defeat the כנענים, and, in fact, they would have lost to the Amalekim if not for משה's tefilos and the fact that Amalek only sent a small force.
