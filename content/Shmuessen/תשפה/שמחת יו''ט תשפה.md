# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;ילקוט שמעוני רמז רעג
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hashem wanted to give כלל ישראל the תורה immediately when they left מצרים, however, they were constantly fighting. On many occasions they suggested to appoint a new leader and go back to מצרים. However, by הר Sinai, they were able to make peace with each other. Hashem then gave them the תורה as they were a nation that loved peace.</span>
## Shmuess
### Question
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">What does it mean that כלל ישראל was a nation that loved peace? They made peace one time. And it's not like they didn't have more fights after this. They argued to appoint a new leader to go back to מצרים on numerous occasions after this. Why are they referred to as a nation that loves peace?</span>
### Answer
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that peace is difficult. Even making peace for a short time is a terrific achievement, and the only way that was able to happen was because כלל ישראל was a nation that loved peace.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how difficult making peace is.</span>

# Shmuess 3
## &lrm;דברים כד ה חזקוני ד"ה נקי יהיה
If a person does ארוסין to a women but hasn't done ניסואין yet, he doesn't go out to fight in the army. However, it isn't right for him to just go on home after having gone out with his friends to the area where they select the soldiers. They are worried for their lives and he just goes on home?
Instead, he helps make sure that the food and supplies are in order and that the roads heading out to the battlefield are okay.
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">*I wrote this over later and I don't know if I got the shmuess exactly right.*</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It doesn't seem that this person is prepping the supplies because they need someone to take care of it and this person isn't fighting anyway. It seems that he needs to do this because of the fear of the people that he went with.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that this is supposed to help his friends with their fear.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">How is he doing this? It seems that by helping out with the war effort, he is providing morale support to his friends. This is even though he isn't going out to the battle. In addition, the people going out still have the same reason to be afraid. They are going to fight a war. It is obviously quite possible that they can get killed or maimed. Despite this, this clearly makes at least a somewhat noticeable difference, or else why is he wasting his time?</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how simply being with someone and showing that you care can make a difference in how feel.</span>
# Shmuess 4
## &lrm;ויקרא יב ג מושב זקנים שם
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One of the reasons that we do bris milah is because is creates a certain bond between all of the Jews that do this מצוה as this is a physical sign that all male Jews have on their bodies and it represents a person's dedication to Hashem that they are willing to do this because Hashem said to do this.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is a major reason for the מצוה of bris milah and may be a reason that one is not considered to have completed the whole תורה and its מצוות without doing a bris.</span>
## Shmuess
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This obviously isn't something that people see. Despite this, it still creates this bond between people.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We see how powerful of a connection to others we can feel, even for things that we would not expect to make such a difference.</span>
<span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We also see how important having these bonds are.</span>
