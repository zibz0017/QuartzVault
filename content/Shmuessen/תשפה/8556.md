# Shmuess 1
## &lrm;במדבר א יח רמב"ן שם
When כלל ישראל was counted with the machatzis hadhekel, משה and the nesiim would be standing by the collection and everyone would introduce themselves.
## &lrm;במדבר א מה רמב"ן שם
משה and Aharon would go around to every household and instead of simply asking the head of house how many people for the legion there were in the household, everyone eligible for the count would come out and present themselves to משה and Aharon and to be personally counted.
## Shmuess
Doing this obviously takes a lot of time for משה, Aharon, and the nesiim. They could have been doing other useful things, like learning תורה.

And the people are not with משה, Aharon, or the nesiim for very long. It seems to be a few moments. As such, why was this done?

We see how even a little bit more self-worth can make a big difference, that it is worth it for משה, Aharon, and the nesiim to spend so much time just make sure everyone has this little improvement in it.
# Shmuess 2
## &lrm;במדבר רבה ג א (ג ו)
Hashem responds with force to anyone that start up with כלל ישראל. We see this from שרה. When פרעה took her, Hashem smote him.
## Shmuess
What does the מדרש mean? שרה was a tremendous tzadeikes. Maybe Hashem defended שרה because of her tremendous level, but Hashem wouldn't do the same for the rest of us?

It seems that we see how much Hashem values כלל ישראל. Obviously שרה was on a much higher מדרגה and that matters a tremendous amount to Hashem. But simply the fact that we are כלל ישראל matters far more. Of course being a tzadik or tzadeikes adds to Hashem's love and care, but compared to how much Hashem loves us just for being part of כלל ישראל is so much larger, that the additional amount Hashem loves צדיקים barely adds anything compared to that.
# Shmuess 3
## &lrm;ויקרא רבה כ ד (טז א) with מתנת כהונה ד"ה מאת פני הקדש
The cousins of Nadav and Avihu were commanded to take out the bodies of Nadav and Avihu because having them exposed in front of Aharon caused him additional pain.
## Shmuess
What about the fact that the bodies are avi avos hatumah and they were in the משכן, which is obviously a bizayon to Hashem (even someone who is tamei to a lesser degree is not allowed into the משכן)? Why isn't that mentioned?

In addition, the main tzaar of Aharon was not that he sees the bodies. The main tzaar was that his sons were dead. Taking the bodies away does something but the significant part of the tzaar is still there.

We see how much Hashem cares about the tzaar of people, that even tumah in one of the holiest places is not mentioned next to the increased tzaar of the bodies being there in front of Aharon.
# Shmuess 4
## &lrm;שמות כ יז רש"י שם
Hashem spoke to כלל ישראל by הר Sinai so that the nations of the world will look up to כלל ישראל. In addition, this is to instill awe and fear into כלל ישראל. These are important to keep כלל ישראל from doing עבירות.
## Shmuess
We see how important it is to us for others, even the nations of the world, to view us in a positive way to keep us from doing עבירות.

This is the first reason given for why Hashem spoke to us directly at הר Sinai, even before the reason of instilling awe and fear. That means that the reason of being looked up to by the nations is actually the main reason, more so than the awe and fear that resulted from being talked to directly by Hashem. Obviously, that has a powerful effect, and yet, the effect from the way the others nations view us is even more powerful.
# Own Thoughts
## Shmuess 1
I am not sure what the different רמב"ןs are talking about. They sound like a contradiction. I assume it is clear from reading the chumash what is going on.

Another point to make is that while we are talking about meeting משה, Aharon, and the nesiim and that certainly has a powerful short-term effect, it is hard to imagine that that really makes a difference in the long-term.
## Shmuess 2
I believe that we are learning out from Hashem's protection of שרה to what Hashem would do for a chunk of כלל ישראל, not just for an individual person. I don't know if it makes sense to say that we can see from here to what Hashem does for a random individual person. The חובות הלבבות (Shaar הבטחון) discusses how a person who does עבירות should not expect Hashem to save them.
