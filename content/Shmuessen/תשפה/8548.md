# Shmuz 1
## Lekach טוב Vayigash מט ז
יעקב cursed Shimon and לוי's anger. This resulted in them not asking for eitzah before making their decision.
## Shmuess
Wouldn't the fact that they killed a whole city be a much sharper point that occurred from their anger? Why is not asking for eitzah mentioned?

It seems that we see how integral asking for eitzah is. Not asking for eitzah is such a violation of normal behavior that this is what is mentioned as for what really showed their anger.

Obviously killing a city of innocent people would be a far greater illustration of anger. In this situation, the people were guilty (as discussed by many מפרשים there). As such, killing the people of the city did not show the same level of anger as not asking for eitzah.

# Shmuz 2
## &lrm;מלכים א פרק ב פסוק ב רלב"ג ד"ה וחזקת והיית לאיש
דוד was dying and he was giving Shlomo some final advice. He told him "חזקת והיית לאיש". The רלב"ג interprets this to mean that Shlomo should make sure to ask for eitzah. Only with asking for eitzah can Shlomo act properly. Without asking for eitzah, he will act like a child.
## Shmuess
We see how integral asking for eitzah is. This is one of the final pieces of advice that דוד is giving Shlomo.

In addition, while Shlomo may not have gotten the ברכה of wisdom yet, he was still a tremendous תלמיד חכם. Clearly we see that this would not have been enough.

Also, the לשון of the רלב"ג is very strong. He makes it seem that asking for eitzah makes a massive difference and that without asking for it, it is nearly impossible to acheive greatness.
