# The אסורים of חמץ
## On ערב פסח
### About זמנים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">זמנים discussed are שעות זמניות.&lrm;[^1] בשעת הדחק (like if one did not sell his חמץ before the זמן איסור הנאה and the person stands to suffer a significant financial loss), one can rely on opinions that count hours like we normally do (meaning, 60 minutes per hour).[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">לכתחילה,&lrm; זמנים should be counted from עלות השחר through צאת הכוכבים (like the מגן אברהם and others). However, one can rely on count the day from הנץ החמה though שקיעה (like the גר"א and others).[^3]</span>
### The אסורים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is עובר a לאו (not a כרת) for getting הנאה from חמץ on ערב פסח after after chatzos.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The רבנן assured eating חמץ from the beginning of the 5th hour on ערב פסח, whether the חמץ is חמץ מדאורייתא, or מדרבנן.&lrm;[^5]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is also the איסור of [[#Tashbisu]] (see there).</span>
## Bal Yeirah Uval Yimatzei
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">On a דאורייתא level, owning חמץ on פסח alone does not violate an איסור. The problem is finding this חמץ.&lrm;[^6]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">On a דאורייתא level, one does not violate an איסור if they did the בדיקה, even if they did not do the בטל. However, you do violate an איסור if you find a nice piece of חמץ and delay in going to burn it.[^7]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[#Mixtures of חמץ and non-חמץ on פסח]] for additional violations of Bal Yeirah Uval Yimatzei.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The איסור of Bal Yeirah Uval Yimatzei only begins once פסח starts.[^8]</span>
## Tashbisu
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One violates the עשה of tashbisu every moment that they do not destroy חמץ,&lrm;[^8] either beginning from the beginning of the 6th hour,[^9] or the end of the 6th hour,[^10] on ערב פסח.</span>
# When to Be Bodek
## The Ideal Time
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should check on the night of the 14th. חז"ל were mesaken this because people are home, and the light of a candle shines more clearly at night. This is important for checking in cracks. Once חז"ל were mesaken the night of the 14th, that becomes an additional svarah to check on the 14th night.[^11][^12]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should begin the בדיקה immediately after tzeis.[^13] One may wait some amount of time but nothing significant.[^14]</span>
## &lrm;בדיקה Before the 14th
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can do בדיקת חמץ at night before the night of the 14th if they are careful not to bring חמץ in after they check. There is a חומרה to recheck on the night of the 14th.[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not check during the day before the 14th night. If one does, they are יוצא בדיעבד and do not need to check again. However, they should recheck one room on the 14th night. Checking by windows, skylights or an אכסדרה (room with only 3 sides[^15] or a room with a lot of windows[^16]) is fine, לכתחילה.&lrm;[^17] (It seems that one does not make a ברכה on this but this is not clear. The משנה ברורה says that if one checks by day in places that are well lit (will be discussed soon) or at night, then one does not make a ברכה. But what if one checks in places that are not well lit during the daytime.)</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Two reasons why we are against doing a בדיקה by day:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Candles are less effective by day (and sweeping is never particularly effective as it does not reach into cracks.)[^18]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Once the first reason is true, חז"ל were mesaken it this way and once they are mesaken it this way, we want to stick to it.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one does check before the 14th, they should leave one room unchecked so that on the 14th, they will be able to do a בדיקה and make a ברכה&lrm;[^19] (Does hiding a few pieces of bread before, as we are נוהג, work to allow you to make a ברכה on the בדיקה?)</span>
## &lrm;בדיקה On the 14th Day
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should not wait for the 14th day to do the בדיקה.[^20]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one did wait, then they do not need to use a candle when checking by windows, skylights, or an אכסדרה. For everything else, one should use a candle, just like by night.[^21]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">They should do the בדיקה as soon as possible in the morning. They should not eat before doing the בדיקה.[^20] If one initially forgot and then remembers, they should do the בדיקה right when they remember.[^22]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One is מבטל the חמץ when they finish the בדיקה (unless it is beyond the 6th hour) {<font color="#ff0000">Need to add link to the הלכה of בטל after the 6th hour</font>}</span>
# Why We Need Both בדיקה and בטל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מדאורייתא, either a בטל or בדיקה is enough. מדרבנן we require both.[^23]</span>
## Reasons for not Relying on בטל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You may not really want to be מבטל the חמץ in your heart and so the בטל will fail.[^23]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You may see some delicious looking חמץ and come to eat it.[^23]</span>
## Reasons for not Relying on בדיקה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are concerned that one may find חמץ on פסח, despite doing a בדיקה and then delay in going to get rid of it, which is an איסור.&lrm;[^23] {<font color="#ff0000">I don't understand the mechanics of the איסור. Why does delaying make one violate the איסור?</font>}</span>
# What Should Be Used
## Flashlight Vs Candle
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to Rav Elyashiv,[^24] Rav Aharon Kotler, and Rav Moshe Feinstein[^25] one can do the בדיקה with a candle, לכתחילה, if the beam is narrow enough to fit into cracks.</span>
   Rav Moshe holds that it is in fact better to use a flashlight than it is to use a candle.[^25]
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach[^26] holds that it is preferable to use a candle because that is the way that חז"ל were mesaken בדיקה.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to Rav Nissin Korelitz[^27] and the אור לציון&lrm;,[^28] one should not use a flashlight.
   According to Rav Korelitz, one can use the flashlight to assist, but in conjunction with the candle.[^27]
   According to the אור לציון, if one only has a flashlight, they should use it, but they should not make a ברכה.&lrm;[^28]</span>
## Leaving the Ceiling Light on
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it helps to leave the ceiling light on, according to the Shevet לוי,&lrm;[^29] Rav Moshe Feinstein,[^30] Rav Elyashiv,[^24] Rav Nissin Korelitz,[^27] the חזון איש,&lrm;[^24] Rav Yaakov Kanievsky,[^31] and Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach,[^32] one should do so.</span>
# Before the בדיקה
## What One May and May not Do Before בדיקה
### &lrm;מְלָאכָה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person may not do any "מְלָאכָה" {<font color="#ff0000">I don't know what is included in this</font>} beginning from a half hour before tzeis on the 14th.[^33]</span>
### Eating
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One may not eat more than a kibeitza of mezonos/hamotzi food once it is within a half hour before tzeis. There is no limit on non-mezonos and non-hamotzi food.[^34]</span>
### Learning
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is a mechlokes if one can begin learning at home within a half hour before tzeis.[^35]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one learns in shul, then everyone agrees that this is okay, because the person will have to head home at some point.[^35] (I think that the משנה ברורה means that in those days, people didn't stay out so late. As such, the person will head home before it is too dark so they won't miss the זמן. Assuming that this is the correct understanding, then what is the הלכה בזמן הזה?) If one is learning deeply then even learning in shul doesn't help.[^35]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone appoints a shomer than that it is okay.[^35] However, the shomer himself can't be learning.[^36]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if someone has a set time to learn, he is not allowed to learn within a half hour before tzeis (unless he employs one of the previous mentioned tachbulos).[^37]</span>
### Davening
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should daven before doing the בדיקה as it is difficult to gather a minyan together when it is so late and we are not concerned that Maariv will extend beyond the time to do the בדיקה.[^38] (From the first reason, what is the הלכה if someone lives in a place with set late minyanim?)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is davening ביחידות, he should appoint someone else to begin the בדיקה while he is daving so the בדיקה begins on time.[^38] (I think that we don't want the person to daven after because the בדיקה may last until chatzos, which is that latest one should say krias shema. בזמן הזה that we do most of the בדיקה before the night of the בדיקה and so the בדיקה does not take until anywhere near chatzos, would it be okay to wait until after to daven maariv? Also, I don't know why the person can't daven before after shkiah but before tzeis.)</span>
   If he can't appoint someone else (because, there is nobody else to appoint, let's say), he should daven first and then do the בדיקה.[^38]   
### If One Started at a Time that it Was מותר
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one started doing something at a time that it was מותר, (meaning, before half an hour before tzeis) everyone agrees that the person may continue doing whatever it was until tzeis.[^39]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Once tzeis comes, it is a מחלוקת מחבר (one may continue) against the רמ"א (one must stop).[^39]</span>
### If בדיקה Was not Done at Night
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one did not do the בדיקה at night, these הלכות apply right away in the morning.[^40]</span>
## Preparations for בדיקה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should sweep their house before doing the בדיקה.[^41]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The מנהג is to do this on the 13th day, so that way one is ready to go once the night comes.[^41]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is also a מנהג to sweep out cracks using a feather.[^41]</span>
# What Requires בדיקה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you are unsure if you brought חמץ into a room during the year, you need to do a בדיקה on that room. If you מסתמא did not, then you do not.[^42] (Hard to know what the line is. How unsure do you need to be to say that you need to do a בדיקה. For example, do you need to clean out a closet? It is unlikely that you brought חמץ there. Perhaps חמץ was kicked in and in ס"ק 29 the משנה ברורה talks about חמץ rolling into a hole, however this svarah is not  mentioned here.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you have children (perhaps even if you don’t have kids but you had kids over) then you have to check wherever they might have brought חמץ, including the bathroom.[^43]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Ledges unless they are higher than you can reach or within 3 tefachim of the ground (unless you have kids).[^44]</span>
## Roofs, Porches, and Balconies
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You do not need to check on something with a sloped roof.[^45]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You are required to check on something with a flat roof.[^46] As such, it would seem that one needs to check on a porch.[^47]</span>
## Inaccessible Places
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the חמץ is in an inaccessible, but may become accessible (for example, it is on a ceiling beam, and it may fall off), one needs to get rid of the חמץ.&lrm;[^48]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If it will remain in the inaccessible location, one only needs to do בטל and does not need to get rid of the חמץ.&lrm;[^48]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one was not מבטל the חמץ until the זמן איסור, then at that point, בטל does not work and one would have to actually get rid of the חמץ.&lrm;[^49]</span>
## Courtyards
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכות by [[#Animal Cages|animal cages]] apply to courtyards.[^50]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In all cases, you have to check the cracks in the walls on the side of a courtyard. [^51][^52]</span>
## Pets
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you own a pet, you might not have to do a full בדיקה. You might only need to sweep out large crumbs from underneath furniture and do a בדיקה in the kitchen and dining room.[^53]</span>
### Animal Cages
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you made sure not to bring חמץ into an animal coop within 30 days of פסח, then they do not need to be checked.[^54]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there was חמץ there within 30 days but before the זמן בדיקה then you should ask a rav.[^54]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there was חמץ after the זמן בדיקה, then you actually need to check. You cannot rely on the animal eating the חמץ.&lrm;[^55]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You might need to check in cracks in the side of the coop, as is the הלכה by [[#Courtyards|courtyards]].</span>
## &lrm;בדיקה On Sefarim
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The חזון איש says that one must do a בדיקהh in sefarim.[^56]</span> ^d974e7
   He would use a flashlight, or do it by a window during the day.[^57]
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach disagrees because he says that you only need to do a בדיקה on crumbs bigger than a kezayis. [^58] (Others disagree about crumbs smaller than a kezayis. See [[#Have to do a בדיקה|here]].)</span>  ^9dbfff
## Crumbs
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Crumbs are automatically considered to be מבטל.&lrm;[^7] (Practically, one still has to destroy the chamtez, though, to avoid accidentally eating it.)</span>
### Smaller than a Kezayis
#### Do Not Have to Do a בדיקה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Shlomo Zalman Auerbach [[#^9dbfff|here]] says that one does not need to do a בדיקה. The משנה ברורה [[#^33c449|here]] implies that one does not need to do a בדיקה on less than a kezayis.</span>
#### Have to Do a בדיקה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The חזון איש [[#^d974e7|here]] would seem to argue as well as the Machane ישראל&lrm;[^59] and the Chayei אדם.&lrm;[^60]</span>
## Holes in the Wall
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One has to check holes in the wall for חמץ as far as one's arm can reach.[^61]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is חמץ beyond one's reach, one does not have to get it. They can rely on the bitul. This is even if there is ודאי חמץ.&lrm;[^62] Also, see [[#Inaccessible Places|here]].</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">(I don't know if this applies today. It seems that it used to be that people stored חמץ in holes in their walls. I don't know if that applies now.)[^47]</span>
## Shuls and Batei מדרשים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Shuls and betei medrashi require a בדיקה.[^63]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The shamash should do this on the night of the 14th and make a ברכה. However, he can not be מבטל the חמץ as it is not his.[^63]</span>
## Purses and Pockets
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one puts חמץ into their pockets or purse from time to time, they need to do a בדיקה, even if one is sure that there is not חמץ in them.[^64] (I assume that the pockets of clean clothing do not need to be checked. Only the pockets of clothing that one has worn and not yet cleaned.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should check them during the בדיקה, and shake them out during the ביעור because even after the בדיקה, one may have absentmindedly slipped חמץ back into their pocket or purse and not remember it[^65] (I assume that this refers to the pockets on the clothes one wore after the בדיקה, or a purse only if one used it after the בדיקה, not every pocket on ones clothes.)</span>
## Walls and Chairs
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a חומרה to scrape down the walls and chairs that חמץ touched.[^66] {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't know if scraping down the walls applies בזמן הזה because we have finished walls}</span></span>
# After the בדיקה
## What to Do with the חמץ
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">After the בדיקה, one should put the חמץ in a secure location where kids and rodents can't get to it (this refers to חמץ one found during the בדיקה as well as חמץ one plans to eat the next day). Practically, this means putting your חמץ in some kind of box or container, not just leaving it on a table or counter.[^67]</span>
## &lrm;בטל
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should be מבטל the חמץ, immediately.[^68]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">At night, one is only מבטל the חמץ that they did not find. One is not מבטל the חמץ they would eat the next day or that one plans on burning as you are supposed to burn your own חמץ when doing the ביעור.[^69]</span>
## Someone Bringing חמץ into a Room that Was Already Bodek
### A Child
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a child brings חמץ into a room that was already checked and one finds crumbs on the floor, he can assume that this is all that there is in the room and the child ate the rest. He does not need to recheck the room.[^70]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you don't find crumbs, but the child reached the age of חינוך and says that he ate the whole thing, he can be trusted and so you do not need to recheck the room.[^71] (this may also be similar to what was said [[#Relying on Women and Ketanim for בדיקה|here]].)</span>
### A גוי
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a גוי brings חמץ into some a room that was already checked, we can assume that the גוי did not hide the חמץ there and so the room does not need another בדיקה.[^72]</span>
# Leaving One's Home Before פסח
## Returning After פסח
### Within 30 Days of פסח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If nobody will be in one's home to do a בדיקה, one should do a בדיקה on the night before they leave. One should follow the standard הלכות for doing a בדיקה.[^73]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason for this is because חז"ל were mesaken that 30 days before as the time that the chiyuv begins. This is because this is when one is supposed to begin earning the הלכות of the יום טוב from 30 days before.[^74][^75]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One exception to following the הלכות is that one does not make a ברכה. The ברכה is על ביעור חמץ. We view the בדיקה as part of the ביעור process when one does the ביעור shortly after, however, when one does not (like in this situation), one does not make the ברכה.&lrm;[^76]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one doesn't do the בדיקה at night, they do it in the morning.[^77]</span>
### Before 30 Days of פסח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does not need to do a בדיקה.[^78]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When פסח comes around, one would be מבטל the חמץ to avoid violating בל יטמין.&lrm;[^79][^80]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does not make a ברכה on the בטל.&lrm;[^81] (It would seem that one never makes a ברכה whenever they are only doing a בטל and not a בדיקה[^47])</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is a מחלוקת if one has to get rid of חמץ that one knows about if they leave before 30 days.[^82] We come out מקל&lrm;[^83] (as we do by [[#^53cb8c|someone piling things on top of חמץ more than 30 days before יום טוב]]).[^84]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one did a בדיקה before leaving, there is no need to be מבטל the חמץ.&lrm;[^85]</span>
## Returning on פסח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One must do a בדיקה before one leaves, no matter how much before פסח one leaves. This is even if one is מבטל the חמץ.&lrm;[^86] [^87]</span>
## Returning Before פסח
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This all depends on how far one travels and how much before פסח one plans on returning (the farther on travels and the closer to פסח one returns, the higher the possibility that one returns later than anticipated, leaving no time to do a בדיקה).[^88]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בשעת הדחק, one can be מקל and not do a בדיקה.[^89]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should ask their LOR for how this הלכה applies בזמן הזה (now that travel, even from distant places, is a lot more predictable than it was).[^47]</span>
# Pile on top of חמץ
## Man Made Pile
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one would like to pile things in a room that may have חמץ in it (to use the room for storage, for example), one must do this over 30 days before פסח.&lrm;[^90]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It seems that the pile needs to be over three tefachim high to count. If it is less than 3 tefachim then one must uncover the pile and do a בדיקה.[^91]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you would like to do it within 30 days, you must do a בדיקה first. If you didn't do a בדיקה and just filled the room up, one is required to empty the room and do a בדיקה.[^92]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the room will be emptied before פסח, the room must have a בדיקה before being filled, even if this is over 30 days before פסח.&lrm;[^93]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one knows that there is חמץ in the room, then it is a מחלוקת if one has to do a בדיקה before storing things there before 30 days before פסח.&lrm;[^94] We come out מקל.&lrm;[^83]</span>
## Naturally Made Pile
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the pile is three tefachim or less under which might be חמץ, one must uncover it to do a בדיקה, unless they are concerned for snakes and scorpions.[^95]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one knows that there is חמץ under the pile, the pile must be uncovered and a בדיקה must be done, even if one is concerned for snakes and scorpions (you have to make sure to do it in a safe way)[^96]</span>
# Being מבטל Someone Else's חמץ
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should not use a shliach to be מבטל their חמץ.&lrm;[^97]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One cannot be מבטל someone else's חמץ for them without their express permission. Even if one was appointed by the owner to do the בדיקה, this does not allow one to do the bitul.[^98]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A wife can be מבטל the חמץ of her husband. She should say כל חמירא דאיכא *ברשות בעלי דידע ביה*. One should not rely on this לכתחילה (as it is the husband's חמץ and it is not so simple that a person's wife can be מבטל it).[^99]</span>
# How to (and not to) Get Rid of חמץ
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You may throw your חמץ into rishus harabim and be מבטל it.[^100][^101][^102]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Hiding חמץ (like burying it in your rishus) does not help. If the חמץ gets buried on its own (a wall fell down onto the חמץ), it is a מחלוקת if this is a problem on a דאורייתא level.[^6]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One cannot put חמץ into an inaccebile location and then be מבטל it.[^103]</span>
# If בדיקה Was not Done Before יום טוב
## On יום טוב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one did not do a בדיקה before יום טוב, if the חמץ was מבטל, then one should wait until chol hamoed to do the בדיקה.[^104]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there was no בטל, one should do the בדיקה on יום טוב itself.[^104]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One would make a ברכה on this בדיקה, whether they were מבטל the חמץ before פסח or not.[^105]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One shouldn't touch the חמץ. Rather one should flip a כלי upside-down over the חמץ.&lrm;[^104] {<font color="#ff0000">this should link to some הלכה in סימן 446</font>}</span>
## After יום טוב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one didn't even do the בדיקה on פסח, they should do a בדיקה after פסח to get rid of the חמץ because one is not allowed to get הֲנָאָה from the חמץ שעבר עליו פסח.&lrm;[^106]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One would not make a ברכה on this בדיקה.[^105]</span>
# Finding חמץ on פסח
## Making a ברכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One would not make any ברכה if one finds and gets rid of חמץ on פסח if they did a בדיקה before פסח.&lrm;[^105]</span>
# Finding חמץ After Doing the בדיקה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one finds חמץ after doing the בדיקה, he does not need to be concerned that there is more חמץ that he missed.[^107]</span>
# Renting a Living Location
## Renting From a גוי
Background: A person has a chiyuv to do a בדיקה, not just to make sure that they are not עובר on Bal Yeirah or to keep themselves from accidentally eating חמץ.&lrm;[^108]
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone is renting from a גוי and leaving within 30 days of פסח, they don't need to do a בדיקה because the חמץ is mafkired and so we view it likeמשליח אותו ברחוב and they'll do a בדיקה on the house they move into.[^108]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If they move into a house with a Jewish owner who already did a בדיקה, they still don't need to do a בדיקה on the house they left because we view the Jewish owner like the shliach of the tenant so it is as if the tenant did a בדיקה.[^109]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Similarly, if another Jew is going to move into the house of this גוי after the original Jew left, the Jew who left does not need to do a בדיקה because the Jew moving in after him has to do a בדיקה and we view him like a shliach for the Jew leaving.[^110]</span>
   If this Jew moving in afterwards is not going to do the בדיקה (he is not shomer מצוות) then the Jew leaving will have to do a בדיקה.[^111]
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the Jew leaving is not going to be living anywhere else at all (like if they are traveling over פסח), and nobody is moving in after him, then he does need to do a בדיקה.[^108]</span>
   However, if the goyish owner of the house is going to move back into the house after the Jew leaves but before פסח, then they Jew does not need to do a בדיקה.[^112] {<font color="#ff0000">I don't understand why this is.</font>}

##### Why the Jew May Destroy the חמץ
Two reasons:
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">While it is אסור to steal from a גוי, it is muttar to damage a גוי.&lrm;[^134]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Something that a Jew is watching for a גוי is considered ownerless regarding that Jew if it will cause him to be עובר on an עבירה.&lrm;[^135]</span>
#### Jew Did not Accept Responsibility
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the Jew did not accept responsibility then there is no problem.[^136] However, if the גוי asks the Jew on פסח, it is preferable for the Jew not to watch it, even though he is not accepting responsibility.[^137]</span>
##### &lrm;גוי Brings His Own חמץ
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A גוי can bring his own חמץ into the house of a Jew.[^138] There is no concern that the Jew will eat the חמץ because the גוי is watching it. Also, Baal Yeirah is only for one's own חמץ.&lrm;[^139]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The Jew may not eat with the גוי eating חמץ, even if both have placemats (which works for בשר vichalav).[^140]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The table the גוי eats on must be cleaned well afterwards and the גוי takes his food with him.[^141]</span>

2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The mechitza must be solid material (not cloth).[^142]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One cannot place an upside-down כלי over the חמץ as we are concerned that someone may accidentally use the כלי, exposing the חמץ.&lrm;[^143]</span>

## Complete Change
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If חמץ has completely changed in a way that it is barely recognizable (such as one mixes flour and water to make glue, or flour and milk to wash clothing), one does not need to get rid of it, even if one can tell that it is חמץ.&lrm;[^160] (I believe that this is only if this has changed to a non-food purpose. Also, see [[#^7f05fb|here]]. I think the difference may be that our case is dealing with a more complete change of the חמץ than over there, but I am not sure.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת between the מחבר and רמ"א about how much it has to change to be considered "changed". However, if this mixture was over 30 days old by the time פסח came around, it is considered "changed" according to both opinions.[^161]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should make sure not to put such things on the table (or any place that will have food) because it is still אסור to eat.[^162]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Similarly one doesn't need to get rid of חמץ that is mixed in with non-foods (even if it is edible, and sometimes even eaten by a sick person), although there is a מחלוקת about if one can eat such a mixture.[^163]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a mixture is for a non-food purpose, then if there is less than a kezayis in one place, there is no issue. This is even if there is a kezayis within the whole כלי (for example, if someone uses a חמץ glue to hold together a כלי. There is no issue unless there is a kezayis of חמץ is all in one spot. There is no issue if there is more than a kezayis of חמץ throughout the כלי).&lrm;[^164]</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the חמץ was originally food and it just happened to get trapped in the כלי, there is a מחלוקת if all of the chamtz throughout the כלי gets combined together to count as a kezayis. However, if the חמץ is a little metunaf, it does not combine according to all opinions.[^165] {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't think this belongs here.</span>}</span>
## Spoiled Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does not need to get rid of food that spoiled before the זמן איסור. However, if the food spoiled after the זמן, one still needs to destroy it.[^166]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">"Spoiled" means beyond ראוי לאכילת כלב, not just ראוי לאכילת אדם&lrm;[^167]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Once the זמן איסור comes, even if the food reaches אינו ראוי לאכילת כלב after then, the food must be completely destroyed. אינו ראוי לאכילת כלב is not good enough[^168]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If חמץ that was אינו ראוי לאכילת כלב was not destroyed and accidentally fell into other food, see [[#^f6f6d4|here]].</span>
# &lrm;חמץ Utensils
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Certain kneading bowls which are comprised of different parts and therefore have large cracks or crevices need to be sold to a גוי (as we are concerned that there is a kezayis of חמץ there). Even if regular mixing bowls which do not have these cracks or crevices may not be used for פסח (as we are concerned that there is still a משהו of חמץ, which is אסור to eat).[^169]</span> ^33c449
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is unclear if this previous הלכה applies to other כלים that may similarly be difficult to clean. Logically it would seem that it should apply. However, in ס"ק 51 the משנה ברורה says that a bread basket which is also hard to clean just needs to be put away for פסח and doesn't need to be sold.[^47]</span>
# &lrm;הנאה From חמץ
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">הנאה from חמץ includes selling the חמץ, giving it to a גוי, and feeding one's animals. As such, whenever one may not get הנאה from חמץ, they may not do any of these things.[^170]</span>
