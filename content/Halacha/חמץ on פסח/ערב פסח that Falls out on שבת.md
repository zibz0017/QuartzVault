# When to Do בדיקה and ביעור
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does the בדיקה on the 13th at night (Thursday night) and then does the ביעור sometime on Friday.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does the בטל and makes the ברכה.&lrm;[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One shouldn't do the בדיקה by day for the same reason one may not ever do בדיקה by day.[^2] <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to add link to the source of this.</span></span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can't do the בדיקה on שבת because one can't hold the candle (as it is מוקצה).[^3]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One needs to save bread for two meals for the סעודות שבת (as will be discussed shortly).[^4]</span>
# Eating the סעודות שבת
## First Two סעודות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For the first two סעודות, one must eat bread as one can't eat מצה on ערב פסח. As such, for the day meal, one must make sure to finish before the זמן איסור אכילת חמץ <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">add link for where that is discussed</span>. Practically, this requires one to daven שחרית early and not drag out the meal.[^5]</span>
## שלוש סעודות
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For שלוש סעודות, one may not eat מצה, as discussed, and one may not eat חמץ as, according to most, שלוש סעודות can only be eaten after מנחה (which is after the זמן איסור אכילת חמץ).[^6] What one may eat is a מחלוקת:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מחבר: One eats "מצה עשירה"&lrm; (מצה made with fruit juice).[^7]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רמ"א: One may not eat מצה עשירה on ערב פסח, so one must eat fruit or vegetables, fish or meat, or foods made from מצה meal.[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is eating מצה עשירה or food made from מצה meal for שלוש סעודות, one must make sure to finish eating before the 10th hour. (Even if one started before the 10th hour, they may not continue eating past it.)
   If one eats fruits, vegetables, meat, or fish one may eat after the 10th hour. One must make sure that they still have an appetite to eat the מצה at night, though.[^9]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Since, according to some, one may eat שלוש סעודות in the morning, if possible, one should try to eat two bread meals on שבת morning (as it is preferable to wash for שלוש סעודות). One just has to be mindful not to be עובר on making a ברכה שאינה צריכה.&lrm;[^10]</span>
