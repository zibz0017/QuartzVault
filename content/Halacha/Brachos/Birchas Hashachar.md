1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In the days of the gemarah, everyone would say birchas hashachar as they were getting up and getting themselves ready.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There was no problem of the fact that they touched covered parts of their body while they were sleeping and so they would not be allowed to say the ברכות before washing their hands because they were מקפיד to keep their hands clean, even when they slept[^2][^3][^4]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We don’t do this anymore for two reasons:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We do not keep our hands clean when we sleep.[^3]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We want to make the ברכות in shul for the sake of the am haaratzim who do not know how to make ברכות to be yotzei in shul.[^3]</span>
