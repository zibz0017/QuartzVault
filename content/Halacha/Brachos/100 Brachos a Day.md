# Basic הלכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person is חייב&lrm;[^1] to make 100 ברכות a day.[^2]</span>
# Natural Amount of ברכות One Makes in a Day
## Weekdays
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">On weekdays, one makes over 100 ברכות.&lrm;[^3]</span>
2. <span id="971701" style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The חשבון is as follows: Hamapil (1), על Netilas Yadayim (1), Asher Yatzar (1), Birchas התורה (3),&lrm;[^4] birchas hashachar with hamaavir Sheina and Elokai neshama (16), Tallis/Tzitzis (1), Tefillin (2), Baruch Sheamar (1), Yishtabach (1), Birchas Krias Shema by Shacharis and by Maariv (8), Shemona Esrei three times (3 \* 19 = 57), two meals in which one washes and makes an על netilas yadayim (2 \* 1 = 2), makes hamotzi (2 \* 1 = 2), bentches (4 \* 2 = 8), on a cup of wine (2 \* 1 = 2), and makes an על Hagefen (2 \* 1 = 2).[^3]</span> ^971701
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This adds up to 108.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בזמן הזה, many people do not eat two bread meals a day (and certainly without bentching on a cup of wine), and some only make one ברכה on tefillin, so if we remove the ברכות for the meals and one ברכה on tefilin, we are down to 91 ברכות.&lrm;[^5]</span> ^5f3326
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, even if we have three non-washing meals in a day, and we average three ברכות per meal (2 different ברכות rishonos and a borei nefashos), we would be back to 100. This would not include additional ברכות for eating snack or asher yotzar for going to the bathroom.[^5]</span>
## &lrm;שבת
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">On שבת, we have four shemona esreis of seven ברכות a piece (7 * 4 = 28) instead of the usual three shemona esreis with 19 ברכות each, puting us down 29 ברכות (57 - 28 = 29). We also miss out on Yiru eineinu (1) and ברכות on tefilin (2).[^6]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, we also have a third seudah on שבת (8, as calculated [[#^971701|before]]), birchas meien sheva (the ברכה we say on Friday night beginning with מגן Avos), kiddush by night (2) and by day (1).</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This brings us back up to 87 ברכות.&lrm;[^3][^6]</span> ^362d64
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, we may have less than this as many people do not bentch on a cup of wine by any of the meals, removing the ברכה on the wine (3 * 1 = 3) and the ברכה achrona (3 * 1 = 3). This would bring us down to 81.[^5]</span> ^ae15e7
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">They should make sure to eat different foods.[^3][^6][^7] ע"י הדחק one can be yotzei by being mechavein to the birchos התורה by the aliyos and haftorah.[^3][^6]</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One needs to be careful not to make a ברכה she'eino tzricha to get to the 100 ברכות.&lrm;[^3][^6]</span>
7. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is using the birchos התורה, he should make sure not to answer baruch hu uvaruch shemo during the ברכות, as one is not supposed to talk when being yotzei with a ברכה through shomeiah keonah. {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">need source</span>}</span>
## &lrm;יום טוב
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">יום טוב is not explicitly discussed but it would seem that if one does not have a third meal, then the חשבון would come out that the person would be missing 8 ברכות compared to the [[#^362d64|87 on שבת]], bringing the total down to 79.[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In addition, as [[#^ae15e7|discussed]], 87 on שבת assumes that one bentches on a cup of wine. If one does not do, that, they are missing an additional 6 ברכות. So on יום טוב, one would be missing an additional 4 (as there are only 2 meals), bringing the total down to 75.[^5]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can be mechaein to the ברכות on the 5 aliyos by shachris (10), as well as the haftorah (5), bringing us up to 90.[^5]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person can then also be mechavein to the ברכות on the aliyos by מנחה (6), and the haftorah (4), which would complete the 100.[^5]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Also, this does not include going to the bathroom or if a person ate snacks between meals.[^5]</span>
## Fast Days
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">On fast days that begin in the morning, a person just misses the meal during the day. As such they are only down 8 ברכות off the 108 of a regular day and so one still has 100 ברכות.&lrm;[^6]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As [[#^5f3326|mentioned]], we don't usually bentch on a cup of wine. As such, we may not have 100 ברכות (as we have 92 ברכות, not including the night meal, which adds 8 ברכות if one washes and bentches on a cup of wine).[^5]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It would seem that one should wash during the night meal, and so even if one doesn't bentch on a cup of wine, as long as one makes two additional asher yotzars, one will still make 100 ברכות.&lrm;[^5]</span>
## &lrm;יום Kippur
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">On Yom Kippur, one only has 97 ברכות&lrm;[^8] so one should supplement with birchas hareiach (in addition to being mechavein to birchas התורה). אפשר ע"י הדחק one can be yotzei by being mechavein to chazaras hashatz.[^3]</span>

## Tisha Bav
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Tisha Bav is not explicitly discussed in the משנה ברורה, but based on what we said, it should come out that one only makes 92 ברכות (based on the fact that there are 108 ברכות per day with two meals of 8 ברכות each which are now missed on Tisha Bav.) and so one should try to mechavein to the ברכות on the aliyos and haftorah.[^5]</span>
