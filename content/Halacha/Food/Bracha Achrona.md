# How Long One Can Wait Before Making a ברכה Achrona
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One loses the ability to bentch or make a me'ein shalosh {<font color="#ff0000">not sure about borei nifashos</font>} when the food one ate begins to digest. When you start feeling hungry again, that usually means that you digested the food.[^1] (If one doesn't eat their fill and they are hungry immediately, that doesn't mean that they digested the food.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The clock begins after the end of the meal, not the beginning. As such, if someone has a long meal, the clock begins at the end of the meal.[^2]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if you ate and then you were meysiach דעת and then ate more, we do not say that the eatings combine. The clock for the first eating began after the end of the first eating.[^2]</span>
## Minimum Amount of Time One Has
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you aren't sure if you digested the food yet (like, for example, someone ate less then their fill), one can be confident for 72 minutes that the food was not digested.[^3] (The implication is that even beyond 72 minutes, the food has not been digested. It's just that one can't rely on it, though, if one is unable to tell.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even so, it is best to eat a kizayis of bread before bentching, just to make sure that the bentching is not a ברכה livatala. If someone was משיח דעת, they make a new ברכה on this kizayis.[^3]</span>
# How Long One Can Wait Before Needing to Make a New ברכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if someone waits too long so they can't bentch, if they were not meysiach דעת, they do not make a new ברכה.&lrm;[^4]</span>
# Minimum Amount of Food Eaten to Require Bentching
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one eats less than a kizayis, they do not bentch.[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one eats a kizayis but is not full, they bentch מדרבנן. (There is no objective measure to "fullness". It changes for every person. If one consistently eats to less than they are full, that is considered "full" for them.)[^5]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is full, they bentch מדאורייתא.&lrm;[^5]</span>
# Bentching/Making A ברכה Achrona in One's Original Location
## What Kind of Food
### Bentching and על Hamichya
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One needs to make betch/make an על hamichya in one's original place.[^6]</span>
#### Leaving One's Location Before Making a ברכה Achrona
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For more details about these הלכות, see [[שינוי מקום#Hamotzi and Mezonos|here]].</span>
##### If One Did not Eat in the New Location
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one forgot to bentch/make an על hamichya before leaving one's location, one may bentch/make an על hamichya in the new location, בשעת הדחק.&lrm;[^7]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one purposely left his location, knowing that he did not bentch/make an על hamichya, he must go back to the original location, even בשעת הדחק.&lrm;[^8]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, in all cases, if one has already bentched/made an על hamichya in the new location, he is yotzei.[^9]</span>
##### If One Ate in the New Location
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one had more bread/mezonos in the new location (even less than a kezayis[^10]), you can bentch/make a ברכה achrona there, לכתחילה.&lrm;[^11]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is even if one purposely left his original location, knowing that he did not bentch/make a ברכה achrona yet.[^12]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is even if the person didn't originally have in mind to change locations at the time that he made the ברכה.&lrm;[^13]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one needs to bentch, making a mezonos in the new location does not help.[^10]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one already waited so long that he lost can't make a bentch/make a ברכה achrona anymore (as [[#How Long One Can Wait Before Making a ברכה Achrona|discussed]]), then obviously this trick doesn't work and you would have to make a new ברכה rishona.[^14]</span>
##### How Far Away From One's Original Location Can One Make a ברכה Achrona
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One can bentch/make a ברכה achrona anywhere in the room that one ate.[^15]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is a מניעה in the room that one ate {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't know what is included in this</span>} one can bentch/make a ברכה achrona in a different room in the same house as long as one can see the original place he ate.[^15] {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I don't know if it has to be the exact place, or within four אמות.</span>}</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone is outside in a place not surrounded by walls, every four אמות is considered a "new place" from which one is not supposed to leave. One can leave because of a small מניעה.&lrm;[^16] {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Again, I don't know what is included in this but it seems to be smaller than before. Also, I don't know if it literally has to be surrounded by walls. # Repeating Bentching
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is not full after eating, then bentching is only a דרבנן. As such, if one is unsure if they bentched, they do not bentch becuase of ספק ברכות להקל.&lrm;[^22]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is full then they do need to bentch if they are unsure if they bentched. One needs to repeat the whole bentching (even the ברכות which are only דרבנן).&lrm;[^23]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A yarei shamayim should wash without a ברכה {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to link to the הלכות of washing</span>}, eat a kizayis, and then bentch.[^24]</span>
