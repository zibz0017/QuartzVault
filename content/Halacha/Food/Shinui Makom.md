# Halacha If One is משנה מקום
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is משנה מקום, they have to make a new bracha rishona before continuing the meal, but they do not need to make a bracha achrona before continuing. They just make a bracha achrona at the end of the second part of the meal and that counts for both parts.[^1]</span>
# Different Changes of Location
## Inside a House to Outside
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Considered shinui makom.[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Doesn't matter if the person comes back or wants to eat in a new place.[^3]</span>
### Leaving Before Bracha Achrona
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For bread, the person would have to return to bentch.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is better not to leave before bentching.[^4]</span>
## Room to Room Within the Same House
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one had in mind to do this at the time of the bracha, this is not considered shinui makom.[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As long as one has in mind to change rooms (to a specific location or not) this is considered having this in mind. This is even if one had one room in mind, and went to a different room instead.[^6]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one sees the original place (or close to it) that he made his bracha, this is not considered shinuo makom.[^7]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a machlokes if this means that one can see the actual place (or close to it) that the person made the bracha, or just see any part of the room that the person bracha made the bracha.[^8] As such, it seems that one should try to make sure that they can still see the original place, rather than just the room (or make a bracha achrona before leaving).</span>
## Within the Same Room
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is not considered shinui makom.[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is even if the room is very large and/or there are mechitzos between the person's original and current location.[^9]</span>
## In All Cases
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In cases where the change of location is considered shinui makom, this is even if one leaves and comes back immediately.[^10]</span>
# Eating in a Group
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone is eating a meal as part of a group and the group leaves in middle of the meal, if at least one person stays behind then this is not considered shinui makom.[^11]</span>
2. {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Seemingly fine lechatchilah because the reasons discussed [[#Bidieved|here]] don't apply since you have people you left behind, you aren't going to forget about them and you are going to go back.</span>}
## Eating in a New Location
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone goes and eats in a new location, leaving people behind doesn't help. This would be considered shinui makom (depending on [[#Different Kinds of Foods|what the person ate]].)[^12]</span>
# Different Kinds of Foods
## Hamotzi and Mezonos
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone ate at least a kezayis food that requires a bracha in its original place (mezonos and hamotzi), it is not considered shinui makom.[^13]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the person ate less than a kezayis, they do not need to go back to their original place to make a bracha achrona. As such, if they change places, this is considered shinui makom.[^13]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a person left and was meisiach daas, even if they ate a kezayis of bread/mezonos, this is still considered shinui makom.[^2]</span>
### Changing Location
#### Lechatchilah
##### Has in Mind Initially
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one plans on changing locations during a hamotzi or mezonos meal, they should have this in mind when making hamotzi/mezonos. The person is then permitted to change locations and continue his meal there, lechatchilah.[^14]</span>
##### Appoints a Shomer/Sets an Alarm
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According the Rav Chaim Pinchas Scheinberg, if someone appoints a shomer or sets an alarm to remind him to go back to his original place and make a bracha achrona there, this is fine, lechatchilah.[^15]</span>
##### Comes Back Immediately
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person may leave and come back immediately ("immediately" means "ממש מיד"), lechatchilah.[^16]</span>
#### Bidieved
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one did not have in mind to change locations when they made their bracha rishona, it is preferable for them not to change locations.[^17] This is because:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are concerned that the person may forget to make a bracha achrona.[^17]</span> ^47852b
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are concerned that the person may accidentally make a bracha achrona in the new location.[^17]</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the person knows he is going to eat food in the new location, this is still not preferable to bentch there (meaning, the person should make a braccha achrona there if he did eat there, but it would have been preferable for the person not to have eaten there and instead made a bracha achrona in his original location).[^17]</span> ^eaebb7
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even though it is a bidieved situation, one does not need to make a new bracha rishona when one comes back.[^18]</span>
##### Bracha Achrona Before Leaving
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is confident that they will come back within the time to continue the meal (see [[Bracha Achrona#How Long One Can Wait Before Making a Bracha Achrona|here]] for those halachos) and they want to continue eating, they should not make a bracha achrona before leaving because this can lead to a shayla of bracha she'eino tzricha.[^19]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is unsure if they will make it back in time, they should make a bracha achrona before leaving.[^19]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is a mitva overes (like it is about to be zman mincha) and the person doesn't have time to bentch before leaving, then it is preferable for the person to leave, take care of the mitzva, and then come back.[^20] (I assume this is where the person is confident that they can make it back before the time to make a bracha achrona expires. I would assume that if the person was not confident, then they should not miss bentching for the mitzva, but I am not sure.[^21])</span>

#### Where to Make a Bracha Achrona
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">See [[Bracha Achrona#If One Ate in the New Location|here]].</span>
## Shehakol, Haadamah, and Haeitz
### Continuing One's Meal Elsewhere
{<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Need to come back to this later. Mostly discussed in Sif 1</span>}
### Bracha Achrona Before Leaving
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is preferred that one makes a bracha achrona before changing locations, as we are concerned that one may accidentally forget to make one, [[#^47852b|as discussed]] in regards to bread/mezonos.[^22]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if one comes back immediately, this is fine, lechatchilah.[^23]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It would seem that if one has a reminder to make a bracha achrona in the new location, it would be fine to leave before making a bracha achrona, as well. This would be obvious [[#Appoints a Shomer/Sets an Alarm|according to Rav Scheinberg]], but even the Mishna Berura would seem to agree with this as he said that the concern was that a person would forget to make a bracha achrona, or make it in the new location (see [[#Bidieved|here]]). However, if one has a reminder, this is not an issue (and even the Mishna Berura over [[#^eaebb7|here]] seems to agree with this), and when it comes to these kinds of foods, there is no issue of making the bracha achrona in the new location.[^21]</span>
