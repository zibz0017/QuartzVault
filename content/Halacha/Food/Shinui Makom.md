*Note: These halachos are for Ashkenazim only.*
# Considerations
1. Where the person came from and went.
2. If the person was eating in a group and a member from the group stayed behind or not.
3. What was the person eating.
# Different Changes of Location
## Inside a House to Outside
1. Considered shinui makom.[^1]
2. Doesn't matter if the person comes back or wants to eat in a new place.[^2]
### Leaving Before Bracha Achrona
1. For bread, the person would have to return to bentch.[^3]
2. It is better not to leave before bentching.[^3]
## Room to Room Within the Same House
1. If one had in mind to do this at the time of the bracha, this is not considered shinui makom.[^4]
2. As long as one has in mind to change rooms (to a specific location or not) this is considered having this in mind. This is even if one had one room in mind, and went to a different room instead.[^5]
3. If one sees the original place (or close to it) that he made his bracha, this is not considered shinuo makom.[^6]
4. There is a machlokes if this means that one can see the actual place (or close to it) that the person made the bracha, or just see any part of the room that the person bracha made the bracha.[^7] As such, it seems that one should try to make sure that they can still see the original place, rather than just the room (or make a bracha achrona before leaving).
## Within the Same Room
1. This is not considered shinui makom.[^1]
2. This is even if the room is very large and/or there are mechitzos between the person's original and current location.[^8]
## In All Cases
1. In cases where the change of location is considered shinui makom, this is even if one leaves and comes back immediately.[^9]
# Eating in a Group
1. If someone is eating a meal as part of a group and the group leaves in middle of the meal, if at least one person stays behind then this is not considered shinui makom.[^10]
# Related Halachos
1. These halachos also apply to hilchos kiddush bimakom seudah.[^11]
