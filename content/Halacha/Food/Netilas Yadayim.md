# Reasons for Washing Before Eating Bread
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Kohanim need to wash their hands before eating תרומה because hands are busy and they might be tamei. We make everyone wash before eating bread to make sure kohanim do this (as most תרומה was grain products).[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">&lrm;אסמכתה from the פסוק והתקדשתם והייתם קדושים.&lrm;[^2]</span>
# Requirements
## Food One Washes on
### Bread
#### How Much One Must Eat
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One washes and makes a ברכה if they plan on eating more than a kibeitza of bread.[^3]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is having less than a kibeitza, they wash without a ברכה.&lrm;[^3] If one is having less than a kizayis then they don't have to wash, but לכתחילה, one should, anyway.[^4]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One combines the sizes of small pieces together in terms of what one must do.[^5]</span>
##### Exceptions
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the bread is being used as a chaser (you eat of drink something sharp and need the bread to get rid of the taste) and it is less than a kizayis, one doesn't need to wash, even לכתחילה.&lrm;[^5]</span>
### Being קובע סעודה on Mezonos
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We only require washing on things that one is קובע סעודה on.[^6] As such, if one plans on being קובע סעודה on mezonos, they must wash, make a hamotzi, and bentch.[^7]</span>
#### Changing One's Mind After Starting
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one didn't plan on being קובע סעודה but changed their mind once they began eating, if they will continue eating enough to be קובע סעודה just on what they will be eating now, they wash with a ברכה, make a hamotzi, and bentch.[^8]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If they did not eat enough to be קובע סעודה on just what they will continue to eat, but ate enough to be קובע סעודה on what they ate previously combined with what they eat now, one bentches, but does not wash or make hamotzi.</span>
### Wet Food
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One washes without a ברכה for wet foods.[^9] (We don't make a ברכה because there is a מחלוקת if this הלכה applies בזמן הזה, so even though we פסקען we do, we don't make a ברכה.&lrm;[^10] (Practically, this may usually refer to fruits and vegetables.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is only if it is normal to get the food wet (like washing or dipping it).[^11]</span>
#### What Counts as Liquids
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One of the "7 liquids".[^12] (Practically, water, milk (including whey[^13]), jarred[^14] bee honey,[^15] olive oil,[^16] and wine.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Juice from meat that emerges when it is cooked, if the meat was washed before being cooked.[^17]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A mixture of liquids if רוב of the mixture is one of the "7 liquids".[^18]</span>
#### What Does not Count as Liquids
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Fruit juice.[^14]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Non-bee honey.[^15]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Honey straight from the honeycomb. This is because honey straight from the honeycomb is not viewed as a liquid, while honey that has been jarred is viewed that way.[^15]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Honey or oil that congealed to the point that it is no longer runny enough to be טופח על מנת להטפיח (If you touch them, you will not be wet enough to make something else wet), even when they liquefy.[^14]</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Non olive oil.[^16]</span>
6. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Melted butter.[^16]</span>
7. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Juice from meat that was cooked without being washed first.[^17]</span>
8. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A mixture of liquids if less than half of the mixture is one of the "7 liquids".[^18]</span>
#### Additional הלכות About Wet Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if part of the food is dry and one is only touching the dry part, one must wash.[^19]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The הלכות about how much one must plan on eating to require washing is the same as by bread (see footnote for discussion).[^20]</span>
### Liquids
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does not wash on liquids, even if one dips their finger into it and sucks it off their finger,[^11] or drinks from his cupped hands.[^21]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is because one usually does not touch liquids when drinking, so חז"ל were not גוזר on it at all, even in cases that one does touch the liquid.[^21]</span>
### Additional Non-Mezonos/Hamotzi Foods
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does not wash on non-mezonos/hamotzi things, even if they are קובע סעודה.&lrm;[^6] This includes non-grain breads (like bread from almond flour).[^22]</span>
## The כלי
### Basic Requirements
#### Minimum Size
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A כלי must be able to hold a רביעית.&lrm;[^23] There is a מחלוקת about how big this is in modern day measurements:[^24]</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">חזון איש&lrm;: 150 cm<sup>3</sup></span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">רב חיים נאה&lrm;: 86.4 cm<sup>3</sup></span>
### If there is a Hole
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the כלי has a hole in the side or bottom large enough to let a tiny amount of water out, the כלי should not be used except בשעת הדחק.&lrm;[^25]
   If it is dripping water from the hole, it is totally not allowed.[^26]
   (Practically speaking, I don't know where the exact cutoff is.)</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the hole is large enough for water to get in, it is totally not allowed.[^27]</span>
### Using Disposable כלים
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת of modern פוסקים about whether one can wash with a disposable כלי:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מנחת יצחק,&lrm;[^28] אור לציון:&lrm;[^29] One cannot use a disposable cup,</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Rav Moshe,&lrm;[^30] Rav Wosner:[^31] One can use a disposable cup. However, one just has to make sure that the כלי isn't leaky (as is common with some disposable כלים).
	   (I asked Rabbi Semmel about this and he said that מסתמא, we פסקען like Rav Moshe. Rabbi Semmel agreed to the דיוק of דרשו. However, he, himself, had a recollection that one was not to use disposable, but he didn't know why he thought so, so ultimately, he was tentatively מקל but he wanted to do more research.)</span>
## Additional Requirements
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a person is eating a food that generally requires a utensil one does not need to wash, even if they happen to touch the food. If the food is generally eaten with the hands, using a utensil does not help, even if the food is not touched.[^18]</span>
# How to Wash
## Order of Washing, Drying, and the ברכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One washes his hands, dries them, and then makes the ברכה. There is no problem with the fact that the person did the מצוה before doing the ברכה (we usually do the ברכה before the מצוה) because:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One's hands may be dirty, in which case he can't make the ברכה. As such, the רבנן set up the מצוה in this fashion, namely, that the ברכה is done after the מצוה.&lrm;[^32] Because of this reason, one is allowed to make the ברכה even after one dried his hands if he forgot to make it earlier. See [[#One Forgot to Make the Bracha Until After Drying|here]].</span> ^64e689
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Drying is part of the מצוה as well.[^32]</span>
## Which Hand First
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Everyone (even lefties), washes their right hand first.[^33]</span>
## The ברכה
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We say "וצונו" even though this is a דרבנן as Hashem told us to listen to the רבנן.&lrm;[^34]</span>
## Drying One's Hands
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is important for one to dry their hands well. This is because:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">מים ראשונים are tamei. The מים שניים doesn't fully get rid of it. It is removed from one's hands only in conjunction with the drying.[^35]</span> ^98aa47
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One's hands are still considered disgusting when they are wet.[^36]</span> ^f44713
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Despite this, one is allowed to make a ברכה, even if one's hands are still wet with netilas yadayim water.[^37]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should not dry his hands off on his shirt as this is קשה לשכחה. It is a ספק if this applies to other garments as well.[^36]</span>
### Specific Cases
#### Less Than a רביעית at Once
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One must dry their hands for the reasons mentioned ([[#^98aa47|here]] and [[#^f44713|here]]).</span>
#### רביעית or More at Once
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">When one pours a רביעית at once, the water does not become tamei.[^38]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, according to the שיטה that the purpose of drying one's hands is to get rid of the tamei מים ראשונים, one would not have to dry one's hands in this case, as the מים ראשונים are not tamei.[^38]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According to the שיטה that one's hands are disgusting, one would still have to dry their hands.[^38]</span>
#### Dunking One's Hands
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The water by tevillah does not become tamei.[^38]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such, once again, there is no tamei water so according to the opinion that the purpose of drying one's hands is to get rid of the tamei מים ראשונים, one would not have to dry one's hands.[^38]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In this case, the תורה explicitly does not require one to dry themselves off after toveling in a mikvah. As such, even the opinion that says that drying is because wet hands are disgusting would agree in this case that one doesn't have to dry one's hands, as it is explicitly fine from the תורה.&lrm;[^38]</span>
### &lrm;הלכה Lemaisa
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If having wet hands bothers someone then they need to dry their hands.[^38]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In all cases, one should be still be מחמיר to dry one's hands.[^39]</span>
# One Forgot to Make the ברכה Until After Drying
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one forgot to make the ברכה until after drying one's hands, one is still allowed to make the ברכה because the רבנן set up the mitva for the ברכה to be made after, as [[#^64e689|previously discussed]]. However, once one makes the hamotzi, they cannot make a ברכה anymore.[^40]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Obviously, this should not be relied upon if one can avoid it. One should try to make sure to make the ברכה before drying one's hands.[^40]</span>
# One's Hands Were Already Washed
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one had already washed their hands but not for a chiyuv, and then one decides that they want to eat bread, they have to rewash their hands.[^41]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one was not מסיח דעת, they do not make a new ברכה when rewashing, but if one was מסיח דעת, then he does.[^42]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is because according to some, even if one didn't wash his hands for bread, the washing still counts. As such, according to them, one wouldn't have to make a new ברכה. If one was מסיח דעת, then one always does whenever one is מסיח דעת.&lrm;[^43]</span>
# One's Hands Got Dirty in Middle of a Meal
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one's hands got dirty in middle of a meal (such as by touching a covered part of their body), whether it is a bread meal, or one is eating dipped foods, one washes again with a ברכה.&lrm;[^44]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For one who goes to a the bathroom in middle of a meal, see [[#If One Goes to the Bathroom in Middle of a Meal|here]].</span>
# One Needs to Go to the Bathroom and Wants to Eat Bread
## The Issue
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Going to the bathroom and washing for bread at the same time sets up a conundrum. Possibilities and their problems are presented:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One washes for the bread, makes an asher yatzar, and then makes an על netilas yadayim. The problem with this is that this creates a hefsek between the washing and the ברכה.&lrm;[^45]</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One washes for the bread, makes an על netilas yadayim, and then makes an asher yatzar. The problem with this is that this creates a hefsek between the washing and eating the bread.[^45]</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One only makes the asher yatzar after washing for bread, and eating it, (making the על netilas yadayim and the hamotzi at the usual times). The problem with this is that there is a long wait between going to the bathroom and making the asher yatzar.[^45]</span>
## Solution
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One washes his hands to make them clean (doesn't wash multiple times with a cup) and makes an asher yatzar. Then the person washes again and makes an על netilas (followed by hamotzi and eating the bread).[^46]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One doesn't wash with a cup the first time because if one does, one wouldn't make a ברכה on the second washing,[^45] as [[#One's Hands Were Already Washed|discussed]].</span>
## If One Already Washed
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one washed his hands after leaving the bathroom for bread, at that point they should make an על netilas yadayim followed by an asher yatzar and the hamotzi, as many say that asher yatzar is not considered to be a hefsek between the על netilas yadayim and the hamotzi as they are all involved in being metaher one's hands. {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">I am unsure about what happens if one washed his hands with a cup as one does when leaving the bathroom. Is the הלכה the same?</span>}</span>
## If One Goes to the Bathroom in Middle of a Meal
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one goes to the bathroom in middle of the meal, they need to wash again, as [[#One's Hands Got Dirty in Middle of a Meal|discussed]].</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">They should wash for bread, make an על netilas yadayim</span>
# Rewards and Punishments
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One has to be careful with these הלכות. One who does not is חייב נדוי, נעקר מן העולם, and becomes poor.[^47]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The more water one uses, the more reward one sees from doing this mitzvah (definitely wealth, and perhaps other things). However, one should perform this mitzvah for the sake of Hashem, not for reward, as we say about all מצוות.&lrm;[^48]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one does not see reward from this mitzvah, this is because they view the mitzvah as a burden.[^49]</span>
