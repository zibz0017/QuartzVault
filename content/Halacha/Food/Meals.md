# By When One Should Eat Breakfast
## Regular People
1. One should make sure to eat by the beginning of the 4th shaos zman into the day. Otherwise, it is like "throwing a stone into a wine pouch" (seemingly, it isn't healthy).[^1]
## Someone Learning
1. Should eat by the beginning of the 6th shaos zman.[^1]
## What Counts
1. Eating a little does help.[^2] {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">What about drinking?</span>}
2. However, one should still not go past the beginning of the 6th shaos zman without eating a meal. [^2]
## Latest
3. One should absolutely make sure not to go past the end of the 6th shaos zman.[^3]
# How Many Meals in a Day
1. One should eat one meal by day and one by night.[^4]
2. If one is weak, they should have an additional meal by day.[^4]
3. One's big meal should be at night.[^4]
# Preparing Food in Advance
1. One should not prepare food from one day to the next. This is to force a person to really rely on Hashem for their food.[^4]
# Before Eating
1. One should defecate before eating a meal.[^4]
