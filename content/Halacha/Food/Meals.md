# By When One Should Eat Breakfast
## Regular People
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should make sure to eat by the beginning of the 4th שעות זמן into the day. Otherwise, it is like "throwing a stone into a wine pouch" (seemingly, it isn't healthy).[^1]</span>
## Someone Learning
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Should eat by the beginning of the 6th שעות זמן.&lrm;[^1]</span>
## What Counts
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Eating a little does help.[^2] {<span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">What about drinking?</span>}</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, one should still not go past the beginning of the 6th שעות זמן without eating a meal. [^2]</span>
## Latest
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should absolutely make sure not to go past the end of the 6th שעות זמן.&lrm;[^3]</span>
# How Many Meals in a Day
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should eat one meal by day and one by night.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is weak, they should have an additional meal by day.[^4]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One's big meal should be at night.[^4]</span>
# Preparing Food in Advance
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should not prepare food from one day to the next. This is to force a person to really rely on Hashem for their food.[^4]</span>
# Before Eating
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One should defecate before eating a meal.[^4]</span>
