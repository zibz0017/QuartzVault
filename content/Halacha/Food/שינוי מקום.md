# &lrm;הלכה If One is משנה מקום
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is משנה מקום, they have to make a new ברכה rishona before continuing the meal, but they do not need to make a ברכה achrona before continuing. They just make a ברכה achrona at the end of the second part of the meal and that counts for both parts.[^1]</span>
# Different Changes of Location
## Inside a House to Outside
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This depends on whether one can see the original location that one made his ברכה rishona. If one can see his original location, this is not considered שינוי מקום. If one can't see his original location, this is considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^2]</span><span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">

2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It doesn't matter if the person comes back or wants to eat in a new place.[^3]</span>

### Leaving Before ברכה Achrona
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">For bread, the person would have to return to bentch.[^4]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is better not to leave before bentching.[^4]</span>
## Room to Room Within the Same House
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one had in mind to do this at the time of the ברכה, this is not considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^5]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As long as one has in mind to change rooms (to a specific location or not) this is considered having this in mind. This is even if one had one room in mind, and went to a different room instead.[^6]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one sees the original place (or close to it) that he made his ברכה, this is not considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^7]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">There is a מחלוקת if this means that one can see the actual place (or close to it) that the person made the ברכה, or just see any part of the room that the person ברכה made the ברכה.&lrm;[^8] As such, it seems that one should try to make sure that they can still see the original place, rather than just the room (or make a ברכה achrona before leaving).</span>
5. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">בדיעבד, one can change rooms even if he didn't have it in mind when one made their ברכה and can't see their original place. He doesn't make a new ברכה. לכתחילה, though, the person should have in mind when making the ברכה originally.[^9]</span>
## Within the Same Room
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is not considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^2]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">This is even if the room is very large and/or there are mechitzos between the person's original and current location.[^10]</span>
## Different Locations Outside
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one moved from one "garden" to another, this is like going from [[#Inside a House to Outside|inside a house to outside]] (it is considered שינוי מקום).&lrm;[^11]</span>
## In All Cases
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">In cases where the change of location is considered שינוי מקום, this is even if one leaves and comes back immediately.[^12]</span>
## Additional Cases Where These הלכות Apply
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a person made a brachs rishona and then changed locations, the standard הלכות of שינוי מקום apply. For example, if a person made a ברכה rishona on a food and then changes rooms, the standard הלכות of שינוי מקום for going from room to room apply.[^11]</span> ^fd5f6c
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a person is walking around eating his food the standard הלכות of שינוי מקום apply since he didn't have this change of location in mind originally[^11]</span>
## Eating on the Move
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a person never established his original location (he was eating on the move and had in mind to change locations) then there is no problem of שינוי מקום, even if the person goes from inside to outside and can't see his original location.[^13]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a person had in mind to eat just a little bit before leaving, this is not considered to be establishing one's location, and so one would not need to make a new ברכה.&lrm;[^13]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one had in mind to leave, even if he did not leave yet, once he does some action that makes it clear that he is about to leave (he stands up, for example), this is considered as if he left. As such, if one eats at this point, this is not considered to be שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^14]</span>
# Potential Cases of הסח הדעת
## Note
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are not talking about the הלכות of שינוי מקום in these cases. We are just talking about whether these cases are considered הסח הדעת. Just because something is not considered הסח הדעת does not mean that you don't need to make a new ברכה rishona as it still may be שינוי מקום.</span>
## Taking a Nap
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If sleep overtakes someone (meaning, they just fall asleep), one does not need to make a new ברכה rishona, even if they slept for an hour. However, they will need to wash netilas yadayim, but without a ברכה.&lrm;[^15]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone goes to take a nap in bed, one does need to make a new ברכה rishona.[^16]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason for the difference is that in the first case, this is not considered to be hesach hadaas. The person didn't decide to break his meal, he was just overtaken by sleep. In the second case, the person made a decision. As such, that is considered הסח הדעת.&lrm;[^16]</span>
## Going to the Bathroom in Middle of the Meal
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">One does not need to make a new ברכה rishona if one goes to the bathroom in middle of a meal.[^17] However, he will need to wash netilas yadayim.[^18]</span>
# Eating in a Group
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone is eating a meal as part of a group and the group leaves in middle of the meal, if at least one person stays behind then this is not considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^19]</span>

## Eating in a New Location
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone goes and eats in a new location, leaving people behind doesn't help. This would be considered שינוי מקום (depending on [[#Different Kinds of Foods|what the person ate]].)[^20]</span>
# Different Kinds of Foods
## Hamotzi and Mezonos
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If someone ate at least a kezayis food that requires a ברכה in its original place (mezonos and hamotzi), it is not considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^21]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the person ate less than a kezayis, they do not need to go back to their original place to make a ברכה achrona. As such, if they change places, this is considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^21]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If a person left and was meisiach דעת, even if they ate a kezayis of bread/mezonos, this is still considered שינוי מקום.&lrm;[^2]</span>
### Changing Location
#### &lrm;לכתחילה
##### Has in Mind Initially
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one plans on changing locations during a hamotzi or mezonos meal, they should have this in mind when making hamotzi/mezonos. The person is then permitted to change locations and continue his meal there, לכתחילה.&lrm;[^22]</span>
##### Appoints a Shomer/Sets an Alarm
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">According the Rav Chaim Pinchas Scheinberg, if someone appoints a shomer or sets an alarm to remind him to go back to his original place and make a ברכה achrona there, this is fine, לכתחילה.&lrm;[^23]</span>
##### Comes Back Immediately
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A person may leave and come back immediately ("immediately" means "ממש מיד"), לכתחילה.&lrm;[^24]</span>
#### &lrm;בדיעבד
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one did not have in mind to change locations when they made their ברכה rishona, it is preferable for them not to change locations.[^25] This is because:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are concerned that the person may forget to make a ברכה achrona.[^25]</span> ^47852b
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are concerned that the person may accidentally make a ברכה achrona in the new location.[^25]</span>
	3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even if the person knows he is going to eat food in the new location, this is still not preferable to bentch there (meaning, the person should make a ברכה achrona there if he did eat there, but it would have been preferable for the person not to have eaten there and instead made a ברכה achrona in his original location).[^25]</span> ^eaebb7
	4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Even though it is a בדיעבד situation, one does not need to make a new ברכה rishona when one comes back.[^26]</span>
##### &lrm;ברכה Achrona Before Leaving
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is confident that they will come back within the time to continue the meal (see [[Bracha Achrona#How Long One Can Wait Before Making a ברכה Achrona|here]] for those הלכות) and they want to continue eating, they should not make a ברכה achrona before leaving because this can lead to a shayla of ברכה she'eino tzricha.[^27]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one is unsure if they will make it back in time, they should make a ברכה achrona before leaving.[^27]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If there is a mitva overes (like it is about to be זמן מנחה) and the person doesn't have time to bentch before leaving, then it is preferable for the person to leave, take care of the מצוה, and then come back.[^28] (I assume this is where the person is confident that they can make it back before the time to make a ברכה achrona expires. I would assume that if the person was not confident, then they should not miss bentching for the מצוה, but I am not sure.[^29])</span>

### &lrm;ברכה Achrona Before Leaving
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It is preferred that one makes a ברכה achrona before changing locations, as we are concerned that one may accidentally forget to make one, [[#^47852b|as discussed]] in regards to bread/mezonos.[^31]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">However, if one comes back immediately, this is fine, לכתחילה.&lrm;[^32]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">It would seem that if one has a reminder to make a ברכה achrona in the new location, it would be fine to leave before making a ברכה achrona, as well. This would be obvious [[#Appoints a Shomer/Sets an Alarm|according to Rav Scheinberg]], but even the משנה ברורה would seem to agree with this as he said that the concern was that a person would forget to make a ברכה achrona, or make it in the new location (see [[#&lrm;בדיעבד|here]]). However, if one has a reminder, this is not an issue (and even the משנה ברורה over [[#^eaebb7|here]] seems to agree with this), and when it comes to these kinds of foods, there is no issue of making the ברכה achrona in the new location.[^29]</span>

#### Surrounded by Walls
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">A garden surrounded by walls is considered one room. As such, if one eats on one side of a tree and moves around to the other side of the tree, one does not need to make a new ברכה, even if they didn't have this in mind originally.[^36]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If one goes from tree to tree, it is a מחלוקת if they needed to have in mind originally.[^37]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The reason for this is because we say that in usual cases when a person is in a room, when he makes a ברכה, it is assumed that the person will eat anywhere in the room. There is an automatic דעת that this is what the person intends. However, in the case of tree to tree, this is not necessarily expected. As such, the person needs to have this in mind originally.[^39]</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">As such it seems that when eating outdoors in an area surrounded by walls, it seems that whether one requires דעת originally may depend on what is expected, and this may depend on how big the area is (although perhaps not. If he means literally, then the difference between the case of one side of the tree to the other and the case of tree to tree is simple. The case of tree to tree is like going from room to room, while the case of one side of the tree to the other is like being in the same room. The question on this is *why* this is. Why would the case of one side of the tree to the other be like within the same room while going from tree to tree be like going from room to room?</span>
