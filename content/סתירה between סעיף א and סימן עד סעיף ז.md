# Question
- In סימן עד סעיף ז we say that if someone cuts a piece of meat in a כלי ראשון with a בן יומו dairy knife, they only need to remove כדי קליפה.
# Answer
- This is because in that case, the בליעות really spread through the whole meat, and the case is that the meat is 60x the amount of the בליעות or shamnunis on the knife. Only for a technicality, does one even need to remove the קליפה.
