# Changing the Terms of Probation
## If the Terms Were not Violated
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Court may add/remove restrictions and restrictions to probation at any time throughout the person's sentence. This is even if the terms were not violated and the person did not commit an additional crime.[^1]</span>
## If the Terms Were Violated
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If the terms of the probation were violated (like if the terms of the probation were that the person should stay sober and they did not), or they committed a crime, the court can impose additional restrictions, lengthen the probation, or cancel probation and send the person to prison, instead.[^1]</span>
