# Lavishness of the Kollel אור ישראל Dinner
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The Kollel אור ישראל dinner is incredibly fancy. However, Rabbi Hodkin is not concerned about the excessive gashmius because:</span>
	1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">We are dealing with כבוד התורה and the sacrifices made by the kollel members their wives. There is no honor that is too great for them.</span>
	2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The kollel is largely dealing with peoeple in Queens. The nisayon for people in Queens is not an abundance of gashmius.</span>
