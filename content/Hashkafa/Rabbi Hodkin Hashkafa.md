# Lavishness of the Kollel אור ישראל Dinner
1. The Kollel אור ישראל dinner is incredibly fancy. However, Rabbi Hodkin is not concerned about the excessive gashmius because:
	1. We are dealing with כבוד התורה and the sacrifices made by the kollel members their wives. There is no honor that is too great for them.
	2. The kollel is largely dealing with peoeple in Queens. The nisayon for people in Queens is not an abundance of gashmius.
