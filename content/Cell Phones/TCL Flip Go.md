# Cons
1. No customizable T9 dictionary, meaning, you can't add your own words to the dictionary. In addition, some common contractions (like "can't" and "isn't") are not in the T9 dictionary.[^1]
2. Alarm time is tied system volume, meaning, if the phone is on silent, or a specific volume, the alarm will go off on that volume. You can't set the alarm to go off on a specific volume if the phone is on a different volume, or only on vibrate.[^1]
3. Forwarding texts rarely works, and there is no way to copy texts.[^1]
