# Cons
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">No customizable T9 dictionary, meaning, you can't add your own words to the dictionary. In addition, some common contractions (like "can't" and "isn't") are not in the T9 dictionary.[^1]</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Alarm time is tied system volume, meaning, if the phone is on silent, or a specific volume, the alarm will go off on that volume. You can't set the alarm to go off on a specific volume if the phone is on a different volume, or only on vibrate.[^1]</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Forwarding texts rarely works, and there is no way to copy texts.[^1]</span>
