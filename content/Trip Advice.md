# Airbnb
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Trust the reviews over the pictures. Even if the pictures look glamorous, the reviews are probably more accurate.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">The house is important, even if you plan on being out all day.</span>
3. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">If you stay in a more country-like area, there will probably be bugs in the house.</span>
4. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You may not get around to using an outdoor hot tub (if there is one) in the summer. As such, it may not be a good idea to choose one house over a different one because of a hot tub.</span>
# Packing
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Don't bring everything. You can probably buy stuff there, especially with Target Shipt/Walmart+/Amazon Fresh.</span>
# Cooking on the Go
1. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">You can't plug a grill or a Betty Crocker or some similar device into a power converter in your car.</span>
2. <span style="display: block; text-align: justify;">Some parks have outlets in covered picnic areas. Perhaps those can be used. However, you may need a few feet of extension cord as the outlets are sometimes a few feet off the ground and away from the nearest table.</span>
